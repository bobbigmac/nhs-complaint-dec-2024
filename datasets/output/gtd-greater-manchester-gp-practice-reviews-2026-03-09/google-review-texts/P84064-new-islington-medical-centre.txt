I’ve recently joined New Islington Medical Practice as I started to take more responsibility for my health issues, which lingered over the years. I’ve had a 10/10 experience through every consultation and clinician I’ve encountered so far. The facilities are clean and modern, and the reception/back office staff have been very polite and prompt in responses. The new appointment booking system has been very efficient. Having many personal/professional experiences at other GP practices as a GP doctor myself - I have to say New Islington Medical Practice is by far one of the best that I’ve encountered within the region. The waiting times are optimal. I’ve had multiple health issues which have either been resolved or I am now receiving further support for. For sensitive issues, the clinicians are non-judgemental and empathetic in their approach which makes a huge difference. Kudos to Dee and management for their successes thus far and keep up the great work.
