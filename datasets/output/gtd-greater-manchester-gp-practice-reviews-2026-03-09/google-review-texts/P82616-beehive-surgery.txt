Author: N Bolton
Date: 6 years ago
Rating: 1 star
Whilst the service has improved since Dr Caswell’s departure this practice is inconsistent when delivering good patient care. I have been at this surgery for over 5 years. It is not unusual to wait 2 weeks for an appointment unless you push for a same day appointment. However if you have work or family commitments as most people do it isn’t always possible to drop everything at such short notice. Seldom seen at appointment time, sometimes nearly an hour later. Incredibly inconvenient when I’ve just ‘nipped out’ of work and return 2 hours later. You can ring at 8am or 3pm for a same day appointment but they aren’t guaranteed. You can be on hold for up to 10 minutes before you receive an infuriating message telling you the practice is now closed when it is not. I no longer telephone but go in in person as I can’t be ignored then. Some receptionists are pleasant, some are rude, sometimes the pleasant ones are rude! I have used BARDOC myself without prompting from the practice as I can not rely on an out-of-hours service. I have been told twice recently there are no appointments during an acute episode requiring an emergency prescription. The male doctor seems more open to further testing and investigations when presenting with what could be symptoms of a potentially serious or underlying condition ; The female doctor not so much. Money appears to play a part in decisions, such as what medication is prescribed or potential tests to be carried out. I don’t need to know the cost of a blood test just if I need one thanks. On one occasion I had to ask the nurse to close the curtains upstairs in a room that faces residential properties , whilst removing my clothing and underwear. I shouldn’t have to ask. The building is currently being refurbished but would be irrelevant to me if I was receiving good quality care. I have also often partly heard consultations through the walls/doors when stood by reception or in the waiting room. They should invest in sound proofing as consultations should be private and confidential. My biggest concern is inconsistent care, as sometimes I feel satisfied and others times I feel fobbed off or just a patient number. The service is not personal and doctors don’t seem to familiarise themselves with your notes. I work in the NHS and patient numbers here are low compared to other surgeries across Bolton, where I have experienced knowledgeable and proactive doctors who appear to know the patient even if it’s just because they have skimmed the medical notes. Would not recommend this surgery if you have high expectations or previous experience at a good surgery as you will be disappointed. Never mind the building Beehive Surgery, invest in some customer service training and learn the value of your patients.
