Author: PC Mumble
Date: Edited 8 months ago
Rating: 4 stars
I am updating my review because after speaking to various friends who visit other GP surgeries across Trafford, I appear to be quite lucky with Washway Road. My friends have awful problems getting someone to pick up a phone, they're always number 20+ in the queue, they can't get an appointment. I rarely have to wait to speak to a receptionist, they have some lovely ones who answer the phones these days, very helpful. I have had some very pleasant experiences and interactions with both admin staff and GPs over the last 6 months so I actually cannot complain. The AskMyGP system seems to work well now they have changed it to logging a request between 8 and 10, it's no longer a race to get your request in before it closes/gets full. The on site pharmacist is very good, he can resolve a lot of medication queries so you don't have to wait to see a GP. ******* GPs do not take women's pain seriously. Was ignored and told that 3 periods in a month was absolutely nothing to worry about and was offered a flu jab instead. Completely uninterested so had to go private and seek a second opinion. Currently recovering from a total hysterectomy inc. ovaries and removal of severe stage 4 endometriosis. I also had Adenomyosis. When I wrote to advise them of this, they couldn't even be bothered acknowledging me. This practice has gone totally downhill in the last year or so. Utter disgrace. Edit - it's worth noting this same review was rejected by the NHS website 'because it includes a serious complaint about a specific practice'. So do not trust their NHS review score.
