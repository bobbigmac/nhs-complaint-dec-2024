I had a very distressing experience at Medlock Medical Practice. I arrived on time for my blood test and waited as instructed, while several patients who arrived after me were seen first. After over 20 minutes, I calmly approached reception to ask for reassurance, as I was worried I had been forgotten. I am currently receiving treatment for anxiety and depression. Instead of reassurance, I was spoken to harshly and repeatedly called “rude” in front of other patients. English is not my first language, and I believe there was a misunderstanding, but my attempt to explain was dismissed. I was left feeling humiliated and extremely distressed and broke down in tears during my appointment. I would strongly encourage the practice to provide staff with additional training in compassionate communication, mental health awareness, and supporting patients with language barriers. Healthcare settings should feel safe and supportive, especially for vulnerable patients. Unfortunately, that was not my experience.
