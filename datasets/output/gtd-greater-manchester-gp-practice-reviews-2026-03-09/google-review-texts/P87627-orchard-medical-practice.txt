Author: Skeet
Date: 6 years ago
Rating: 5 stars
I have been with Orchard Medical Practice since the mid-1990’s. I came here because of dire medical consequences with my previous G.P. What a difference! I have always found that the reception staff, nurses and doctors who work here try and make my appointment as friendly and welcoming as they possibly can. Not all patients visiting may feel the same, due to their condition, but come on. They have a job to do and they do it very well. I feel like I’m treated as a person and not just a number, going through the motions. They haven’t, over the years, got everything 100% right, who hasn’t, yet when they do they have the decency to apologise and learn from it. The simplest way to book and cancel appointments and order repeat prescriptions is online via Patient Access (organised through the surgery) or through the ‘My GP’ app on your mobile. Appointments are readily available and if it’s urgent you may be able to pick up one that day by telephoning the surgery after 8.00 am for a five-minute appointment. With medication, it’s all laid out and everything just gets sent straight through to the pharmacist, waiting for pick up within 48hrs, maybe sooner. I feel that the doctors, nurses and associated health professionals attached to the surgery, are always looking after my best interests health-wise. I believe they put patients first. My family and I feel very fortunate to be patients here. This is a First Class surgery, make no mistake. Thank you so much, Howard.
