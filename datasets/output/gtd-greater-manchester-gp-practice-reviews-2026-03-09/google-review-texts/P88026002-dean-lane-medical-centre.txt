I ordered my repeat prescription via the app several days ago. When I attended the pharmacy, I was told the prescription had not been authorised by the doctor. On Thursday, I attended the surgery and was informed by the receptionist that the particular doctor was “not in”. I explained the urgency and asked for the prescription to be pushed through. I was then assured that my medication would be ready to collect from the pharmacy on Saturday morning. Today (Saturday 17/01/26), I attended the pharmacy at 1pm, only to find that it was closed. Both the pharmacy and the GP surgery are now closed. As of today, I have completely run out of my prescribed medication, including Ramipril and Atorvastatin. I have disabilities and suffer from high blood pressure, and being without this medication places my health at risk. I am extremely concerned that the online prescription service is being promoted, yet it is ineffective if prescriptions are not authorised in a timely manner. The system only works if doctors take responsibility for approving patients’ medication. I would like this matter urgently reviewed, an explanation provided as to how this situation was allowed to occur, and assurances that this does not happen again. I also request immediate advice on how I can obtain my medication as soon as possible. I look forward to your prompt response. Yours faithfully, S
