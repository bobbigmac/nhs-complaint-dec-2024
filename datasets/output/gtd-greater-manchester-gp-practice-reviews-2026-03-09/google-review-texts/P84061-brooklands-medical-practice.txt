Used to love this practice, the doctors and staff were brilliant, but many have left now. The new appointment booking system is awful. It's great if you have an urgent requirement for a doctor and don't mind who you see or when, as that is the only option. It is impossible to book a non-urgent appointment, with a specific doctor at a time that suits you. This means if you would like a follow up or review appointment, you have to know what days your doctor works, remember to fill in the form on that morning (before they reach capacity) and hope that the appointment you are given fits in with your schedule. Really not good if you work and/or have a chronic condition.

Author: Amrit Kaur Swalley

Rating: 1 star
During my call, I was advised by reception staff that I should take my mother to the dental hospital instead. This advice appeared to be a medical decision, which I found concerning, as reception staff are not clinically qualified to make such determinations. I later spoke with the reception manager, who confirmed that reception staff are permitted to make these decisions. This is unacceptable and raises serious concerns about patient safety and appropriate triage procedures. In addition, I do not feel that my complaint was handled effectively or taken seriously. There was no reassurance, clear explanation, or appropriate escalation of my concerns. I believe further training is required for reception staff, particularly regarding the limits of their role and the importance of not making or influencing medical decisions. As a patient, I expect clinical advice to be given by a qualified healthcare professional. This matter is particularly concerning given the Care Quality Commission (CQC) requirements, which state that patients must be treated safely, appropriately, and with clear clinical oversight. I trust that this issue will be reviewed promptly to ensure patients are not placed at risk in the future. Thank you
