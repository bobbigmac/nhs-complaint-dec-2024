Over the last few months I have needed a few appointments. To my surprise these appointments have been easy to get. Usually I phone between 8 and 9am, I've been number 1, 2 or 3 in the queue, but, I have still been given an appointment for the same day. For non urgent appointments, these have been offered at alternative times at Moorgate, which is helpful for work purpose.
