Author: margaret miller

Rating: 4 stars
Over the last few months I have needed a few appointments. To my surprise these appointments have been easy to get. Usually I phone between 8 and 9am, I've been number 1, 2 or 3 in the queue, but, I have still been given an appointment for the same day. For non urgent appointments, these have been offered at alternative times at Moorgate, which is helpful for work purpose. I have seen a few different doctors and each and every one of them has been lovely, efficient and responsive to my needs. They always have time to listen to you and wish you well. I've never felt rushed. The nurses who run the clinics and do bloods etc are so nice, it's good to chat and laugh with them. The office staff are always polite and ready to help if they can. The young man on reception has improved over the last couple of years. It's a good medical centre, though could do with decorating and making it feel a bit more welcoming.
