Lottery appointment system, You need to call between 8am to 8:30am to win a appointment, but everyone else is also calling at the same time. If your call actually connects during this period, congratulations you have a ticket that gets you a 30% chance of an appointment ( providing they have not all been taken already) , if not try the lottery again the next day. You have 1/5 chance for an appointment if you follow my advice. I have a hack for those needing urgent doctor, use all the phones you can get access to and call them one after another, one will likely get through. Edit: It is possible now to go into the doctors before 8 am to obtain a golden ( yellow ) ticket, which helps you bypass the lottery phone system and be seen the same day. While the lottery system is not ideal, I can see its a way to deal with a already struggling health system and therefore I am rating this place higher for finding a way to deal with a failing system.
