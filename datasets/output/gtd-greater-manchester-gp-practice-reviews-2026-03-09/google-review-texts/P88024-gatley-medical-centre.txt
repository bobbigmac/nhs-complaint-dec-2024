I have been with this practice since moving to Gatley 28 years ago. Both my children , now adults were cared for at Gatley medical practice and the same for my husband and I. In our current climate of underfunding and lack of resources this practice continues to provide an excellent and exceptional service to its patients. I have always got through to the practice and have managed to get an appointment 99% of the time. Reception staff have been helpful and friendly. I have always been treated with courtesy and dignity having a long-term condition. I do think people should be realistic. Use the pharmacy or 111 for advice. Urgent problems go to ED. The practice is not a walk in and expectations that someone should get a same day appointment then get annoyed because they can't is unfair on an already over stretched service doing their best for the local community. Its a 10/10 from me. Thank you Gatley Medical Centre
