Practice response:
Dear Matt Thank you for reaching out to share your concerns regarding your difficulty in securing a GP appointment over the past two days. To provide some context, General Practice currently manages around 90% of all NHS patient contacts, yet it receives in the region of 5-6% of the total NHS budget. This imbalance puts enormous pressure on practices, especially at a time when demand is at record levels and the number of GPs has not grown to match the needs of our communities. Nationally, funding per patient has not kept pace with inflation or the increasing complexity of healthcare needs, with practices receiving in real terms over 20% less funding since 2018 alone. Even though we are receiving less funding now, in that same time as an organisation we have: - Increased our registered list of patients by 34%, so we have increased the number of GP appointments by 43%. - Increased the number of pre-bookable appointments from 13% to 20%. - Have dedicated additional pre-bookable appointments to solely discuss results of tests (bloods and scans). - Invested in the wider team of clinicians to include practice pharmacists, physio services, Mental Health practitioner, social prescribers, and more to free up the GP’s to see patients for issues that only they can deal with - Increased the number of support staff. - Invested in technology to operationally become more efficient such a cloud-based telephony system. - Invested in the premises to expand the number of car park spaces, increase the number of consulting rooms and more to ensure that the patient experience is enhanced. That said, we recognise that these efforts may not always meet your expectations, especially during periods of peak demand. I am unsure if you have tried to book a pre-bookable appointment, but these are available for up to two weeks in advance. If you’re still unable to secure an appointment, I encourage you to first try to book an on the day appointment via the NHS app – the appointments open online at the same as the phone. You can also contact our reception team, who can guide you to alternative care pathways, such as NHS 111 or other local services, if appropriate. Please remember, that we ourselves and our families are patients also – we are on the same side of this national crisis. Until General Practice receives the funding to enable us to provide more for our patients then unfortunately, we cannot do so. Thank you for taking the time to raise your concerns. I would strongly advocate you speaking to our local MP, Graham Stringer, for him to discuss this crisis more with Wes Streeting, the Secretary of State of Health and Social Care, to see what he can do for us all. Kind regards Mike Neville Managing Partner

Practice response:
Dear Ntombizodwa Thank you for taking the time to share your experience. We are deeply sorry to hear that your interactions with our practice have been frustrating and that we’ve fallen short of your expectations. Your feedback is valuable and helps us understand where we need to improve. Firstly, we’d like to address the issue of phone access. The individuals answering calls at our practice are not nurses but trained care navigators. They work diligently under significant pressure to handle a high volume of calls, often within limited resources. We understand that long wait times and rushed interactions can feel impersonal, and we are exploring ways to improve phone communication, including encouraging the use of our online tools such as e-consults, which allow you to contact us without needing to wait on the phone. These tools are designed to streamline the process and ensure that your concerns are addressed promptly. Regarding appointment availability, we recognize that it can be challenging to secure a slot due to high demand. This is a widespread issue resulting from chronic underfunding in general practice, which has limited the number of appointments we can offer relative to the growing needs of our community. Since 2018, in real terms out funding has reduced by 20%, yet in that time, we have increased our capacity of appointments by 33% (with a reduced budget). We are continuously advocating for increased resources to better serve our patients, but we acknowledge this doesn’t ease the immediate frustrations. The only persons who can change this are MP’s and specifically Health and Social Care secretary Wes Streeting, as well as Rachel Reeves – chancellor of the exchequer – so we would fully support any letter to our local MP, Graham Stringer, explaining your dissatisfaction of the chronic underfunding of general practice. The two-reasons limit per appointment is a policy designed to ensure that every patient is seen thoroughly and within the allotted time. General practitioners are allocated 10 minutes per appointment, and addressing more than two concerns often requires more time than is available, potentially impacting the care of subsequent patients. We understand that this can feel restrictive, but it is a necessary measure to balance the needs of all patients. We also have notices up in the practice that do state that if you need to discuss more than two, then you should inform the team and they can offer you a double appointment if necessary. Finally, we regret that you felt unsupported during your consultation. Our aim is always to work collaboratively with patients to understand their symptoms and provide the best possible care. If it is that case that avenues have already been explored that a patient does not agree with, more often than not, the individual has a reason or diagnosis in mind themselves, so a clinician will ask this to either explain why it cannot be the case, or you may provide further information that may support further investigation. We are sorry that you feel that this was not your experience and will use your feedback as a reminder to our team of the importance of clear, empathetic communication. We truly appreciate your patience and understanding as we work within the challenges of a constrained healthcare system. If you would like to discuss your concerns further or share specific details to help us improve, please don’t hesitate to contact us directly. Kind regards Mike Neville Managing Partner
