Thank you for your review We understand that you may feel frustrated, however the tone and comments within your message about our staff are not acceptable. At the time of your request, your symptoms were assessed as non-urgent and a routine appointment was offered. We very rarely offer telephone appointments, so this would have been selected as the most appropriate way for a clinician to review your concerns initially. We have some of the best access in the city.
