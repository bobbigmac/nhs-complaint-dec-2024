Author: L B

Rating: 4 stars
GP's and Nurses are brilliant. Always friendly and easy to talk to. Both nurses I've seen there have been bubbly, happy and chatty and put me at ease, even when having a cervical screening :') One of the reception staff comes across very rude on the phone at times which isn't great when you're ringing up about mental health issues. She has at other times when I've physically been in the surgery been fantastic and extremely helpful. I can sympathise with them to an extent as they must get a lot of abuse but it shouldn't be pushed onto others. When you're physically in the building though they're all spot on. Struggle to get through and get appointments if you don't keep trying to ring at 08:00. They do seem to have introduced a phone queuing system which helps massively. With there only being a limited amount of appointments I think the reception staff need to be firmer in turning time wasters away and directing them to the correct place (pharmacy/a&e/self care). Also, they should tell people who repeatedly don't attend their appointments and dont have the decency to advise reception they will be struck off if they don't have a valid reason. Surgery is bright, airy and clean. Chairs could do with being spread out a bit more in my opinion but I'm claustrophobic so perhaps a bit biased. Overall, best doctors surgery I've been too in Heywood and I would recommend. All surgeries seem equally difficult to get appointments so can't hold that against them. As for the occasionally rude receptionist, this is just my experience and how I perceive something that's been said and that will differ from person to person. We all have bad days, we're only human :)
