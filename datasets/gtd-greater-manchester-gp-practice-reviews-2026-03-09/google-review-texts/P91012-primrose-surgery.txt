I've been here for my whole life, I used to give 1 star but I do believe some aspects of the surgery have changed in recent years which is why I changed to a 3. A lot of the doctors here are absolutely lovely and compassionate (especially with mental health) and I cannot fault them on how they have cared for me - for this I would like to give a special mention to Dr Graham and Dr Duncan (this could be due to how recent they qualified as a GP and there now being more awareness of mental health). For anything fertility related Dr Wareing is next to none also! Dr Heron I've struggled with for years due to past experiences and I will not see him, however in pregnancy he has been decent which shows he does take SOME things seriously and is willing to help. The prescription service is very fast and all of my repeats have normally been approved and sent within an hour of requesting. I thought the reception staff had improved over the last year or so however recently they have gone back downhill and are back to being the stereotypical NHS dragons on reception who refuse to help or even acknowledge you past 9.01am when the slots are full - I have even had a GP apologise for this! However it must be said that for me the real star of the show in primrose is Nurse Sharon! She is one of the most kind and compassionate people you could ever meet and I'm sure anyone who has ever crossed paths with her would agree!
