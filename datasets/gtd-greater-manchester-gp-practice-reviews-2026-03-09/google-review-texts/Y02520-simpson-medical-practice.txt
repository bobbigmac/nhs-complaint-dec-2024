A call was initiated to ascertain why an appointment for a mother and child was declined by a GP, providing clinical advice to the service user, and you are not part of the medical team. NHS staff must not unreasonably deny access to services, and patients are entitled to be treated with dignity, receive clear information, and file a formal complaint regarding inadequate care or discourteous conduct. However, a staff member and a receptionist unjustly canceled an appointment, and I have requested the call log. Non-clinical reception staff should not act as if they are GPs; clinical decisions regarding whether a baby or parent requires a review should be made by a qualified health professional (GP, nurse, ANP) following appropriate triage protocols. Professional guidance underscores that healthcare professionals must not unreasonably refuse a patient access to care that addresses their needs and must communicate with kindness and clarity, particularly with vulnerable patients such as parents of very young children. A receptionist informing a practice or service user that she does not consult a GP also provides clinical advice to the service user. Merely asserting that a one-year-old “does not need to see a GP” without a proper clinical evaluation or justification is unlikely to adhere to good practice standards regarding communication and access to care. It is unfortunate that this matter is not being addressed; I intend to escalate this issue further due to the appalling service.

I am always really happy with and satisfied with all interactions with the surgery, and actually recommend it to others. The 2 main doctors and advanced clinician are always very good and are through, listen and take concerns seriously. The patches system is very good and easy to use - myself and spouse have had to use it a few times recently, and we have always then been contacted with an appropriate appointment, if that was face to face, urgent phone, or routine that has always happened quickly. There are some people complaining about lack of appointments etc, I have never found this to be the case. I work in primary care and regularly deal with different GP surgeries, and believe me, simpson medical are one of the better ones!! Perhaps everyone needs to have realistic expectations, and consider that there is high demand for healthcare, which exceeds capacity. The old days of face to face appointments are gone, unless it is necessary this does not happen anymore - often phone calls, including photos etc are often sufficent, and more a more efficient use of staff resources. If a face to face appointment is necessary it will be made whilst anything regarding mental health has always been taking seriously and urgently. I am very happy with being a patient here, and think given the demand they do a good job, and are one of the better surgeries I deal with. Thank you to the staff, I appreciate it
