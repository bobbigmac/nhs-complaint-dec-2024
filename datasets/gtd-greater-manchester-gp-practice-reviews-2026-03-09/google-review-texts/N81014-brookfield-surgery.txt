Author: Eric Quirk
Date: 4 months ago
Rating: 5 stars
When you (as an adult) really need urgent attention, Brookfield is excellent. Twice I have had real concerns as a result of a test or chest pains. Each time I was given the choice of a same day face to face or telephone appointment (after the 8am rush hour period). If you need more than 9 minutes interview it is readily available. Caring and thoughtful doctors and staff (including many on reception) despite the heavy pressure of increasing patient numbers. Also often answering queries on the phone.
