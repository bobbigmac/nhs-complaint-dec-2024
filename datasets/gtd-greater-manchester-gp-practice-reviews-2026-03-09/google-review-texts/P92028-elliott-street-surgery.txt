I, and all my family have been lucky enough to be a patient at Elliott Street Surgery for over 44 years, and the level of care we receive has always been outstanding. It has never failed to be anything less than 100%. When Mum was finally discharged to bed rest at home, from Hospital after a new days, she was complaining that the feet were sore I thought she could be developing bed sores, I called the Surgery at 5.00pm, and the next day at 9.00am a Nurse called to examine Mum when she put us at ease and luckily, it was not bed sores! A week later I was worried about Mum again as her good leg swelled extremely fast at the foot and doubled in size - so I called the Surgery and I was called back within 5 minutes to say the Practice was closing in 30 minutes but Dr Shah would personally visit Mum on his way home and he did within 40 minutes, the door bell rang and Dr Shah was stood waiting to see Mum – I initially thought she had a blood clot in her leg, Dr Shah thoroughly examined her for over 10 minutes and explained things to us in great depth, he explained the swelling could be just because she has had the operation and was bed bound and not able to walk and be mobile, that was one scenario or, it could be water retention and a few other scenarios were discussed too. Dr Shah advised he would send a Nurse to collect Mums bloods the next day to see what the bloods revealed if anything. The next day, sure as his word a nurse attended to see Mum – bloods were taken and sent off. The next day we were called that evening by Dr Shah to say the results had not come back yet and he would call us the next day. That evening a Locum GP Called and said the bloods were elevated and we needed to go for a scan an appointment was made for 2 days later. That day, Dr Shah called to discuss Mums blood results, and I explained what had happened. Anyway, as a precautionary measure, a scan internally and Mum - no clot Dr Shah and all the staff, have always taken the time to listen carefully, explain everything clearly, and make sure we all felt reassured at every stage of Mums recovery. Nothing ever felt rushed and every concern — big or small — was treated with genuine care. It’s rare to find someone who combines medical expertise with such kindness and patience. Thanks to their guidance, my mum has felt safe, supported, and confident throughout her healing process. We are incredibly grateful for the dedication and humanity shown during what could have been a very stressful time. The team ALWAYS go above and beyond to make patients feel heard and supported and welcome, even during busy periods. They are exceedingly compassionate. The Practice is also great for reminding you that you need to see the nurses for check-ups and also medication reviews etc. Another point I wish to make is they have been great with Mum as she has smoked for 68 years, and the Practice now supports her with patches so to my amazement, she has not smoked for over 2 months The reception staff deserve special recognition too. Their kindness sets the tone from the moment you walk through the door. This review seems mainly to be about my Mums care at this difficult chapter in our lives but when I or any of family ever need to see the Doctors, everything is always made so easy with so much empathy. I have never in 44 years experienced anything negative from the Practice. Our Community Practice is a true example of what excellent healthcare should look like. Again, I’m incredibly grateful for the dedication and hard work of the entire team. I would 100% Highly recommended. My final thought… In a time when NHS services are under pressure, this practice stands out as a true asset to the area. I feel fortunate to have such exceptional care right on my doorstep. Thank you, Dr Shah and your Wonderful Team x,
