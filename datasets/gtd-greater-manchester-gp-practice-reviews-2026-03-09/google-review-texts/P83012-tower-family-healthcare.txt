Author: Paul Rimmer
Date: a year ago
Rating: 1 star
Simply cannot get an appointment to see a GP at this place now. Call in or phone up during the day and you'll be told (politely, in fairness to the receptionist) that there are none available so you'll have to phone them at 8am the next morning. So you call at 8am the next morning, and of course there are dozens of other people doing the same thing. Sit in the queue and you'll be told there are "more than 30" people ahead of you. Wait patiently for 20 minutes, then get cut off. Call back and be told again that there are "more than 30" people ahead of you. If you choose the option to get a call back, they'll send you an SMS with a link to a website where you can see exactly where you are in the queue. I did this the other day and I was 113th in the queue. That's not a typo: one hundred and thirteenth. So that "more than 30" you hear while you wait patiently, could mean there are 31 callers ahead of you, or it could be 112 callers. Or more. Go on the app to book an appointment and, if you're lucky, there'll be one or two available - in a month's time. I guess if you're going to fall ill and need to see a doctor, plan it a month in advance.
