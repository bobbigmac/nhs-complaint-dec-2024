Access to this surgery is extremely poor. It is almost impossible to secure an appointment that isn’t same-day, and online forms are frequently unavailable. The system is clearly not designed for working patients and has repeatedly left me with no option but to attend the Woking emergency department. The standard of care has been equally disappointing. Diagnostic processes are slow, and on multiple occasions I was not physically examined at all. Instead, consultations focused on what outcome I was “hoping for,” followed by referrals rather than assessment. This does not inspire confidence and feels like a box-ticking exercise rather than proper medical care. Based on my experience, I would strongly caution others before registering here!!!
