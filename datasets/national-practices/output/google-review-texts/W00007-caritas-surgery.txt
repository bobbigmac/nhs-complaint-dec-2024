Author: Grant Rogers
Date: Edited 11 months ago
Rating: 5 stars
I have never been so grateful to the hard work and effort the staff here make at the surgery. The pressure they must be under is intense. I like the fact that I can request prescriptions/alterations to my medication via the NHS Wales app which keeps the phone lines free for more important patients requiring more urgent care. You actually get a timely response and informed on when these requests have been passed over to a doctor for review not left hanging around wondering if your request is sat in a long admin queue. They have always been good to help me with my on going medical problems. They have never refused me an appointment if I have asked for one urgently. They do their best for telephone triage if they can. I fully support the use of the website "Klinik system" to be able to get alot of things done without having to jam up the phones when I need to make an appointment. (subject to availability). In my view this is great to enable the reception staff to keep phone lines free for others who might struggle with technical applications or for those who may want to discuss their requirements over the phone. Nobody enjoys being on hold. Sometimes the parking can be abit hit and miss but that does not reflect the standards of the practice. Inside the surgery it looks very clean and a safe distanced environment. Unfortunately not a great deal of privacy is available at reception to discuss anything as jthe waiting room chairs are immediately behind you so nothing can be discreet but I'd probably write it down if it was that personal and hand it over to them. Hand sanitiser in the main entrance has once or twice been empty which maybe just needs a closer eye on but thats my own personal preference. As a whole to date, I fully respect this surgery and its staff for the hard work and effort it provides it's patients and I do feel safe being with them. Please note all of my feedback is about the Caritas surgery at Coedpoeth only. I have never been to the Cefn Mawr branch to provide any feedback. Hope you found my review helpful and informative.
