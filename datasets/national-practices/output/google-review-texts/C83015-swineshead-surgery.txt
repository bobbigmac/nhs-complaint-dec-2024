I have experience of the surgery for my step kids and everyone i dealt with was brilliant. Having now moved to the areas i have become a new patient and sadly the experience has not been good. Firstly a medical review appointment was booked without any correspondence or explanation - for a time i couldn't make. I called and rebooked - however all text communication didn't update and i had no idea which appointment would happen. The person who did the medical review had no idea what the appointment was for and seemed more confused than i was. The result of that review was 1 out of 3 meds required on repeat where approved and none were pushed through to the app as promised. The result being more work and confusion for myself and her colleagues. On speaking to the practice this was quickly resolved which was great. I haven't received any correspondence from the Practice explaining the patient onboarding process, steps to take or guidance. The staff seem to think we should know what happens and their default is to pop into the surgery which really shouldn't be necessary. Moving doctors is stressful, practices should own the migration and help patients with the process and most of all ensure repeat medication is migrated properly.
