Author: M Safyan
Date: 9 months ago
Rating: 1 star
I had a very disappointing and frustrating experience at Cobridge Surgery while trying to register my pregnant wife for NHS care. We started the process nearly five to six months ago, and despite repeated visits (around 15 times) and following all their instructions, she was still not registered. At the beginning, they asked us to come in with her passport and proof of address, which we provided. We were told everything was fine and that her NHS number would arrive within a week. After waiting over a month and a half with no update, we went back, only to be asked to submit the same documents again. From that point on, every visit and call was met with excuses and empty promises, such as: – "It will be done in a few days" – "Your NHS number will come next week" – "We’ll call you back" – "The manager hasn’t processed it yet" – "I will pass it to the manager" This went on for months, and it was clear that nothing was being done seriously. One receptionist was calm but always gave us false reassurances, while another was rude and unhelpful. When I asked about the delay and explained that my pregnant wife urgently needed her NHS number for her midwife to prescribe medicine, the response was simply “I don’t know,” with no further support. After months of stress, we finally decided to try a different surgery — and to our shock, they registered her in just three days. That showed us how poorly Cobridge Surgery handled something that should have been simple and urgent. I strongly do not recommend Cobridge Surgery based on this experience. The lack of professionalism, poor communication, repeated false promises, and lack of care — especially for a pregnant woman — was completely unacceptable.
