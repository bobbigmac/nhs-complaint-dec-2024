I would like to provide feedback regarding my recent experience when trying to arrange a routine blood test. On Monday morning, 2nd of March, last week, I visited the reception to request a blood test, specifically including a glucose resistance test. The receptionist informed me that I needed to be added to the system first. She said she had completed this and asked me to return the following day. I came back on Tuesday at around 9:30 am. At that time, I was told that I would need to see a doctor and that one was available at 10:10 am. I confirmed that this was fine and said I would return for the appointment. I came back at 10:10 am as agreed, but unfortunately I was left waiting until approximately 11:20 am without being seen. During that time, I asked the receptionist if everything was in order and was told that I was next to be seen. However, when I asked again later, another member of the reception team checked and informed me that I was not even in the system and that no one would be able to see me. At that point, I requested that the whole process be cancelled. I found the situation quite disappointing. What should have been a straightforward routine blood test turned into a very frustrating experience. I had requested the test because I had been feeling unusually fatigued and low for several days and was concerned about the possibility of diabetes, as it runs in my family. It is unfortunate that the process was handled in such a disorganised way. I will now try to contact the hospital directly to arrange the blood test and will likely avoid using this service in the future. I hope this feedback can help improve the process for other patients so that similar situations can be avoided.
