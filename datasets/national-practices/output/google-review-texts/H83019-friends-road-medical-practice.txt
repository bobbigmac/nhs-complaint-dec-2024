Practice response date: a week ago
Practice response:
I am sorry that you have experienced such difficulties in obtaining an appointment. I was surprised to read that you have tried on so many previous occasions, not least because there are Telephone Consultations available both today and tomorrow. I do however, recognise that there can be delays in obtaining an appointment particularly if you want to see a specific GP and the Face-to-Face appointments do get booked up quickly. If however, a patient feels that they cannot wait, we will normally offer you a Telephone Consultation with a GP within a few days or if urgent then usually on the same day. If the GP, during this consultation feels that a Face-to Face appointment is necessary, they will book you an appointment themselves which will usually be within a day or two. In addition to booking appointments on the telephone and in person at the Practice, if you have access to the NHS App then you can also book a routine Face-to-Face appointment to see a GP online. In terms of your comments regarding the way in which you felt you were spoken to by reception, this is an exceptionally busy Practice and the Reception Team often work under immense pressure, with the telephones ringing nonstop throughout the day. This is not however, an excuse for rudeness and if this is how they came across then please accept my apologies and be assured that this will be discussed with them. I do need to add that the demand for appointments can frequently exceed the number that we can safely provide, hence we sometimes have no other choice than to advise patients to contact an alternative service such as 111. I will ask Kunal Trivedi, who is our Operations Manager to contact you to check if you still require an appointment and if so, he can arrange this. He can also discuss your comments in more detail, if you feel that this would be helpful. Once again, please accept my sincere apologies. Kind regards Karen Northwood Managing Partner
