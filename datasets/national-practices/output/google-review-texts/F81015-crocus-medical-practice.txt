Practice response date: 3 weeks ago
Practice response:
Thank you for your feedback. We are sorry to hear that you felt frustrated with your recent experience. We have reviewed your case in detail. A clinician called you twice and left messages when there was no answer. As we were unable to reach you, you were asked to complete another eConsult so that we could safely reassess your symptoms. You did submit a further eConsult, and on the same day our Senior GP Partner contacted you to provide a full consultation. Due to the nature of clinical work and working through medical cases as safely as possible, we are unable to give patients an exact time for telephone calls. However, clinicians will always attempt to call more than once where possible. Although many patients choose to use eConsult online, completing the form yourself is not the only option. Patients can: Call the practice and a member of the reception team will complete the eConsult over the phone with them, or Visit the practice, where the reception team will happily help complete the form in person. We never expect patients to struggle with technology or manage the process alone—our team is always here to help. We appreciate you taking the time to share your concerns, and we will continue working to improve the service we provide.
