I had a really frustrating experience with Dr. Hiep Nguyen that I feel needs to be shared. About a month ago, I went in to request blood tests to check for diabetes. I specifically asked about testing insulin levels, as I understand this can be important. I was told, in a dismissive way, that “we don’t do that in this country! Maybe in a different one” which already made me feel like my concerns weren’t being taken seriously. He then said I would instead do a 2-hour glucose test, explaining I would need to fast, have blood taken, then return after two hours for another sample. While going through the forms, he actually came across one for an insulin test and mentioned it could be done at the main hospital, yet still didn’t provide me with that form. In the end, I was given forms for a glucose test and two general tests, and told to call a number and book the 2-hour glucose test. I called as instructed, explained exactly what test I needed, and was booked in at Cheshunt Community Hospital. I then waited a full month for this appointment, fasting beforehand as required, only to arrive and be told that this test couldn’t even be done there. I was told it needed to be done at the main hospital because it involves consuming something sugary and waiting. The nurse even said that the GP should have known this. So after a month of waiting and unnecessary stress, I left without having the test done at all, and still without the insulin test form I originally asked for. This isn’t the first issue either. Dr. Hiep Nguyen has also mishandled my stepdad’s care in the past, which resulted in him being unable to work for several months. Overall, this experience has been extremely disappointing. I felt dismissed, misinformed, and completely let down by a lack of basic knowledge and organisation.
