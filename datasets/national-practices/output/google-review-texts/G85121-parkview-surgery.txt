Rating: 3 stars
I am pleased that I am able to secure appointments, and so far, all clinical staff have been excellent. However, while sitting in reception, I noticed a lack of clear signage to help patients navigate to clinic rooms. One patient, who did not speak English fluently, struggled to find their way, and the existing signs are quite small. I also found myself getting lost while trying to locate the clinic rooms. Additionally, there is minimal staff presence at reception. The staff sit low behind a screen, which does not create a particularly welcoming atmosphere. One window displayed a sign instructing patients to go to the next window, while another had a notice stating, “Please do not tap the glass.” Despite this, staff took some time to acknowledge patients with enquiries. They could see patients getting lost but did not intervene, leaving me to assist them instead. I believe greater attention is needed in this area to improve the patient experience. Otherwise, my experience has been satisfactory.
