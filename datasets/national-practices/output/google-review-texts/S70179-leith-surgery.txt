Author: Kazim Hussain
Date: 2 months ago
Rating: 3 stars
My experience with this practice has been okay overall, but the biggest issue is trying to actually get an appointment. It always seems to be “no availability for two weeks” — and strangely, it’s consistently two weeks every time, never 7 days, 10 days, or 21 days. It definitely makes you wonder what’s going on with their booking system. When you finally do get seen, the doctors themselves are great. They’re helpful, take the time to listen, look into your symptoms properly, and arrange any tests or follow-ups needed. No complaints there. The frustration really lies with the admin side. Reception can feel unhelpful at times, and you’re often asked to justify things like blood test bookings, even when they’ve been requested by the doctor. It sometimes feels like the reception team are acting as gatekeepers rather than just helping you access care. Overall, once you’re in the room with a doctor, the care is good — but the process of getting there needs serious improvement.
