Author: 123 Draw!

Rating: 1 star
I don’t usually leave negative reviews, but unfortunately this one is warranted. Some of the reception staff are consistently rude and impatient, particularly on the phone. I have been rushed through calls and even had the phone put down before the conversation had finished, which is unacceptable. The practice’s insistence on booking appointments strictly via an online form makes access unnecessarily difficult. You may be given an appointment time or date you cannot attend, which then requires phoning the surgery, often waiting 30 - 45 minutes to get through, even outside peak times. If the form is not completed in exactly the right way, you may be redirected to A&E despite being well enough, meaning the process has to be repeated. While I have mixed experiences with the appointments themselves, the main issue is access to care, particularly the reception experience and booking system.
