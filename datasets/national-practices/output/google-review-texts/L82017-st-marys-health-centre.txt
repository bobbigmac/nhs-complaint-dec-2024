Author: Carole Sage
Date: a year ago
Rating: 2 stars
Needing treatment for a recurrence of a medical problem during my holiday I contacted St. Mary’s Health Centre for help and I was shocked when I was told that I would have to contact my own GP and get them to send an electronic prescription to the pharmacy on St. Mary’s. When I contacted my own GP practice as instructed the GP I spoke to said it was “outrageous” that St.Mary’s Health Centre would not provide me with medical care. She said that the Health Centre on St. Mary’s should have registered me as a temporary patient and provided the care that I need. The service from the pharmacy at the Health Centre was, thankfully, excellent - the pharmacist was lovely and very helpful to me so I would have been glad to give the pharmacy a 5 star review if it was separate from the Health Centre. I am thankful to have received the medication I need, but so disappointed, and frankly rather worried as a regular visitor to the islands by the response I got when I phoned the Heath Centre asking for help. I love Scilly, and until this happened it would never have occurred to me that I might not receive medical attention here if I needed it (this is the first time that I’ve needed medical help here in many years of visiting the islands).
