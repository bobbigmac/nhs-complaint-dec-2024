Practice response:
Hi David, I'm sorry to hear you have had problems getting through on the phone. Today, Monday 20th June, staff arrived in at 7:30am and realised there was no internet or computers working, meaning no telephones. This was an external problem to the practice, but we mobilised our business continuity plan, but there has been an unavoidable impact to appointments and answering the phones. Our telephone system was down between 8am - 10am. We put a message to advise of this on the phone system itself, our website and our Facebook page. As soon as this was resolved we've also tried to update people. Clearly during a busy Monday morning this can be problematic but my staff have worked as hard as possible to ensure those patients with pre-booked appointments can still be seen or have their appointment re-arranged in a timely way. Once our phone lives re-opened we were able to book patients on the day. In terms of 'hiding behind Covid' I'm not sure I understand you. The practice has been open throughout the pandemic and we continued to see patients during this time, we just asked that they speak to a clinician first and they would discuss and decide, if it was safe to and/or if you needed to be seen face to face. This would be an individual conversation between the individual patient and the treating clinician. In terms of social distancing, in line with NHS England policy we have removed all social distancing measures in our waiting room and stood down lots of covid related pre-cautions, but again in line with national guidance we are still asking patients and clinicians to wear face masks. This is for the protection of both staff and patients. Kind regards Craig Lee Practice Operations Manager
