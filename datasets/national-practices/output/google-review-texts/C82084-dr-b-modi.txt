I have been a patient at Canon Street Surgery for over 35 years and I always received good care from Dr B V Modi as well as other staff under his leadership. Before retiring Dr Modi used locam doctors who were also very good, especially Dr Ali. However, since Dr Modi’s retirement, the surgery has become part of the Willows group and whilst the care given by most of the staff has been good, there is room for improvement because on my previous appointments have left the surgery feeling not being listened to and not fully advised of my care plan, which is very important for someone who is a heart patient. There have been delays with medical certificates, for example though appointments are generally easily available, which is important for me. The surgery itself also needs a good clean and a bit of organising because the TV on the wall is dirty. The console table between 2 waiting chairs is broken - it fell apart when someone just touched it - this is a H&S risk. The floor was filthy and there doesn’t appear to be any understanding of the how to discuss private matters, especially by patients as conversations are being held for all to hear whilst waiting for their appointments! The Receptionist staff are doing their best especially Maheck, who has always been very helpful.
