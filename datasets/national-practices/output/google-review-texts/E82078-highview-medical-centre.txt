Practice response:
Dear Kim Louise, Thank you for taking the time to share your feedback. We are very sorry to hear about your experience and understand how frustrating it is to feel unable to access care when you need it. Demand for GP appointments is extremely high, and like many NHS practices we currently use a combination of telephone consultations, online requests, and face-to-face appointments to try to manage this safely and fairly. Telephone appointments are often used so clinicians can assess urgency quickly and ensure face-to-face appointments are prioritised for those who need them most. However, we appreciate that this does not work well for everyone and that choice is important to patients. We acknowledge your concerns about access, appointment availability, and we are looking into a Clinical Led Assessment Service where qualified clinicians (like GPs, nurses, or paramedics) assess a patient’s needs and decide the most appropriate next step in their care.
