Author: Umair Ali

Rating: 1 star
Unfortunately, it has been one of the worst experiences I have had dealing with a medical practice, largely due to the conduct of the reception staff. There appears to be a constant and concerning disregard for patient confidentiality. After speaking to one staff member on the phone, it often feels as though everyone at reception is aware of your personal medical issues and queries. This lack of discretion is both uncomfortable and unprofessional. In addition, the telephone manner of the reception team is poor. There is no proper introduction at the start of calls, and when politely asked for their name, staff have refused to provide it. This lack of basic communication etiquette makes interactions feel dismissive and unaccountable. Even more worrying is the perception of racial bias toward non-white patients. The difference in tone and treatment is noticeable and deeply concerning in a healthcare setting where equality and respect should be fundamental. I hope this feedback is taken seriously and that meaningful improvements are made, particularly around staff training, confidentiality, professionalism, and equality. Patients deserve to feel respected, heard, and protected when dealing with their healthcare providers.

Author: U Khan
Date: 2 months ago
Rating: 1 star
My experience with South Queensferry Medical Practice has been very disappointing. I was contacted by the surgery advising that a doctor needed to assess me before issuing my repeat prescription for nerve pain medication. When I called to arrange this, I was told to ignore the message and request the prescription online, despite the medication not appearing on my online record. As my medication was running out, I contacted the surgery again and was advised to attend in person. I was then told I could either take a printed prescription to the pharmacy or call the next day to book a same-day appointment. When I did so, I was informed that only emergency appointments were available. Despite explaining my situation, the staff member was dismissive and ended the call when I asked for her name. Subsequent calls were handled in a similarly unprofessional manner. When I attended the surgery in person, no identity checks were carried out, and I was told I already had an appointment booked, which had never been clearly communicated to me. Dr Wilson later spoke to me but stated she would support her staff without listening to the recorded phone calls that led to the situation. Overall, the lack of communication, professionalism, and willingness to properly review events was extremely disappointing. Patients should feel listened to and supported, particularly when dealing with ongoing medical needs. I hope the practice reviews its procedures and staff conduct to improve patient care.
