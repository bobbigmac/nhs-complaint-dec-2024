It says something about a practice when you're put off before you can even register. I was willing to register at this practice because of the location, even despite how outdated and run down it seemed, but was immediately discouraged when I met the reception staff. In all fairness, one lady I spoke to was perfectly helpful, but when I tried to hand in my registration forms, the second older woman was completely rude. It makes me beg the question why someone would take a role in a customer facing job when they have no compassion or social skills. You’d think that they would treat people with a little more understanding considering their position. I was treated as if I had ruined her entire life by entering the front door. Apparently, the practice was in training, and I should have known this even though there were no signs informing me so, and the door was open for people to walk in. I recommend you try a different practice if you’re looking. Unless you like being huffed at, and having people roll their eyes at you for even dreaming of wanting the help of the people who are supposed to be there to help you. I suppose I should be grateful that I was able to see what the experience would be like before I registered.
