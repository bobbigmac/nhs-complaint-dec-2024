Practice response date: 7 months ago
Practice response:
Dear Kishor, Thank you for taking the time to share your experience. I am sorry to hear that you’ve found it difficult to access appointments and I understand how frustrating this can be. As you will appreciate from your many years of service in the NHS, ensuring fair and equitable access to a limited number of appointments is a constant challenge. The Partners and I regularly review our appointment system with the whole team, taking into account feedback from patients. As is the case for many GP practices, demand is extremely high. We aim to offer as many access routes as possible. Our receptionists use the same pool of appointments that are available online, at the desk, and over the phone. These are offered as a mix of 7-day and 14-day pre-bookable slots, along with appointments released on the day. We routinely review the balance of appointment types and booking methods and make adjustments where we can. Despite this, our appointments are often booked up very quickly. When that happens, our team does their best to signpost patients to other services or advise trying again the following day. We have also recently recruited a new GP to help increase our overall capacity. We would welcome broader reform in how Primary Care is funded and structured. The ethos of the practice has always been to invest any new funding directly into patient-facing services. While we continue to do all we can within our means, we also encourage patients to raise their concerns with their local MP, as ongoing investment and support at a national level are vital to ensuring the sustainability of general practice. Thank you again for your feedback Yours sincerely Daniel Sayliss, Practice Manager
