Terrible experience. Trying to keep it as brief as possible, I saw two doctors in the space of two weeks (after being in severe pain a week before but them not allowing me to have a telephone appointment as I was away at the time) - first doctor: didn’t leave with a diagnosis of any kind, got given antibiotics. A week later still in agony I made another appointment and ended up with a different doctor who again provided no diagnosis and a different course of antibiotics. He also booked me in for an appointment for the next day which I didn’t make it to as I landed up in A&E and being hospitalised for undiagnosed tonsillitis that led to a deeply infected pus filled pocket (quinsy) that ruptured in the middle of the night and had to be further drained at the hospital. The hospital said that they have no idea why the second round of antibiotics was prescribed to me as it was not relevant to my condition and stated that doctors should have immediately picked up on tonsillitis as well as a quinsy. After being in severe pain for just under 3 weeks, being undiagnosed by two doctors and ending up in hospital, I can confidently say that this practise is poor and I am not only truly shocked but am deeply disappointed and dissatisfied by my experience here.

This is an okay GP’s surgery. GP experience is variable depending on the GP you see. There has been a shortage of practice nurses in the last couple of years at various times so waiting times to see a nurse when required were quite long. The dispensary stuff is great and give very efficient service. The receptionists are variable depending on the receptionist. Some of them are very good, but I have found some quite rude and condescending and take the power to take medical details too far so it can difficult to get to see a GP if the receptionist decides your medical problem isn’t serious in their opinion.
