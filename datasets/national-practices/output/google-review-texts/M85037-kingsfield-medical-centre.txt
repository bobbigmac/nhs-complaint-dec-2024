Rating: 3 stars
Reception tend to be very helpuful here. Unfortunately the website seemed to be less helpful on this occassion. I filled in the sick note request form first thing this morning. This was purposefully done to avoid filling up the queue. If the website can be used instead of queueing, then that is helpful for everyone. However, after filling in the form for a "fit note" (see photo), I received a text saying I would have to ring the practice. I rang the practice, and was told they don't issue fit notes in this manner, and they only do them via calls through the normal appointment system (i.e. queueing first thing in the morning). The fit note form, I was told, is only for renewing fit notes already given. Nowhere on this webpage for fit notes does it state that this is for renewing a current fit note. What is the point of this form if it means I have to call anyway? Making the website more informative would help users know what to do, and also relieve the already busy reception team from receiving additional calls to clarify what the website means, or what you can and can't do.
