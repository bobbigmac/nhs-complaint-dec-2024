Dr Nandish Jayappa, the Medical Receptionist team, the Nurses, and every other staff member encountered did a fantastic job. Waiting times are rather long however, so if you can't wait to be seen I recommend A&E (for immediate attention) or GMEDs, and the waiting room is a little small so be sure not to linger as it could get crowded, but as a General Practice it seems good thus far. They also offer their services online through Engage Consult, which has been fast and pretty effective. Traffic can get a little busy as it is near some main roads, but if you're not taking the car there are bus stops not too far. Overall, pretty good.
