Author: Alima Begum

I have been registered with this GP practice since childhood and, until recently, my experience has generally been positive. The team has typically been accommodating, attentive, and appointments have been reasonably accessible. However, I am writing to express concern regarding a recent experience with a newly appointed doctor, Sonal Mahesh Thakkar. Unfortunately, I found her approach to be lacking in empathy and professionalism. During my consultation, I felt dismissed, unheard, and not adequately supported. Communication was poor, and my concerns were not explored in a meaningful way. When I asked questions to better understand my condition and seek reassurance, I did not feel that these were addressed appropriately, rather it felt like a conflict each consultation. There was a noticeable lack of engagement, and no clear plan, advice, or follow-up was provided. I was particularly concerned when I was advised to seek private care, rather than being offered appropriate support or solutions within the practice. This felt inappropriate given that I was actively seeking help and reassurance. This experience was disappointing and did not reflect the standard of care I have come to expect from this practice.
