Patient for about 8 years. Here's a rough breakdown: Booking an appointment is not easy. The website has improved and is easy to use, but the practice is oversubscribed so good luck getting a timely appointment. The practice is in a temporary location sharing with Firs Medical (which is better than the original location) until they move to a permanent new building. I haven't seen a resident GP for the last few years, I have only seen 1 locum GP, and the rest of my requests have been answered by phone calls only. They really don't want to see you in person. If you need bloods taken, for the moment this is happening at Comely Bank clinic which is reasonably pleasant. I have had a poor experience overall. This ranges from significant administrative mistakes (no labelling on samples or giving me someone else's results) to almost prescribing an incorrect antibiotic (I had to point it out). I really do hope things improve at the new premises, because I would consider the current patient experience as the worst I have ever had, or have ever heard of.
