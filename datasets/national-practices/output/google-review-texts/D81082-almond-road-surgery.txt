We have had several disappointing experiences with this practice, and this incident prompted me to finally write a review. Our toddler, who is nearly 3 years old, has a history of chronic tonsillitis. Based on previous poor experiences, we waited until day five of his illness before seeing the GP. He had high fever , difficulty in swallowing food. I explained everything to the doctor very clearly. During the appointment, the doctor examined him and reassured us that everything was clear and that there was no issue. However, just 2–3 hours later, his temperature spiked to 41°C. His face became red, swollen, and his eyes were puffy. We rushed him to A&E, where the doctors diagnosed him with tonsillitis and an ear infection. We were fortunate to be seen by a doctor at A&E who took his condition seriously and showed genuine care for our child’s health and wellbeing.
