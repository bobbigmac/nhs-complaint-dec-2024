I had disappointing experiences on two occasions with one of the Drs at this practice. From the beginning of the consultations, I felt rushed and dismissed. The doctor frequently interrupted me as I tried to explain my symptoms, which made it difficult to communicate my concerns. It seemed like she was more focused on getting through the appointments quickly than actually listening to what I had to say. Her demeanor lacked empathy, and I left feeling unheard and unsatisfied with the care I received. I understand that doctors are often under time pressure, but patients deserve to feel that their concerns are being taken seriously. Unfortunately, I didn’t get that impression during my visit. Currently looking for a new GP practice, certainly not going back here.
