I do and have always felt supported by the team. There are lots of positive changes and teething troubles with implemented new systems… but I would say.. if you ask for help with the team.. or share your challenges, they will do their very best that they can as a team , with the resources and government changes behind the face to face community service they provide. So where people are quick to give the practice 1 Star, I ask you to think how tough their directive actually is in the delivery and powers of the current NHS. If you, like me face surgery/practice challenges-go in, ask for help. At times, it can be very frustrating. I have been there too… even a few days before Christmas as decisions were not right by a GP for a backdated fit note- a new GP not my usual - I explained- they listened, then they had a solution. I do have a GP here who should of retired, who is an absolute star, beyond supportive as my care and illness is complex and crosses over in a few areas of illness- but he does his best for me at all times for many years. Thank you all. It means a lot.
