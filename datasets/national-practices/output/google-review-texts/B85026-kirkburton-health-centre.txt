With regard to e-consultations, I would like to take the opportunity to explain their purpose and how they support patient care. E-consultations have been very well received by the majority of our patients and are often the most effective way to contact the practice. We encourage patients who are able to use e-consultations to do so, as this helps our reception team remain available for those who need face-to-face or telephone support and are unable to use digital services.
