I’ve been with this practice for 7 nearly 8 years and here’s what I can tell you… We don’t have an over flow of GP surgeries so it’s slim pickings. The receptionists over all are helpful, minus one or two which are ridiculously rude for zero reason and usually a stern word and they will hang up, follow it with a complaint and they will marginally improve. Think that’s down to a power trip. With our daughter the GP’s were excellent, receptionist don’t really argue when it comes to kids because they know that’s going too far. For myself and husband they were rubbish, then sadly our daughter died at the start of the year and they have been absolutely amazing since, Whilst it shouldn’t take a child passing away for them to pull their socks up I think it’s important to acknowledge when they have. They have recruited newer members of reception who you can tell are trying to improve what people think. GP’s have always been good they just like to keep the appointment efficient so if you go rambling off about an ache you had for a few months that doesn’t really bother you much then yes they will shut you down. The nurses are ok, They are very good at taking blood and don’t attack you with a needle like some nurses do. Dr Khan in particular has been quite literally a god send for our little boy who has struggled quite significantly with allergies and GERD since the day he was born, thanks to Dr Khan he was referred to the RBH peads team who all fast tracked his refferals. Can’t thank Dr Khan enough for his patience, knowledge and great beside manner with our little boy. There’s room for improvement but in the last few months, improvement has been started and is most noticeable, particularly with the reception team, hooray for getting a decent bunch in that don’t act like they own the keys to you receiving decent healthcare!
