Author: Victoria Smith
Date: 4 months ago
Rating: 1 star
I’ve been a patient at Horsley Medical Practice for years, but the service lately has been extremely disappointing. My last two appointments have both been around 40 minutes late, with no communication, update, or apology from reception. I understand delays happen, but leaving patients waiting with no explanation – especially those with sick children – is really not acceptable. On one occasion I was waiting with my poorly 16-month-old baby, and still no one acknowledged the delay or checked in. That level of disregard for patient experience is quite shocking. The attitude at the front desk also doesn’t help – the staff in person are consistently cold and unapproachable, yet bizarrely the tone is totally different when speaking to the practice on the phone, where people are much friendlier and more helpful. So it’s clearly not a lack of ability, just a lack of effort in person. I was also told I might need antibiotics but to wait until the next day, which I was happy to do. What wasn’t explained is that it would then take another 24 hours to actually receive the prescription. A simple bit of communication would have avoided all of this frustration. The main issue here is poor communication and lack of basic care. Patients shouldn’t have to chase, guess, or feel like an inconvenience. A GP surgery should feel like a place of support – at the moment, it doesn’t.
Practice response date: 4 months ago
Practice response:
Thank you for taking the time to share your feedback. I have investigated the issues you raised regarding your recent appointments. Our records show that for your last two appointments, you waited approximately 25 minutes for one appointment, and on your most recent visit at 4:30 pm, you were seen on time. I have reviewed earlier appointments and have not identified any instance of a 40-minute delay. However, I completely understand that a 25-minute wait can feel frustrating, and I apologise for the inconvenience caused. Unfortunately, our reception team are sometimes unaware when clinicians are running late, as they are managing multiple demands such as phone calls and patients at the desk. We are currently exploring the use of display screens in reception to keep patients informed of any delays, which we hope will improve communication. Please be assured that we take patient care very seriously and are continually striving to provide the best possible service. I am sorry if you felt we fell short of this on your recent visits. With regard to your comments about the attitude of our reception staff, I have passed your feedback on to our Reception Supervisor. If any additional training or support is needed, we will ensure it takes place. While we usually receive very positive feedback about our reception team, I appreciate your perspective and will ensure your comments are reviewed constructively. Regarding your query about the prescription, the most likely explanation is that the doctor who originally saw you was not in the following day to sign the prescription. To prevent a delay in issuing your medication, an appointment was made with another doctor so the prescription could be completed immediately. If you would like to discuss this matter further, please contact the surgery and I would be happy to review the situation in more detail. Thank you again for your feedback, which helps us to identify areas for improvement.
