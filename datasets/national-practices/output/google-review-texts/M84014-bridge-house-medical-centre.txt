Practice response date: 7 months ago
Practice response:
Hi pippa, I’m really sorry to hear about the experience you received. If you could get in touch with us to explain what happened, we would really appreciate it so we are able to provide feedback and training to our staff. Regarding the online form system, all triages go through to a GP who reviews the urgency of our patient’s issues, who is in contact to inform that patient of the outcome. We do feel that this is a much safer way to process our patient’s issues, due to a medical professional being the first to view the details. This was decided by our partners and slowly being enforced by NHS England. For all patients that are not capable of accessing the online triage, our receptionists are more than happy to fill this out for them.
