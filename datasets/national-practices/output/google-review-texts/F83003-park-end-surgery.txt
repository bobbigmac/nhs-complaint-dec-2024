UPDATE AUG 2025: I feel it is worth updating this review which is now several years old to say that now I have two small children the practice is always incredibly quick and responsive when it comes to their care and I couldn’t ask for more from its staff. I joined the practice 3 years ago when I moved to the area and always thought the standard of care when I needed it was very high. But recently I had a baby and have found it to be lacking for new mothers. When I developed a breastfeeding related infection I was only given a telephone appointment even though I asked to see someone in person. I wasn’t prescribed antibiotics — only a cream — and it later developed into something much worse (mastitis) and an emergency trip to a private GP for help. When I went for my baby’s 6 week check up the lift was broken so I had to leave my pram unattended and carry him up and juggle all our stuff. And lastly there is no longer a regular practice nurse which means when it comes to his injections I was directed to Camden central services to seek an appointment. None of the clinics are remotely close to where I live for what is a stressful appointment for mother and baby. The month of experiences at a time in life when you want to be able to rely on your local GP have made me consider whether I need to go elsewhere.
