The staff did not listen, did not attempt to understand, and made no effort to help. Instead, I was abruptly told that I “can get the bus” back to the GP I was previously registered with, despite the fact that I now live across the road from this practice and have a newborn with additional needs. For a mother who is trying to rebuild her life, stay safe, and provide stability for her children, this response was not only unhelpful — it was shocking. I have overcome significant challenges, including addiction recovery, rebuilding my confidence, and caring for my baby entirely on my own. The very least I expected was a baseline level of respect and consideration. Instead, I left feeling dismissed, judged, and completely unsupported. This has been one of the most disheartening experiences I have had with any healthcare provider. A GP practice is meant to be a place of care, safety, and understanding — particularly for vulnerable women and children. Sadly, this practice failed to show any of those qualities. I truly hope the staff reflect on how they communicate with and support individuals who may be going through more than they realise. A little compassion can make the biggest difference.
