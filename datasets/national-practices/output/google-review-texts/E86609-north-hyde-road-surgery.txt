Author: Hayes “Town” Reviews
Date: 2 weeks ago
Rating: 5 stars
North Hyde Practice is an excellent GP surgery, with fast service, high-quality care, and good waiting times. I recommend them to anyone living in, or around, Hayes. I registered at NHP as it was in a good location, and I had heard good things about the surgery. I am a young person who is going through mental health challenges, and I had been suffering for many years before finally making the better decision to seek support. I have been suffering from depression and anxiety, and also found out I had a diagnosis of ADHD. Through this surgery, I have been able to access pharmacological treatment, and referrals to both Talking Therapies and a specialist ADHD clinic for diagnosis. I have been seen by Dr (M) Salman Gauher, Dr (M) Zeeshan Gauher, and Dr (F) Harmeet Grewal for main consultations, alongside other staff for different appointments. All of the GPs and other staff have been understanding of my problems and effective in providing the best steps forward. The reception staff have never failed to answer my queries, are prompt to pick up the phone, and have always been helpful. All of my appointments have been seen either on time, or at worst, a few minutes after, and I have been to other surgeries where I have had to wait much longer, especially in dental practices. The GPs have allowed me to access CBT to help manage my wellbeing, alongside sertraline, which is working well for me. Through their referral to ADHD clinic, I also have the treatment via methylphenidate, which is also excellent. Thanks to the healthcare provided by this surgery, I feel like I've been able to finally take steps to overcome such a severe barrier, and I hope that anyone that is in a similar situation is able to get support. The only thing I would change is to have done this sooner! (If this review gets responded to, please maintain anonymity.)
