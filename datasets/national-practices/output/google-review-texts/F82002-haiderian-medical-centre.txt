I am grateful to the Nurse Prescriber, Katie and the team at Haiderian. Yesterday, 12th August was my second meeting with her. During my first meeting, she diagnosed me for diabetes based on blood test results. Amazingly, my previous surgery in Dagenham missed the diagnosis despite blood test results showing then that my sugar levels were too high. If it weren't for Nurse Katie's knowledgeable intervention, my clinical condition would probably have degenerated to a critical state by now - thank you so much, Nurse Katie! She placed me on medication and in some months, my blood glucose level has made a significant improvement heading closer and closer towards the normal range. She and the other members of Haiderian's medical team also diagnosed my high cholesterol levels and with medication, I am now in the normal range. Compared to many surgeries, Haiderian is a centre of service delivery excellence in my opinion. By employing and retaining very competent and very profession staff like Nurse Katie, Haiderian medical centre is living to my expectations of high standards of excellent service delivery.
