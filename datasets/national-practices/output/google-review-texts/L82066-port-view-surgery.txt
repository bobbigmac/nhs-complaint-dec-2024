Author: Hannah Hepton
Date: 11 months ago
Rating: 2 stars
I have been registered with the surgery for approximately 16 years and have always appreciated the care provided by the doctors, which continues to be excellent. However, I feel increasingly frustrated by the current appointment and e.consult system. On several occasions, I have tried to complete an e.consult for issues that require GP attention but don’t necessarily need a face-to-face appointment. Each time I access the website, I’m met with a message saying that e.consult is paused until a specific day or time. When I return at that time, the message has changed again — and this cycle continues. I’ve attempted to book appointments by phone, but despite the answerphone message advising to “call after 11am” for non-urgent appointments, reception staff tell me I must call at 11am precisely. When that doesn't work, I’m told to try e.consult — which, again, isn’t available. Some staff can also come across as dismissive or rude when this is raised, which is upsetting — especially when I’ve always approached the practice with respect and understanding. I appreciate that the NHS is under immense pressure and that some patients may behave poorly, but it’s disheartening to feel that long-standing patients are being blocked from accessing the care they need. I hope you are able to review the current system for non-urgent access to GP advice and support, particularly for patients with ongoing conditions. When I am fortunate enough to get an appointment, I have no complaints about the medical care itself. Thank you for taking the time to consider this feedback.
Practice response date: 10 months ago
Practice response:
Hi Hannah, Thank you for sending us your feedback, in such a constructive manner. We are very much aware of the current limitation on routine appointment availability and understand that this is frustrating for our patients, it is for our team too. We have limited capacity and have to try and prioritise those most in need of urgent help, which means the wait for more routine issues gets longer. We are working as hard as we can, with limited funding, space and resources. We have many different services now that patients can access for help and our team will direct you to the most appropriate option, which is not always a GP. There have been changes made recently to the contract that practices work to and this means that our appointment system, and methods of accessing services, will need to change over the next few months and we are currently working out how we can implement those changes. Thanks again for your feedback.
