This past 6 months, due to being moved around a lot. I've had 3 gp's, the first being in cefn mawr, the second Adderley green longton, now I'm under the care of gwrych medical centre in abergele. They say that bad luck comes in 3's, but out of the 3 gp's I've had, only one has been a negative, that being the Adderley green surgery, but I digress. What can't be said about gwrych medical centre, its got everything you need for all medical issues. The building is beautiful, tasteful, with stained glass design windows throughout. The staff are fantastic, really helpful & friendly😁👍. It's not difficult to get an appointment with the gp, or practice nurse, unlike other practices. You are welcomed with a smile each time. They are courteous & respectful at all times. The mental health outreach is 👍. Plus, having a boots pharmacy on the same site is 👍. Honestly, I cannot sing their praises enough, I'll be here all day.
