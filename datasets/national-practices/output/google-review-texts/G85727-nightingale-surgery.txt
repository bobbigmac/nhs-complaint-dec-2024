Practice response:
Hi Jane - thank you very much for your 5* review. We have teams working hard to improve appointment availability as much as possible within the resources available to the NHS. To support this, we are: - Continuing to recruit additional GPs where possible, although this can sometimes be challenging. - Reducing duplicate bookings so that appointments are used appropriately and efficiently. - Limiting double appointments, except where clinically necessary (for example, when an interpreter is required), so that we can see more patients in each clinic. - Reducing the impact of missed appointments. Unfortunately, a significant number of appointments are lost every month when patients do not attend and do not cancel. This prevents other patients from being seen. Where patients miss multiple appointments without providing a reason, we review their registration with the practice. Thank you for your understanding and support while we continue working to make our appointment system as accessible and responsive as possible. I am really glad that you have found our team helpful and professional, and hope you continue to be happy with the healthcare service we provide. Julie Patient Experience Manager
