I have been registered with this surgery for years and the service has just gone from bad to worse. It is virtually impossible to get an appointment. One receptionist will say there are no appointments for two months, yet if you call the very next day and speak to someone else, suddenly there's one available the following week. That makes absolutely no sense. Either appointments exist or they don't - it shouldn't depend on who answers the phone. The prescription handling has been even worse. The very next day, after messaging through the NHS app, I was told the prescription couldn't be issued at all. So which is it? That directly contradicts that I was told on the phone. Patients should not be told one thing one day and the complete opposite the next. There is a serious lack of consistency, transparency, and basic professionalism. After being with this surgery for 23 years, it's incredibly disappointing to feel dismissed and misled. Patients deserve honesty and proper access to care - not confusion and contradictions.
