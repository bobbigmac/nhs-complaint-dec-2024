Author: jo polley
Date: a year ago
Rating: 4 stars
I have nothing but praise for the GP’s I have been treated by at this surgery. Although I have never yet seen one face to face, I really haven’t needed to. All queries have been dealt with via the askmygp service from the gps with nothing but understanding, sensitivity and a totally thorough approach. Nothing is ever too much and they are happy to keep the askmygp thread going until every angle is covered to ensure the most thorough result. Definitely 10/10 for me. Unfortunately I cannot say the same for the repeat prescription side of things that are dealt with by someone else. “Out of issue” (meaning not able to receive your repeat of controlled meds) on the day they’re due is a regular occurance resulting in me having to chase around the surgery, the pharmacy and an outside dept to avoid not receiving my medication on it’s due date, with no prior warning. Exactly as someone else has said in their review. I personally feel that controlled drugs should be dealt with in a much more professional way and this practice of just stopping them until such a time they are reviewed at a later date is dangerous. Such a shame that that such a casual approach is taken in this area when the gps themselves are so thorough, fast and caring.
