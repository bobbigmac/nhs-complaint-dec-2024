Dear Mr Whitford We are sorry to hear that you have felt unhappy with your experience at the practice. Unfortunately, we are not able to go into personal or specific details in an open forum, but the best way to resolve any specific concerns is to contact us directly so that we can look into them properly. For context, the practice operates a system called total triage. This means that every acute appointment request is reviewed by a senior clinician, usually the same day and often within two hours. The clinician decides the most appropriate way to address the issue, which might be by signposting elsewhere, giving advice, arranging a telephone consultation or booking a face-to-face appointment. Patients are directed to the most suitable member of the team, whether that is a GP, nurse or another specialist, so that the most complex cases are managed by the highest skilled staff while everyone can benefit from the skills of the most appropriate staff member. In your case it sounds as though the clinician felt your needs could be best managed by a nurse, which is why that appointment would have been offered. This approach helps ensure that as many patients as possible are seen promptly and appropriately. We do value feedback and would encourage you to get in touch with the practice directly if you would like us to review any specific concerns in more detail. Kind regards Chris (Practice Manager)
