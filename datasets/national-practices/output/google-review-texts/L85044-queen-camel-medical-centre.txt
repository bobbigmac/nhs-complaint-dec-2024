Author: Antonia Peacock
Date: a week ago
Rating: 2 stars
I've debated whether or not to write this for some time and/or whether to write a formal complaint directly to the surgery. I initially presented to the GP at QC around March '25 when I was in my third trimester of pregnancy and experiencing severe indigestion and regurgitation of food. I assumed symptoms would ease post partum and was advised by one GP it was most likely hormonal changes. Symptoms worsened and were starting to be accompanied by difficulty swallowing some foods, so I returned to the GP and spoke Dr S Ahmed; explaining my symptoms and concerns to her. Unfortunately, she was dismissive and I later saw she had written 'anxiety' in the presenting complaint next to my telephone consultation with her. Not long after this, Dr S Ahmed left QC medical surgery. More time passed and the symptoms worsened, now accompanied with epigastric discomfort. I spoke to Dr E Jones who was very good. He trialled me on various different medications, unfortunately none of which eased the symptoms. I then spoke to Dr A wood and explained my symptoms, at this point I had been experiencing around 1kg of unintentional weight loss a month which was unusual for me. He explained that as it was not at the 10% unintentional weight loss marker, that he wouldn't be concerned. Soon after this, I made the decision to see a gastroenterologist privately as I felt like my concerns were not being listened to. In February '26, I saw a private consultant in Dorset who has arranged for me to have investigations done within the NHS in Dorset under his care, instead of Somerset. Based upon the treatment resistant indigestion alongside the unintentional weight loss; I believe a referral should have been made to secondary care for it to be investigated properly. Instead, I was left feeling completely unheard and have sadly lost trust in the GPs that they'll take me seriously in the future.
