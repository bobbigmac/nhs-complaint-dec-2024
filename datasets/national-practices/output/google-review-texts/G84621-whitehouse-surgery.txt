Rating: 5 stars
My family has been registered at this practice for many years and I felt that I should give our opinion after reading so many negative reviews. Generally, people are very quick to criticize, but few give praise where it’s due. We’re all aware of the many difficulties faced by the NHS at the moment - budgetary constraints, shortage of staff, an increasing population etc but our personal experience is that despite all these problems and very long waiting lists at hospitals, this GP practice has done its very best for our family over the years. We personally, have no complaints at all about the doctors, other health professionals who work at this surgery or the Reception team. We accept that living in a densely populated area, our health concerns are triaged and if it’s deemed urgent, we have nearly always been seen quickly and appropriate action taken. If the problem isn’t urgent, we’ve been happy to wait to talk to a doctor. Both Dr Fenuyi and Dr Oginni have always been caring, compassionate, listened to our concerns, made appropriate referrals if necessary and provided excellent care with follow up telephone calls when needed. This high level of care and expertise was also shown by a locum doctor and the wider team of health professionals employed by Whitehouse surgery. Similarly, we have also had a good experience with the Reception team at this practice, who have been polite, helpful and efficient and if there have been any queries or problems which needed addressing, they have always done their best to do so. Thank you to everyone at this practice.
