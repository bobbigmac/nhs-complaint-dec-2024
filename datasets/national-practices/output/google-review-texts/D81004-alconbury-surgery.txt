I have had a lot of positive experiences with this surgery, however if you are going for menstrual health or contraception I would recommend that you advocate for yourself and prepare for your appointment. I had a ln IUD fitting which had some difficulties and was managed well, but the follow-up and addressing of my concerns and issues with GPs has been appalling. Six months after insertion, I began experiencing uncomfortable and abnormal side effects. I was told by two separate GPs that this was normal. No follow-ups, blood tests or physical examinations were carried out. I have now switched GP due to unrelated reasons, where they have quickly conducted further investigations and found that my IUD is not correctly placed. Month of discomfort and risk could have been avoided had the GPs chosen to request an ultrasound, especially as this is a likely cause of my symptoms.
