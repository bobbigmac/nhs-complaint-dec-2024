Thanks to all the Greenfield Medical Centre team!

I booked an appointment via the Patchs online system as a necessary follow-up after a recent car accident. This incident has significantly exacerbated my pre-existing limited mobility and chronic spinal pain. In my Patchs request, I provided a comprehensive account of my symptoms, including details of the hospital where I was held overnight for further essential investigations. I explicitly stated that, due to the severe and acute nature of my condition—which includes difficulty walking and a potential risk of paralysis in my upper and lower limbs—I required a consultation with a Senior Doctor. Despite the gravity of my medical situation, Dr. Saloni K made the clinical decision that my problems were minor and scheduled my appointment not with a doctor, but with a paramedic, and insisted it be face-to-face. At today's appointment, the attending paramedic, Miss. Josephine N, was exceedingly rude and unprofessional. She inquired about my symptoms while simultaneously reading my hospital discharge letter. She then aggressively admonished me for speaking, stating she could not read while I was talking. She continued in a dismissive and abrasive tone, informing me that helping me was beyond her remit, that she did not have my hospital letter in the system, and that she was unable to assist me. She concluded by stating that I needed to book an appointment with a doctor. Following this unacceptable interaction, I went to the reception desk to provide a copy of my hospital discharge documents. I also recounted the entire incident. The reception staff, however, attempted to dismiss my concerns by claiming they had no record of my Patchs request in the system at all. They further suggested that an appointment for such a serious matter would not have been booked via Patchs (despite the system being designed for this purpose), and initially insisted they could not locate my request, which they later found. Most disturbingly, I was then accused of being rude myself for daring to raise a legitimate concern and for pointing out the chaotic operation of the practice, which jeopardises patient health and wastes their time. It is my firm belief that the management of Greenfield Medical Centre is engaging in these practices consciously to generate additional appointments and thereby charge the NHS for unnecessary follow-up visits, at the direct expense of patient well-being. I expect a thorough investigation into this matter and a detailed written response addressing the conduct of both Dr. Kapoor, for her poor clinical triage, and Ms. Newsome, for her unprofessional behaviour. I also require confirmation of the steps that will be taken to ensure this does not happen to another vulnerable patient.
