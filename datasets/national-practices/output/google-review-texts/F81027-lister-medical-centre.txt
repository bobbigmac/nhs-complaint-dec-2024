I am extremely dissatisfied with the service at Lister House in Harlow. The booking system is chaotic and almost impossible to use. The practice keeps telling patients to book appointments online, but the online system is constantly “at capacity”, making it impossible to secure a visit even for urgent medical issues. Reception staff are unhelpful and repeatedly tell patients to use forms on the website instead of offering any real support. I was in severe pain and needed a medical assessment and a sick note, but I kept being redirected online with no clear guidance and no access to a doctor. Communication is poor, delays are long, and there is no sense of continuity of care. Patients are left without help and forced to chase basic medical services. Very disappointing experience. This practice urgently needs to improve patient access and support.
