If I could 0 stars I would. I don't normally write reviews but after the amount of problems my family and I have encountered with this incompetent service I feel forced to leave one. My family have been patients with Eagle eye surgery (good service back then) for many years and then the surgery was merged and became GP pathfinder clinics and since then the service has been atrocious. It is impossible to get an appointment. You have to fill out an online questionnaire for EVERYTHING. They tell you they will contact you and then I've had no call back/contact! I've had to chase them up. (My sister is still waiting for a callback for a rash from 4 weeks ago!!!) - and no we should not have to chase up appointments all the time. They don't bother taking your availability into account (apparently we're all sitting at home twiddling our thumbs so can answer their callback phones at the drop of a hat). I've spoken to a pharmacist before for blood results and he told me to start iron tablets. I asked him a very specific question related to my medical history that needs to be checked to ensure I actually require them. (The GP has prescribed me iron tablets in the past by mistake for the same reason and then called me back to stop taking them). The pharmacist quite obviously had no clue what I was talking about but maintained I should start the tablets anyway (Clearly the question was out of his scope). On a separate all the doctors I have encountered at the Eagle eye surgery have been amazing! This purely seems to be a management issue in how the surgery is run and I could go on and on about the incompetency of this place.
