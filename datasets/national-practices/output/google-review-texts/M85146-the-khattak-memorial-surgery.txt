Practice response date: a month ago
Practice response:
Thank you for taking the time to share your experience with us. We’re very sorry to hear about the confusion and frustration caused during your wife’s first registration appointment. This is not the level of clarity or service we aim to provide. There are 2 different types of health checks we carry out for patients. The first is a new patient health check.When booking a new patient health check, a blood test is not routinely carried out for under 40s. This is only done if the GP refers the patient for a test. The second is an NHS health check which is done for 40-74 year olds who do not have certain conditions, described in the link below, and will include a blood test. Please click on the link for NHS Health Checks for further details. https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/nhs-health-check/ We understand how important it is for patients to receive clear information and consistent explanations, especially when attending a new surgery. Your feedback about the mixed messages regarding forms, changing processes, and blood tests has been noted, and we will review this internally with the whole team (receptionist and clinical staff) to help prevent similar situations in the future. From January 2026, stricter guidelines have come into place regarding NHS health checks which the nurse was referring to. We can reassure you this was not about unwillingness but miscommunication in what can and cannot be done routinely. Your feedback has highlighted our need to improve on communication. We will share this with our team and hope to provide a better patient experience going forward.
