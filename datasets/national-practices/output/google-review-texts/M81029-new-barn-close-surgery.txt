Author: Kelly Dunning
Date: 7 months ago
Rating: 5 stars
I needed some medical care while on a vacation walking in the Cotswolds. When I tried to get help at a pharmacy in Winchcombe they sent me to the clinic in Winchcombe - where the staff were pretty unhelpful and unwilling to treat me. I get that I am not a local resident (Canadian travelling in the UK) and I don't have an NHS number, but the treatment I got there was pretty cold and dismissive. So, since we were on the way to Broadway that day, I called ahead to New Barn Close Surgery and explained my situation. A lovely staff member named Nina answered the phone and was so warm and helpful. She reassured me that they could treat me right away and that all I'd need to do was fill out a temporary patient form with the address of the hotel where I was staying and my home address. I went directly there and the form was waiting for me on reception. I got the treatment I needed as quickly as possible and it was so much easier than I expected. Everyone at New Barn Close was wonderfully helpful, offering clear information and support and soon I was feeling better! Not sure why the folks in Winchcombe were so resistant to helping me. Maybe they were having a bad day?! Either way, I want to thank Nina and everyone else (sorry I don't remember any other names) at New Barn Close who helped me.
