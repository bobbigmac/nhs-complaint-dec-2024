Fortunately I don't go often, but whenever I have truly needed them they have been great. The staff in reception as well as the GPs are trans friendly and with the practice assistant particularly I felt comfortable enough to come out and start my journey through the Welsh Gender Service. To say that this was a key moment for me isn't doing it justice really! The only disappointment is that they clearly follow a set of rigid guidelines and I couldn't convince anyone to help with bridging hormones to start the process off there and then, but I do appreciate that this is only possible through the correct channels. A combination of politics and fear of litigation is undoubtedly the reason they can't act. Changing my name was easyz and it's nice to see my new name on the screen when I'm called in to see someone. I don't understand those complaining about the staff, they do everything they can! Shout out to the Pharmacy staff too, also a nice bunch.
