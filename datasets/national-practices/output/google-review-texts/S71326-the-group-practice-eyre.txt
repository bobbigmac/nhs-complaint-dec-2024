They flat out refuse to have anything to do with you unless you phone them. If you phone, you only get a chance for one hour of the day (between 08:30 - 09:30) and they may or may not let you see your 'regular' doctor, usually not. There is no ability to phone at any time in the day and ask for a future appointment. The staff are totally inflexible and the doctors are only there to refer you to other parts of the NHS, which are even worse. When you do go into the practice the place is very uncomfortable to wait in, and doctors are happy to be 20+ minutes plus late. Maybe it's not just this practice and the entire NHS in Scotland is unfit for purpose?
