That said, we recognise that for each individual team member, there can occasionally be small gaps in knowledge. Your feedback is extremely helpful as it allows us to reflect on where we can improve the information and support we provide. We always welcome feedback as an opportunity to learn and strengthen our services. If you would be willing to contact us at the practice, we would really appreciate the opportunity to speak with you directly, apologise personally, and help ensure you receive the information or care you were looking for. Please feel free to contact us either by telephone: 020 8864 4868, or via our online PATCHS system, which can be accessed here: Patchs You are welcome to request to speak to our management team and one of us will call you back at the earliest opportunity. Alternatively, some local pharmacies are now able to provide certain contraceptive pills without the need for a prescription. Thank you again for your feedback. If you are able to get in touch with the practice, we would be very happy to assist you further. Our apologies again.
