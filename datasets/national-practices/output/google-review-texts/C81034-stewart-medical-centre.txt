Author: Laura Mobey

Rating: 5 stars
(Laura Kenyon) I felt compelled to leave a review (as a former Elmwood patient) after my recent interactions with the staff at Stewart Medical Centre. Although I have no axe to grind regarding my historical dealings with Elmwood, I always received professional and timely help during my 40 plus years with them, I have noticed a marked positive difference since Stewart medical centre acquired the Elmwood practice. The administrative staff will go above and beyond to ensure that enquiries are dealt with swiftly and this helpful attitude is always accompanied by a cheerful disposition. It makes ringing the practice a pleasure, knowing that you will receive efficient yet empathetic advice and resolutions! It is much easier to find an appointment now, whether that be prebookable or on the day. I admittedly have struggled before the takeover when trying to get appointments for ill children with tonsillitis, ear infections etc. My daughter, who has suffered with tonsillitis has been seen by the pharmacist (and prescribed antibiotics) more times than a GP or Nurse during her time at Elmwood; I could never get her an appointment. This problem appears to have been well and truly resolved as my son needed antibiotics for an extended period and easily secured several appointments at Stewart. Well done Stewart Medical Centre staff. You run an efficient and customer friendly operation which is truly appreciated!
