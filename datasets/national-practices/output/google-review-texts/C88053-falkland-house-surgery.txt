I switched doctors in the early 90s to become a patient at Falkland House Surgery. In all the years since, the care and support received has been — simply brilliant! I have to attend quite regularly and find that all the Dr’s, nurses, reception and support staff are friendly, professional and are more than competent at their jobs.
