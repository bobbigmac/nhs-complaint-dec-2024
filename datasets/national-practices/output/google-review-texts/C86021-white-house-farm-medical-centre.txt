I will start out by saying that receptionist is very lovely and caring, she's very well suited for the role, but it feels as if many of the doctors there do not care about you and it feels as if they want you out as quickly as possible, whereas the kind and caring are a rare few. It takes days of calling over and over to get a simple paperwork problem sorted, as doctors are muddled up and they don't know who is available and not. The practice is quite disorganized and there are too many patients for the little staff there, which is no fault of their own, but still a negative in terms of care. I've even had incidents where I've been waiting over half an hour for my doctor past my appointment time (eg: appointment at 10:30, doctor arrives at 11:05). I feel like some work on organisation needs to be done, but also that there is a cap on patients to alleviate some stress on the practice, as it is affecting performance and effectiveness of care. TLDR: Many of the doctors want you out asap, Receptionist is lovely, needs a patient cap, practice is overloaded and disorganized, takes ages to get a simple problem sorted with doctors. Mainly problem with many of the doctors I've met there, not all of them.
