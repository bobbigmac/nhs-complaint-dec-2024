Author: Magdalini Papapetrou
Date: a year ago
Rating: 1 star
Today 18/02/2025 called this GP practice to book an urgent appointment as I suspected I had shingles, a serious condition that requires timely treatment. Before someone answered, there was a recorded message stating that they would ask about my condition and that verbal abuse would not be tolerated. The receptionist picked up, the interaction felt robotic and lacking in empathy. After explaining my concern, I was simply told that the earliest available appointment was on March 12th—nearly a month away in a careless way. Is this not a sort of verbal abuse, I am wondering! When I reiterated that shingles is a serious condition requiring prompt attention, I was then told to call back at 9 AM for an urgent appointment with a comment around her non competency and obligation of advising. A little empathy and clear communication would go a long way, especially for patients who are already feeling unwell. A GP receptionist should understand the urgency of certain conditions and provide guidance in a more considerate manner. Unfortunately, this experience left me feeling frustrated and unheard. I hope the practice improves its approach to patient interactions in the future.
