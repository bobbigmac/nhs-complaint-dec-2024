Author: Bex K
Date: 2 months ago
Rating: 1 star
I have been meaning to write this review for years on behalf of my husband. As I understand it from local word of mouth, one doctor at this surgery is very laxe and works only 2 or 3 days a week, while others are OK, so it is luck of the draw who you are assigned to. My husband some years ago called up after several days of ongoing stomach pain, frequent toilet visits and bloody stool. He was given a telephone appointment with his GP and it was concluded this is not something to get an in-person examination for as it is quite normal, and he should wait for it to pass. It was his first call to the surgery since he had been to CWH with stomach pains about 7 years earlier. Then he had been given steroids which quickly cleared the problem. He called the GP back around a week later when his symptoms felt worse, and was given a similar message. After several weeks of pain, one Saturday the pain became completely unbearable and he called 111. The call handler said he must immediately take himself to the closest hospital for an emergency appointment. He was immediately told he needs a colonoscopy and that this was an emergency which should have been raised weeks earlier. Upon re-booking to see his GP for prescription medication, the GP laughed and said it seemed he had forgotten to tell him 7 years ago that the hospital had found that he has ulcerative colitis. He was supposed to have passed on the message but had forgotten to do so. The later colonoscopy revealed the Ulcerative Colitis had advanced and had become Crohn's Colitis - who knows if it could have been better controlled if my husband was told 7 years earlier that he has inflammatory bowel disease. I see from other reviews this clinic has previously missed Crohn's cases in other patients, so probably somewhere to avoid with any stomach related issues. Personally I go to Cassidy Medical Centre nearby because they offer Saturday appointments, and the contrast in quality of these surgeries is incredible.
