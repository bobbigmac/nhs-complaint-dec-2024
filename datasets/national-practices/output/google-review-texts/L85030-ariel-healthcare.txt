Author: Marlene Clifton

Rating: 1 star
I woke up Tuesday 10th March 2026 with a seriously swollen left eye and pain. Went to Ariel surgery Summerfields to see a doctor but told there were none available. The receptionist could see how badly swollen it was but was told to go to Chard MIU or Taunton. I went to Chard MIU and was told that Robert Frith would see me that afternoon to look at the back of my eye. He said I needed antibiotics so emailed through to the surgery. I went back to the surgery expecting to receive a prescription for antibiotics but no, they hadn't received it. We have heard nothing more from Ariel to date. Next day went back to MIU Chard and received the long awaited antibiotics. They passed on to Taunton opthalmic dept a report to say that it was pre-septal cellulitis and they rang me in the afternoon to see how I was. Extremely disappointed with Ariel and their receptionist, and only gave 1 star as I couldn't give less.
