This is an update from a previous review. Unfortunately, I have started to experience increasing problems with the staff here, mainly the admin staff and the practice manager. I have sent four different letters to the practice, which are usually passed on to the practice manager. In October, I sent a letter about the difficulty I was having obtaining my blood test results and my lack of access to my online records. As I understand it, patients are supposed to be given access to their digital records within 31 days of registering, but I was not given access. The practice manager ignored the letter, although she did deal with a reasonable adjustment request and other concerns in December within about 3 days. When my son raised the issue with her, she said she had been covering reception and that she would address it, but she never did. It was not until I spoke to a different member of staff in February 2026 that I was finally given access. I also raised concerns by letter about an appointment with a pharmacist, which I will not go into here. Admin staff then tried to claim the letter had been placed in the feedback pile because it was titled “feedback”. However, their complaints page explicitly says that they welcome feedback. The same staff member who later helped me gain access to my records spoke to the practice manager and said she knew the letter needed addressing, but that she had been on vacation. Again, the excuse of covering reception was also used, not responded to, which is not good enough. After gaining access to my records, I reviewed them because I have previously had problems at other medical centres with conditions either not being recorded or coded correctly, being marked as resolved when they should not have been, or being left out altogether. Some of the missing or incorrect information relates to my swallowing issues, which is a significant matter. Because of this, I sent two rectification letters, one on 16/02 and one on 23/02. However, these were not even logged by the practice manager until 10/03. The law states that if requests are complex, or if they cannot be dealt with within 31 days, the practice is supposed to contact you, explain the delay, and let you know that they need an additional two months. They have not done this. One of those letters is now overdue. I have contacted the ICO to confirm that my letter is indeed a rectification request, so that the practice cannot use another excuse, as they did with one of my other letters. The ICO confirmed that it is. Another concern is that a GP has now offered or prescribed me tablets or capsules on two separate occasions, despite him being told about my swallowing difficulties. I feel this is at least partly because my records do not properly state these issues, due to failings by my previous medical centre not recording and coding conditions correctly. The delay by the practice manager in dealing with my rectification requests isn't at all helpful. I have since contacted NCGPA, who say that the practice manager is reviewing my email and will get back to me by the end of the week. If the issues aren't addressed, I'll have no other option than to raise a complaint with the ICO.
