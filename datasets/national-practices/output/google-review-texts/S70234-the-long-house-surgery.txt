Author: Dale Davies

Rating: 5 stars
The Long House Surgery has been an exceptional medical practice for my entire family of three. They're incredibly reliable at providing medical assistance as best as they can with the availability they have, including phone appointments (which have been a brilliant at providing advice - and settling nerves! - with a child at home). One drawback, if I have to share any, is that I rarely see the same GP on the occasion that I do need to see someone, but I believe this is probably the way of modern general practice, where making medical advise available throughout the day is more important than trying to develop a 1:1 relationship between GP and patient.
