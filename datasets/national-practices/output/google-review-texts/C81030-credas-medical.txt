Author: Rachel DarleyDale
Date: a month ago
Rating: 5 stars
Saw Dr Tallantyre as the first appointment of the day, practice was exemplary. The appointment entirely unrushed despite taking longer than the allocated time, I felt listened too and understood throughout. Dr Tallantyre demonstrated in depth understanding of neurodiversity and the use of a personalised care approach. Would highly recommend this GP, particularly for those in need of support where neurodivergence is a consideration. Nurse Shadbolt later took bloods and was wonderful. She spoke very highly of her team and colleagues, providing a card to allow me to feedback - at my request. The NHS have a tough job of meeting a demand that will always exceed capacity, but members of staff at this practice conduct themselves professionally and compassionately, relatively consistently. Of course they will handle the occasional complaint, but it is really important that we feed back when things go well to support the staff working in such demanding roles. Would urge anyone thinking about leaving a negative review to remember that staff are human - if you call the surgery and are rude to them, that can be quite upsetting for the receptionist, who may then appear to be unhelpful. They’re actually upset, and probably have a few tears after some of these calls. Don’t shoot the messenger. The likelihood is that staff are just as frustrated that they can’t do what you are asking - the reception team don’t create the policies and procedures, and are doing their best. The practice can’t function without the reception team and people taking out frustrations on them just means they don’t stay in these front line posts, and next time you call it will be a new trainee answering, who can help you even less! Be polite, thank them for their help, if you need to grumble take a moment to acknowledge it is *not their fault* and explain your frustration politely. If everyone stays on the same side, there is more chance they are able to think of another suggestion - the reception team have more insight than you will ever know if you don’t give them a chance to help you. (And no I don’t work there, am entirely unconnected, but I do understand people!!!) Well done and thank you team Credas!
