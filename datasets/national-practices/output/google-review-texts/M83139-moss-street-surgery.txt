I injured my index finger and attended A&E in Wolverhampton, where I received only a basic check-up and no medication. Initially, I thought things were fine, but about two months later I developed an infection. I visited my GP twice - the first prescribed antibiotics that didn’t help, and during the second appointment, the doctor mentioned he was unsure whether it was even an infection. The condition worsened to the point where I had to travel back to my home country, where it was confirmed to be a serious infection, and I finally received the correct treatment. I wish the issue had been investigated more thoroughly earlier, as it could have prevented a lot of pain and an operation. The staff is overall good with behaviour, but the diagnosis capability is not good.
