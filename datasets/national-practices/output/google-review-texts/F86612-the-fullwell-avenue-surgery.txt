We’re sorry to hear about your experience and that you felt disappointed by the outcome, particularly while you were in pain — we understand how distressing injuries that affect mobility can be. Following review, the advice provided was based on clinical safety. GP practices are not always able to arrange urgent MRI scans directly, especially when an immediate assessment or hospital-based imaging pathway is required. In situations where symptoms may need urgent reassessment or same-day investigation, patients may be advised to return to A&E so that hospital teams can review and organise the appropriate imaging without delay.
