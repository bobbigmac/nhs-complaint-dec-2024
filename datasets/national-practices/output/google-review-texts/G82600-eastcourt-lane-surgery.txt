I’m deeply disappointed and distressed by the treatment my mum received at the East Court Lane practice. She was told that she simply “needed to go on a treadmill and lose weight “ — this was said without any physical assessment being carried out on any part of my mums body. This response was not only dismissive but completely inappropriate, especially considering the reason for her visit. Prior to this appointment, the doctor had agreed with the ambulance service that my mum would receive a full check-up due to ongoing breathing difficulties. The ambulance crew themselves advised that I needed a thorough examination. That agreement was ignored, and instead, she was brushed off with assumptions and no investigation. As a result of this failure, she ended up in and out of A&E for two weeks straight, still with no proper answers or care. Eventually, after relentless effort on my mums part , she was diagnosed with five different serious health conditions — one of which is cancer. This entire experience highlights a total disregard for basic human rights and medical duty of care. Every person deserves to have their health concerns taken seriously, especially when symptoms are severe and ongoing. That didn’t happen here, and it’s had devastating consequences. No one should have to fight this hard just to be seen and properly assessed.
