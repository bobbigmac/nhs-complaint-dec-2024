Anonymous review for safety For a long time, I hated GP practices. The anxiety of calling, the constant feeling of being a burden, and previous experiences where I felt blamed or judged for my illnesses meant I avoided seeking help whenever I could. I came to this practice purely by chance and out of necessity. I’d seen the reviews and wasn’t hopeful, but it turned out to be the best decision I could have made. I’ve been registered here for around four years now, so I feel I can honestly comment on what this practice is really like. Nothing has ever felt like too much trouble. I’ve never been blamed for my health issues, and for the first time, I genuinely feel seen and heard. That feeling only grew stronger when my daughter was born. From day one, they supported me — even when I was probably being a very anxious first-time mum. When my daughter was just eight weeks old, I was convinced she had a UTI. Everyone else dismissed me and made me feel like I was overreacting, but not this practice. The doctor listened, gave us plenty of time, and helped us get a sample (which was very awkward and took a while with an 8-week-old). It turned out to be a UTI — rare, but real — and she was treated promptly because we were listened to. Any time I’ve had concerns, we’ve been seen straight away or as soon as physically possible. I’m never made to feel like I’m overreacting. Every single time I attend with my daughter, they ask how I am too — something that hasn’t gone unnoticed. On tough days, that simple kindness can carry me through. I’ve been in more than most. I hate being sick, I hate taking medication, and I hate needing doctors, but the anxiety I once felt even making a phone call has dramatically reduced since joining this practice. When we walk in, they sign us in before we even reach the desk. They know my daughter’s name, comment on her outfits or bows, and speak to her like she truly matters. They treat her as if she’s one of their own — and as a mum, that means absolutely everything. I’ve had truly awful experiences elsewhere: judgemental receptionists, doctors commenting on my weight or appearance, and being labelled as aggressive. I used to have panic attacks just trying to call and suffered in pain for years because I was too afraid to ask for help. Since coming here, that has changed completely. Every doctor I’ve seen has been kind, unrushed, and non-judgemental. They know me — and that might not mean much to some people, but for me it’s life-changing. It feels like I can finally stop apologising for illnesses that are outwith my control and actually receive the care I need. I know the NHS is under huge strain, and I’ve seen that my whole life, but even now — including hospital care — I don’t receive the level of treatment and compassion that I get here. If I’d listened to the reviews four years ago and gone elsewhere, I genuinely dread to think where I’d be now or how much I’d be suffering. I can’t recommend this practice enough. They have been, and continue to be, an incredible support to both me and my daughter, and I’ll be forever indebted to them. x
