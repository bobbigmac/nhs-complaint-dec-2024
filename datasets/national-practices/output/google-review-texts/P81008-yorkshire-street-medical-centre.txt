Disgraceful behaviour from horrible receptionist / staff member. I registered online and was asked to come down to the surgery with photo/address ID to complete the process. When I arrived, instead of being assisted, I was set upon almost immediately with shocking rudeness and passive aggression from one of the three reception/admin staff. This individual was intentionally argumentative from the outset, somehow managing to manufacture offence at almost anything I said. Rather than helping me register, she seemed intent on creating a fake conflict. When I calmly asked for her name to make a formal complaint, she refused and continued being confrontational. Eventually, I said I would rather deal with one of her colleagues and she stood up and walked away in anger, leaving me to be assisted by a colleague. In stark contrast, the colleague was polite, professional, and processed my registration in under three minutes - showing how straightforward and painless this process should have been. First impressions matter, and this was a truly appalling one. Such behaviour is entirely unacceptable in a healthcare setting where people attend usually because they are not feeling well but certainly not to be abused at the same time. The staff member in question is clearly unfit for a public-facing role and if she is to stay there needs some anger management and basic patient care training (or possibly support if there are underlying issues). I will be submitting a formal complaint and urging the practice to review CCTV/audio recording, if available, which will prove utterly damning evidence. Patients deserve respect and professionalism, not hostility and abuse – my first impression: truly disgusted. One has to wonder whether a culture of impunity exists at the Surgery that might allow a receptionist - employed to assist and care for patients – wrongly believes that her persistently reprehensible and abusive behaviour was somehow acceptable? While it is not the welcome any newly registering patient should have to experience, in the spirit of fairness, I can only hope that this abhorrent behaviour is not a reflection on the doctors or indeed on other reception staff, who I can only hope will be professional and polite...
