Author: Kate Packham

The worst GP surgery I've ever been with. I know the enormous pressure and strain health services are under but there is no excuse for inhumane and negligent practice. I've worked in the healthcare sector for years and would never dream of treating anyone the way they do, no matter how bad of a time I was having or if I was on an 18 hour shift. Never. I've been pushed pillar to post for years and frequently felt completely unheard or cared for. The worst experience I had was waiting for transport to A&E and being left collapsed in the rain in November, while they locked the door behind me and plunged me into darkness... because it was closing time. Must have been an exceptionally riveting Antiques Road Show episode on to rush home to! Hope it was worth the years of nightmares it gave me. Thankfully, I managed to get to A&E, who were lovely. There are some really good staff there, who treat patients like people, do their best to help within the parameters of very challenging roles, and have been upset by their colleagues' behaviour. Unfortunately, staff who clearly detest their job (and apparently humanity), are the majority. I advise anyone thinking of moving within their catchment zone to reconsider, especially if you have any chronic health issues. I wish I'd moved years ago. If you do end up with them, may the odds be ever in your favour!
