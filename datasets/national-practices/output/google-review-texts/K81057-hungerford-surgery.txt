Author: Dhelliwe Ngonyama

Rating: 5 stars
Rang the surgery with a sudden onset cough. Was given an appointment to see the doctor same day and the doctor prescribed inhalers for me promptly. My condition changed overnight and the next day I rang the surgery again and had antibiotics prescribed. Even though the first course did not work I rang the surgery again the following week and another appointment was arranged at short notice . Another course of antibiotics and steroids were prescribed. At each occasion the doctors insisted on seeing me In person before prescribing anything. As my condition changed over the course of the 3 weeks each week I rang , I was given an appointment same day as an emergency. Staff at reception were very professional and polite but also empathetic. I felt confident and safe with the service being provided at The Hungerford Surgery. I was treated with care and compassion by both the doctors, nurses and receptionist at the surgery. The blood pressure machine at the entrance of the surgery is beneficial as it saves time and gives the doctor more time to assess the patient during consultation. The blood pressure print outs are also good for the patient to take their blood pressure at home as it gives the doctors a snapshot of the patient’s blood pressure and helps in decision making. My prescriptions were sent to boots pharmacy in hungerford electronically which saves time . The txt messaging service is also a good reminder as it shows the surgery is using all forms of communication to communicate with the patient.I received an excellent service .
