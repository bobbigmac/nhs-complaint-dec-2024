Cannot speak highly enough of Park Medical Centre, they’re actually so brilliant all my family who live out of area are jealous! I have multiple complex chronic conditions and the practice manages this impeccably, all the GPs I’ve seen have known my medical history (and there’s a lot of it!) and have been kind, patient, and attentive. I always feel like I’m playing an active part in decisions that are being made which is really important to me. The new digital front door is wonderful, super easy to get an appointment (no 8am scramble and waiting on hold!) and it’s always with who you need to see and when works for you. The reception and admin staff are all lovely too, and very polite and understanding. The pharmacists and nurses too have been wonderful when I’ve needed them and really thorough. Massive thank you to everyone involved in the running of the practice, I’m very grateful and it certainly makes my life easier having such a brilliant GP.
