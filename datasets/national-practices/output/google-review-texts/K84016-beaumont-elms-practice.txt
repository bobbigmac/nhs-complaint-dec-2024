Author: Adam Suttle
Date: 3 months ago
Rating: 1 star
Register with Jericho health instead as it is faster and they actually allow you to book over the phone. When I called 19 Beaumont St, I was directed to use the online form, despite clearly emphasizing the urgency and pain (but not an A&E need). I was promised a clinical call-back within a few hours, which never occurred. The online portal closes completely over the weekend, making it impossible to submit a medical request or book an appointment until Monday morning - when most people are busy with their work week. This forces a painful delay in receiving necessary treatment and prescription changes. Due to the lack of timely access and the severity of my pain, I ultimately had to reregister with a different practice (Jericho Health). They were able to speak to me immediately over the phone and booked a same-day appointment without issue.

If you would like to discuss this further, please do not hesitate to get in contact via our website, and I am sorry you had a negative experience with us. Best wishes, Beau HR and Reception Manager
