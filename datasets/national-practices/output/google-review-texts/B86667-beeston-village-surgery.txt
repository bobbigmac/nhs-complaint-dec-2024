I want to offer a different perspective to the one star reviews which often seem to be based on a single negative encounter. I have been with this surgery for just under 10 years and I predominantly get to see same clinician (Dr Umar who is an excellent doctor ⭐️) I work in healthcare myself and believe me when I say this I am incredibly particular when it comes to my own care. Over the years I have received a lot of support from the practice team for both minor and more significant health issues. I have been treated by APCs, GPs, physiotherapists, practice nurses, had my travel vaccinations here as well as various screening tests. You name it, I have probably experienced it. I have always found the care to be very good and competent. My prescriptions and admin requests are dealt with quickly, phone calls are answered. I can always get appointment when I need it, the triage process is reasonable and the reception staff have been helpful. The surgery also continues to make improvements in care and service delivery which is great to see.
