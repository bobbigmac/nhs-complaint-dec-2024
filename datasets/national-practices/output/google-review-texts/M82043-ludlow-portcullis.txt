Author: Andy Metcalf

Rating: 5 stars
I am not a regular poster of reviews on google - nor I suspect are 90% of Portcullis Surgery patients. I am certain this means the google reviews do not reflect the typical patient experience of Portcullis Surgery. I am a 73 year old male and have been a patient at Portcullis for over 10 years (dr Beanland). I have had an array of health issues in that time, some serious, most less so, and I have used a lot of Portcullis’ many services.. I have always received responsive, professional and caring support from everyone that works there. Of course they (like the entire NHS) are under immense pressure, there are limits on what they can do, and sometimes issues arise. However, every time I have discussed concerns, politely, they always go the extra mile and ensure my needs are met. If you are disappointed or frustrated with your experience with Portcullis Surgery please try a polite email or phone call rather than posting negative comments and reviews - you will get better results personally, and the staff at an excellent practice, that we are lucky to have in Ludlow, will feel the appreciation they deserve.
