Needs serious improvement. Some doctors are good, but the reception and process issues let this place down. The check-in PC has been broken for months, phone queues are often 20+ long, and the front-desk staff can be unhelpful or slow. Even when it works, the wait can still be up to two weeks. It’s not the worst surgery ever, but the bar is way too low, and things could be much better with basic fixes.
