Author: Rebecca

Update as of December 2025: I’ve upped my rating to 3 stars as this surgery has experienced huge improvements this year and has restored much of my confidence. The availability of appointments is far better, reception staff seem less stressed/rushed, and there is a much greater consistency of care. Dr Abdulkareem is the best GP I have ever had here — he truly does make you feel seen, heard and supported, working with you to determine the best course of treatment and making the appropriate referrals when necessary. Emily and Monica have also been wonderful with regards to mental health checks, medication reviews and blood test results. Original review written in 2022: My GP will tell me they need me to make another appointment for in 3 weeks time (for example) but this surgery only does "on the day" appointments - you can't book in advance and you must call at 8am to be able to get one of these... but if you're caller 15+ then you'll get cut off, lose your place in the queue and by the time you finally get through after dialling again appointments have all gone. I don't think I have ever seen the same GP in my entire time being there so have completely inconsistent care and advice, and multiple times, I've seen somebody I was led to believe was a GP but isn't so they have to go and check with someone who is! My appointments are almost always over the phone and I've been left on the same medication for 7+ years now without regular medication reviews. In the past, I've even been left in the waiting area on my own until closing time and it was only when the final member of staff was due to leave that they informed me that the GP I was there to see hadn't been in the surgery that day... I was left for over an hour waiting for an appointment with someone who wasn't there and no one thought to tell me when I had "checked in"! In my opinion, the surgery has taken on too many patients and is no longer effective at the role it's supposed to provide to our community. Undoubtedly they will take on new patients from the new housing estates being built locally. The 1-star is because a) I can't give 0 stars and b) Because I've had positive experiences with the nurses upstairs and when I had my COVID booster there. Original review written in 2023: I posted my original review almost a year ago and this surgery has become even worse to the point that I have complained about negligence and feel that it should no longer be open at all. Letters from specialists at the hospital detailing medication et al that I should be given were not read let alone actioned, my online form submission received a text message which clearly demonstrated it had not been read at all (and only got a response after chasing them) and when I finally did get a face-to-face appointment after escalating my complaint, I was told by Dr Kleine I would need to go private to receive the help and treatment I required - despite it being available on the NHS had she made a referral - and that only 1 aspect of why I was there could be discussed despite them being related. Likewise, my mother has been unable to get even a phone appointment despite calling each day at 8am recently - we have already had to pay out crazy amounts of money for private consultations just so she could get a diagnosis because even when she has managed to speak to a GP at this surgery they have dismissed it and left her with no treatment plan or medication to the point she is now disabled and currently bed-ridden. We have also recently learned from the Social Prescription Service that my mother’s official diagnosis (from a private consultant) had not been added to her NHS records despite the evidence being in their possession and another patient’s consultation notes/diagnoses were added to my mum’s health record. Having incorrect patient records is hugely irresponsible and dangerous. This place is an absolute disgrace. I don't have any empathy because even pre-COVID this surgery was a disaster and it's just fallen into further decline that it's not fit for purpose.
