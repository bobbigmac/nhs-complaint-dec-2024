Author: Inna V.

Rating: 1 star
This GP surgery has seriously declined, and the level of service is no longer acceptable. It is almost impossible to get an appointment. Calling first thing in the morning makes no difference, you’re either stuck in a queue that goes nowhere or told that all appointments are gone within minutes. The online system is no better, with little to no availability. Patients are effectively being shut out of accessing basic healthcare. To make matters worse, I was offered a telephone appointment a week later while I am on holiday, and expected to keep the entire day free from 9:45am just to wait for a call. This is completely unreasonable. Patients have lives, work, and commitments, and expecting someone to block out an entire day for a vague phone slot is simply not acceptable. This isn’t just inconvenient, it’s unacceptable. People rely on their GP for timely medical support, and the current system completely fails to meet that need. It creates unnecessary stress and leaves patients without proper care. There appears to be a complete disconnect between patient demand and the surgery’s ability (or willingness) to provide access. A GP service should not be this difficult to use. Serious improvements are needed, because right now this surgery is not delivering an adequate standard of care.

Author: Deludo (Deludo)
Date: 2 months ago
Rating: 1 star
I have been registered with West4GPS my whole life and, until recently, the service was excellent. I rarely need to see a doctor; however, over the past year I have been dealing with a complex medical issue and the quality of care has significantly declined. I have undergone six tests and was not contacted with the results after any of them. Each time, I had to rebook an appointment myself and was refused because the results had not yet been reviewed. I was left completely uninformed after every test and forced to repeatedly rebook and wait an additional two weeks. I understand the NHS is under significant pressure and I accept that patients may need to take a more active role in following up their care, even though this is not NHS standard. However, what is unacceptable were two separate phone appointments that felt rushed and left me with the clear impression that my concerns were not being taken seriously and that I was expected to manage the issue myself. I do not contact my GP unless I genuinely need medical help. Being left feeling dismissed while experiencing significant pain is distressing and unacceptable. Apologies to the dedicated staff at W4GP — some of you are excellent and do not deserve to be included in this criticism.
