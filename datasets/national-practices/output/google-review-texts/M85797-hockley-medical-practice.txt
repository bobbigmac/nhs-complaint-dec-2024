I’ve had such a great experience at this doctors’ surgery. I’ve been around 10 times now and always see the same doctor for my son, which feels incredibly rare these days. Dr Naeem is genuinely brilliant - he’s very understanding of individual circumstances and explains everything in a clear, easy-to-understand way, even if you don’t have a medical background. Nothing ever feels like too much to ask and I always feel truly heard, which makes such a difference. He takes as much time as you need and somehow always seems to be on time, or very close to it. Partway through leaving this review, I did notice the overall rating and I honestly don’t really understand why it’s so low. In my experience, people are often quicker to leave negative feedback than positive, especially when it comes to GP surgeries, and I think it’s important to highlight the many good experiences I’ve had here. The reception staff are also wonderful and always friendly, which I know isn’t always common in medical practices. I’ve also always had a great experience with the pharmacy. Overall, I can’t recommend this surgery highly enough.
