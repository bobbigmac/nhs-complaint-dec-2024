Author: Carol Commins
Date: a month ago
Rating: 4 stars
Overall Brinsley Medical Practice are a genuinely caring and professional organisation however, there are a few little niggles I have experienced! I completely understand that the practice is both large and busy but……prior to an appointment with any medical professional it would be pertinent where possible, to have some idea of my medical history so time spent on explaining and repeating history by myself is minimised. I find the NHS App excellent, and the practice are on the ball in entering and updating information however, some automated information isn’t always accurate for example: complete a Statin questionnaire following blood tests despite Statins being recorded as a total intolerance on my medical history and NHS App, as I’ll not be completing this questionnaire I don’t want to be considered as not being compliant hence incorrect automated information. In summary, Brinsley Medical Practice are a commendable and caring organisation. I feel confident that they look after me and I would recommend them without reserve.
