Practice response date: 4 months ago
Practice response:
From October 1st 2025 we have been online consultation access between 08.00am and 6.30pm Monday through Friday for Routine medical and Admin requests, if you complete the online form you can be sent a self booking link to make an appointment, our extended access surgery at Muxton is open on Saturday for patients who dont have availability during the week. You can access our online forms through your NHS app or our website at https://www.donningtonmedicalpractice.co.uk/ and select online consultation. This initiative was brought in by NHS England for all practices to follow. Calls can still be made to the practice, but you will not be instructed to call back in the morning, you will either be asked to go online and submit an online consultation, or one of the team can process the submission for you should you not have online access. This has been the proceedure since October 1st 2025.
