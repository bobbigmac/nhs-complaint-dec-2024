I tend to find all staff here are lovely, they go out of their way to help you, to be as kind as they can to you and all while dealing with the ever growing network of patients as Eccleshall continues to grow and grow around them and they do their best to accommodate the ever rising numbers. However my last quick pop into the Practice left me feeling unheard and unseen. I’ve got advice from a professional to book an appointment, I’ve got an ongoing physical medical problem that now requires a further appointment after more clear test results. So I went in Tuesday ordered a prescription and then tried to book a double appointment. I got told no appointments were currently available for booking and to come back in a couple of days (Friday) when they’d be up on the system. I returned to the Practice on the instructed day only to be asked at reception: “Does my request require a computer?” I took it as either the receptionist didn’t want to help me or something was wrong with the system. When I told her I wanted to book a double appointment it was immediate dismissal. Apparently they had nothing available still and I’ve been instructed to return again the next week and they’d have them up by then. Ive never had such a problem like this one before and its really starting to shake my trust in the Practice.
