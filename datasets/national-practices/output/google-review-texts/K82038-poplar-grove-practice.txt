I can’t fault the surgery at all. Reception staff have always been lovely to speak to and helpful and I do think they do get a lot of unnecessary grieve for things which are completely out of their control which is unfair. People forget that the issues are the same across the NHS, and they’re not responsible for over subscribed surgeries, lack of appointments etc they’re doing their job the best they can under immense pressure - they deserve some recognition and credit for the hard work they do. GPs which I’ve seen in person and also over the phone, have always made me feel heard, have massively listened and actually have taken the time to ensure the correct treatment is given, especially involving mental health and ongoing health issues, including follow ups go check ups. Best decision I’ve made in terms of my health registering with the practice when moving here. I cannot fault any of the care I’ve received over the last 9 months. A mention also for Dr McCormack who was brilliant with me and patient especially as I am a very anxious person with new faces. Put me at ease within minutes and listened to the background of the ongoing issue without rushing me and was great with the plan for treatment. All in all, not that I am a frequent visitor to the Practice, but when I have had appointments, those dealing with it have never once made me question they don’t care or can’t be bothered to listen. I can only speak from my own experiences with the Practice, but for me, absolutely no concerns at all and I’m grateful
