The actual procedure and care from the nurse who did the microsuction was excellent but the appointment system is a joke! You can’t make an appointment whilst you’re there and have to wait for someone to call you back later in the week. I waited until the Wednesday and eventually contacted Nimbus myself, to be told that it was in hand and someone would call me. Not a pleasant experience, having to unnecessarily continue with ear drops for that length of time!
