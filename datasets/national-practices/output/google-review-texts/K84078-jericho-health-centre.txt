Author: J Smith
Date: a year ago
Rating: 1 star
I had frustrating experience with the receptionists here when seeking urgent medical attention. Several years ago, I developed facial paralysis, with a numb cheek and a drooping eyelid, causing dryness and discomfort in my left eye. I sought an urgent appointment but was refused by the receptionist, who suggested I see a pharmacist. The pharmacist, of course, could do nothing to address my symptoms. I was then referred back to the GP, only to be told that the next available appointment was in 7 days. I declined that slot, and was instead sent to Boots Opticians. After a thorough examination, they found no issues with my eyesight (there was none to beging with) and were prepared to refer me back to the GP again. I informed the optician of the receptionist's lack of assistance, and, after some persistence, she agreed to refer me directly to the eye clinic at JR Hospital. There, I received a swift diagnosis and treatment. The doctor at JR was appalled when I shared my story and said I made the right call ignoring the receptionist's advice, as delaying treatment for facial paralysis, which is most effective within 72 hours, would have been detrimental. A similar situation occurred recently and the receptionist was again not willing to schedule timely treatment for me until pressured to do so. While the doctors and nurses at the practice have been professional, the work of the receptionists alone has prompted me to transfer to a different GP.
