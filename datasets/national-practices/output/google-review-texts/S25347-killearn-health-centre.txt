My family of 5 registered here for 20 years. Throughout this time, there has NEVER been a consultation or member of staff that has been less than first class. The GP’s and allied professionals are friendly, approachable; non-judgemental; supportive; encouraging and they have encyclopaedic knowledge of medicine and the human condition. Waiting times for appointments are brief; referrals are made promptly; communication is excellent and is next to a pharmacy. Offers a range of on-site services. Website comprehensive. I cannot fault them and cannot think of anything they could do better.
