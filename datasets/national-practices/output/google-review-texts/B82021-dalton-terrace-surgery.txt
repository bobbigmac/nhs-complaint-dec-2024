I’ve only been a registered patient at this practice a week having chosen it for its positive reviews. However I’ve been flabbergasted at the level of incompetence the staff demonstrated today. I called in the morning to make an appointment for my 2 year old daughter who had UTI symptoms and having been advised to do so by 111. Instead I was told to just drop off a urine sample aa early as possible and it would be tested and a prescription sent to my pharmacy if needed, likely by lunchtime if I was quick. I thought it was a little odd given my daughter’s age and questioned it, but as a new patient didn’t know any better when told that was the process. Long story short, after waiting for hours with no message or call back, making multiple calls to the practice and finally speaking to a GP at 5pm, it turns out I should have been given an appointment after all and that process was for adults only. Several receptionists and at least one nurse failed to pick this up, with the upshot that my daughter was only seen at 5.30 by an admittedly very sympathetic and helpful GP. However, due to the late hour and no one having stock, we’ve not been able to get a prescription until tomorrow leaving my 2 year old in pain, missing another day of nursery and risking a kidney infection or worse. I know this practice has good reviews but I’m seriously concerned about their processes and systems as the staff don’t appear to know their own policies or have common sense when it comes to triage. I’d think twice about registering here especially if you have young children. I’ll be moving practices immediately.
