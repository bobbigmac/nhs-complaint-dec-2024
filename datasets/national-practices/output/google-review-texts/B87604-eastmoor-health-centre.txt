This practice is appalling. I’m not sure if the gps are part time but I can never get an appointment. They use an NHS internal system where medical professionals are supposed to communicate with each other, however I ended up having my stent procedure delayed significantly as they advised they’d not received correspondence when it was very clear they had. It took the consultants secretary at LGI to contact them directly before they admitted they’d got it wrong whilst my condition deteriorated rapidly at this time. One of the GPs didn’t read a simple report properly and as a result, until I managed to get an appointment with an advanced nurse practitioner, I put up with months of pain. I was then unable to get the right pain medication as 2 gps disagreed with each other leaving me unable to sleep for weeks and function properly. My works occupational health were disgusted and so was my heart consultant. To be fair to the I’ve found receptionists Julie and Kay very helpful and appreciate they’re up against it. The ANP and nurse who takes bloods are lovely but if you have the misfortune to get one of their colleagues, who is a receptionist, she’s rude, dismissive, obstructive and has even been sarcastic towards me. When I challenged her about this politely, rather than apologise, she said that everyone has off days but she seems to be having an off day every time I’ve been unfortunate enough to speak to her. The practise is poorly run and managed in my opinion and despite writing a complaint to the practice manager several months ago, I’ve had no response. Following a heart procedure my consultant and rehab nurse used the system to tell them I need rigorous checks including regular bloods. This was in January and I had bloods taken yesterday at a different surgery as they didn’t have an appointment until May. If you have any serious ailments avoid this surgery at all costs. I very rarely complain and please see my review of LGI and others if you’re in any doubt as they’re great but I feel this surgery are negligent at best.
