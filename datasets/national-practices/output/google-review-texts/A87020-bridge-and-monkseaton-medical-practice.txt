Rating: 2 stars
Doctors are absolutely excellent and extremely helpful, but a few of the receptionists let the practice down. They seem to forget that the people ringing up are human beings too, and to treat everyone with a bit of compassion should be a basic requirement for such a role, especially when it’s regarding health. I am sure they are lovely people deep down and I empathise that it is likely a stressful and repetitive job, but please don’t treat callers like they are a nuisance or a bother. When you are the first interaction people have when they are suffering, find it in your hearts to try and be a little kinder. I am positive none of them would appreciate the very treatment they provide, and that is very sad.
