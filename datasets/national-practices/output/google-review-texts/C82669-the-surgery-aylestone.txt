Author: Natasha H
Date: 3 days ago
Rating: 3 stars
I was with the surgery for over 5 years, but moved to another practice in March 2025 following a poor experience. Overall, I had no real issues with getting treatment for temporary or physical ailments, and these were generally dealt with quickly, providing I could actually get an appointment. There was also an issue wherein the main front door was “out of order” the entire time I was registered at the surgery, making a one minute walk from the main road, longer, and through a car park and up a bit of road with no pavement. My treatment and support for mental health, however, was poor. Follow-up appointments would be forgotten, prescriptions not approved on-time, call-backs would not be made when promised. I spent a lot of time calling and chasing things up, which was extremely stressful. The experience with reception varied depending on which team member you spoke to, with some actively chasing things up, and others just sounding hassled and passing you off to either call back again, or wait for a follow-up call that never came. I visited Dr Kidy with joint pain and was given thorough assessment and a knowledgeable discussion regarding next steps. A specialist referral was made to Rheumatology by her, and a follow-up with her was also a good experience. I also visited Dr Tompkins at another appointment, and felt that she was very helpful, supportive, and addressed my concerns with my mental health seriously. She also made a specialist referral, and was diligent at following-up with me. My experiences with other doctors at the surgery were poor. I spoke to Dr Masson a number of times over a few years regarding mental health, and she often would sound frustrated with me or not offer any additional support, or not review my ongoing treatment. I complained to the surgery following a number of similar interactions with her, and wasn’t dealt with by her again; and hoped this would be where my negative experiences with the surgery ended. Unfortunately this isn’t the case. I had two appointments with Dr Sahdev in early 2025. The first one, I felt so gaslit and invalidated in regards to my mental health, and with my ongoing investigative treatment with physical issues. I then took a family member with me to my follow-up appointment. During the follow-up appointment he explained that there was “nothing wrong” with me. He continued by saying he didn’t understand why I’d been referred to a Rheumatology Specialist, and would also no longer be supporting me with some ongoing mental health concerns. I left feeling deflated and exhausted. My family member was appalled by this interaction. The day after this appointment, I had my Rheumatology Specialist appointment. Upon receiving a formal diagnosis (despite Dr Sahdev stating the day before there was no issues with my health), and the hospital referring me back to the surgery for “Primary Care” (treatment managed by GP); I left the surgery. I felt that due to my interactions with Dr Sahdev I would not be receiving good treatment for this new diagnosis and would not be taken seriously by this surgery. My experiences in the 5+ years were extremely mixed. With some staff and treatment being outstanding (but sadly this would only last for a couple of visits), and others being unsupportive, rude, and frankly on the verge of maltreatment. Having changed to another surgery and my experiences being drastically different in the last 12 months, compared to my time at the Surgery @ Aylestone, I would not recommend this surgery. The treatment and experiences you get are a lottery, and that shouldn’t be the case. I would eagerly encourage the surgery to reflect on ensuring service and treatment for all individuals is improved, and consistent across all team members.
