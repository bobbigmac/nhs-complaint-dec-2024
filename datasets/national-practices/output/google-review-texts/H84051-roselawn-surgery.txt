Author: tegan dudley
Date: 19 hours ago
Rating: 1 star
I’ve been with Roselawn for about 3 or 4 years, they’ve got worse and worse. The actual treatment received has mostly been fine- the phlebotomist and cervical screening staff were good. However the administrative side of things is shockingly bad. Frequent mistakes causing a lot of unnecessary stress. They don’t seem to know how to use the online services properly. I’ve found myself always having to go above and beyond to manage my own healthcare. They’ve mixed up appointments, forgotten referrals, simply not responded, hours late for arranged phone call appointments, and some staff just plainly lack empathy. They blame it all on the times “that’s just what it’s like for all health services”. As a healthcare professional myself, I’m not ignorant to the demands and barriers, but in this case it’s just excuses for sloppy work. It’s honestly been a nightmare and I am so relieved to be with Kingston Health Centre now. I think for people who rarely visit the GP it’s fine, but for anyone with chronic health conditions I would definitely not recommend this surgery.
