Author: Sam Gray
Date: 2 months ago
Rating: 1 star
I have no complaints about the medical staff at this practice. In almost all of my encounters, they have been very helpful, knowledgeable, and caring and, as a long-term patient I appreciate the support I have received over the years. The problem, however, is that you'll most likely never get the opportunity to speak to them. The administrative staff frequently come across as unhelpful and dismissive, and the appointments system seems designed to prevent patients from actually seeing a doctor. The 8am scramble for appointments is particularly frustrating. If you do get through, you're typically offered only a telephone consultation. The results line is only open after 2pm, which is impractical for anyone with a job. Worse still, if a test comes back negative, you simply drop off the system with no follow-up, meaning you have to start the whole process again with another 8am phone call. The overall impression is that the practice is hoping your illness simply goes away unless you persistently chase them. While I'm capable of advocating for myself, this system will inevitably fail those who cannot, including the elderly, the vulnerable, and anyone who struggles to navigate bureaucratic obstacles - often the people most in need of care. I understand the NHS is under immense pressure nationwide, but this practice seems to be handling it worse than most. Friends and family registered at other surgeries have been able to get same-day appointments and proper follow-up from their GPs. Something that feels unimaginable here.
