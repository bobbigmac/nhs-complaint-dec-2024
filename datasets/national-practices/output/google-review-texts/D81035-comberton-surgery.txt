Author: Philip Grey

Rating: 5 stars
This surgery was struggling a year or two ago. It has been transformed since new practice and dispensary managers came in. It used to be the norm to queue out of the door for the dispensary to find out that your medication was only partly ready. Those days are gone. Of course they are still very busy and if you want to see a named GP you might have a long wait, but that is the case in every NHS General Practice now. I realised this morning that I had stupidly failed to order a repeat prescription. I went into the surgery, they could not have been nicer about it and I had the pills within 15 minutes. All of the doctors are helpful and efficient. Those who are running the practice now deserve a massive pat on the back!
