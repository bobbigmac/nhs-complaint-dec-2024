Author: Elisabet Bedwell

Rating: 5 stars
We think we are very fortunate to be registered at this practice after moving house three years ago. We find the reception staff always very friendly, helpful and professional. It is a very busy practice and of course sometimes it isn’t always easy to find an appointment as quickly as we would like, but if there is a real problem and it’s urgent, we are seen quickly. The doctors are really good and thorough, if there is a problem they will request tests and deal with it quickly. At one point when I wasn’t happy about a consultant and hospital, a second opinion was requested and it led to a better and faster experience. The different clinics they hold are great, and deal with blood pressure, diabetes and other conditions which means we are properly followed up, and so worse conditions later on are prevented. They also have their own pharmacy and now conduct blood tests there, which I think adds to their efficiency.
