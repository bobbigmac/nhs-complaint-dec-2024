Author: Alfie Savage

Rating: 1 star
I have significant concerns regarding the duty of care and service at this GP surgery. Firstly, I have consistently found that doctors demonstrate a lack of attention to detail during appointments. There's a noticeable tendency to rush through consultations, which leaves little room for thorough assessment or discussion of health concerns. This approach often leads to a feeling of being unheard and can compromise the effectiveness of treatment. Secondly, I have encountered what I perceive as discriminatory practices related to ADHD. The surgery refused to continue shared care for my ADHD, citing internal "policies." The communications I received, particularly via SMS, conveyed an impression of prejudice towards mental health conditions and mental disabilities. This stance has caused considerable distress and has significantly impacted my ability to manage a debilitating disability easily. Finally, while I acknowledge the receptionists are generally satisfactory in person, phone interactions are often blunt and come across as rude. While I understand the need for efficiency, there is a clear opportunity for staff to maintain a more professional and empathetic demeanour when speaking with patients over the phone. Overall, these experiences have led me to question the surgery's commitment to comprehensive patient care and inclusive practices.
