Author: Jaimie Sarah
Date: Edited a week ago
Rating: 1 star
Leaving my last review again to reflect my experiences over the last couple of weeks. I’ve been going to Ashley Medical Practice for the last 5 years. I've always found Dr Delaimy helpful when I've had an appointment with him, I haven't felt rushed and I've always felt that he genuinely cared. However, I had a call with him a few weeks ago following being worried about recurrent upper respiratory and chest infections (I have called about this issue at least 15-20 times over the last 5 years, I have also been sent to A&E twice by 111 and they sent an ambulance to my house once as my breathing was so restricted and I was getting severe chest pains). I have also had several blood tests at the practice to try and understand the root of this recurring issue. Dr Delaimy told me after my last blood test last month that there was nothing physically wrong with me, (i.e. implying that anything I was experiencing/worried about was in my head... which isn't possible with chest infections and asthma lol... these are very real very physical issues with clear physical symptoms) I then got more sick and more worried (especially as my father died from lung cancer very young), and ended up coming into the practice in person to see Dr Dendukuri and Federica the pharmacist and they clearly got to the bottom of the issue, which is a return of childhood asthma. Dr Dendukuri was amazing and figured it out immediately and then Federica took me through some spirometry tests which verified this and showed an improvement after giving me a puff of an inhaler which I am now taking daily. As a result, I can finally breathe again and my chest is starting to feel clearer for the first time since October. I feel relieved but I also feel so much sadness that I had to call so many times about my chest and upper respiratory issues and Dr Delaimy never made this link to asthma, and so it's been untreated and I've been suffering unnecessarily all this time. I also had no idea that untreated asthma is a classic cause of recurrent respiratory infections and makes them feel much worse, makes it much harder to breathe and makes it much harder to overcome them and recover. For most of the time I've been a patient, I've also felt fear/discomfort about making appointments because I found the reception team very short and quite rude and dismissive. However they recently got a new receptionist who is warm, friendly and a huge breath of fresh air, and also a new phone system, so that has improved. I feel disappointed though that Dr Delaimy didn't pick up on my asthma earlier as I've now gone several years of repeated chest infections without treatment that could have dramatically improved the quality of my life. I've now taken out private health cover with Vitality so that I can receive a fully reliable service and not be told that there's nothing wrong with me when I was looking for help to resolve something that was genuinely debilitating for me on a daily basis. It was a private GP at Vitality that explained how undiagnosed asthma can contribute to and worsen chest and respiratory issues and now I finally have the answers I've been trying to get for years. P.S. I also asked for a copy of my blood test results as well as chest x ray and they have refused to provide them, they just said "they're fine". This goes against The Data Protection Act 2018. Under this law, you have the right to access your personal data, which includes medical records and imaging. The NHS explicitly states that patients can request copies of their medical records, including X-rays, scans, and test results and they must respond within 1 month. EDITED 10/3/26: I've reducing my rating even further after the practice's response below (I don't have IT difficulties, they refused to provide or upload my results to the NHS app). It also wasn't Dr Delaimy who requested my asthma check, it was Dr Dendukuri. Don't tell people they're misunderstanding when you're the one who doesn't understand basic human courtesy or communication. Have now left for Forthouse Surgery.
