I have been a patient at Mitchison Rd surgery for 10 years or so and have generally been satisfied with the service I’ve received. I have always found it pretty easy to get an appointment and have been able to get same day appointments for anything that felt particularly urgent. Dr Sherlock is a very kind and patient GP who you feel really cares for his patients. Appointments with locum doctors have been a bit hit and miss in my experience. The current reception team are always very efficient and helpful and often go above and beyond what you would expect which is greatly appreciated. The evergreen app is useful for ordering repeat prescriptions and accessing your records, letters, test results etc. I would recommend this surgery.
