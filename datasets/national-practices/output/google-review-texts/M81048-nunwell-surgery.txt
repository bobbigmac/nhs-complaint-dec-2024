Author: Matt C
Date: Edited 4 months ago
Rating: 2 stars
This GP surgery operates in a way that feels unnecessarily complicated compared to others I've used. I followed their preferred process by completing the online triage system via the Nunwell Surgeries website, only to receive a text 10 minutes later instructing me to call the surgery instead. It’s worth noting that GP practices in England are now legally required to offer online access for patients to request appointments and services throughout the day. Unfortunately, this surgery doesn’t seem to fully comply. Their current system is clunky and doesn’t prioritise patient convenience. A few years ago, they offered a far more user-friendly online booking system where you could choose your GP, date, and time—this was genuinely patient-centred. Now, for the average working person, booking or arranging anything feels unnecessarily difficult. Whenever I visit, there are only a few people in the waiting area, which makes me wonder: where is the demand being managed? It’s time for this surgery to make appointment slots fully available online, as required by law. Additionally, I’ve had instances where I was given sample bottles for urine tests but never received any feedback, which makes me question whether they were even processed. I’ve also been told that late afternoon appointments are released at lunchtime, only to call at the advised time and be told none are available.
