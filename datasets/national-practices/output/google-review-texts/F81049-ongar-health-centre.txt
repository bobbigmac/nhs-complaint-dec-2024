Author: Kirstie Broughton
Date: 4 days ago
Rating: 1 star
I completed the lengthy online form to request an urgent appointment for my 8 year old daughter, who is suffering with constant headaches, severe migraine symptoms, heart palpitations and chest pain with wheezing at one point. She has previously been under a cardiologist and neurologist and we were advised to monitor her elevated heart rate, so this flare up is extremely concerning. At the moment she can’t take part in PE, is missing lessons, and has had to sit in the school nurse’s office because the pain is so severe. She is struggling to sleep, but exhausted all the time and at times can’t even tolerate light because of the migraines. Despite explaining this in the form, the surgery replied saying the first available appointment is in a week’s time. For a small child experiencing these symptoms, that simply doesn’t feel acceptable. I can’t keep filling her with calpol, that’s not even very effective. A day feels like a year to a child and an eternity to a child in pain. I now have no choice but to pay for a private appointment tomorrow because she cannot function while waiting that long. It’s heartbreaking to watch your child in this much distress and feel unable to access care.
