Practice response:
Dear Edna Thank you for sharing your experience. I’m sorry that the interaction you described left you feeling embarrassed and uncomfortable. No one should be spoken to in a way that affects their confidence when coming to the practice. I’ve checked your record, and we did send both email and SMS messages to you from July 2025 to explain that we would be moving to a new triage system from September 2025. We also updated the telephone message and displayed posters in the practice. We also emailed an update on 4th November. Even with all of this in place, it’s clear the change still caught you by surprise, and I’m sorry that happened. To give a bit more background, our practice now uses the AccuRx triage system, which NHS England required all practices to adopt. Patients can send in their medical or administrative queries online, and if someone cannot do this themselves, reception will help them submit their request. Medical requests can be sent Monday to Friday between 08:00 and 18:30, and admin queries can be sent seven days a week. The clinical team review each request and decide the safest and most appropriate next step. This system helps us manage demand fairly, improves access to appointments, and reduces waiting times on the phone. Even with the new process, no patient should ever feel dismissed. I will raise your feedback with the team to make sure information is given in a supportive and respectful way. Every patient should feel able to ask for help without feeling judged. Thank you again for bringing this to us. Your feedback helps us improve how we communicate with patients. Regards Stuart Hall, Practice Manager Woolstone Medical Centre

On your specific points: Accessing appointments • Our online medical request form opens at 8:00am and stays open until we reach the safe daily capacity set to make sure every request is reviewed the same working day. When that capacity is reached, the form temporarily closes and re opens the next working day at 8:00am. Administrative requests remain available, and our phone lines and reception are open for urgent queries until 18:30. • If you struggle with online forms or prefer speaking to someone, our reception team can complete the form with you over the phone or at the desk—this has been our process since moving to Accurx triage. Urgent issues • The online triage is designed for routine problems; if something feels urgent, please call us so a member of the team can escalate to the duty clinician the same day.
