Author: Sash Liyanage
Date: a month ago
Rating: 1 star
am writing this review regarding Old Road Medical Practice in Clacton, based on my mother’s experience since transferring from her previous GP in London. Unfortunately, this transfer has turned out to be a serious mistake. My mother is 65 years old, a heart patient, and does not speak English fluently. Since joining this surgery, she has repeatedly experienced delays with her repeat prescriptions, sometimes every single month. For a heart patient, timely medication is not optional — it is essential. These repeated delays have caused unnecessary stress and risk to her health. In addition to the medication issues, the attitude and behaviour of certain reception staff and nursing staff have been deeply upsetting. On more than one occasion, she has felt dismissed, spoken to harshly, and made to feel unwelcome. There were even comments suggesting she should “go back home,” which is completely unacceptable and discriminatory. No patient — especially an elderly one — should ever be spoken to in such a manner. She has also been requesting a referral to the chest clinic at Colchester Hospital for a long time, yet this request has been ignored or delayed without clear explanation. We feel strongly that she has not been treated with the respect, dignity, or equality that every patient deserves. Language barriers should be met with patience and support, not frustration or disrespect. Healthcare providers have a duty of care, especially toward vulnerable and elderly patients. I sincerely hope the management of this practice reviews its internal procedures, staff conduct, and approach to patient care. No family should have to worry every month whether essential medication will be delayed or whether their loved one will be treated unfairly due to communication difficulties or background. This experience has been distressing and deeply disappointing.
