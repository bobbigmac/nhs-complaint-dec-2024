Author: Kate Logan
Date: 3 weeks ago
Rating: 5 stars
I've been a patient of Crewe Medical on and off for around 35 years, completely for the last 18, since the surgery was in one of the houses opposite Scotmid. My dad and daughters growing up were also patients there. There have obviously been big changes in all areas of the NHS during this time, 99%not fir the better, but this is no fault of the surgery itself. During those 35 years I can count maybe a dozen times when I've felt that things could've been handled better, not negligence, just handled better. And given that I'm speaking about 5 ppl I think that's incredible. My dad recently passed away due to terminal cancer. He refused hospice care and would not go into hospital, so it was pretty much the GP service that we were left with. The care and compassion shown to my dad and family by the Dr's, reception staff, nurses ( both practitioners and district) was second to none. Dr Cooney , more than once, visiting after surgery closes as my dad was unwell. Dr Scarlet writing a prescription, taking it to chemist herself, waiting on it being dispensed then taking it back to my dad as no family member was available, on her own time. Reception staff taking prescriptions over to chemist too, NOTHING was too much, with a special thanks in that respect to Shirley, Lianne and Neil who were incredible and a lifeline at times. Dr Lang, coming up to me asking after my dad when I was at a nurse appointment for myself, this is my experience of the surgery, a whole team of people who are both professional and compassionate. Dr Scarlet also took the time to call me after my dad passed away...... he could not have had better treatment had he been in the private sector. I understand that is not the norm, but not everyone is terminally ill and reliant almost solely on their GP, and I am trying to convey the care and compassion that was shown, to counteract the terrible reviews that have been left, some of which ( with all due respect) I struggle to comprehend , as in grammatically. I can obviously only speak from personal experience, but I would not go past Crewe Medical Centre if looking for a GP. I genuinely don't know what people expect from a GP surgery given the size of the population as opposed to the infrastructure that is in place. I've heard of ppl waiting weeks for an appointment elsewhere, it never happens here. Yes, we all get fed up of the "call at 8am fir an appointment", but if you are genuinely unwell then you will be seen that day , I've experienced it and have no complaints. As a side note, the GP is not there if you have a cold or wake up with a bit of a sore throat that morning, and I would hate to see the amount of appointments wasted for these matters that could be put to good use for people who are genuinely in need. Also the amount of appointments taken up by people with self inflicted health problems who moan the GP can't see them is bizarre. Stop blaming the GP and take some responsibility for your own health, that would probably free up even more appointments than the ones who go with a cold , or even worse , don't attend at all.
