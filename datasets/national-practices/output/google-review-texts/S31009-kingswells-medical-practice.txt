Never have issues getting an appointment. You’re treated with dignity and understanding from reception staff. You’re guided if you don’t quite know what to do but you’re consulted and involved if you feel you can give an input. Dr Charleson is an absolute angel, she has supported me physically and emotionally for 4 years now and I’m finally in the right place after crap treatment at a previous practice. My partner often gets Dr Sutherland who is a good laugh and makes the process feel less intimidating and clinical which eases a lot of anxiety for people. I’ve only briefly seen Dr Houston, Dr Meechan and Dr McKay who we’ve both had good experiences with. The only one I haven’t seen is Dr Sharp but who I’ve heard is excellent and popular with patients. If you land up in the catchment area for this practice, you’ve landed lucky. It’s more or less similar to the care you’d get going private.
