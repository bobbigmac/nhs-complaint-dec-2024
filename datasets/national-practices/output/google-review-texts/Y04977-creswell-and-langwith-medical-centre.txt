Author: Holly Medlock

Rating: 5 stars
I've left a review before but for some reason it seems to have disappeared. I couldn't rate Creswell and Langwith higher; their medical staff are absolutely second to none, I feel so blessed that these are NHS Clinicians, as you get treated as if you are a Privately paying service user. I've only ever had the most fantastic dealings with them. The reception staff are also very professional and always very accommodating, trying their utmost to get you seen as soon as possible (I've always managed to get a same-day appointment when needed, which can't be said for many overstretched GP surgeries). So grateful and I'm reminded of this every time I have an appointment there.
