Author: Andrew Cochrane
Date: 8 months ago
Rating: 5 stars
I’ve always had a really positive experience with Midlock Medical Centre. Every time I’ve needed an appointment, I’ve either been seen the same day or offered a telephone consultation without much delay. Reception staff often get a bad reputation, but that couldn’t be further from the truth here. The team are consistently helpful, efficient, and straight to the point (especially when you’re not quite sure which service you need). Most recently, I had a telephone appointment with Dr Fawcett, who was able to fit me in for an in-person consultation later that same day. Despite it being a last-minute slot, he had clearly taken the time to read through my medical history and was fully up to speed on what treatments had or hadn’t worked previously. I left feeling listened to and properly cared for. Couldn’t be happier with the service.
