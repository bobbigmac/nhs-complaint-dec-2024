Rating: 5 stars
I moved to this Practice from another local one where I’d been a patient for over 20years yet I actually feel cared for here. The place is spotlessly clean, hugely efficient and they care. They keep a check on my medications and my health in its entirety. If I request an appointment I am seen in an amazing speed and my issues they try their best to sort. Yesterday I had a minor procedure at the Doctors Practice. This involved a Doctor and a nurse. They cared for my wellbeing by explaining to me about the restrictions they have to follow for testing. They gave me the options and allowed me to decide if I wanted everything done in one sitting or several so tests can be carried out. As I’d previously had a form of skin cancer I felt it best to take it slowly and have everything tested. The procedure I had was painless and carried out with pure care and the Doctor telling me throughout what he was doing. I cannot remember ever having this level of care and professionalism from my GP. Outstanding care from Reception to my GP. They are all worth mentioning as for this practice all the cogs turn and communicate just as they need to for an outstanding experience. A shout out to Reception as well. Friendly and caring.
