Author: Caitlin Barnett
Date: 2 months ago
Rating: 1 star
If you’re looking for a GP surgery that treats mental health as an inconvenient afterthought, congratulations — you’ve found it. Appointments are rushed, concerns are minimised, and anything related to mental health is met with the same enthusiasm you’d expect for a stubbed toe. I left feeling unheard, dismissed, and frankly embarrassed for having bothered to ask for help in the first place. There’s plenty of lip service about “taking mental health seriously,” but when you actually need support, you’re brushed off, redirected endlessly, or made to feel like you’re overreacting. Apparently struggling mentally only counts if it’s easy, quick, and doesn’t require any effort from them. If you’re already vulnerable, do yourself a favour and look elsewhere. This surgery seems far more comfortable dealing with physical symptoms than acknowledging that mental health is, in fact, health. One star, and that’s being generous.
