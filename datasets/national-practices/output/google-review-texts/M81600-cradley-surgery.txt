Author: Kay Davies
Date: 2 months ago
Rating: 1 star
I felt very strongly about wanting to remain with Cradley Surgery. What has disappointed me the most is the complete lack of discretion and empathy shown towards mental health. Mental health cannot be treated as a one-size-fits-all issue. You cannot compare one person’s mental health struggles with another’s, because every situation and every circumstance is different. Grief is on an entirely different level. It doesn’t matter how long it has been — the person you have lost is not coming back. That pain does not simply disappear with time. When you have family members who are struggling more than others, and you are pleading for compassion and understanding to allow that person to remain at the surgery, being told “we can’t because of rules and guidelines” is deeply upsetting. Saying “if we do it for one, we have to do it for others” completely removes the human element from the situation. He is not “others.” He is an individual with unique circumstances, shaped by profound loss, and he deserved empathy, understanding, and discretion.
