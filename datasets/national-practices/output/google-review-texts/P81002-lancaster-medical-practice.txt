Author: Shane Ogley
Date: 8 months ago
Rating: 2 stars
I missed an appointment, which is my bad. But I never had a booking confirmation or any reminder, so the slot simply slipped my mind. When I realised and rang to cancel, I sat in the call-queue for ages, got a call-back after the time had passed, and the appointment was wasted. It’s frustrating because this could be fixed so easily: • Send an SMS or e-mail confirmation the moment the slot is booked. • Follow it with a quick reminder the day before, with a one-tap “cancel/re-book” link. Trials at other trusts show simple texts cut missed appointments by up to 40 %. • Make sure the phone system lets patients cancel quickly without weaving through endless menus or long hold times. Each missed GP slot costs the NHS about £40 and denies someone else care. I’d much rather the money – and the clinician’s time – went to a patient who can attend. The staff here are polite and professional when you finally get through, but the digital side badly needs attention. A modern reminder and cancellation system would save everyone’s time and improve access overnight.
