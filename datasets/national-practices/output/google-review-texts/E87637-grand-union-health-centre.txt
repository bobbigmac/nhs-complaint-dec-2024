Stay away from this practice. I had a blood test on 29 January and was told the results would be ready within 1–5 working days. More than three weeks later, I still couldn’t access my results on the NHS app. I had to call the practice dozens of times. Every time, the reception staff gave different and contradictory information. One said the results weren’t downloaded, another said everything was normal. When I finally spoke to the GP three weeks later, I was told my cholesterol was actually high, which directly contradicted what the reception team had told me before. The hospital confirmed they had sent the results to the practice within five days. So the delay was clearly at the GP surgery. After all this time, the stress, and even a formal complaint, my results are still not uploaded to the NHS app and I still cannot view them myself. This level of administration and communication is completely unacceptable.
Practice response date: 6 days ago
Practice response:
Dear Patient, Thank you for taking the time to review our practice. We were also very disappointed about the circumstances you describe, which were due to an IT change external to us that we were not aware of. Our management team was already looking into this issue before you submitted your complaint and your review. Your blood tests were ordered initially by a clinician working outside of the practice; all results normally go back to that clinician or to the patient’s registered practice. Unfortunately the results in your case were not loaded onto your patient notes by the Imperial lab in the normal way. When you contacted the reception team, they informed the doctors. Two different doctors downloaded the results, on two separate occasions, to try and get them onto our system and available for you to view. This is how the process has historically been done for thousands of patients. In addition, to reassure you at the time of trying to get them into your clinical record, the doctors reviewed the results, and then ensured that the results were communicated to you. In this case, however, the download system did not work, because of an Imperial hospital IT upgrade. We were not aware that this had happened or that the results would not come directly onto your clinical record. Once the problem had been discovered, we did manage to get a copy of your results emailed to the Practice and this version was uploaded to your patient notes. Our staff tried hard to resolve this for you: they contacted the doctors to resolve, they contacted the lab to resolve and they contacted the NHS North West London IT teams to find the source of the problem. We will be logging this as a learning event and we have escalated it inside and outside the practice, to ensure everyone is aware of this change, and to see if an IT solution can be found. We are very disappointed to receive such a poor review and such reviews significantly affect our staff morale. It is particularly upsetting when the source of your complaint is not due to the team at Grand Union but due to an IT change external to our practice, that we were not aware of. We are sorry for the upset that this delay caused you. Grand Union Health Centre
