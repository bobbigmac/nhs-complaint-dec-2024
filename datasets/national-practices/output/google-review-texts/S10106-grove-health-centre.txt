Sick Patients are being treated unfairly by NHS Grove Health Centre by only offering a daily 8AM telephone slot, for booking a Doctor's appointment, which is an inadequate system for the needs of the community. If you are too unwell to stay on the phone or cannot make this 8AM slot for other urgent reasons, your medical needs are being totally disregarded by NHS Tayside. A fairer system for all sick patients, would be to offer both a daily AM slot and a PM telephone slot for booking Doctor's appointments. A face to face Open surgery could also be offered once a week on a first come first served basis for treating sick patients. This fairer Doctor appointment booking system would also alleviate the pressure on the already exhausted Local Hospitals. I hope the Grove Health Centre takes action on this matter to ensure the safety of the community they are suppose to serve.
