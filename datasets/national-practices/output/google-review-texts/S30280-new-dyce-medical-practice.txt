Practice response date: 7 months ago
Practice response:
Hi Richard, thanks for your review. Primary Care has never been under as much pressure and demand as it is today. Therefore, where we can make any of our services more accessible, more effective and more efficient, we will. The Online Prescription Request Service is certainly a service that is better for all involved rather than leaving a voice message on an answer phone. Some practices have removed the prescription telephone service, we don’t want to do that. Regarding the letter attached to your prescription that you refer to, you have not been told that you “HAD to sign up online to be able to order in future” and it is unhelpful to state that you have. The letter stated: “Our online prescription request service is our preferred method of patients ordering prescriptions”. It also stated: “our telephone prescription request line can still be contacted; however, contacting the telephone line instead of using the online prescription request service restricts our ability to process prescriptions as efficiently and effectively as possible”. There is also no “catch” to the process taking 10 days to register. Similarly, I’m unsure what the problem is on the website. Again, we are encouraging our patients to use the online service, which many now are, for all the reasons mentioned above, but also detailing the telephone number. The paragraph referring to “urgent” requests, is to assist patients who require something urgently. To infer something else is again unhelpful. With regards to appointments, again our online means of requesting medical support has increased our capacity to support patients, but the telephone line remains in place, for those that choose to use it. The Duty Doctor makes the decision on what level of interaction is required, which could indeed be a face to face appointment or a telephone call, however not everyone needs to “see” the Doctor, but that decision is made solely by a Doctor, no one else. If you would like to discuss this further, please email the below for the attention of the manager who will be in touch: • gram.newdyceadministrator@nhs.scot Thanks
