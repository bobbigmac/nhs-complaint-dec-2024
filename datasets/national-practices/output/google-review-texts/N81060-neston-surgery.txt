What a difference between Arrowe Park Hospital and Neston Surgery. Having contacted 111 due to difficulty breathing and had passed out the day before, I was advised to go straight to A&E as the wait was up to 3 hours for an ambulance. I was seen very quickly by triage and then had to have an ECG also very quick but not once did they listen to my chest and the triage nurse said blacking out is common in men, now being asthmatic for over 40yrs not once has this ever happened to me, and due to at least a 6hr wait to see a DR I was advised to go and see Urgent Treatment/ OOH GP. After waiting for over two hours and in that time only 2 patients had been seen we decided to go home and get an appointment at Neston Surgery with an ANP and within 15 mins i had my Temp, BP, Oxygen and chest listened to and was told I had a chest infection and the blackout was due to Cough Syncope were your blood pressure drops due to coughing. I would not recommend APH to anyone especially if you want or need to be seen quickly to get an assessment and medication. I think the OOH GP service must be paid per shift as there was no rush to see any patients. The receptionist was stroppy and said to call 111 if you have any issues, after informing her that we had and been to A&E who sent us to them she just shrugged her shoulders. If this is the care APH is giving then god help us. Also the A&E waiting room was a dump, pools of water everywhere rubbish strewn across the floor. Claims waiting to happen..
