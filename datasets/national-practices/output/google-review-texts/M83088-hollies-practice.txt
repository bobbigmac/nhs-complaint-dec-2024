Author: anna ayres
Date: 2 months ago
Rating: 5 stars
I felt extremely unwell on Sunday morning and called 111, where I was given an appointment for the following day. I was seen by Nurse Practitioner Matthew Howes, who was exceptional throughout. He carried out every possible check available to him and prescribed antibiotics. Unfortunately, I was too unwell to keep them down, so I called again the next morning and was given another appointment straight away. Further checks were done, including a urine sample, which showed I was more dehydrated and still had an infection. I had been so unwell that I had missed my flights to Italy for my Christmas holiday, and Matthew could clearly see how much this had affected me. He arranged a referral to Good Hope Hospital, which meant I didn’t have to wait for hours in A&E. This took a huge amount of stress out of the situation, and I immediately began to feel better once I received fluids, antibiotics, and pain relief. He even called to check on how I was getting on and printed all notes so I could explain the situation to the hospital. Matthew consistently explains everything clearly, listens carefully, and always goes the extra mile to get things done. He has a get up and go attitude — on a previous occasion, he took bloods straight away rather than booking another appointment. This was not only more efficient but meant results came back quicker and treatment could begin sooner. Thank you for your professionalism, kindness, and dedication. Please don’t ever change.
