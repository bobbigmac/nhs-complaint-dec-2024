Admittedly I'm new to practice but I'm very happy with the service provided. I recommend using online form and e consult over phoning or going to reception to book, much quicker. I went with a rash, promptly treated as allergy with steroids, and mentioned getting out of breath lately and having sounded chest now have appointments to see specialists for COPD amongst other things. Whilst this was a bit of shock am happy it's been caught early and confident that they'll look after me well. Parking is a bit tight for such a big health centre but you can park in Tesco round corner and walk if able, there's nearly always disabled spots available right by doors. Also there's a pharmacy in the centre, who are very good, which is handy.
