Author: Lauren S
Date: 2 months ago
Rating: 4 stars
I’m gonna go against the grain here and say in my experience, I’ve had little to no issues booking appointments, granted I’m technically literate so that’s obviously a major factor. There have been cancellations, sometimes admin miscommunications, but overall it hasn’t been terrible. Only qualm I have is that some of my symptoms are dismissed, given all my tests come back normal, but I’m still under investigation you can say. One huge redeeming factor about this health centre is DR Miriam Khan; she listens, advises appropriately, and seems to genuinely care even if it’s a smaller concern. She will always ask if I understand her and allows time for questions in case I missed something. While I am sorry to hear about everyone’s negative experiences, some of the boundaries are faced because of budget constraints and staff should not be blamed for this. Big up Dr Miriam Khan!! ✌️
