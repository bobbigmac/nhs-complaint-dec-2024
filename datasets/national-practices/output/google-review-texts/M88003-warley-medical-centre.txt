Credit where it’s due. In an age of hopeless GP surgeries and receptionists guarding the appointments like their own first born this one is pretty decent. Having not long moved from another surgery and having to wait 6 weeks to get a telephone consultation I’m able to get a telephone appointment here with relative ease. On a couple of occasions I’ve had a telephone consultation and they’ve wanted to examine me I’ve been invited in the same day which was reassuring. At my previous doctors they couldn’t get me out of their office quick enough(that’s if I could even get there in the first place), these guys are miles better. I don’t feel rushed when I’m explaining my concerns and they order tests promptly and they’ve always called me with any results of said tests with a treatment plan. The only issue I’ve found is lack of consistency with seeing the same Dr. Although they all have access to the same system I’ve found it difficult to get a follow up with the same GP I originally might have seen. Everytime I’ve wanted a certain GP when booking I always end up with someone else but I can’t complain when some people can’t get to see a GP in their surgeries. All things considered this surgery is a million times better than the shambles I came from. No issues with the nurses either when seen for dressing check ups. Thank you!!
