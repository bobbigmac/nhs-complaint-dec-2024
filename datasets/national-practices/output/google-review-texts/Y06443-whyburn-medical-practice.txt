It’s not that I have had awful experiences every time that I have been to this practice but this morning really made me question their practices. I gave birth 2 weeks ago, and this morning spoke to the emergency midwife number as I suspected I had mastitis, they advised I will require antibiotics before it becomes more severe and said to call my doctors for an appointment. They said this should be a priority appointment. On calling the doctors, the receptionist was not at all empathetic to the situation, said there was no appointment today and nothing for another week. I was told to go to a walk in centre. Given that I have a 2 week old, breastfed baby, this seems like poor advice. She is currently vulnerable. We had to take her A&E last week where we were put in a separate room but ultimately my partner became ill a few days later. We have had to be cautious at home so that she does not catch it too. Thankfully, after speaking to the midwife team again, I was able to go to the maternity triage, which still resulted in a long wait but I needed antibiotics today. They commented that it was shocking that I didn’t get a doctors appointment for an infection which can worsen very quickly, and having a newborn baby to feed. I understand that receptionists have to ask what the issue is to ascertain whether the case is priority, and I feel in this situation the broader picture and health of a vulnerable newborn baby was not taken into account, which is very disappointing, it makes you question whether they view you as a human being, or just another call to get out the way. Please try better
