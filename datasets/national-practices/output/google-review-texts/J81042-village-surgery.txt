Author: libby cavey

Rating: 5 stars
I have been with this surgery for the three years that I have been at university and can barely fault them. I had a stroke in 2021 and they check up on me regularly to book regulation blood tests and made sure I got my flu jab (unlike my surgery at home). I also had a concern when I was abroad so filled in an online form to which I received a quick response. They also picked up a low thyroid level in one of the blood tests which is now being monitored every 6 months, proving that they are very thorough. Although they do not phone to let you know the results of a blood test unless something is wrong, I can see the results on the NHS app and they are happy to book a phone call appointment with you to discuss. I have always found the doctors and nurses very nice although in a couple of instances the receptionists have come off as a bit rude. I am really grateful for the help they have given me and just wish I could stay under their care for the future!
