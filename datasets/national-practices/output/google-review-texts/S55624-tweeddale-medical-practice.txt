I have been registered with tweedale for a few years and have been extremely impressed with the facilities and services they provide :) The medical centre itself is wonderful. In a great location with a beautiful waiting room with massive windows. It's spacious, relatively modern and very clean. The car park is large but very busy and there is a bike shed and bus stop directly outside. The reception staff have been nothing but helpful during any of my interactions with them. And all of the medical professionals I have had interactions with have been respectful, empathetic and very understanding. I have had great experiences over the phone with polite and helpful reception staff and have been impressed with the SMS service they provide.There is also an automated sign on screen for any appointments if there are no receptionists on the counter. I would also like to say how impressed I have been with Dr Gamble during any time I have been lucky enough to see him. I can't speak highly enough of him and the rest of the staff here. Thanks so much for everything you do. And keep being the amazing people you are ❤️
