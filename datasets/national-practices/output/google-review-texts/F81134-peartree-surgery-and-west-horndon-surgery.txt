Everyone at basildon were great, I had the scan done on the Friday and by Wednesday lunchtime Peartree surgery had text me to say everything was fine. I couldn't fault how fast everything was dealt with that week and Kathy & John were as reassuring as possible whenever i called during the wait. Im very grateful to Dr Reg for referring me so fast- he is always a great listener on the phone and i never feel rushed off the call when I have spoken to him in the past. The stress of the original incorrect findings at Purfleet caused myself & my family immense stress and fear across that week and so i would like to think the technician would maybe be contacted and offered further training as i feel somebody providing an ultrasound should be able to tell the difference between a cancerous tumour which is what he told me he thought i could have and what turned out to be muscle/tissue. I had an ultrasound at Basildon a few days later who couldn't see anything suspicious at all, which tells me the man at Purfleet wasn't really qualified enough to be telling me what he did. Im just grateful all came back ok, but I worry this could happen to other people in the future being scanned by that person

This surgery is failing its patients. Getting help here is incredibly difficult. Reception is often abrupt and dismissive, and you are made to feel like an inconvenience rather than someone seeking medical care. It is extremely hard to get clear information, timely appointments, or any sense that your concerns are being properly taken seriously. There is a serious lack of compassion, communication, and follow‑up. Patients are left chasing answers, repeating themselves, and fighting just to be heard. The overall attitude is undermining and patronising, with very little basic respect shown. The practice appears to be overwhelmed, with too many patients and not enough capacity. This leads to long waits, poor access to treatment, and worrying gaps in care, which is especially concerning for vulnerable people who rely on this surgery. Formal complaints have been raised and escalated to the ICB due to how these issues have been handled. From my experience, the problems here are systemic and ongoing, not one‑off mistakes. Peartree Surgery urgently needs to change how it treats patients and how it is run. Right now, it is letting people down in a serious way.
