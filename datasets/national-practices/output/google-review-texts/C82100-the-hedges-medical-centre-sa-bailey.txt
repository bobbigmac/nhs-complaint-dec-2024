**Initial Review - May 2025** I’ve been part of this surgery for a couple of months now, after experiencing very bad service and gaslighting from a GP at my previous surgery. They have been extremely helpful and efficient every single time I’ve contacted the Hedges. When I use the app to make an enquiry, I get contacted within an hour. If I ask about documents or have a general query, I get a prompt response either via message or telephone. I made a non-urgent health related enquiry the other week, and a GP called me within the hour, and I didn’t get told to call at 8am to make an appointment. GP spoke to me about the concern and suggested treatment after I sent some further information. Very easy and a relief after several years of being passed pillar to post at my other surgery. — **Edited March 2026** I have now been registered at the surgery for over 12 months, and the service is still great. I have had no problems accessing treatment and support, and find the staff to be supportive and thorough. After a few interactions over telephone or via the NHS app for non-urgent queries, I am dealt with quickly. I sent a message with some photos to follow-up another query, and the doctor even followed up with a specialist to ensure that my treatment was effective. I didn’t have to chase, or continue to pursue the same issue repeatedly, and this has been a breath of fresh air compared to other experiences at my previous surgery. All of my in-person interactions have also been positive, I have always felt they were productive and the staff to be friendly and supportive during my visits. Since my initial review, the treatment and support has not faltered or changed, and I’m glad that there is consistency across the board at this surgery. I would recommend this surgery to anyone who lives locally. It is easily accessible via public transport, and has parking at the surgery and nearby if you drive. My experiences have been only positive, and I want to thank the staff at The Hedges Medical Centre for restoring my faith in the practices of GPs in the area.

We specifically attended because we were told that two doctors would be available to properly assess her condition. During the consultation, after around 29 minutes, Dr Malik stated that she needed to see other patients and refused to examine my mother’s leg further. My mother currently has watery bumps on her leg that needed medical attention, but she was told that she would have to book another appointment on another day instead of completing the examination during the consultation. For a patient with a disability who struggles to attend appointments, this is extremely distressing and unacceptable. Healthcare providers should make reasonable adjustments and ensure that disabled patients receive proper care when they attend an appointment. Doctors graduate having taken an oath to prioritise patient care, act in the best interests of patients, and do no harm. Experiences like this make it feel as though those principles are not being upheld. It is also very concerning that the promised double appointment with two doctors present did not happen, despite this being the reason we attended. I hope the practice reviews how patients with disabilities are treated and ensures that situations like this do not happen again.
