I should have reviewed this Practice years ago but today summed up my experience perfectly. I rang at 8.00 (only 2nd in queue) for an emergency appointment which I got at 9.40. Author: Andrea Jones

Rating: 5 stars
We moved to Whitchurch from the eastern side of Cardiff back in 2013 and had a young family at the time. Over the years we have, on occasions, needed medical help from this practice and they've been fantastic every time. From speaking to people in work who live in the areas surrounding Cardiff they have experienced great difficulty trying to get appointments whereas here, they will endeavour to find you one on the day if possible depending on demand, availability etc. Yes, the time to get through can be frustrating but that's the case all around us these days e.g. call centres. So please, be thankful, kind and appreciative of the great work the entire Team do for us and think for a minute, what would you do if they weren't on our doorstep? They're trying their best under difficult circumstances and increasing pressure on the health service. A particular 'thank you' to Dr Ali who has been a valuable addition to the practice . A credit to his profession and a genuine, nice man.
