Atrocious admin failures. Last year after a mental health appointment, they wrote that I'd reported a serious crime/trauma, which I hadn't, they'd just severely misunderstood something completely different I said. To edit the record would’ve taken 2 seconds, and I know they physically can do it because in other situations they have edited my records, but in this situation they inexplicably didn't. Under the UK GDPR there's a legal obligation to respond (correct the data, issue a 3 month extension notice, or refuse with an explanation why) within 1 month, and I said this multiple times, especially when the deadline was fast approaching and then passed, but they didn't even acknowledge it. Many months have passed now and still nothing has been done. A couple of times I asked them to explain why they hadn't made the corrections, and again they just ignored it. In other situations, even while this has been going on, they've been very fast and diligent in responding to my admin requests, or chasing me up to schedule an appointment, so it really comes across like they're deliberately ignoring me here. Some time before my data rectification request, I had made a formal complaint about a completely unrelated matter, and they are now inexplicably trying to treat these as the same matter. Basically, the complaint response kept being delayed, and when they sent an extension notice, it said "we are working through the separate pieces of correspondence you've sent". The issue though is that the extension notice they sent is only valid for the complaint. This is because the UK GDPR states that the maximum extension time for data rectification requests is an extra 2 months (3 months total) and that this must be issued within 1 month of the original request, when in this situation, no extension notice was provided within anywhere near 1 month, and over 3 months have passed now anyway, so this new extension notice is well outside the legal timeframe for data rectification requests and is only valid for formal complaints, which means that no valid extension notice (or even any meaningful update whatsoever) has ever been given for the data rectification request. I had also just already said when I first submitted my request that it was a separate matter to my complaint. I replied explaining this, and asked for a proper acknowledgement and update about the data rectification request, and when they replied they just completely dodged the question and only responded to my other question (asking for an update about the complaint). It's like they're trying to implicitly treat my data rectification request as part of my separate complaint because they can't think of any other excuse to delay it for months, but it's only implicit, because when called out on it and asked for an explanation, they don't acknowledge it whatsoever. They've never attempted to justify or explain themselves. They've never said anything like "sorry, we acknowledge this obligation, but unfortunately we're too short-staffed to uphold it", nor have they ever said "actually, you're factually incorrect, the UK GDPR doesn't mean we're obliged to do this", they're just silently failing to comply with their data protection obligations, even when challenged. It's as if they know they're in the wrong and can't actually defend themselves, so they're hoping that if they just give me the silent treatment for long enough, I'll eventually just give up and let them bury the issue. To anyone reading this, please consider this a warning: this GP practice is ok with blatantly violating their patients' data protection rights, and has little to no accountability. Not to mention, it shouldn't even need to be law in order for them to act. They should already care enough about patient dignity to want to correct such a serious and personal mistake anyway.
