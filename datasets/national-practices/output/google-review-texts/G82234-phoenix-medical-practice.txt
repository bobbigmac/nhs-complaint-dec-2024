Author: Gabbie Bristow
Date: a month ago
Rating: 2 stars
I attended my appointment to discuss ongoing pain management while awaiting spinal surgery for a confirmed lumbar disc issue. During the consultation, I felt that my situation was not fully explored or understood. Although I explained my history - including MRI findings, a steroid injection, and my upcoming surgery - I did not feel that my circumstances were considered in a holistic or empathetic way. I was advised that co-codamol should not be used long term and that it may be lowering my pain tolerance, and it was suggested that some of my pain could be psychological. Given that my pain is associated with documented nerve compression and that I am currently under surgical care, I found these comments dismissive and upsetting. Amitriptyline was suggested as an alternative, despite my explaining that I have previously taken it and found it helpful for sleep but not for pain relief. I was also advised to take naproxen inconsistently, which contradicted prior guidance I have received about anti-inflammatory use. Most concerningly, I was informed that the paramedic could not prescribe co-codamol and that the matter would need to be reviewed by a doctor - despite my explaining that I only had one tablet remaining. This left me feeling anxious about continuity of pain relief while awaiting surgery. I fully understand the need for careful prescribing and do not object to appropriate medication reviews. However, I would have appreciated a more empathetic discussion and clearer planning around ongoing pain management, particularly given my surgical pathway and the severity of my symptoms. I hope this feedback is taken constructively, as my intention is not to criticise individuals personally but to highlight how the consultation left me feeling unsupported during an already challenging period of treatment. Edit: my appointment was at 9:45am and I have still had no text to pick up medication at 4:42pm which means i have zero pain management this evening, overnight and tomorrow. I will most likely have to go to A&E as the pain gets to an unbearable state. Outrages.
