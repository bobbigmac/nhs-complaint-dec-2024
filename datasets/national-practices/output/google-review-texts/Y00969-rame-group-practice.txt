Author: P & C H

Rating: 5 stars
If I could giver a higher rating I would. 5 Star service, exceptional. I was saddened to read so many negative reviews, this certainly was not our experience. I was attending the Dermatology Department. We (my husband came along with me) arrived 1/2 hr early for my appointment as we were unsure how long it would take to get to Torpoint. The reception team were welcoming, extremely helpful, bright and cheerful. Almost immediately we were both greeted by Dr “Mark” Bridle. He put us both at ease with his warm, open manner. I was invited to be given a thorough examination, not just the mark on my leg which had been causing some concern. The examination took very little time and I was given a full explanation about the numerous discoloured marks/blotches on my face and body. They were all caused by a combination of age, skin type and sun damage years ago. I was quickly assured there were no need for concern at all. We both felt we had been given the best possible service with a wonderful Dr. “Mark” we cannot thank you enough. For anyone who has concerns please just make sure you get referred by your surgery and go along. They are wonderful, kind, caring, professional and it’s a bright cheerful practice. A huge thank you again.
