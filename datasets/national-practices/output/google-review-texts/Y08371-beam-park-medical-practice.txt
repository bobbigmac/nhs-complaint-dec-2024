The doctors here are wonderful, patient and happy to help. Unfortunately the admin side of things lets this practice down. You cant book an appointment in person, online or over the phone. You have to fill out an online form and wait for a response. Referrals are slow, and it just seems like things are more complicated than they need to be. For example, a gp requests you make an appointment to discuss test results. Other practices will call and arrange an appointment. Here, they send you a message telling you to book an appointment, then you have to fill in the online form and wait for them to come back to you with a date! Please, give us the option to ditch the form and just book an appointment when we need one without the fuss!!!
