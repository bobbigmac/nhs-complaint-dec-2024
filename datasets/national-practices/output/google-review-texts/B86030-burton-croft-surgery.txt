Author: Richard Mullaney

Rating: 2 stars
Pre covid this was an excellent surgery and it was always possible to get an appointment either the same day or very shortly afterwards. Post Covid it’s incredibly difficult to get anything at all. The phone lines open at 8.30am and even if you ring at the moment they open you get the ‘we are experiencing a very high volume of calls…’. Whatever time of day or week you call this is what you get. The other day I was number 9 in the queue and it took 27 minutes to get to number 1. When my call was finally put through the phone just rang and rang. I hung up after another 10 minutes. The reason I was ringing is because they had failed to send the correct prescription down to the pharmacy. I need the medicine as a matter of urgency but the only way I can get it is to leave work to go there in person. I can’t understand why, 2 years after the pandemic the surgery hasn’t seemed to address the call waiting times. If there’s a high volume of calls at all times of the day/week then hire more staff?!? Patients are without doubt going without vital care because of the way things are.
