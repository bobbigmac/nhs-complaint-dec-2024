This is a shockingly bad surgery. My mother called for an appointment, and a disinterested and abrupt receptionist said a flat no to the appointment request and told her to call 111 or go to the hospital and ended the call. My mother had early stages flu and just needed antibiotics, she is also asthmatic and there is a note on her file regarding the importance of not getting chest infections and medicating quickly to avoid them. Now six days later my mum is bedridden and was an emergency case today. We have the antibiotics now but after much suffering, much drama and a longer recovery ahead because of how far she has deteriorated. The surgery needs a decent manager and a new team who are engaged and qualified and actually realise that they are dealing with people's health and lives, and not vacantly booking hair appointments. I know the jobs are underpaid and the surgeries are under staffed, but that doesn't mean everyone should give up and stop even trying to do the jobs well and provide care for people.
