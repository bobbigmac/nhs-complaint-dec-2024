Author: H R
Date: 3 months ago
Rating: 5 stars
I cannot fault Haslemere Health Centre. Having lived here for 15 years, I have increasingly needed the services of this team and they have been marvellous, listening, supporting, facilitating and liasing with the RSCH to enable CT scans, MRI imaging, X-rays, regular testing of bloods and other fluids plus other tests and eye health referrals. Every part of the team has done their utmost to keep me informed and in the best health possible, given my four score years. I update my weight every few months which they're kind enough to acknowledge via a mssg. I am so impressed that I emailed the CQC to tell them of my thoughts relating to the care received at HHC. Yes, I have very occasionally had to wait in a pleasant light airy room with plenty of seating but that's the same everywhere. I rarely need to visit as they offer call backs or noon appointments where possible, in an 'emergency' and it's thanks to them that I have managed my idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis without intervention or medication since the diagnosis in 2023. Regular reminders of flu and C19 jabs are sent and I have downloaded the NHS app which allows me to input info and monthly requests for 20mg Atorvastatin, the only prescription I have. HCC in its entirety, truly deserves this accolade.
