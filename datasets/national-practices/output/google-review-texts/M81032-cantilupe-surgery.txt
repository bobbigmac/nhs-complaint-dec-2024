Practice response:
Thank you for your comment. This is a national NHS policy change and is therefore happening at every surgery. The demand for appointments is larger than the appointments most surgeries have. The government are asking that surgeries allow patients to submit requests on line so they can be directed to the most appropriate clinician as we now have a wide variety of staff, with different skills, that you can see. Unfortunately, with shortages of GPs nationally, it is important we have a way to make sure patients see the right person for their problem every time. Please call the practice manager if you would like to discuss this further.
