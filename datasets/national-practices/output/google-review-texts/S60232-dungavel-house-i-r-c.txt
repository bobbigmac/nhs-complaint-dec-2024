Author: Neha Patel
Date: 7 months ago
Rating: 4 stars
The staff at Dungavel are genuinely caring, polite, and professional. They listen with compassion and treat you with respect, regardless of your background, race, religion, or skin colour. If you have a problem, they try their best to resolve it promptly. It's easy to approach them, even if you just need someone to talk to, they’ll make time for you. The environment feels more humane compared to places like MSTHF or Yarl’s Wood, staff at those centres could definitely learn a thing or two from Dungavel when it comes to professionalism and respectful behaviour. The food here is good and I truly respect the people working inside this facility. One suggestion would be to allow women the same level of access to the library or outdoor space that men have, it would make a real difference. Also, it would be helpful if there was more action taken when inappropriate comments or behaviour happen, especially from male members. Overall, I’m thankful for the kindness shown here. A much better experience than expected.
