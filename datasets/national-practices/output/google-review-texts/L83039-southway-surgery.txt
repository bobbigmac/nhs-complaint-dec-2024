Author: John E

Rating: 5 stars
I chose Southway Surgery after my previous GP surgery closed due to retirement. I was drawn to the friendly receptionist team & the ease of making an appointment. During my time at Southway, they have cared for me through a diagnosis with kidney cancer & removal of one kidney. I have been treated by every GP in the practice & most of the nursing team. Everyone is helpful, professional, friendly & approachable & are caring. The practice updated its processes following Covid & we now have a superb service called “klinik” which enables you to submit a full report to the GP for review, remote treatment & an appropriate appointment for consultation (in person if required) where no time is wasted as the Dr already has the report you submitted. Recently, the practice endured a challenging time due to the medical retirement of a lovely GP who proved very difficult to replace. Dr Evans in particular worked tirelessly during that time, together with everyone at the surgery, and despite those challenges (& a recent quite unfair CQC visit & report) the practice has emerged successfully with a new GP on the team! Southway Surgery is a superb example of a caring practice which works hard & which genuinely cares about its patients. It communicates well using SMS, the NHS app, klinik - all the benefits of a modern surgery. I have nothing but praise for Dr Evans & everyone at the surgery - they are, without exception, exemplary.
