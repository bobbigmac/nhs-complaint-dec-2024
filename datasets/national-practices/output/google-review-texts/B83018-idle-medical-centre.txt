Author: Mich
Date: 2 weeks ago
Rating: 1 star
I recently had an appointment here. I had to wait over half an hour for a GP that was running late. The waiting room is small and was busy, hot and stuffy, and had Radio 1 blasting out of the speakers. They have a notice on the wall asking patients to turn off their phones and to consider other sick people, yet they fail to realise that loud music from a radio can also be overwhelming to people who are sick, noise sensitive, or neuro-divergent. The receptionist kindly turned it down on my request. The GP didn't apologise for running so late. The whole experience was very stressful.
Practice response date: a week ago
Practice response:
Thank you for your comments and I am sorry for the difficult experience you had. Your comments are totally valid and I have instructed the Receptionists to ensure that the radio is at "background" levels. It does help to make the waiting area less sterile but it should not be intrusive. All the doctors should apologise if they are running late and I am sorry this did not happen. I will remind them all. Kind regards Malcolm MacDonald Practice Manager
