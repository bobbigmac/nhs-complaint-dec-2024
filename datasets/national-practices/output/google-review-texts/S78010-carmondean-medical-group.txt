Author: Michal Dybczak
Date: 3 months ago
Rating: 1 star
Carmondean Medical Group – Livingston I am extremely disappointed with the level of care I received at Carmondean Medical Group. I want to make it absolutely clear that the conversation took place directly with a doctor, not with reception staff, a nurse, or administrative personnel. I reported a recurrence of symptoms related to Helicobacter pylori infection and ulcer-related issues, which I had previously been diagnosed with. I requested a telephone consultation and medical advice, explaining that I am working intensively before the holidays and cannot physically attend the clinic during its working hours. Despite my clear explanation, the doctor insistently pressured me to come in solely to collect a stool sample container, completely ignoring that this is currently impossible. No attempt was made to find an alternative solution or to handle my situation flexibly. I also informed the doctor that I will be traveling after the holidays and will only be available in February. The response I received was that “it’s not a problem” and that I could go for a month and a half with an active, untreated Helicobacter pylori infection. Apparently, this was completely acceptable to the doctor. This approach is medically irresponsible. Untreated or prolonged Helicobacter pylori infection can lead to: chronic gastritis, exacerbation and recurrence of ulcer disease, gastrointestinal bleeding, ulcer perforation, anemia, and in the long term, an increased risk of stomach cancer. Minimizing these risks and reducing the problem to “come for the container or wait a month and a half” is absolutely unacceptable—especially coming directly from a doctor. It seems that in this clinic, procedures are valued more than the actual health of the patient, and individual circumstances are completely disregarded. This is not a standard of care that should exist under the NHS. I strongly do not recommend Carmondean Medical Group to anyone expecting professionalism, empathy, and responsible medical treatment.
