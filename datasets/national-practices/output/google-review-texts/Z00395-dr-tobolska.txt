I would like to thank the Staff at Rockfield Medical Centre for their kindness, care and compassion towards my mother Georgina. Special thanks to Dr Monika Tobolska and Dr Craig Wylie. Their professionalism, care, patience and understanding with each member of our family was exceptional and greatly appreciated during a very difficult time. When you are feeling ill and vulnerable and can't get an appointment it is easy to criticise. I have been frustrated when all appointments are filled by 8.40am. You can't get through on the phone or a member of staff appears to be unhelpful. Please just stop and think. Do you really need the appointment, could you be seen somewhere else? Remember the reception team are doing their job and anyone of them may also be going through a hard time. Take a deep breath and speak kindly. Believe me when you are really ill they are wonderful.
