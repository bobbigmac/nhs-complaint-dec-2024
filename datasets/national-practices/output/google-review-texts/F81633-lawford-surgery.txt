Author: Mike Thomas

Rating: 5 stars
I registered with Lawford Surgery in April and, from the very start, the care has been outstanding. On two separate occasions, my wife and I contacted the surgery for medical advice and were both seen the same day, within an hour or two of first making contact — an impressive level of responsiveness that immediately inspires confidence. In January, after returning from time outside the UK, I was struggling to get my asthma under control. I casually mentioned this to the receptionist while collecting a prescription and was promptly offered an appointment with the asthma nurse (who was excellent and most helpful) within two days. The efficiency and genuine concern shown at every step were exemplary. I cannot praise the reception team, back-office staff, and medical professionals highly enough. Their professionalism, attention to detail, and the quality of care provided are truly first class. Having recently moved house, I was reluctant to leave what I considered an excellent previous surgery and wondered how Lawford would compare. I’m delighted to say I have not been disappointed — Lawford Surgery has exceeded expectations and for me, consistently delivers exceptional patient care. Highly recommended.
