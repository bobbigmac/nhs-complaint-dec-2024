Author: Paris Montgomery

Rating: 1 star
Having been a patient at The Limes Medical Centre has been the contributing factor to my medical downfall. Personal Experience - Was registered for a number of years, requested their assistance upon my dramatic decline in health over the last year to be told by GPs that my symptoms where associated with a long term chronic issue minus investigations which has been stable for over 10 yrs. Despite returning on multiple occasion's with nearly 7 stone unintentional weight loss during this sudden period, unable to eat, pain, inability to now function daily - i was advised to just “watch and wait”, “wait and see what my yearly/annual hospital consultant would advise when being assessed for a chronic issue” and the most laughable piece of advice from GP Dr Maxted “eat a banana to help with the weight loss and fatigue”. Within x2 apts of being registered elsewhere i feel i have now been listened too and have now had the correct testing (just a simple FIT test) and onwards care. Negatives - GP partners have poor communication skills and lack up to date medical training and knowledge on guidelines. GPs over rule hospital consultant advice. Practice manager shows little professionalism and failure to respond to formal complaints. Failure to maintain correct management for complex health conditions. Positives - Reception team fabulous ease to book same day and routine apt via telephone and online (not walk in). Surgery has ability to see and treat quickly minor illness and acute conditions like UTI, ear infections etc. Allied practitioners and ST doctors have shown care/compassion.
