I can't praise this surgery enough. The doctors are very proactive and caring. I've been sent for comprehensive blood tests over the last couple of years, which they have suggested (as a kind of maintenance, not due to any illness). I love being able to see my detailed results in the app, it's very reassuring and they are quick to follow up any results that might be outside the norm. I struggle to relate to the GP horror stories that you read about in the news. New North Road Health Centre is the exact opposite. The receptionists are welcoming and friendly, whether in person or on the phone. The doctors and nurses are professional and a pleasure to deal with. They treat you like a human being, not a nuisance. Add to the fact that you have a great chemist in the same building - it's all put together to make like easier and not more stressful. I've recommended them to neighbours, who have also been amazed at what a great surgery it is. I'd rate it 10 out of 10. 5 gold stars. Thumbs up. I think you get where I'm going with this!!!
