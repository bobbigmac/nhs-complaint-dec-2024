Author: Katarzyna Barnaś

Rating: 1 star
I am very disappointed with the speech therapy support my son received. From the very beginning, we provided full information about our concerns, including specific behaviours commonly associated with autism. Unfortunately, our observations as parents were repeatedly ignored by "specialist", and these concerns were never properly addressed. We also raised concerns about our son’s hearing on several occasions, but we felt that nobody listened to us. During one appointment, a specialist even told my husband and me that she would “prove” our son did not have autism. Eventually, we were referred for audiology testing. However, the hearing assessments were unsuccessful, and we were informed that therapy could not continue because hearing difficulties directly affect speech development. We were essentially discharged without further support. As parents, we arranged private treatment ourselves, including a medical procedure. When we later met a speech therapist at another centre, the specialist was much more professional and quickly identified issues that should have been recognised several years earlier. This experience has led me to believe that sometimes professionals should listen more carefully to parents, who know their child best! Unfortunately, I have lost trust in the previous "specialists" involved in my son’s care. I strongly believe that an extremely valuable period in my child’s development has been irreversibly lost — time that can never be recovered. The waiting time for a diagnosis is already three years. It is deeply shocking and distressing to realise that you entrust your child’s future and wellbeing to specialists who fail to listen, fail to recognise clear concerns, and ultimately fail the child they are meant to support.
