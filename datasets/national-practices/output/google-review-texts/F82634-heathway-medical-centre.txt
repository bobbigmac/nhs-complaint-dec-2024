I’ve been a patient at Heathway Medical Centre for a while now and overall I’d say it’s a 3-star experience. Janie and Nicole are absolutely fantastic receptionists — they always go out of their way to help and are consistently kind, patient, and professional. They really make a difference to the overall atmosphere of the surgery. Unfortunately, Jo has been quite the opposite in my experience. She can come across as rude, often speaks to others while patients are on the phone, and generally doesn’t demonstrate strong people skills. It can make what’s already a stressful situation feel even more frustrating. The real reason I stay registered here is because of the doctors. Dr Gul, Dr Omer, and Dr Magsi are what truly make the surgery great. They are professional, caring, and thorough. If it wasn’t for them, I would probably consider changing surgeries. With some improvements to consistency at reception, this practice could easily be a 5-star service.

I have been a patient at Heathway Medical Centre for some time now and overall I am happy with the surgery and the care I receive. The receptionists Janie and Nicole are absolutely amazing. Janie consistently goes above and beyond to help, always treating patients with kindness, patience and respect. Nicole is also very polite, friendly and helpful, and both of them make what can sometimes be a stressful situation feel much easier to manage. However, my experience with receptionist Jo has unfortunately not been positive. I have found her to be quite rude and unhelpful on occasions, which can feel discouraging when you’re already not feeling well and reaching out for support. In terms of the doctors, Dr Gul, Dr Magzi and Dr Omer are fantastic. They are professional, understanding and make me feel listened to. I feel confident in their care and would only choose to be seen by them. The nurse, however, has come across as a little judgemental and quite cold in her manner. I feel that a more patient-friendly and understanding approach would make a big difference to patient comfort. Overall, the surgery provides good care, and there are some truly excellent members of staff who make a real difference.
