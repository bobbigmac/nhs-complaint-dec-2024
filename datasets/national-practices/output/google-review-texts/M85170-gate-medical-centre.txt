My dad had been unwell out of breath walking up the road when normally he walks 2/3 miles a day. He had just returned 2 weeks from abroad too. I phoned up for an appointment & described my dad's symptoms to reception staff who straight away gave me appointment slot for same afternoon with Nosheen Baig (Clinical Pharmacist). Nosheen checked my dad's blood pressure & asked critical questions. Told us to take dad to same day emergency care. Thanks so much for always being alert, recognising seriousness of my dad's symptoms. Hospital staff were amazing too. Dad had to be admitted for suspected Pulmonary Embolism (blood clot in the lungs) and started medication straight away. Scans confirmed P.E. A very close call for dad, no stroke or heart attack & in his 80's Alhumdulilah. Thanks to Receptionist & Clinical Pharmacist at Gate Medical Centre together with all staff at Heartlands Hospital. My dad was out and about walking within 4 days.
