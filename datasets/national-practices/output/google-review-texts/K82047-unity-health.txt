Author: Stefan Cork

Rating: 1 star
Unity Health are the most disorganised medical practice I have encountered in my professional capacity in 15 years, and have breached regulatory practice with flagrant disregard. My business arranges life assurance which often involves big insurers like Aviva paying the GP around £100 for a medical report for our client. This is so our client can get life insurance approved to repay their mortgage should they die, or to pay an income if they are too unwell to work. In short, to avoid anyone going homeless due to health issues. The GP has 20 working days to return this information under the Access to Medical Reports Act 1988. Despite repeated reminders this practice took almost 9 months to produce the report, exposing my client to large financial risk in the process. The only reasoning that they could offer was that they had one administrator covering half a dozen sites. I am sure the Doctors who own the practice are of high standard, but the same cannot be said of their back office systems. It is reckless to leave a client in a vulnerable position for such an extended period due to lack of resources. Stefan Cork Cork & Co, Thame.
