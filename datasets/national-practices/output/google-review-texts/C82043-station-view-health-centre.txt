Practice response:
Thank you for taking the time to leave your feedback. We are very sorry to hear about your experience and understand how frustrating it is when you are unable to secure an appointment, particularly for a young child. As a GP practice, we are required under current NHS and government regulations to ensure that all patients with new medical problems are offered an appointment within two weeks. To meet this requirement safely, we must carefully manage the number of appointments available each day. Unfortunately, this does mean that there can be pressure on same-day urgent appointments and on routine follow-up appointments for ongoing conditions. Like many GP surgeries across the country, we are working within limited funding and staffing levels. Seeing patients beyond safe capacity would not be clinically safe and increases the risk of missed diagnoses, which we must avoid in the interests of patient safety. When we reach safe capacity for the day and a problem is urgent, patients may be advised to use alternative NHS services such as NHS 111, walk-in centres, urgent care, or A&E where appropriate. These services are part of the wider NHS system designed to support patients when GP capacity is exceeded. We appreciate your feedback and are truly sorry that you have felt unsupported. Please be assured that we continue to review our appointment system and advocate for increased resources to improve access for all our patients.
