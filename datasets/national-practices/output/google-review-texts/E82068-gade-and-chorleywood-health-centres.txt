Author: Macky Gee
Date: a month ago
Rating: 1 star
The practice suffers from major administrative problems that make getting basic healthcare unnecessarily difficult. You'll typically wait 25 minutes past your appointment time, even with the new triage system. Whilst they consider these wait times acceptable, if you're 15 minutes late they refuse to see you. The reception staff often don't check your records properly, jump to wrong conclusions, and get defensive when you question them. Their triage system is designed to push regular patients away. they won't book appointments or handle prescription requests over the phone, and the new triage system frequently turns people away and sends them to Chorleywood instead. The practice manager shuts down complaints immediately and avoids acknowledging problems and the body of the complaint, which implies a level of evasiveness, forcing patients to approach the ombudsman for what could have been solved with a respectful and empathic conversation. The constant administrative battles and dismissive treatment leave you feeling frustrated and unheard, turning what should be straightforward healthcare into an emotionally draining struggle of self advocacy just to get the basic medical attention. The doctors themselves are generally good and professional despite working in this chaotic environment. A few reception staff do go the extra mile, but they're unfortunately the exception rather than the rule. I would strongly encourage anyone who is registered with this surgery to follow up any systems-based booking or request with a telephone call, so that you are not unfairly delayed in receiving treatment due to mismanagement of patient requests. Whilst the staff may not be able to advise why your requests aren't heard or your medications aren't dispatched, you can at least respond quickly to these mismanagements.
