Author: Veronica Ortega
Date: 6 days ago
Rating: 4 stars
I’ve been registered at this surgery for several years, and my experience has been very mixed. About five years ago I had a very negative experience with a doctor who, in very dismissive terms, essentially blamed my symptoms on my weight. Later it turned out that the issue was actually a stomach bacteria, completely unrelated to my weight. That experience made me very anxious about booking appointments for quite a long time. Then in May 2024, when I found out I was pregnant, I called the reception because I genuinely didn’t know how the process worked. The person on the phone was quite hostile and simply told me to “check on Google” and choose where I wanted to give birth, saying I needed to do a self-referral myself. As a first-time pregnant woman asking for guidance, I found that response very unhelpful and quite discouraging. However, I want to be fair. During my pregnancy and afterwards, with my baby, I’ve had several interactions with the surgery and the experience has been very different. I don’t know if the management or staff have changed, but the attitude and care have improved significantly. The staff I’ve dealt with more recently have been helpful and kind, and overall the service now feels much more supportive. For that reason I’m giving four stars. The beginning of my experience was not good, but I do feel things have improved a lot and today I’m generally happy with the care provided.
