Author: Alice Thomas
Date: a year ago
Rating: 1 star
I registered with Cator Medical Centre a few of weeks ago using their online form. As we’ve just moved to the area and I am pregnant and close to my due date it was important this was done quickly. I noted my pregnancy on the application form. Having not heard from them, but having heard from my previous GP that my notes had been transferred, I called today to find out if I had been registered or not. I was told (a) I had not been registered with their practice, but with a different practice (Elm House) that the NHS website confirms I am not in the catchment area for and which I did not ask to be registered with and (b) they have no system either at Cator or Elm House for informing new patients that they are registered. The receptionist seemed baffled as to why they should inform patients of this information, and told me I cannot shift to be registered with Cator, even though their website and the NHS GP finder both say they are still open to new patients, and that is where I wanted to be registered in the first place. As a result of this lack of communication I have missed the opportunity to have an important vaccination (RSV) before my due date. The practice can and should do better.
