Author: Meryem Mery

Rating: 1 star
Very disappointing experience with this GP practice. The reception staff are often rude and not helpful. When calling or speaking to them in person, they frequently interrupt and repeat the same responses without actually listening to the patient’s concerns. It often feels as though patients are treated as an inconvenience rather than people who genuinely need medical help. Basic professionalism and kindness would make a big difference. Being polite and respectful towards patients should be a normal part of the job. Regarding the doctors, I want to be fair: some of them are kind and clearly trying their best. However, others seem unprepared or uninterested. For example, I once visited for back pain and the doctor told me he was not familiar with the issue and needed to speak to a supervisor before calling me back later. Unfortunately, I never received any follow-up call from the doctor or the practice. Overall, the service feels disorganised and very frustrating. Patients deserve to feel listened to and supported when seeking medical care.
