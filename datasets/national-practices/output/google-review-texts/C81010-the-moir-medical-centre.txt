Practice response date: 2 months ago
Practice response:
Dear Deborah, Thank you for taking the time to leave this feedback. We are genuinely sorry to hear that you found the new system frustrating and felt you had to give up in the end. That is certainly not the experience we want for our patients. We would like to gently clarify that moving to this online-first model was not entirely our own choice. As of October 2025, all GP practices in England are under a strict government contractual obligation to implement "Modern General Practice Access." This mandate requires us to keep online consultation tools open throughout core hours to move away from the "8am rush" on the phones. While we understand that this digital transition is difficult for many, we are legally required by the NHS and the Department of Health and Social Care to operate this way. We are working incredibly hard to comply with these national targets while still trying to support patients who cannot use online services. For your reference, you can read the details of these government-imposed obligations here: www.gov.uk/government/news/online-gp-appointment-requests-available-everywhere-from-today We appreciate your patience as our team adapts to these compulsory changes.
