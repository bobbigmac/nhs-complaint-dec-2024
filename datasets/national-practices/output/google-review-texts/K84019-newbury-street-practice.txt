I am sharing my experience to raise awareness, especially for those dealing with more severe or persistent conditions. my experience taught me the importance of asking questions, seeking clarification, and, when necessary, obtaining a second medical opinion. While we trust healthcare professionals, it is also important to stay informed and actively involved in decisions about our own health. If something does not improve or does not feel right, do not hesitate to follow up or seek further advice. Being proactive about your health can make a significant difference. I had developed rashes on my legs and visited the GP at Newbury Street Surgery. During the appointment, I was informed that the condition was hay fever–related. I explained clearly that I do not suffer from allergies and have never had hay fever. Despite this, I was prescribed medication for hay fever. Unfortunately, the prescribed treatment did not improve my condition. The rashes persisted without any sign of recovery. As a result, I sought a second opinion from another doctor. After examination, I was diagnosed with ringworm (a fungal infection). I was prescribed an antifungal cream, and the condition healed completely after using the correct treatment. The incorrect diagnosis led to unnecessary medication, prolonged discomfort, and avoidable delay in receiving appropriate treatment.
