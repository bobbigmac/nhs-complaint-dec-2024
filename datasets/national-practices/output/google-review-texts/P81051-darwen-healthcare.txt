Author: Sami Khan
Date: a month ago
Rating: 1 star
I am extremely disappointed with the service I received. I had an appointment scheduled for 11:30am but was left waiting until approximately 12:40pm before being seen. When Dr Alo finally arrived, I was told she would return in five minutes, yet I was left waiting an additional fifteen minutes. This was particularly distressing as I had undergone jaw surgery on Friday and had not been prescribed any pain relief, despite requesting it. Due to severe pain and no sleep the night before, I attended A&E and was advised to return to my GP for medication. It then took over two more hours at the practice before I was able to see the doctor and receive my prescription. Waiting in significant pain for that length of time was unacceptable. I was also disappointed with the pharmacy service. While patients were waiting, staff prioritised restocking shelves and administrative tasks, even though my prescription was ready and clearly visible. There seemed to be very little urgency or consideration for patients in discomfort. Overall, I felt undervalued and poorly treated. The lack of timely care and compassion was extremely concerning. I would not recommend this practice based on my experience and am reconsidering remaining registered here. I also worry about how elderly or vulnerable patients cope with such prolonged waiting times.
