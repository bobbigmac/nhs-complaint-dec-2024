Author: Ethan Hayes

Rating: 5 stars
Cannot fault them. The fact I'm even reviewing a GP says it all imo. I've had a lot of contact with the surgery for various mental and physical sickness over the last few years - and I'm routinely impressed by the compassion and care provided by the team. The booking system is a pain but isn't that just ever NHS GP service now? And credit where due, the practice does provide routine appointment and follow-ups where on-the-day triage isn't necessarily needed. I think many people expect miracles from the GP; admittedly not ever appointment has gone well and often that's the nature of healthcare and working with people! But the key thing that matters is even when I didn't necessarily feel my point was understood - the staff at the practice create a safe and open environment and that makes all the difference when a patient needs a bit more support than it might first appear. Some of the GPs specifically are beyond good, they are superb. Genuinely compassionate, very willing to listen, and they have helped navigate me through some very serious health concerns with understanding and absolutely no judgement. This is as close to old school "family doctor" style general practice I have personally experienced and I say that in all the best ways. Insane that I've reviewed an GP service - but credit where due, they've been incredible for me and my partner.
