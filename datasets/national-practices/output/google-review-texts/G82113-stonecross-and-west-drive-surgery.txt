Author: Urszula Szymanek
Date: 4 days ago
Rating: 1 star
I am extremely disappointed with the service at this GP surgery. For over a month, I have been trying to book an appointment due to ongoing sinus problems, including constant blockage and headaches, but I have been unable to get seen. As someone working full-time, it is very difficult to call during limited hours, and when I do manage to get through, I am repeatedly told that no appointments are available. I have also been directed to book online, despite there being no availability there either. This creates a frustrating cycle where patients are left without any access to care. What made the experience worse was the attitude of the receptionist, who was unhelpful and came across as rude, offering no real solution or support. Patients should not feel dismissed when they are trying to seek medical help. I am currently unable to get a referral to an ENT specialist because I cannot even see a GP, which is unacceptable given the persistence of my symptoms. Overall, the lack of access to appointments, poor communication, and unprofessional front desk service have led me to lose confidence in this practice. I will be looking to register with another GP surgery as soon as possible.
