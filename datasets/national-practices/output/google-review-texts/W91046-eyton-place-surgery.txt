Author: Paul Marsden
Date: a year ago
Rating: 3 stars
The receptionists are very friendly, helpful and professional. The biggest problem is trying to telephone. First thing in the morning is a lottery to get through and dozens of times I have found the call goes through the standard recorded explanations of Welsh/English etc before you choose to speak to someone and the call goes back on a loop to the beginning. The second problem is the monthly issue of repeat prescriptions. Time and again I have to go to the surgery to request my repeat prescriptions to be sent to Boots because the slip has not been sent to them. Attending in person at the surgery is fine and rarely has queues but not everyone can drive into Flint, park and walk to the surgery, especially since it is rare to find a parking place at the surgery. (Opposite Home Bargains is the best bet.) Dr Reyman is a wonderful doctor and I have no complaints in being with him.

We have been living in Flint for 2 years and found it difficult to choose a new doctors surgery when we arrived. Non of the surgeries had good reviews. So it wasn't an easy choice. We chose Eyton Place and honestly, we can't speak highly enough of the service we have received. The Reception staff, Dr Shamas, Dr Rehman and Mrs Rehman have all been lovely and have listened and acted on our concerns. Yes, you have to be on the phone at 8am and be persistent, but that is true of most surgeries now.
