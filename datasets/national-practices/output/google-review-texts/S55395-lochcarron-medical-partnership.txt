A friend took a tumble whilst out walking near Torridon. Broken nose and nasty deep cuts to his face. Pulled up here and was seen to almost straight away. Doc quizzed me about the incident whilst my pal was being cleaned up, and then he did a fantastic job with the stitches. A year later and there's hardly a scar to be seen. Friendly chap as well, as were all the staff. From a driver's point of view; easy enough to find, decent parking, nice waiting room watching the birds at the feeder whilst I waited for my mate to get fixed up. Very good place.
