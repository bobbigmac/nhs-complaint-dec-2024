Author: Douglas O
Date: a month ago
Rating: 1 star
I’ve been a patient here for over 10 years. The GPs are generally competent and provide good clinical care when you can access it. Unfortunately, the practice is severely let down by its administrative and support staff. Recurring Problems: Administrative errors are frequent - appointments booked for specific tests that then aren’t conducted, medication issues not properly managed, and a general lack of accountability when things go wrong. The real issue is how complaints are handled. Rather than addressing legitimate concerns professionally, the practice actively obstructs the complaints process. Reception staff are often dismissive or outright hostile when patients raise issues. Requests to speak with management are routinely refused, with patients told to “put it in writing” - even when reasonable adjustments are requested for disabilities. There’s a pattern of stonewalling until matters are escalated to NHS England, at which point the practice suddenly engages - but rarely accepts responsibility or makes meaningful changes. Bottom Line: If you have straightforward healthcare needs and don’t experience problems, you’ll probably be fine. But if something goes wrong, expect obstruction rather than resolution. The clinical staff deserve better support, and patients deserve basic professionalism.
