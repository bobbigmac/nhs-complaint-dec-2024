Practice response date: 5 months ago
Practice response:
Thank you for your feedback and we are sorry to hear about your experience. Whilst we understand this may have been frustrating, it is sometimes difficult to obtain a blood sample despite best efforts. Please be assured that all of our nursing team have received appropriate phlebotomy training, and follow best practice when carrying out procedures. Following three failed attempts it is appropriate and in a patient’s best interest to refer for further support. The local hospital phlebotomy clinic is often helpful as they can provide a timely service, additional equipment and experience. If you would prefer a further attempt in a primary care setting, if you speak to our reception team they would be happy to book you in with a colleague.
