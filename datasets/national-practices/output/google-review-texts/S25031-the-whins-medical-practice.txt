Throughout my time in various Scottish locations, I've had the privilege of encountering consistently positive experiences with medical practices—until I interacted with the reception staff at Whinns Medical Practice. Despite these concerns, it's only fair to note that the medical professionals—nurses and doctors—I've consulted with at this practice have been nothing short of polite and helpful.
