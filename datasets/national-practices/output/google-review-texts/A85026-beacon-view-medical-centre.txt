I’ve been attending this surgery for years and overall, it is a good surgery, majority of staff are great, I usually always rate the surgery 5 stars, on this occasion I’m rating it not good for people with mental health issues. I took my 21 year old son to the surgery for his 4.30 appointment, we got there for 4.25. At 4.40 I asked receptionist how long it would be before my son got seen, she said the 4pm person had just gone in. I asked to see practice manager, and explained my son who has severe autism and is non verbal, was getting really distressed. I was told there was only one doctor on and that the doctor was a locom. The same thing has happened with the same locom doctor a few months ago, where we waited 45 mins. By the time I took my son in it was nearly 5.10 pm, he was so upset , I just about explained to the doctor , what the problem was. She did apologise. The surgery needs to improve its reasonable adjustment for people with mental Health disabilities. For parents and carers it’s hard enough for us to look after our loved ones, how do you think this makes us feel. Seeing them distressed and upset in the waiting area, with other patients watching. I hope the surgery is going to look at this review and put something in place for when locums are on, maybe offer a appointment on the next day with a gp, without saying call back tomorrow morning.
