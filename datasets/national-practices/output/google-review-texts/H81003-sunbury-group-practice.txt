Author: Alina Vasile
Date: a week ago
Rating: 1 star
Very disappointing experience with the front desk staff today. I called on behalf of my sister who has just undergone surgery and currently has very limited mobility. She needed her wound cleaned and the dressing replaced, so I contacted the practice to ask what the best option would be. We were told she cannot be seen by a GP or nurse and should go to a walk-in centre instead. While we understand that appointments may not always be available immediately, the way the conversation was handled showed a complete lack of empathy. The surgery not having immediate availability is not an issue, nor the reason for the complaint. We have been patients here for many years and understand how the practice works. What I do not understand is why the front office staff is allowed to be condescending to patients that seek guidance. When I explained that travelling to a walk-in centre would be extremely difficult as she cannot walk properly or stand for long after surgery, the response from the receptionist was: “Well, she would have to walk to us as well, so…” This completely ignores the obvious difference between attending a pre-booked appointment at a nearby surgery and travelling further to sit and wait for hours to be tended to in a walk-in centre after surgery. Patients who are vulnerable or recovering from procedures deserve basic understanding and professionalism when seeking advice. Unfortunately, that was not the experience we had today. If reception staff cannot deal with patients with basic empathy and professionalism, then the practice should reconsider who they place in these vital, sensitive, front-facing roles.
