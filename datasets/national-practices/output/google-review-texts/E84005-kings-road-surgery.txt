Practice response date: 2 days ago
Practice response:
Thank you for your feedback regarding appointment availability at the practice. We understand that booking appointments, particularly with a preferred GP, can sometimes be frustrating. If patients wish to see a specific doctor, including a male GP, there may unfortunately be a longer waiting time due to high demand and limited availability. However, we would like to reassure you that we have a range of other qualified clinicians available, including GPs, nurse practitioners, and other healthcare professionals, who are able to provide appropriate care and support. In many cases, they can offer more timely appointments. Our current booking system is designed to balance advance planning with the need to accommodate urgent and same-day care requests. For patients wishing to book appointments in advance, we encourage the use of our online consultation system, PATCHS. For non-urgent concerns, patients may be signposted to submit a request via PATCHS, which enables our clinical team to review and respond as quickly and effectively as possible. If your husband would be open to seeing another clinician, we encourage him to contact the practice again, and our reception team will be happy to assist in arranging the earliest suitable appointment. Thank you for your understanding. Kings Road Surgery
