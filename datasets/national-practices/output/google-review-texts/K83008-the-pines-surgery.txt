I recently made a complaint about a GP to the integrated care board. The complaint was passed on to the practice manager at the Pines Surgery. I received a letter in response to my complaint from the practice manager. The letter was defensive and personal. I made the complaint about the GP mentioned in the letter, because I was told that the GP was Dr Essayed. Had I been told that it was a duty Dr I wouldn't have made the complaint about Dr Essayed. I would have made the complaint about the duty Dr instead. I'm accused of choosing not to address my insomnia. Which is untrue. I've recently addressed it with a Psychiatrist at Campbell House. Who listened to me. She was capassionate and understanding. She also advised that when I am unable to sleep that the GP prescribe me two days worth of Zopiclone. That's if the GP thinks it is appropriate to do so. She then tries to accuse me of demanding a same day response. Also untrue. She says that duty Dr's are busy dealing with medically urgent patients and not for people who demand a same day response for less urgent conditions. My response to this statement is that I filled out an anima form in regards to my insomnia. The receptionist phoned me that same day and told me that I can discuss my insomnia at the appointment I had on the 5th of February. This was 8 days away and I said that I wasn't happy with the Dr's response. At no point did I demand anything. I wasn't aware that the Dr's were only dealing with urgent cases. If this had been explained to me over the phone then I would have known. I have posted the letter in this review as I think it's important for people to see her response. Everyone has the right to make a complaint about a GP if they feel that is the appropriate step to take. This letter has tried to belittle, minimise and shame. The nurses and receptionists are lovely though. I cannot fault them. Which is why I gave three stars.
