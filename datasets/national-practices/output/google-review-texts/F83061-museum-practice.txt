A formal complaint was filed against Museum Practice and their predatory behaviour. My experience with this practice was deeply upsetting and left me with no confidence in the standard of care or communication I received. I initially attended to discuss my mental health at a time when I was already struggling. During that appointment, I was told that if I needed somewhere private to cry, I could go to a nearby hotel bar, and that I might meet someone there or have someone buy me a drink. I found this response wholly inappropriate, insensitive, and alarming in the context of a mental health consultation. Instead of feeling supported, I left feeling dismissed and distressed. At a later appointment with a different doctor, I was told I would be given a referral for a separate issue. Weeks passed, and I then received a message apologising for the delay and stating that the referral had simply been forgotten. For something important to be missed in this way was extremely concerning and further undermined my trust in the practice. I also made the practice aware that, due to temporary building works, I was unable to receive post at my usual address for a period of time. I asked for a practical alternative, including collecting correspondence in person or receiving it electronically, but this was not accommodated. Important letters continued to be sent to an address where I could not access them, causing avoidable stress and serious concern about how my confidential information was being handled. The situation deteriorated further after I raised these concerns. The communication I then received felt accusatory and distressing, rather than professional or solution-focused. Overall, I found this to be an upsetting and unacceptable experience. I did not feel listened to, supported, or treated with appropriate care, and I would not feel comfortable relying on this practice again.
