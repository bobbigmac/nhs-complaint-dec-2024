Author: Kate Baxter

Rating: 2 stars
Very difficult to make appointments. I've phoned twice for routine (not urgent or emergency) appointments over the last couple of years. A time in which I've been going through cancer treatment and the menopause. Both times I've been told there are none, that all appointments for the month are gone, and that they have not yet released the appointment slots for the following month, which will be released at some undisclosed time and will go quickly, so good luck getting one of them. The advice is to phone at 8am for an 'urgent' same day appointment. This is very difficult to manage for busy working mothers. Firstly, 8am is not a convenient time, if you phone at 8.30, the days appointments have gone. But even if you do get an appointment you have to have kept the day very flexible, no work meetings (as you don't know what time you'll get). If you fail to phone spot on 8am you're left with 'emergency' appointment slots, for something that just requires a routine appointment. For emergency appointments you need to sit and wait in a 'next up' style waiting room wasting a long time. I just want to be able to book a slot that I can schedule into my work diary at some point in the next two or three months, to discus none emergency, but very imortant health issues. This seems a reasonable request, but it is not possible.
Practice response date: 11 months ago
Practice response:
Thanks for your review. I know it is hard to make an appointment with us on occasions but we are always reviewing our processes. The problem with opening up the books in advance too far ahead is the DNA rate increases significantly. We no longer have a sit and wait process for the duty-on call GP. We will try to give you an approximate time in the afternoon for when you will be seen to minimise waiting. Unfortunately, if we do run over time that is the nature of providing an urgent service. We do agree in one sense, that we need more funding for more GP appointments. If you feel strongly about this, then please write to our MS for North Cardiff, Julie Morgan.
