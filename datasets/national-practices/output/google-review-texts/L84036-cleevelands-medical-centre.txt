I am writing to express my significant concern about the difficulty in securing a GP appointment at Cleevelands Medical Centre. A suggested 'safe limit' for a full-time equivalent GP in the UK is 1,600 appointments 3 months. According to figures from May-July 2025, Cleevelands Medical Centre operated at a rate of 943 (over the 3 month period). That is 16 appointments per day per full-time equivalent GP..... This considerable gap may help explain the persistent access issues that I and other patients are experiencing. On a personal note, I have found it nearly impossible to book a timely appointment. The only successful method I have found is to call NHS 111, describe my symptoms, and explain the booking difficulties at the practice. On these occasions, NHS 111 has been able to arrange an appointment for me with an alternative GP, annoyingly based in Gloucester so it takes an hour to get there and back. This situation is particularly perplexing because my direct observations of the surgery do not suggest it is overwhelmed with in-person visitors. On the rare occasions I attend the practice, or when I visit the adjacent pharmacy (1-2 times per month), the surgery consistently appears empty and the reception desk is often unstaffed. This raises questions about where clinical capacity is being directed, especially with the rise of private, app-based GP services. I hope this feedback is useful in reviewing the current appointment system to improve patient access.
