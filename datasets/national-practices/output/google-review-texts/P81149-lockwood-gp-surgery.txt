Author: Hollie O'Reilly

Rating: 1 star
Would give 0 stars if I could. I have been with Lockwood surgery for nearly 15 years, my husband registered a year ago and so I obviously wanted to register my newborn son with the same practice. A week after I registered my son the three of us received letters letting us know we were being removed from the surgery due to our address and had 30 days from the date of the letter to find a new practice. This is completely out of the blue as we have been at our current address for 5 years. We had appointments for my baby, my antenatal care and my husband’s ongoing medical investigations booked in the calendar. Given the fact the letter says that we had 30 days (and these appointments fell within that time) we assumed these would be upheld. However, less than 24 hours before our appointment, and just 12 days into this 30 days grace period, we were phoned to be told they had been cancelled because we were being removed from the practice. So what’s the point of giving 30 days? Doctors have a duty of care to their patients and I think it’s disgraceful that a family with a newborn baby, ongoing medical investigations and required antenatal care have been removed without any consideration. I understand we may fall out of the catchment area but surely a discretionary period could’ve been put in place given the circumstances. We hope our next practice cares more about its patients.
