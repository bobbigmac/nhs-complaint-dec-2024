The NHS has been facing unprecedented demand, and missed appointments (Did Not Attend or DNA) contribute significantly to the increased waiting times and strain on resources. Nationally, missed appointments account for approximately 10-15% of total bookings, resulting in a substantial loss of valuable time and public funds.
