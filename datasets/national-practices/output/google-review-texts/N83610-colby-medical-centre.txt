Receptionist is very rude and I felt she let the whole practice down. Home visits were refused on a few occasions. My mum was elderly and had heart defects and her body gradually became swollen with fluid. I was looking for devices to help her get into bed. I had no idea this was treatable. I rang the GP as she was breathless. They offered a community nurse service. A matron visited and immediately took action. He called the emergency services and requested that she had fluid removed and had her blood tested. My mum was admitted to hospital for almost a week and had a very large amount of fluid drained from her body, she was given blood and afterwards came home looking amazing. As I'm not medically qualified I failed to realise her symptoms could have been treated. My mum has since passed away and I hate to look back and remember how she was treated. I did complain about the service at this practice and extra care was offered which really helped. However, I didn't realise how poor things were until my dad moved house and registered with a new GP which is why I'm making this review now. His new practice has made 5 home visits in 3 months without my asking, they are attentive to his needs and the receptionists are lovely. I regret my mum didn't have the same service. I used to be full of dread dealing with the old receptionist. On one occasion I heard her voice and hung up because I felt it too stressful to cope with her. I'm so glad I don't have to deal with that anymore. I hope they can offer her some extra training on people skills.
