Author: Denise Preston

Rating: 5 stars
The Doctors and all of the staff at Terrington Surgery truly are remarkable and I simply cannot praise them enough. During the past, very tough couple of years due to the pandemic, I have never once found it difficult to obtain prompt medical advice, either by a same day telephone call with a doctor or by way of a face to face appointment with a doctor or nurse, I have also always found that ordering repeat prescriptions to be easy by way of ordering online or over the telephone. Whenever I have had cause to visit the surgery I have noticed the staff to be practically 'rushed off their feet' but they have always found the time to speak to me about my needs in a friendly, polite and professional manner and have always dealt with my medical problems very thoroughly and on a couple of occasions have gone above and beyond to ensure that I have received the prompt medical help that I needed at the time. I feel extremely lucky to be a patient of Terrington Surgery, many of my family and friends, who are registered with other practices in the Ryedale area, do not have the same excellent medical care which I receive, many of them are having to wait more than two weeks for a face to face appointment or a telephone call from their Doctor including my 80 year old mum who has several complex and painful medical conditions. I would like to say a very big thank you to every single one of the staff at Terrington Surgery and please please keep up your excellent work!
