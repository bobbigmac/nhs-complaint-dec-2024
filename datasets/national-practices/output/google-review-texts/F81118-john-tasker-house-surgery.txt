Author: Stuart Winder
Date: a year ago
Rating: 5 stars
Reading some of the reviews of JTH, I do wonder if the reviewers are talking about the same place??!! I transferred here from another fairly local surgery, where my experiences had not been great, about a year ago, which coincided with a decline in my formerly great health. I honestly can’t fault anyone I’ve had dealings with; from several of the GPs, nurse practitioners, dispensary and reception staff. Everyone has been professional, patient, kind and caring, but most of all, proactive. Any concerns I have had have been immediately addressed, and my mind put at ease. They are determined to help me get on top of my health issues. I really can’t thank, praise or recommend the entire team at JTH highly or strongly enough. I’m so happy that I’m now in the hands of such a great team. Thank you JTH 🙏🏻🙏🏻🙏🏻
