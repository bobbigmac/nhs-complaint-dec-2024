Author: Naomi Mayer Baker
Date: Edited a month ago
Rating: 5 stars
I have been with this surgery for 3 years, first arriving in the non face to face era and masks. Even then, if we needed to be seen face to face, we were. All of my family have visited over the 3 years at some point and I can hand on heart say that we received great care. Reading all the reviews, I agree that sometimes reception can be a little abrupt but I have also seen first hand, how busy they are and I would imagine I might also be like that sometimes, in their shoes. The Drs we have seen have always been kind, caring and considerate. Dr Chapman, Dr Firth, Dr Sultana and Dr Franklin, all very different in personality but also very good at what they do. I am truly grateful for them and the services they offer. Having spent many years living overseas and paying for healthcare and every Drs visit, you appreciate the NHS that bit more and we need to do all we can to save it. Lastly, if you use the surgery's website and register to use their services online, it's very easy to book non urgent appointments, without even having to go through the reception booking system. Edit: I want to add to my review that I managed to see a Dr on Saturday that was fantastic and I felt very cared for. Brilliant surgery.
