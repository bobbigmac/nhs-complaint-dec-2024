Rating: 2 stars
I have been a patient at Petersfield since 2002 when I moved to Cambridge. The GPs who I originally dealt with were very caring, particularly when I was diagnosed with secondary bone cancer. However, the changes in staff, the constant restructuring of the NHS and Covid have led me to lose faith in the surgery. I had a telephone conversation with a GP last Friday, which wasn't easy as I'd lost my voice. The GP invited me to come to the surgery, but I didn't feel well enough and, as I'm no longer allowed to drive, couldn't face the thought of getting dressed and going out in the rain. Instead, I was advised that I had a viral infection and was prescribed liquid paracetamol. I'm still waiting for the pharmacy to deliver it. I was also advised that I could contact 111 which seemed a bit OTT to me when I live no more than a mile from the surgery. Today (Sunday) I was fed up with constant coughing, no voice and no sleep, so I did call 111. They sent a taxi to take me to A&E
