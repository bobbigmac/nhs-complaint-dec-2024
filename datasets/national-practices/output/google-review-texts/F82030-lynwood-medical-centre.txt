Author: Simone Manning
Date: 4 days ago
Rating: 5 stars
I would like to take a moment to compliment the staff at Lynwood Medical Centre, particularly Ms Lisa Parson. I have been registered with this clinic for nearly six years, and I generally find all the staff to be very approachable and friendly. However, Lisa truly stood out to me last month when my son, who has autism and learning disabilities, had a skin lesion that appeared to be spreading on his face. When we walked into the clinic to make an appointment, Lisa was not only friendly but also took the time to engage with my son. Despite him being non-verbal, she asked him how his day was and what he liked to do, which I thought was so lovely. It is often the case that people may overlook children with additional needs, so her kindness made a significant impact. We were able to secure a face-to-face appointment that day, and my whole experience was extremely positive. I want to publicly thank the staff, especially Lisa. I wish there were many more people in the world like her who are kind and take the time to interact with our children while listening to the concerns of parents. Thank you, Lynwood Medical Centre!
