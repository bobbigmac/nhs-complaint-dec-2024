We want to express our heartfelt thanks for the outstanding care provided to our elderly parents during the later stages of their lives and in their final days. The compassion and professionalism shown by the entire team made an immeasurable difference to them—and to us as a family. Our parents were part of the local community for over 50 years and had always been registered with this practice. In their final years, they faced multiple complex health challenges, and throughout it all, the support from the practice was consistently excellent. The team was always responsive, kind, and went above and beyond to offer reassurance. Regular home visits, compassionate communication, and prompt access to medication—even at short notice—were just some of the ways they provided exceptional care. What meant the most was how the team honoured our parents’ wishes to remain in their home right to the end. At no point did we feel that home visits were an inconvenience; the staff approached each situation with care and empathy. We also greatly appreciated the guidance offered to us during an emotionally and practically challenging time. From help with ReSPECT forms and advice on lasting power of attorney, to supporting us in accessing additional care services, their support was invaluable. One moment that particularly stands out is how swiftly and thoughtfully they responded when one parent’s health declined rapidly. They ensured we had the necessary medication and paperwork in place ahead of a weekend, and gave us clear guidance on what to expect. When our other parent’s health deteriorated shortly after, the practice once again stepped in with calm, efficient, and compassionate support. We would especially like to acknowledge Dr. Elke Thijskens. She was consistently kind, compassionate, and respectfully firm when needed. Her advocacy, particularly in supporting complex care around dialysis, showed a deep understanding of both the medical and emotional aspects of care. Her persistence in navigating the wider healthcare system on our behalf was incredibly appreciated. Thank you to the entire team for the extraordinary care and dedication you showed our family. Your support helped our parents remain comfortable and dignified in their final days, and we will always be grateful.
