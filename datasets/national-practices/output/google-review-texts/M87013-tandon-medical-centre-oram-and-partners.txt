Have been at Tandon Medical Centre for several years now and other than the universal constraints of the NHS (e.g. difficulty getting appointments, wait times etc) I have to say they are fantastic. The GPs and nurses have been consistently attentive and helpful, and if I have ever gone away feeling something required further attention, this has been provided in a second visit. The receptionists are also very pleasant (Which is unusual in GP surgeries!) I think Dr Hynes and Dr Rakhra deserve a special mention. Dr Hynes is very warm, transparent and actually listens to your concerns, and Dr Rakhra has always gone out of his way to accommodate my very fidgety child and put them at ease!
