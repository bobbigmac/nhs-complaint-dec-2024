Over a prolonged period, I have experienced repeated failings in my care at Mill Road Surgery which have had a severe impact on both my physical and mental health. I believe these failings amount to a serious breach of duty. Prescription Issues I repeatedly received the wrong prescriptions. For example, I requested Doverbet, but the surgery consistently sent a different medication that was ineffective. Prescriptions sometimes took two to three weeks to be allocated. Some items were split between “Pharmacy2U” and a local pharmacy, forcing me to go out unnecessarily even though the intention was to simplify things by using a delivery service. This caused significant hardship and frustration. Dermatology Referral & Psoriasis I requested a referral for psoriasis, but the surgery repeatedly failed to place me on the list or process the referral despite my repeated calls. As a result, my psoriasis spread to cover around 75% of my body, causing extreme physical suffering. At times, my skin was splitting and bleeding, making it difficult or impossible to walk. My worst period was over Christmas, when my sister had to pick me up because I could barely move. Proper care only began after I made a formal complaint. Once I finally received the correct medication, my condition began to improve, highlighting how much harm was caused by repeated failures in care. Complaint Handling I have already made two formal complaints regarding these issues. Instead of resolving them, the surgery asked me to retract my complaints, which I initially considered in the hope of improving the relationship. After the most recent events, I cannot in good conscience withdraw them, as the failings in care remain serious and unresolved. Incident at Surgery I want to be transparent about the incident that led to my removal. On that day, I was so distressed, angry, and erratic that I do not even fully remember the conversation that took place. I was not in control of myself. This was not deliberate aggression — it was the result of my mental health being in crisis after years of inadequate care. I deeply regret what happened, but I hope NHS England will see this as a sign of how badly my health has been affected, rather than as a reflection of who I am as a patient. Impact The repeated prescription errors, delayed dermatology referral, and failures in complaint handling caused physical pain, mental health deterioration, and unnecessary suffering. My psoriasis became severe and widespread because of delays in treatment and referral, which I believe was avoidable had I received timely and correct care. What I Am Requesting 1. Immediate support in registering with a new GP practice – I cannot be left without primary care. 2. A full and independent review of Mill Road Surgery’s handling of my care, specifically the delays, prescription problems, and failures around my dermatology referral and treatment. 3. A review of how my complaints were managed, including the request for me to retract them. 4. Recognition of the impact on my health – the surgery’s repeated failures caused unnecessary suffering and deterioration in my condition. I want to be clear that my priority now is to move forward positively. I am committed to working respectfully with healthcare providers. However, I believe Mill Road Surgery must be held accountable for their failings, and I urgently need NHS England’s support to ensure I receive the ongoing care I am entitled to. Yours sincerely, Shame I couldn't give zero stars
