Author: Aminah Z

Rating: 1 star
I am extremely disappointed with my experience at Crosland Moor Surgery. In addition to concerns regarding a GP consultation, I found the reception staff to be unprofessional, dismissive, and at times outright rude. As someone who has previously worked in a GP practice and now works within the NHS, I am fully aware of standard procedures, patient rights, and how systems operate. On more than one occasion, I felt I was being fobbed off or given incorrect information. When I challenged this and made it clear that I understood the correct protocols, the tone and response from staff noticeably changed. This is deeply concerning. It raises serious questions about how other patients particularly those who may not understand the system are treated and whether they are receiving the access and care they are entitled to. This is genuinely the worst GP surgery I have come across in terms of reception staff conduct. There appears to be a significant lack of professionalism, consistency, and basic patient communication skills. Serious training is clearly needed to ensure staff understand patient rights, NHS processes, and how to speak to people respectfully. That said, I do want to acknowledge that my interactions with the Practice Manager have been positive and professional. I have also seen a brilliant male locum doctor on several occasions who has been thorough, kind and reassuring. Additionally, Dr Luk has been excellent. It is unfortunate that the standard of reception support does not reflect the quality of care provided by some of the clinical team. Reception staff are the first point of contact and set the tone for the entire patient experience. Unfortunately, the current standard in that area falls well below what should be expected within the NHS.
