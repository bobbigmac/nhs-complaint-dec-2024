This week I have had to make contact with the GMC on 2 different days to arrange an appointment to see the nurse and another to follow up on recent blood tests. I followed the new online booking system which was straight forward and submitted my query on time as requested. I had a confirmed appointment sent to me later the same day and in the case of my request to discuss my blood test results, I received a call from a Doctor that same day too. Cant fault that process at all. It worked like clock work. I have been a resident on Pepys since 1964 and have seen the one man Doctor and his receptionist develop into the amazing medical centre offering so many services. From the girls on the front desk who ALWAYS smile and help stress levels by explaining or helping us as much as they can.....and the Doctors and Nurses are enthusiastic and so dedicated AND ALWAYS explain in laymans terms what is happening. Thank you Grove Med Centre!!!
