Practice response date: 4 months ago
Practice response:
Maizie, For urgent/Acute issues we ask patients to complete an online eConsult Consultation form, which provides our triaging GP with the detailed information about your issue to enable them to triage you and ensure you are seen by the appropriate clinician at the appropriate time, be this today, in the next few days or couple of weeks. Often they are able to undertake a prescription or give appropriate advice without the need for an actual appointment with this information. Information with regards this is on our website and initial phone message. Access to eConsult is either through our website, or the NHS App. We strongly recommend that patients are registered with the NHS App as this is also the quickest way to complete the eConsult form, see test results and request repeat prescriptions. We appreciate that patients may feel that if they have a relatively simple issue completing the eConsult may seem inconvenient, but by asking all patients to use our eConsults means that our GPs are able to review and triage all requests most efficiently. We have processes also in place for those unable to use a PC or smartphone to submit an eConsult, such as the elderly. Our system has proved to be highly effective, and has meant we are able to meet the needs of patients in a very safe and effective way, and ensures all patients receive the care in a timely manner that meets their medical need. Rothschild House Group
