I have been a patient at this surgery for about six years, and I am extremely disappointed with the recent decline in service, especially over the past five months. As someone with a severe back injury requiring weekly pain medication, the repeated failures in handling my prescriptions have been incredibly frustrating. On three occasions, I waited two days believing my medication had been ordered, only to be told it hadn’t been. This meant unnecessary trips to the pharmacy, which are particularly difficult for me due to my condition. When I confronted the staff with my call logs proving I had ordered the medication, they bluntly denied it and never once apologized for their mistakes or their embarrassing behaviour. The issues extend beyond prescriptions. Recently, I called to book an appointment and was given a date and time. However, when I arrived, the receptionist rudely claimed I never made an appointment. Despite showing my call logs as proof, she lied to my face, saying no appointment had been booked. This happened when I had an urgent medical issue. Her response to my distress was a curt, “We are really busy, so do you want another appointment or not?” just after dealing similarly dismissively with another patient. The lack of accountability is staggering. Today, yet again, they missed my prescription after two days of waiting, with no apologies and the same rude tone insisting it wasn’t ordered. This surgery needs a serious overhaul of its reception staff and their training. Their behaviour is unacceptable, discriminatory, and has caused unnecessary distress to many patients, including myself. I will be contacting NHS Greater Glasgow and Clyde, Healthcare Improvement Scotland, and the Scottish Public Services Ombudsman to file a serious complaint. I hope management takes these reviews seriously and makes the necessary changes to ensure better service for all patients.
