Pharmacist Karin Hadeq suggested to me after my last Lanzaprozal medication review that, after more than 15 years on the medication for chronic heartburn, I should consider other ways to control the acid reflux, such as changing my diet. After a few months of following the new diet I stopped taking lanzaprozal and have not had heartburn at all for the past 18 months. It’s been incredible to learn that my diet has been the cause of the heartburn my entire life, and I am now pain free.
