I was extremely disappointed by the way I was spoken to by one of the receptionists when I called to request additional pain relief for my mother following a spinal procedure. She was in significant discomfort and required further medication, yet the manner in which the receptionist addressed me was abrupt, dismissive and lacking any empathy. As a practice nurse working across Lambeth and Southwark, I would never speak to patients or their relatives in this way. Compassion, empathy and patient advocacy are fundamental to providing good care and the interaction I experienced fell far below that standard Unfortunately, this is not the first time we have had a negative experience with reception staff. On a previous occasion, I was left waiting on the phone without explanation or apology and then asked bluntly what I wanted, rather than being spoken to politely and professionally. I would like to emphasise that the clinicians at the practice have always provided good consultations and care. However, the behaviour of some reception staff significantly undermines that experience. Having heard many patients complain about rude receptionists in GP surgeries, I now unfortunately understand why. I have colleagues, including another practice nurse and a practice manager, who were present during the call and also heard the interaction. I informed the receptionist that I would be escalating this matter further, as patients and their families deserve to be treated with respect especially when seeking help during vulnerable moments
