Author: Janvi Gandhi

I had an appointment today at Sunflower GP (Harness Hub) for a critical and serious health issue. Due to unexpected traffic, we arrived just 5 minutes late. We immediately explained the situation to the reception staff and apologized for the slight delay. Despite this being a major health concern, and even though there were no patients waiting after us, we were refused the opportunity to see the doctor. We repeatedly requested — more than 50 times — explaining that this was not a routine visit but an urgent medical matter. I am currently suffering from a serious condition and needed medical attention. Unfortunately, the staff showed no empathy, patience, or understanding. Instead, they behaved very rudely and dismissed us without offering any alternative solution, such as waiting to see if the doctor could fit us in or rescheduling for later the same day. Healthcare services should prioritize patient well-being, especially in urgent situations. While I understand that appointments need to run on time, a 5-minute delay for a patient with a critical issue should have been handled with more compassion and professionalism. This experience was extremely distressing during an already difficult time. I hope the management reviews their patient care approach and ensures staff are trained to handle urgent situations with empathy and respect.
