I’m very sad to say that I used to love the service at this surgery, but unfortunately, that’s no longer the case. The recent decision to remove the emergency clinic services feels like a step in the wrong direction. Replacing it with a triage process has made things more complicated and less responsive. When people feel unwell, they come to the clinic seeking help—not to be filtered through a system that delays care. Reception staff should be empowered to assess urgency and offer appointments accordingly. It’s also concerning that we now have only one receptionist actively assisting patients, while the other seat is occupied by someone doing admin work. If the role is administrative, it shouldn’t take up space meant for patient-facing support. That seat matters—especially for parents and vulnerable patients who need quick access and reassurance. I hope the management will reconsider these changes and restore the level of care and accessibility that once made this surgery so dependable.
