Author: Kate Seymour

Rating: 5 stars
Moved to the area about 18 months ago and have had to use this surgery a few times. I'm honestly really impressed. They have massively improved the booking process in the time I've been here, making it easy to book online and get an appointment at the surgery you want. Phone appointments are also available which can be really helpful. Most of all I've repeatedly had excellent interactions with the reception and admin staff. This feels like a rarity in the NHS. Admin requests are actioned so rapidly. For example, it took a matter of hours, not days or weeks, to register with the surgery. I've since made a request for old medical records too, which was processed in a couple of hours. Everything confirmed and communicated by text as well, which is super helpful. Reception staff are warm and friendly, and go above and beyond to help every time I've had to call the surgery. 10/10
