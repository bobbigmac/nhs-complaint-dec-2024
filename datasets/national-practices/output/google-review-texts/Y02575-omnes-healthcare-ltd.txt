We recently registered with this GP after moving homes and had a really great experience with the nurse who did our initial health screening. She noticed my mental health journey and asked how that’s going and I let her know I’ve given up after 7 years of trying to get help. She reassured me that they have a great lady who is very nice and listens and I should book an appointment and pour my heart out. So I booked. Today, the day of my appointment, really excited and full of hope, here I go to my appointment. Once I sat down the chair her first question was “ so what’s the problem”, that’s when I explained how I have tried almost everything, from meds to the different levels of talking therapies and I’m still struggling to cope with daily life. To which she tells me for people with personality disorders, they should try manage it themselves ( Anyone who known anything about bpd or personality disorders should know how unrealistic that is), being told that she doesn’t prescribe meds to which I very strongly refused anyway due to my previous experience. She just kept repeating “so what do you want from me” what do you want me to do”, to which I told her, with tears in my eyes, that all I want is ways to manage my anger outbursts so it doesn’t ruin my life the way it has been. She then said it’s something I need to find myself. I then told her that she’s doing the same thing like everyone else, just dismissing me, and how I already was so skeptical of booking because of prior experiences. I just left the office and GP crying… something that shouldn’t have happened with someone who’s a “mental health nurse”. How does one expect people not to bye bye themselves if this is the kind of help we’re getting? Absolutely disgusted with her attitude and the way she handled my appointment. Seriously debating about moving GPs because what on earth.
