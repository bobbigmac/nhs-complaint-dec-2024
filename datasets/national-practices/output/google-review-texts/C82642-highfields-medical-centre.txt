⭐☆☆☆☆ I had a very disappointing experience at Highfield & Belgrave Medical Centre during a recent consultation with Dr. Adil Vania. I recently moved from London to Leicester and registered with this practice due to ongoing chronic health conditions, including heart disease, type 2 diabetes, and high blood pressure — all of which require regular monitoring. On 10th June 2025, I booked an appointment due to persistent leg pain and requested a blood test. To my surprise, Dr. Vania refused, stating that blood tests are only done once a year. When I tried to explain my symptoms and concerns, he responded in a dismissive and unprofessional way, even saying, “This is not a supermarket where you can buy anything you like,” and “If you want to change your doctor, go back to London.” I found his comments rude and disrespectful, especially towards someone managing serious health issues. While he eventually agreed to the test, he made it clear that future requests would not be accepted. He also abruptly opened the door at the end of the consultation in what felt like an aggressive gesture. Patients deserve to be treated with compassion and dignity — not dismissed or spoken to in this manner. I’ve since submitted a request to change my doctor within the practice.
