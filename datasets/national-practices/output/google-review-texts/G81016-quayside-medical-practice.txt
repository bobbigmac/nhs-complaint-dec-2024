Dr Moore is one of the most condescending and dismissive doctors my family has unfortunately had to deal with, both in relation to my own care and my child’s. Incorrect and, at times, dangerously inaccurate information was recorded in my medical notes, by him, which later had to be formally corrected. This is particularly concerning given that I now have multiple formally diagnosed, complex health conditions after years of being dismissed and gaslit within the NHS. Despite these confirmed diagnoses, I am still treated as though my conditions are not real or not medically established. Out of three doctors we have encountered at this practice, only one, a male doctor who attends intermittently from London, has provided competent and respectful care for my daughter. The rest of our experiences have been extremely poor. One female doctor, I think named Dr Galan, told me that she had never heard of palpitations being associated with anaemia and suggested the symptoms were “in my head.” This is not only dismissive but factually incorrect. Palpitations are clearly listed as a common symptom of iron deficiency anaemia on the NHS website, in addition to being associated with several of my other diagnosed conditions. Even a receptionist at the practice was aware of this. Imagine being told it is in your head when the last time my Transferrin saturation was checked it was 7% and just before 4%. This is very low, and would account for many symptoms, no one needs a doctorate to figure that out. On multiple occasions, letters have been sent from A&E requesting follow-up or support, yet I have received no response. As a result, I am now managing aspects of my health independently simply to avoid further distress from interacting with this practice. I am actively hoping to move and no longer be registered here. I would not recommend this surgery to patients with complex or chronic health conditions who require informed, compassionate, and accurate medical care. These are just the main points.
