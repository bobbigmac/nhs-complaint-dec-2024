Author: auntie bip
Date: a year ago
Rating: 5 stars
I have been a registered patient with the University Health Centre since 2006 and am shocked to see the negative reviews on here. I cannot fault this health centre and the high quality of care I have always received from all the medical staff. I chose to register here because of the size of the practice, it’s opening hours and ease of access. I am not a student or in any way associated with the university, but live relatively close by. I have had continuity of care with the same GP (loveliest person in the world - listens and cares) and the practice has seen me and my family through all of our medical needs - including the birth of my child and ongoing treatment for various medical issues. The ease of access reduced somewhat during and after the Covid pandemic, but this is not a fault of the health centre but the fact that the pandemic laid bare the shortfalls and underfunding of the wider NHS.
