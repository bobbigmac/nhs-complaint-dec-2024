Rating: 4 stars
Manager Maureen is a very approachable person. She listens attentively and responds promptly. There were a few issues with staff, but each was resolved swiftly. All reception staff are kind. One receptionist in particular (the one with a son?) spoke to me when I was going through a difficult time. When I mentioned this upon meeting her again later, she told me she herself had undergone surgery. I wish her and her son good health and happiness. Dr Hedley, Dr Hashimi, Dr Alzoki, Mr Schukter, and Mr Williams provided excellent care. Senior Nurse Cole was consistently kind throughout my treatment period, demonstrating genuine care for patients (she has since left the hospital). Unfortunately, I had a very unpleasant experience with certain doctors. The issue lay in the inappropriateness of their words and actions. My experience with the mental health professional involved from the outset was negative. Believing I couldn't keep changing the mental health practitioner every time when I had issues, I summoned the courage to tell a specific mental health professional that her words and actions had hurt me, attempting to build trust, but it was futile. I did speak to the manager. When asked whether I wished to make a formal complaint, I declined, concerned that my complaint might adversely affect their career. I had assumed it would be fine as long as my experience with other doctors was positive. However, it has since had a significant psychological impact on me. It has also affected my ability to resolve matters concerning my own medical condition. This is the first time I have written about this in a review. I am not good at causing trouble or making complaints. I regret that I should have addressed it frankly when it happened. I intend to take this as an opportunity to deal with matters seriously. Thank you.

I am very disappointed with my recent consultation with Dr. Camelia Rus regarding my 14-year-old son. I clearly explained that he had a cough and had recovered from fever, yet she advised buying paracetamol, ibuprofen, and cough syrup over-the-counter. She refused to offer a prescription, which would have allowed 14years old patience to get the medicine through the NHS for free. No follow-up questions were asked about the cough’s nature, severity, or other symptoms. There was no assessment of causes, risk, or guidance on when to seek further care. The advice felt generic, rushed, and not tailored to my child’s condition. A doctor should listen carefully and apply clinical reasoning, especially in remote consultations. This interaction lacked both. I strongly feel Dr. Rus needs additional training in patient communication and assessment. Parents expect attentive, thoughtful care, not broad, one-size-fits-all recommendations.
