The doctors and nurses here are brilliant. They listen and are thorough and don’t just throw a prescription at you and send you on your way, HOWEVER If like myself, you request your prescriptions online, I would highly recommend that you do it AT LEAST 3 weeks in advance because the time it takes the GP to approve it is astronomically long. It is supposed to be approved within a couple of days of submitting it. Also, they have an eConsult on their website however you can’t use it because every day you’ll find the same notice about it being open from 8am-5:30pm - This is a lie because it never actually opens, ever. If you’re hoping to book a same day appointment, make sure you ring the very second their phone lines open else you’ll be waiting an extensive period for your call to be answered.. usually long enough for your symptoms to get better on their own. If booking routine appointments, it would seem that there is no good time to call back. The receptionists will tell you to call after lunchtime to book one and then the afternoon receptionist will tell you to call back in the morning. Staff communication would be beneficial here. Whilst I appreciate that this is the NHS and it IS free healthcare; the word healthcare should be taken lightly as you’ll find it’s difficult to achieve any such thing where the Old Fire Station Surgery is concerned. I don’t knock their knowledge and I appreciate that they’re constantly snowed under but where does the line get drawn?
