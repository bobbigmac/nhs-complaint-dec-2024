I have nothing but high praise for this surgery! I relocated to Leeds a couple of months ago and registered with Newton Surgery. When the operation was done, 3 abscesses were found as well as early signs of necrosis. Since my op, he has booked me in for several wound reviews to monitor my healing and attend to the wound. The receptionists are lovely and all administrative processes (sick notes etc) are dealt with very efficiently. I highly rate this surgery and cannot thank Dr Khan enough for his wonderful work in such a short space of time.
