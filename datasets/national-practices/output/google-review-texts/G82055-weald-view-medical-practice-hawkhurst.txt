Medical staff have always been very very good, however the current system for bookings by phone twice a day is not fit for purpose. Supposedly it is to "level the playing field", so I was told today, so that everyone has an opportunity.... It doesn't really. It loads it in favour of those who don't work and/or don't have young families. It puts pressure on those that do and likely results in them either not being seen or having to travel miles to get seen elsewhere. A suggestion would be to 50/50 the appointment allocations between walk ins and bookings, during the day to, allow for those who need the flexibility to work around busy work/family lives. In terms of front of house, I have had more welcoming and personable interactions with members of staff. This morning was brusque from the start and lacking in empathy, just the ticket when you are feeling aweful and worrying about work/the family. Fortunately, I was able to get an appointment at the earliest, an hour and 20 minutes after 08:30 just in time to interfere with my commitments, rather than being able to see someone as a walk in soon after 08:30.....definitely a level playing field.
