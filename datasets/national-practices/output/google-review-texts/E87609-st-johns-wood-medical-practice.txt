Mum requested it immediately, received a reply saying to email them back which she’s done same day. Next thing was the message from the practice that they’re looking into it and if no one comes back to us within 2 days we have to call them. Which already doesn’t make sense after walking in, completing patches request and sending an email with exactly the same information. Well, no one bothered to contact her and we finally called them to be told almost a week later that unfortunately they won’t be able to book her in together with my dad. When I walked back in saying that I specifically requested an appointment for two of them last week and being advised by their staff that it is possible if we do it on patches same day then they’ll be able to provide it, staff at reception said to me today “well you called us too late and we can’t help” ignoring the fact that last Wednesday we have done everything that we were asked to do. Where are the standards? Why you made us do all above and still provide no service? Manager of course wasn’t in to speak with. It is extremely stressful for older people to make appointments especially when they try their best and followed all instructions given to end up without the date that was promised. Awful service and treatment for their clients.
