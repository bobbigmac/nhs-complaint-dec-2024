Author: Ty Watt
Date: a month ago
Rating: 5 stars
When I first registered here I was concerned due to the amount of negative reviews regarding reception, however, after multiple phone calls and lovely conversations, it’s clear that these reviews are wrong. The receptionist here is absolutely amazing. I had some issues with blood test results and was worried I’d have to spend weeks trying to resolve the issue (like I would’ve in my last surgery) however the receptionist went above and beyond to help me. She checked my files, chased up an on-call Dr to discuss my results, reviewed the information with me and then spoke to a senior nurse to ensure I would have the correct care for my next appointment (which she booked immediately.) All of this was done within a couple hours. Absolutely phenomenal experience so far. The nurse who took my blood was lovely, she was patient with me, very careful and did it very quickly. I was barely left with a bruise from the needle! (As someone who gets blood taken monthly, this is rare!) The surgery itself is spacious and has a very comfortable waiting area. So far, this surgery has exceeded my expectations and I recommend people look past the negative reviews and experience first hand what the clinic has to offer.
