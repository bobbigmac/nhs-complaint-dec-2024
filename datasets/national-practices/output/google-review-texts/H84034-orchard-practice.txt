Author: chandni chopra

Rating: 1 star
This surgery has been extremely disappointing and stressful to deal with. You fill in an online form and then hear nothing back for days. When you call to follow up, you are often met with an unprofessional attitude from some reception staff, as if patients are inconveniencing them by asking for help. Meanwhile, when attending appointments in person, reception staff are often chatting amongst themselves while patients are left waiting on the phone for long periods of time. I am currently 38 weeks pregnant and recently needed to speak to a GP urgently regarding alternative medication, as advised by my midwife and the hospital. I submitted a form on Friday and was told by a staff member that he would chase it up. By 6pm there had still been no call or update, leaving me to deal with the condition over the entire weekend. On Monday morning at 8am I filled in another urgent form explaining that my condition was making me extremely uncomfortable and that I needed to be seen or advised on alternative medication. I called at 10am for an update and initially spoke with a kind receptionist who said she would check on it. I later received a message, replied promptly, and then heard nothing further after 10am. At around 6pm I called again to ask what was happening and unfortunately had to deal with the same two receptionists, Caroline and Hailey, who on several occasions have spoken with a negative and dismissive attitude that made me feel uncomfortable as a patient. This is not the first time I have experienced this with them. It is now Tuesday and I am still waiting for a response. As a patient who is 38 weeks pregnant and asking for medical advice that was recommended by my midwife and hospital, this level of communication and service is extremely concerning. Patients deserve to be treated with respect and urgency, especially when dealing with health concerns during pregnancy. I sincerely hope the practice reviews how patient requests and communication are handled. If you are considering registering here, I would strongly recommend researching other GP surgeries in the area.
