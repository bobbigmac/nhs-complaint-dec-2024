I don’t usually do reviews but to say I was NOT impressed is an understatement. Firstly my appointment was at 2pm this afternoon. I checked in at reception and took a seat. At 2.30pm still nothing. At 2.40pm, the gentleman sat next to me got up to ask the receptionist how much longer he would have to wait, as he had been here over an hour, she said she would chase this up. In fairness, 5 minutes later the doctor came out, saying that the next person she had on her list was myself, saying my full name in a waiting room full of people, and then saying that I apparently hadn’t turned up for my appointment? I politely corrected her saying that I was here and had been waiting here since 2pm, so she called me in. Being the nice person I am, I told her that the gentleman sat next to me had been waiting longer and insisted that he go before me. It was all very disorganised and quite unprofessional… Not only that, and the biggest thing that bothered me, was the receptionists. Now, everyone blames the receptionists at a doctors surgery but what I heard today, I didn’t want to believe! Whilst waiting in said waiting room full of people, another gentleman went up to the reception and asked how much longer he would have to wait as his appointment was also at 2pm and I believe it was around 2.30pm he asked the question. The receptionist looked up and said to him quite abruptly ‘well you’re late then aren’t you!?’, obviously not realising that he’d already been waiting patiently for 30 minutes prior. Before this, which was the icing on the cake for me, was the same receptionist making a phone call to a patient….. ON LOUD SPEAKER!!!!!! So the whole waiting room of people could hear this poor lady and her problems without her knowledge that she had an audience…. Now if that isn’t a breach of confidentiality then I don’t know what is, and to say that I was stunned that she then made ANOTHER phone call on loud speaker to another patient!? Thank god that I was only here for a one off appointment, but safe to say I won’t be returning!!!
