The reviews do not do justice to my experience at Covent Garden Medical Centre. Dr Cheong was incredibly welcoming and sociable, which immediately put me at ease. He really took the time to listen and made sure I was given the right prescriptions based on my condition. You can tell he genuinely cares about his patients. That personal touch is something you do not always get, and it made a real difference. I left the consultation feeling reassured and satisfied. The reception was pleasant overall. Overall, I would definitely return, especially for the quality of the face-to-face consultation and the care shown by Dr Cheong.
