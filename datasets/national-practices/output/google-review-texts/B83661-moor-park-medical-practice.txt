I am a patient at Avicenna Medical Practice and recently attended an appointment at the surgery. When I arrived, I sat down on some chairs in the waiting area where there was a sign indicating that patients should sit there, as multiple surgeries operate from the same building. While I was sitting there, a receptionist from Moor Park Medical Practice approached me and told me I could not sit there and asked me to move. This was both confusing and unnecessary, especially as the signage clearly suggests that patients attending surgeries in the building can sit in that area. The way this was handled was quite rude and made me feel unwelcome as a patient simply waiting for my appointment. If there are restrictions on where patients can sit, this should be clearly communicated through proper signage rather than staff approaching people in this manner. As it is Ramadan, I chose not to respond at the time out of respect and to avoid any confrontation. However, the interaction left a very poor impression and I believe staff should be more respectful and mindful when speaking to patients.
