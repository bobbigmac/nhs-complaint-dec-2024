Rating: 1 star
I am extremely disappointed with the service at this GP practice. For several days, I have been trying to book an appointment both online and over the phone, and every time I am told there are no available slots. Even when I attempt again at 8am the next morning, when new appointments should be released, the system still shows nothing. The situation becomes even more concerning when you try to call the practice directly. Even if you explain that you are in pain and urgently need to see a doctor, the staff can be incredibly dismissive. If they don’t like the way you speak or the fact that you’re asking for urgent help, they simply hang up the phone. This behaviour is unprofessional, disrespectful, and completely unacceptable especially in a healthcare setting where patients rely on compassion and support. This repeated pattern of no availability, poor communication, and lack of respect makes it nearly impossible to access proper care. It leaves patients feeling ignored and unsupported at times when they need help the most. Unfortunately, this has been the worst GP experience I have ever had, and the practice urgently needs to improve its service and attitude toward patients.
Practice response date: Edited 3 months ago
Practice response:
Dear Damarisa, Thank you for taking the time to leave feedback. We appreciate all patient comments. We operate a total triage model of appointment booking so every GP appointment request is reviewed by a GP first to ensure patients are booked with the most appropriate clinician and that vulnerable groups are prioritised. This system, which is encouraged by the government and NHS England, has dramatically increased appointment capacity as GP appointments are no longer used for requests that do not require GP input. Whilst we cannot comment on your specific experience, over the last couple of weeks we have seen a dramatic increase in patients getting unwell with flu-like illnesses which has increased pressure on all parts of the NHS system including GP practices (you may have seen this in the news: https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c74xqn2zg1eo). This has also coincided with LESS eligible patients opting to have the flu vaccine that is offered by the NHS. These two factors have meant there have mean much more demand for our services over the last couple of weeks. Despite this, the practice continues to offer GP appointment access that is above other local practices in the area. The latest published NHS appointments data (October 2025) shows the practice offers more overall appointments per 1,000 patients than the average practice in Enfield, slightly more than average face-to-face appointments and the same number of GP appointments per 1,000 as the average practice in Enfield. Whilst this may not align with your experience, your feedback is still nonetheless valuable and the team will explore how we can improve access. If you wish to discuss this matter further, please speak to our site manager. Thank you. Ordnance Unity Centre for Health
