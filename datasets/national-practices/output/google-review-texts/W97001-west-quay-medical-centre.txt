Practice response:
Thank you for taking the time to leave a review. We’re sorry you feel that your recent experience didn’t meet your expectations. GP appointments are scheduled for 15 minutes, and this time is set nationally to help ensure that all patients are seen as close to their booked time as possible. To provide safe and thorough care, we ask patients to prioritise the most urgent issue during a standard appointment. When someone has several concerns, this often requires a double appointment, so the GP has enough time to address each issue properly without delaying care for other patients. We understand it can feel more efficient to cover multiple problems in one slot, but doing so often means appointments overrun, which then impacts everyone else waiting to be seen. Our aim is always to provide fair access and high‑quality clinical care to all of our patients. If you’d like to discuss your experience further or need help arranging the right type of appointment for your needs, please contact the surgery and we’ll be happy to assist.
