This is exactly why I do my best to stay well clear of GP surgeries, but when it comes to children, this is just shocking. Nursery staff weren’t overly concerned but suggested seeking a professional opinion from a pharmacist or GP. I explained to the receptionist at this GP that I was hoping a doctor could take a quick look to advise whether it required any further treatment, as I was trying to avoid attending an already busy A&E department unnecessarily with two very young children. Unfortunately, the response from reception was very cold and dismissive. I was made to feel like a complete inconvenience for even asking. I was told the doctor was “far too busy catching up with today’s work,” despite the waiting area being empty.
