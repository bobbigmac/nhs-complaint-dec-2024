Author: Boxing Spectator
Date: Edited 4 months ago
Rating: 5 stars
A GP surgery that doesn’t process prescriptions for locals. 1 mile rule. Crazy. However it is obvious the surgery cares greatly about their reputation & the surgery itself is always trying to help.
Practice response date: 4 months ago
Practice response:
Thank you for taking the time to leave a review. It's extremely frustrating for us too, as we would love to dispense to all our patients who would like us to. Unfortunately, NHS regulations strictly limit which patients we are allowed to dispense to — only those who live more than one mile (as the crow flies) from a community pharmacy. We are audited every year to ensure we comply with these rules, and failing to do so could mean losing our dispensing licence altogether. We do feel a one-star review is an unfair reflection of the Surgery, as these regulations are set nationally and are not within our control. If you would like to discuss this further, please ask to speak with our Dispensary Manager or Patient Services Manager, who will be happy to explain the rules in more detail.
