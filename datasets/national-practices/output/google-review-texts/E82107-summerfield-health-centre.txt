I registered at Summerfield surgery on the recommendation of a friend, and had also heard that a lot of people in my social circle had switched from the one in Radlett I was unsatisfied with. I found all their staff helpful and friendly and the doctor was respectful and a good listener. She referred me to the hospital. The surgery itself was comfortable and pleasant. It's not far from the bus stop. The only thing was I had quite a long wait, but I do know how overstretched the NHS is in general so it's understandable. Unfortunately it's too far for me to go in the winter at my age especially as I don't drive.. If ever I needed a taxi there it would cost a fortune. So I've had to revert to the old place as there's nowhere else nearby that is NHS. There's a private doctor but I think good health should be available to everyone and not depend on income or ability to pay.
