Author: Miriam Anthony
Date: 9 months ago
Rating: 3 stars
I didn’t think accessing healthcare could get more difficult, unfortunately, it has. I chose this surgery because it appeared to offer convenient online booking and communication, but in reality, it’s misleading. You’re required to fill out an online form, only to receive an email days later telling you to call, which just delays everything unnecessarily. Appointments are often arranged at short notice without checking your availability. I was once booked for an appointment the next day while I was abroad despite the surgery being aware I was away, as I had to jump through hoops to refill my medication before traveling. If I hadn’t had a direct admin email, I would’ve missed the appointment and potentially been marked as a DNA. Transferring my care from my previous GP was also stressful. It was a hassle getting my diabetes medication moved over, even though it was a continuation of care. The surgery initially insisted the hospital prescribe it, leaving me uncertain about whether I’d have access to essential medication. I was also told a GP would call, but no one ever did. To be clear, the staff I’ve interacted with have always been pleasant in attitude. It seems obvious they’re just following instructions from management, who appear to be gatekeeping care at every possible turn. I honestly couldn’t imagine being a receptionist there and having to constantly turn people away or provide misleading information because someone higher up doesn’t want to take responsibility or provide proper access to care. The lack of communication, poor coordination, and unnecessary obstacles make this surgery incredibly frustrating to deal with. Basic healthcare access shouldn't be this hard. Patients deserve honesty and directness, not to be strung along or constantly passed from one person to another. It would ease stress on not only patients but the staff as well I am sure.
