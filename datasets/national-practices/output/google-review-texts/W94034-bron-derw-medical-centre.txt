Author: Eurwyn Parry
Date: a week ago
Rating: 1 star
I have been registered with this surgery since I was a baby, which makes writing this review particularly disappointing. Over the years the service has declined to the point where it is now extremely difficult to get even a basic appointment. Trying to see a doctor feels almost impossible. Phone lines are constantly busy, and when you finally get through you are often told there are no appointments available. For a practice that serves so many long-standing patients, the access to care is simply not good enough. What is even more frustrating is how quickly patients are directed to A&E rather than being offered proper assessment at the surgery. A&E should be for emergencies, not a routine alternative because the practice cannot provide appointments. This puts unnecessary pressure on hospitals and leaves patients feeling abandoned by their own GP practice. On top of this, the attitude from some members of staff has been quite rude and dismissive. Patients who are already worried about their health should be treated with patience and respect, not made to feel like a nuisance. After being with this surgery for so many years, it is genuinely sad to see how far the service has fallen. Long-term patients deserve better access to appointments and a more professional, caring approach from staff.
