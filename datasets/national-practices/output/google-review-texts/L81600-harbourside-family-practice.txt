I recently moved house and registered with Harbourside. What a difference in experience from my previous practice. All the staff are friendly and helpful, from the receptionisits through to the GP's. Clinical staff make sure that I understand everything and make ibformed choices about my health - and I can even see them in person if I need to! They apologise if there is a short delay in accessing an appointment (at my last practice I couldn't get appointments for weeks on end, and when I did they were telephone and very rushed). The practice has offered me free wellbeing coaching and when I developed a problem with my shoulder I only had to wait 2 weeks to access NHS physiotherapy (they have one in house). This is an excellent experience so far, I wish all practices would consider investing their funds the way Harbourside do!
