I was a bit apprehensive about using the new online booking system (ANIMA-GP). It asked precise questions after ensuring you don’t need urgent medical treatment initially. I needn’t have worried as the system was simple enough to use and after being checked, I was sent a link to book an appointment. I have been with the practice for @25 years, I do have only 1 GP within the practice who I prefer to see due to past dismissive appointments which actually required further treatment. The preferred GP shows true empathy, really listens to the issues I have and always gets me to the right follow on medical professionals for surgery and general treatments / investigations. The Receptionists have guidelines to follow and I have always high regard to help them help me. I am upfront and honest with the Receptionists and other professionals, which is most likely why I have never encountered any rudeness or disrespect that other reviews talk about. I receive texts for boosters and routine tests each time they are due. THANK YOU to everyone who works hard behind the scenes and face to face, I truly wouldn’t be here today if it wasn’t for the support I have received, even if I have had to persevere at times ❤️
