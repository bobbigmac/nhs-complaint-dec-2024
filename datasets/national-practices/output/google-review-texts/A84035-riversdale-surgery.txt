Author: Iris Priest (Iris)
Date: a year ago
Rating: 5 stars
We have been with Riversdale Surgery since January this year (5 months) and have had, across the board, positive experiences. The reception and administration staff are always warm, polite, and efficient (it only takes a few rings to get through to appointments)! And even though the NHS is stretched at the moment and appointments are often a wait away, there are always emergency appointments available when you call first thing. The GP we've been seeing, Dr Issy Knowles, has been above and beyond. She reads all our notes and is extremely thorough, contentious, and compassionate. She takes the time to look at the big picture and explore all the moving parts, offering support and, wherever possible, tests and/or referrals. It makes such a huge difference to have a doctor just see and hear you and the difficulties you are experiencing, even if sometimes there is no service or treatment available for aspects if a chronic health condition. We are so appreciative of the time Dr Knowles takes with our case and feel reassured by the care and attention to detail she always has.
