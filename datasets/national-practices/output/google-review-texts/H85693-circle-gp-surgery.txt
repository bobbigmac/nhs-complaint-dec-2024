I had a very stressful experience during an appointment for my special needs son at the surgery. The appointment was scheduled for 15 minutes and we had two issues to discuss. After about 7 minutes discussing the first issue, I asked the nurse about the second problem. Even though there were still around 8 minutes left in the appointment, she refused to discuss it and told me I had to book another appointment because she only deals with one issue per visit. I understand that appointments are limited and that sometimes another appointment is needed, but in this case there was still time remaining and my son has special needs, which made the situation more difficult. I spoke with the manager afterwards, and they arranged for a GP to see my son regarding the second issue. The GP addressed it and gave us advice in only about 3 minutes. I appreciate that the manager helped resolve the situation, but the interaction with the nurse was quite stressful and lacked flexibility and understanding, especially considering my son’s additional needs. I hope the practice can take this feedback into account to improve the experience for families in similar situations.
