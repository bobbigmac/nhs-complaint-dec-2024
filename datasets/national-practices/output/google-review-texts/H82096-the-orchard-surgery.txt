Author: Julie Stevens
Date: Edited 5 months ago
Rating: 5 stars
Surgery has always dealt with issues in a timely fashion & waiting is part & parcel of life. They have a massive patient list & therefore have to prioritise urgent calls and emergencies. Do not be rude to receptionists, they try to ensure each patient gets the best care but if you feel your condition requires urgent attention, you need to explain this & you will be prioritised but do not abuse this system as someone more in need of care than you could suffer. Drs will sometimes be stressed as they have a huge patient database each so even if you feel unhappy about anything, remember they are people too & save lives and trained for years in order to save lives which is their primary focus. You don't know what sort of day they're having & so don't judge as you wouldn't want to be judged yourself. Be kind, courteous, understanding & patient when waiting to speak to or see a Dr. It's easy to get angry if you're stressed, frightened or worried yourself but remember their day could be far worse and they're in the front line of making people better & are putting themselves at risk of contracting all sorts just by seeing you & passing it onto their families, so next time you feel like being opinionated, please take these issues into account, take a breath in, think & be a better person.
