Author: Martin Reviews
Date: Edited a month ago
Rating: 1 star
Goodcare Practice is very local to me, and as a disabled person with stroke and seizure conditions, access to a reliable GP surgery is essential. Unfortunately, my experience has been consistently poor and, at times, dangerous. For months, my physiotherapist requested that an updated discharge letter be uploaded to my NHS records so other consultants could access my medical information. The letter was ignored repeatedly. It was sent by post, delivered in person, emailed multiple times, and discussed directly with the Principal GP, who agreed to upload it — yet it still never happened. In total, the same letter was sent nine times. Shortly after, I tried to order my seizure medication via the NHS App and was blocked due to a supposed medication review. I contacted the practice and was told the app was “wrong” and that I wasn’t due a review. I was then told to email the practice. After several days with no response, I called again and was told I had used the “wrong” email address and that there is a separate email specifically for medication issues — something I had never been informed of. I emailed again and was told to wait 48 hours. By this point, my seizure medication had been blocked for five days. I began experiencing small seizures, and due to the risk of a serious or fatal seizure, I was forced to attend emergency services. No patient — especially one with life-threatening conditions — should be put in this position. The administration is disorganised, communication is unclear, and critical medication access was mishandled. Reception staff appear to act as gatekeepers without urgency, and management oversight feels severely lacking. This practice’s failures directly endangered my health. I would strongly urge anyone who relies on timely medication or coordinated care to think very carefully before depending on Goodcare Practice. Off to the hospital .
