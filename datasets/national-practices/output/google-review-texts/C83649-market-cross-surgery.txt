I have been with many GP practices over the years, had referrals to many specialists and even considered saving up thousands for private healthcare before joining market cross surgery. Dr Steel and Dr Elliott have worked with me for months now to diagnose some long standing chronic health issues I have suffered with for years and never had any help or treatment or even tests. For once in my life I have felt heard and my health been taken seriously, I’ve been referred for scans and treatments that other GP’s have laughed in my face for asking for. For years I was told my symptoms were all down to anxiety and hormones, and after just 6 months at this surgery I actually have solid diagnoses and treatment plans! As a woman it is often hard to be taken seriously in the medical field, and not once have I felt like I have to fight to be believed by these doctors. I couldn’t say a bad word about them. I’m always able to get an appointment either in advance or as an emergency and the care feels so much more collaborative, personal and understanding than anywhere else. These doctors and nurses (specifically Dr Elliott, Dr Steel and nurse Donna) know exactly what to say and how to help, they never rush you and never make you feel like an inconvenience. Thank you ❤️
