Author: Seynab A

Rating: 1 star
I suffer from excruciating period pain every month, which often affects my ability to work, and I work for the NHS myself, so I know what good care and compassion should look like. Unfortunately, my experiences at this GP practice have been consistently disappointing. Despite repeatedly visiting about my period pain, I’ve often felt dismissed, gaslit, and not taken seriously. The doctors I’ve seen, who were also female, showed little compassion or understanding. One even said, “maybe your pain tolerance is low,” which is extremely insensitive to anyone experiencing severe menstrual pain. I had to practically beg for a referral to a gynaecologist and even had to raise my voice just to be taken seriously enough to get a scan. No patient should have to go through that just to be heard. Because I have medical knowledge, I’m able to advocate for myself and I know what the guidelines should be for women presenting to their GP with symptoms of potential endometriosis , but for those who don’t, I can only imagine how poorly they are treated and how easily their concerns are dismissed. I now dread coming to this practice whenever I need help for my period pain because I know I’ll likely be met with the same lack of empathy and support. For any woman suffering from severe period pain or other gynaecological issues, please avoid this surgery at all costs. They do not take women’s health seriously, and you deserve to be listened to and treated with respect.
