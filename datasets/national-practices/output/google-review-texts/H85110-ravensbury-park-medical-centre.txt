Myself and my family have been patients for many years at this surgery, and I can never fault them. Getting an appointment is very easy via the NHS app. They are incredibly efficient when it comes to medication, and usually in less than 24 hours of requesting online, a repeat prescription is issued. Any online medical requests, or hospital letters requiring GP action are dealt with in a very timely manner, as are any medical forms that need filling in. Dr Khaliq in particular is always very thorough, and makes you feel listened to and at ease. And the reception team are always polite and helpful whenever I have spoken to them.
