I’ve only recently moved to Rugby and haven’t had a need for GP/Nurse services for probably over a decade. However, over the last couple of months, I’ve had a few issues that I’ve needed help with. Central Surgery have provided excellent support, have been responsive to getting me appointments in very good time and all of the staff I have seen I have had great interactions with. Firstly, I saw one of the male paramedic practitioners who was welcoming, helpful and got my issue sorted promptly. I then saw Dr Caleyachetty - who again, took the time to listen to my concerns, didn’t dismiss anything (which can be difficult when speaking about female issues) and again - helped me with that specific problem. Yesterday, I saw Dr Armstrong (along with a trainee Dr) and she was kind, helpful, very informative and again - prescribed relevant medications that are already helping. I didn’t feel rushed or pressured at all through the appointment as can sometimes be the case. And finally - I saw Nurse Avril Webb (I hope that was her last name!) today who was exceptional. Going in for female testing is really never a pleasant experience - but when you find a nurse who is as personable, kind, patient and empathetic as she is - the whole process feels so much more bearable … maybe even easy! She explained everything so thoroughly and helpfully that I felt I was in such safe hands. Thank you for some lovely experiences at a bit of a difficult time!
