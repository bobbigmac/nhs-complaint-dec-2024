Based on a few negative experiences, my partner and I lost trust and confidence in the quality of care and communication at Chiddingfold Surgery. We decided to move to a different practice. On two occasions, advice from reception regarding our appointments led to the sought-after patient care not being provided. In one case, the end result was us paying for private care and my partner having a negative, unsubstantiated claim put on her NHS record. In the other case, it involved reception providing misleading information about how my request for an in-person diagnosis and digital uploads would be handled. We are happier at a surgery that does what they say they will, and that look after a wider range of demographic groups.
