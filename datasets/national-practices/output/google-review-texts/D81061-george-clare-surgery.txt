Why do I get so anxious about going to the doctor for anything? Oh I don’t know, maybe because when I get a UTI and call the doctors I get told to go to the pharmacy to get the normal 3 day course of antibiotics (Nitrofurantoin), but when I explain that I have a history of UTI’s and know from past experience that they don’t work, that I need and have previously always needed a stronger longer course, that I have a history of kidney infections (that’s on my doctors notes), I’m now taking medication that is known to cause and worsen both UTI’s and kidney function (I have yearly blood tests to keep an eye on them) and need to actually talk to a doctor to discuss this I just get told to bring in a urine sample and fill out a form… that has no space to explain why it’s not a straight forward issue. I then get a text saying a prescription has been sent to my pharmacy… of Nitrofurantoin… a 3 day course. No phone call. No contact. Nothing. I don’t know what the point is anymore.
