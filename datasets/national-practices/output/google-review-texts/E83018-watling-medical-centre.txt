Author: Anna Dorant-Hayes
Date: a month ago
Rating: 5 stars
Watling Medical Centre is the best run surgery in Barnet if not in the whole of North London. You can get an appointment relatively easy and if that's not possible you can rest assured that a doctor will call you back at least to discuss your issue over the phone and provide an advice. Receptionists are courteous and helpful and more importantly the medical staff really listen to the patients and take on board their concerns, and act promptly. The surgery always tries to improve its services and ensure patients have better experience - eg. introducing longer working hours until 20.00 on some days, accessing advice via e-consult. I've been registered in this surgery for close to 20 years and my experience has always been positive. I was particularly impressed by the way they quickly and successfully adapted their work during the Covid pandemic providing an efficient and prompt service in very challenging conditions. My thanks go to all the excellent staff but in particular Dr Patel and Dr Santhouse for their expert help over the years. Thank you.
