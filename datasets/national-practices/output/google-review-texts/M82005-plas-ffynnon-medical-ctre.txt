Practice response date: 9 months ago
Practice response:
I am sorry to read about your experience. Due to patient confidentiality I can't discuss your treatment here, but please email us (our email address is on our website at pfmc.co.uk), so that we can discuss the matter with you directly. Our appointments system is designed to ensure that patients do not have to wait weeks to have a consultation with a clinician. Generally, if you can phone between 08:00 and 08:30 you will be offered either an in person or telephone appointment that day. This appointments system was created several years ago as patients were typically having to wait more than 3 weeks to be seen. We try to strike a balance between having enough appointments available for patients who need a consultation as soon as possible and those who can wait a few days. It is very fine balance as we have found that when we have tried to reduce the number of same day appointments and offer more pre-bookable appointments, more patients were having to be directed to the Minor Injuries Unit, to the Walk-In Centre in Shrewsbury or to Accident & Emergency Departments. We try to do our very best with the resources that are provided to us by the NHS. Our GP services cover 9 and a half thousand patients, so unfortunately, we can never guarantee appointments will be available at the ideal time for each patient, but each month we have nearly 4000 face to face appointments and 1500 telephone consultations. Although our appointments system works for many, we do understand that it doesn’t work for all although the reality is that there is no ‘one size fits all’ system. For that reason, we do offer pre-bookable appointments which can be booked a few days in advance by telephone, and it is also possible to book online a pre-bookable appointment a few days in advance. Nursing and Health Care Assistant appointments cannot be booked online because of the different procedures that Nurses and HCAs undertake. We must ensure that the right Nursing appointment is booked with the right Nurse/HCA to avoid wasting time for the patient and clinician if the clinician cannot undertake the required procedure. You can also submit an online at https://accurx.nhs.uk/patient-initiated/m82005, which will be responded to by a clinician within two working days. If you do not have online access, please download the NHS app or pick up an online registration form from Reception.
