Author: Stephanie Rathbone
Date: 2 weeks ago
Rating: 5 stars
Despite the huge amount of development in the area and the clear increase in demand, this surgery continues to provide excellent care in my view. If you genuinely need help, they will do their best to support you. The reception team handle an enormous workload yet remain kind, calm and professional which really deserves recognition. The dispensary is also under huge pressure, and the team work incredibly hard while still managing to deliver a good service. The doctors are, in the main, excellent. I recently saw a trainee doctor and, although I initially felt a little unsure, he thoughtfully arranged for me to see another GP shortly afterwards without me having to ask. I really appreciated that as it showed care and that good escalation and support systems are in place. The practice is well run and clean, and the nursing team are fantastic, especially the asthma care team with whom I have had more dealings in the last 18 months or so. Overall, a hardworking and compassionate surgery that continues to deliver high standards despite the pressures they face.

Author: Isabelle Clews
Date: a year ago
Rating: 2 stars
Avoid if you are neurodiverse, they are unwilling to help. The practice has admittedly been good at times, fairly quick appointments and referrals etc. however I was completely left in the lurch during a crisis as the practice doesn't accept any sort of shared care agreement. It’s within their right to choose not to do this, I imagine it’s profit related and it ultimately is a business. However, the lack of compassion or guidance was shocking. I was close to being without vital medication, which was only resolved by switching GPs, something they never suggested was an option. Avoid if you want your mental health and wellbeing to be taken seriously.
Practice response date: a year ago
Practice response:
Thank you for your review recognising previous good experiences and we’re sorry to hear you felt we weren’t able to help on this occasion. At Bidford Health Centre we take mental health and wellbeing seriously and have a team made up of GPs, a health and wellbeing coach and social prescriber available to help. We currently are involved with a pilot scheme that has made a care co-ordinator available for young people to help provide support whilst patients are on long waiting lists to access help from the hospital and community mental health teams. We would like to clarify that we do enter into shared care agreements where an NHS consultant is responsible for overseeing the prescriptions, meets the locally approved shared care criteria which is entirely based on patient safety. We have clear advice for our patients on this available on our website under the Neurodevelopment tab here https://www.bidfordhc.nhs.uk/selfreferrals
