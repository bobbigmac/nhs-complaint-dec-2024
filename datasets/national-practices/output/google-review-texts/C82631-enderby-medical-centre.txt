I am extremely disappointed with the ongoing lack of access to GP appointments at this practice. For the past two years, almost every time my family or I needed to see a GP – whether for flu symptoms, my child’s ear infection, medical advice or more serious concerns – we have been redirected elsewhere instead of being assessed at the practice. Most recently, we were again told to call NHS 111 and attend the urgent care centre in Loughborough – approximately 28 km away. What is the purpose of being registered with a GP surgery if patients are repeatedly sent to another town for examination? Not everyone has access to a car, and paying for taxis simply to receive basic medical assessment is not reasonable. While I understand NHS pressures, consistent redirection rather than direct care has significantly reduced confidence in the practice’s accessibility and service. This has been an ongoing and very frustrating experience for our family.
