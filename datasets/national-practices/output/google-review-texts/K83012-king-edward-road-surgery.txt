Just about given up with this place. They just wring their hands of proactively helping. Had a chest complaint for over 3 months. Pharmacist and practice nurse, first class. Nurse strongly recommended I speak with a doctor, but she couldn’t arrange it. The ONLY option is to join the daily bun-fight at 8am, online, there is NO other way of getting an appointment, or call back from any doctor. I’ve physically gone in and I’ve explained that after 13 weeks of this, I’ll see any doctor, in the near future. A week, two weeks, doesn’t matter, I don’t expect one tomorrow, or the next day, but the staff there cannot apparently interfere with the system. Flip the coin, I’m a lifelong asthmatic. Have an annual review. My review coincides with this time period. Umpteen calls and texts on this one from the Surgery, can’t escape this one ! I tried to suggest that my current condition made a review a little pointless, but when they’re in box-ticking/compliance mode they’re all over you. I was told outright, after 50 years of prescriptions, if I didn’t undergo a review my prescription for a lifelong condition would be stopped, straight away !!! As I said the nurse I did see was excellent, couldn’t have been better, but despite co-ordinated external tests/referrals/full examination for a prolonged condition she had no internal process by which to even set up a call back, let alone an appointment any time in the future with a actual doctor. The practice doesn’t seem capable of operating with any common sense, or compassion, hiding behind “we have to be fair to everyone” as if the whole thing doesn’t need to be managed and can conveniently be blamed on “systems” Shocking double standards !!
