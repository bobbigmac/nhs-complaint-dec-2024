I was seen today by Dr. V. Garg and, sadly, left the consultation feeling extremely disappointed and disheartened. No questions were adequately answered. Dr. Garg appeared rushed, disengaged, and showed little to no interest in providing support or guidance. Each attempt to seek clarity was cut short, and no advice was offered throughout the consultation. As an NHS doctor myself, I am genuinely shocked and saddened by the lack of empathy and professionalism I encountered. It is difficult to reconcile how a fellow clinician could respond with such bluntness, offering nothing more than “I don’t know” or “I’ve never heard of that,” with no effort made to investigate further, reassure, or signpost. Every patient — regardless of background or circumstance — is entitled to compassionate, respectful, and communicative care. Unfortunately, that was entirely lacking in this encounter. Furthermore, I was taken aback when Dr. Garg stated she was “not here to take abuse.” At no point were voices raised, nor was there any disrespectful behaviour. I was simply advocating, calmly and reasonably, for a referral and some essential blood tests. It was, in fact, my husband and I who left feeling dismissed, unsupported, and emotionally affected by the exchange. On a more positive note, I would like to sincerely commend Dr. Bajwala, whose professionalism, empathy, and patient-centred approach stand in stark contrast. He is a credit to the profession.
