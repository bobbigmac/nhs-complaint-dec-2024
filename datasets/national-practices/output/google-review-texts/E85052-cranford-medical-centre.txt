Author: Hannah Lane

Rating: 2 stars
I have been registered with this practice since 2018. I would like to acknowledge that Mrs Rai, the nursing team, and the reception staff have consistently treated me with kindness, professionalism, and genuine compassion. For that, I am grateful. Unfortunately, my recent experiences have been deeply frustrating and have left me feeling unsupported as a patient. A hospital referral to the neurology department was requested to be actioned by my GP on 26 March 2025 by West Mid. As of December 2025, this referral has still not been completed. During this time, I have continued to experience ongoing symptoms without appropriate specialist input, through no fault of my own. I have also been left confused and disappointed by the suggestion from a staff member that delays in follow-up appointments are the responsibility of patients. Hospital correspondence often states that follow-up will occur within a specific timeframe, and patients understandably rely on this information. When these timeframes are not met, the impact falls directly on patients’ health and wellbeing. Due to these delays and a lack of communication between departments, I am now being told I must wait until March 2026 — “6–8 weeks” from September as my letter would suggest — before my prescription can even be reconsidered. I did not choose to require medical care, but I did expect reasonable communication, timely referrals, and continuity of care. Sadly, this has not been my experience.
