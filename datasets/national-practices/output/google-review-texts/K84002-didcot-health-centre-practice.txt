Practice response date: 4 months ago
Practice response:
I am sorry that you have had difficulty in getting an appointment. You are obviously aware of the recent contract changes. Unfortunately the problem is not one of access, it is about capacity. Whilst the contract changes mean that we are obliged to leave our online triage system open throughout our opening hours (and to continue to take phone call requests all day, which we do), the changes do not provide us with more capacity. We still have exactly the same number of clinicians and the same number of appointments as before. And even if we took on more doctors, we no longer have the space to put them. To protect our patients, as well as our clinical staff, there comes a point when we have to stop accepting more patient requests. In the case of our duty doctors this is often once their morning or afternoon list has reached the point where to increase their workload could be unsafe. I know it may sound like we have been using the same 'excuse' for years, but Didcot urgently needs a fourth practice. All three of the Didcot practices really struggle to meet the demands of our growing population. If you have not already used it, you may find the new online triage service helpful. You can contact us via the online form that can be accessed at 7.30 am every weekday: - in your NHS App or Patient Access - via our website at www.didcothealthcentre.com/appointments - through your NHS online account at https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/online-services/nhs-login/ Requests about a health problem are reviewed by a GP within six hours, and we respond to routine and admin requests within one working day. If you wish to speak to a particular GP you can say this in your request, but please note that we may not be able to offer this for a day or two, or longer if they are away. But if ever you feel your need is urgent, please understand that it may be safer and better for you to speak to a different doctor sooner, rather than to wait for your preferred GP. Of course, you can still contact us by phone during the day after the online health problem request form has closed. I hope this information is helpful. Gill Suter Assistant Practice Manager
