Author: T. Rowan Goodfellow
Date: 2 months ago
Rating: 1 star
Consistently Poor Access, Communication, and Support - Phones routinely unanswered - Reception appears inadequately staffed - Concerns and formal complaints dismissed - Poor cooperation with other NHS services - Refusal to provide time-sensitive medical evidence - Performance significantly below comparable GP practices I don't offer this review lightly, I have tried to reach out to the practice to remedy these issues, and I hate to be a complainer. I hope that with some public statement of my experience the practice can strive for improvement. I want to acknowledge at the outset the very real pressures currently facing the NHS, including staffing shortages, funding constraints, and increased patient demand. I understand that GP practices are operating in extremely challenging conditions. However, even allowing for these limitations, my experience with Long Furlong Medical Centre has consistently fallen well below the standard of care I have received from other GP practices in recent years. Due to work commitments, I have had to move frequently and have therefore been registered with several GP surgeries during the same period and under the same NHS constraints. In contrast to Long Furlong Medical Centre, those practices have not only been adequate but, in many cases, have gone above and beyond to ensure patients could access care, communicate concerns, and receive timely support. Access remains a significant problem. It is extremely difficult—often impossible—to get through on the phone, regardless of the time of day. Throughout my time as a patient, the reception desk has never appeared to be adequately staffed, making even basic communication unnecessarily difficult. Concerns and complaints are handled poorly. Despite submitting formal complaints through appropriate channels, they are routinely dismissed without meaningful investigation or resolution. Responses, where provided, fail to address the substance of the issues raised. There also appears to be a lack of cooperation with other NHS departments, leading to avoidable delays and complications. On multiple occasions, the practice has refused to provide necessary medical evidence, even when requests were clearly time-sensitive and essential for medical, administrative, or safeguarding purposes. Overall, while I fully recognise the strain the NHS is under, the level of service at Long Furlong Medical Centre does not reflect what is achievable under the same conditions. The persistent lack of communication, poor complaint handling, and refusal to support essential documentation have significantly undermined my confidence in the practice. I hope this feedback is taken seriously and used to make meaningful improvements for the benefit of patients.
