The online form to book appointments is excellent, and they always seem to call really soon after you fill it out (like within an hour) to schedule an appointment - often the appointment is even the same day. Amazingly fast appointments, and the GPs are great. My one complaint is that every appointment I've had has been with a different GP, so I have to keep re-explaining my health issues. This is despite me using the "request a specific GP" part of the form, in which I normally request whoever I had an appointment with last. Personally, I would be happy to wait a few days to see the same GP every time rather than have immediate appointments with random GPs, but can't complain too much as the appointments really are so efficient. Bear in mind when reading all the negative reviews that people are generally only driven to review when they have a bad experience - they is not representative. This is a good GP surgery. (a lot of reviews complaining about receptionists - I personally have had no issues, and think this is a petty thing to complain about anyway. You're getting healthcare for free, be grateful lol)
