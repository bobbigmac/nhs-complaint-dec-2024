It's only having now found a new GP, I realise just how bad Broom Road Medical Practice was, sadly not just one individual. the rot comes from the administrative staff, through the pharmacist and the doctors. Maybe because its a small practice that they are able to all get on the bandwagon to treat to treat people disrespectfully and unkindly, this they do well. The only positive I can say is that the nurses are really good and it's a shame that they are associated with people who behave in such an unprofessional and uncaring way. If you feel even remotely uncomfortable about the way you are being treated, that they are not helping you, you're not imagining it, change GPs as soon as you can. Good ones exist, and it's so nice to finally be treated like a human and have concerns dealt with.
