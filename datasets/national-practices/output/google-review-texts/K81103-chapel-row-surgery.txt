Rating: 5 stars
I moved to this surgery after my second child was born, following a disappointing experience with my previous practice who refused to see my 4-day-old baby with a sticky eye because he wasn’t yet registered. My Health Visitor was horrified and recommended Chapel Row, and I’m so glad she did. From the very first visit, the service has been second to none. The nurse who carries out the vaccinations is absolutely wonderful – warm, kind and so caring. She recently gave my 2-year-old her flu vaccination and was just as lovely as ever; my daughter felt instantly at ease with her. I’ve also had to call about my 6-month-old, and despite ringing after the usual appointment slots had gone, we were seen within the hour. Two professionals checked him over thoroughly and the care he received was excellent. The reception team are equally fantastic – always polite, helpful, and welcoming, never making you feel like a nuisance. Overall, I couldn’t recommend Chapel Row highly enough. The whole team goes above and beyond, and I feel so reassured knowing my family’s care is in such good hands.

Practice response date: a year ago
Practice response:
We are very sorry to hear that you are unsatisfied with the service we provide to our patients at Chapel Row Surgery. We have a number of skilled clinicians who are very glad to see our patients and discuss their needs and experiences. Our intention is to ensure our patients see the most appropriate clinician and this is why we ask questions about a patient's condition, situation and what the patient feels may be causing or agitating their condition. Once we have this additional information, and most importantly our patient's views about how they are affected by their condition, then we can ensure the patient is seen by the most suitable clinician, who can discuss the patient's needs and create the most appropriate care package. We are sorry if you are feeling ignored, as that is not our intention, and we would welcome the opportunity to speak with you, so we can develop a care plan / package to address how your medical issue is affecting your daily life and how we can help to improve this, wherever possible.
