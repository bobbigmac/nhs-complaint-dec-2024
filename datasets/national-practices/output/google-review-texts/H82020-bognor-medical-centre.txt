I have been registered at this practice for over 40 years. The doctors have always been helpful and I am on a range medication to treat various ailments that I have collected over the years. On my yearly review it became apparent I would possibly need to increase medication. I was referred by the nurse to Lenka the new practices well-being coach. Lenka took time in our first session which was unhurried, and had lots of time in a relaxed way, to review my health and how I was feeling, also taking into account work family etc. I admit I was skeptical at first as, but with each coaching session it became clear that much of my health problems stem from diet and lifestyle. I listened and have implemented the clear diet and lifestyle changes that have been recommended. The program is simple and clear, to my amazement in just a few months I have lost weight, inflammation as gone from my hands, and I feel so much better than I have for some time. I have more energy and feel so much better, as a result. I am now really motivated to learn more of what Lenka shares at my sessions. Not only do I feel better my overall health is improving. Lenka is encouraging me along a path that eventually could mean my medication could even be adjusted down. (at review by doctors) It really changes my view that getting older leads to a deterioration in health. I cant recommend enough this new program that has been implemented by the practice through Lenka. It is such an encouragement to take responsibility for my own health in an holistic way knowing that what I am being told is fact and does make a real difference.
