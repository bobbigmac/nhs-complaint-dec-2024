On the morning of Friday, 28/11/25, I completed a form to schedule a DOCTOR appointment for my 5-month-old son, who was having difficulty breathing, suffering from a chesty cough, and was congested. The receptionist called me to schedule an appointment with a practitioner instead of a doctor. I inquired why my son could not be seen by a DOCTOR, and her response was that my son's symptoms were minor, which left me shocked by her statement. Despite my concerns, I agreed to have my son examined by the practitioner. After conducting her checks, she diagnosed him with a chest infection and prescribed Amoxicillin to be taken three times a day for five days. By Saturday night, I had not observed any improvement in my son's condition; in fact, he had deteriorated to the point where he refused to drink his milk and had no wet nappies. On Sunday evening, I took my son to A&E, where the doctors informed me that he had bronchiolitis and that the antibiotics prescribed by my GP had only worsened his condition. My son has now been admitted to the hospital and is being fed through tubes. I am utterly heartbroken and appalled by the actions of the receptionist and the practitioner who were on duty on 28/11/25.
