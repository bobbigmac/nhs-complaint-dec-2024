Practice response:
Dear Dot Mc Thank you for your review. We are sorry to hear that you were dissatisfied with your recent interaction with the practice. GP practices are required to follow strict confidentiality, GDPR, and safeguarding guidance. This means we are unable to discuss any aspect of a patient’s care or arrange supplies unless the patient has given explicit consent, or the request is made by a formally nominated next of kin or authorised representative. In this situation, you were not recorded as next of kin, and the patient was not present to provide consent. As the patient is elderly and considered vulnerable, the receptionist acted appropriately by not seeking consent on the spot and by declining to proceed without the patient or her nominated representative making contact themselves. This decision was taken to protect the patient’s privacy, wishes, and wellbeing, in line with professional requirements. We would like to reassure you that once the patient, or her nominated next of kin, contacts the practice directly, we are always happy to assist with appropriate requests, including advice regarding continence supplies. For clarity, if urgent medical assistance is required, any person may contact the ambulance service on a patient’s behalf, and this does not require consent. We appreciate that caring for a vulnerable person can be stressful, and we thank those who show concern for patients. Please be assured that our staff aim to remain courteous at all times while also adhering to their legal and professional responsibilities. Kind Regards Yardley Medical Centre
