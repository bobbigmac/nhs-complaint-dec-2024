Author: E Y
Date: a month ago
Rating: 1 star
This clinic is a fascinating case study in how disorder can be institutionalised. The administrative staff demonstrate an impressive consistency in getting things wrong: appointments that mysteriously never existed, cancellations that were allegedly never made, and warning letters sent with great confidence to patients who did exactly what they were supposed to do. Responsibility, regrettably, does not appear to be part of the workflow. The doctors are a lottery. Some are competent and reassuring; others are clearly very new, frequently having to leave the room to consult a more senior colleague before making any decision. This arrangement manages to waste time while simultaneously eroding any sense of professionalism or confidence. As for management, they deserve special mention for their commitment to silence. Complaint forms can be filled in carefully and conscientiously, with the comforting assurance that they will be met with absolutely no response whatsoever. One can only assume these forms are filed directly into a drawer marked “Ignore”. The clinic’s sole redeeming feature is that if you begin phoning repeatedly from 8:20 a.m., with the persistence of someone who has nothing else to do, you may eventually secure a same-day appointment. Overall, this clinic offers not so much healthcare as an extended exercise in frustration, lowered expectations, and administrative disbelief.
