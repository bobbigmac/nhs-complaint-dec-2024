Haydock Public Information: NHS England requirements. All GP practices in England are required to maintain online booking functionality during working hours. However, this does not eliminate telephone booking options. NHS England guidance clearly states that "patients should feel confident that when they book or request an appointment, whether online, on the phone, or in person, their needs will be assessed swiftly and safely" . Furthermore, the Department of Health guidance indicates that practices must continue to offer multiple access routes including telephone, online, and in-person services to "ensure fair access to advice and treatment" . The government specifically implemented these changes to "end the 8am scramble" while maintaining telephone access, with the expectation that "GP phone lines across the country will be freed up" by having online options available .
