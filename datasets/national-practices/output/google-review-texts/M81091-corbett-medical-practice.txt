Author: Joe Haywood
Date: 4 months ago
Rating: 5 stars
I had reason to call The Corbett Medical Practice towards the end of last week. The Care Navigator who answered my call was extremely helpful and was able to offer me an appointment that afternoon. (I was actually offered an appointment later in the morning but could not make it.) The doctor I met was a model of professionalism and expertise and duly prescribed a treatment. She forewarned me that the pharmacy might not be able to supply it and explained the process to follow. The pharmacy was unable to supply it and so I returned to speak to the receptionist (as I had been advised to do). She was similarly friendly and helpful and assured me an amended prescription would go to the pharmacy that afternoon. It did, so the following morning I returned to the pharmacy and collected the treatment. This all worked because of their first class organisation and planning. It was also, of course, a reflection of the excellent people they have in place, be they medical or admin staff. It is, in short, an excellent GP practice.
