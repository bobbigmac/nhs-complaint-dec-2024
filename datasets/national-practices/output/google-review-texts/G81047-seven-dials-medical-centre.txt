Author: zain nabulsi

Rating: 1 star
I am very disappointed with my recent visit to the GP. I have been experiencing pain in my arm and forearm for over a month. For weeks I was taking Panadol, using Voltaren suppositories, and applying heat rub cream, but nothing helped, so my daughter booked an appointment for me. When we arrived, the doctor addressed me incorrectly as Mister instead of Mrs. My daughter corrected her and explained she was there to help translating for me. However, the doctor still did not seem to understand and kept asking me who I had brought with me. During the appointment, she spent the first minute telling me it was only a 10-minute appointment and then spent most of the time asking me what I, the patient, thought caused the pain. I kept saying I did not know, but she insisted so much that in the end, I just said maybe it was from carrying shopping bags. The doctor then noticed the hump on my neck, which I have had for many years due to my age (I am over 70), and she decided to blame my arm pain on this, even though it has been there for a decade without causing any problem before. She offered me no solution at all. No treatment plan, no tests, nothing, just waste of time. She just told me to keep massaging it. I am elderly – how am I supposed to massage my own arm that is in pain? If I lived alone without my daughter’s help, what would I do? Throughout the appointment, she spoke to me in a robotic way with a constant fake smile, which felt uncomfortable and weird. When my daughter showed dissatisfaction, the doctor asked her what she was expecting from the appointment?!!! This simply felt rude and unprofessional. Expectation of every single patient is to either find solution to the problem or to do something towards that at least … Overall, I left feeling dismissed, unheard, and with no help for my pain till today.
