The level of neglect is genuinely shocking. Thankfully, I trusted my instincts and took my child to a private clinic. And surprise, surprise: EVERYTHING Dr John said was wrong. Completely wrong. My son had pus-covered tonsils, an enlarged adenoid the size of a walnut, and a blocked ear — he was practically deaf on one side. His oxygen level was 93. For a nearly 3-year-old, it should be above 95. The private doctor said it was only a matter of time before things got seriously dangerous — the infection could have spread to the ears or even the brain. I want an explanation — a real one — as to why a doctor would treat patients with such complete lack of attention and care. It is an absolute shame to the profession. Someone who dismisses a struggling, sick child so casually should not be practising medicine at all. This behaviour is not just unprofessional; it is unsafe and unacceptable. My son is now on THREE antibiotics and additional medication, yet according to Dr John, there was “nothing wrong” and “no need for treatment.” Disgraceful. I will be taking this further.
