Practice response date: 8 months ago
Practice response:
Thanks for your review. We would like to clarify that we are not a walk-in service, and appointments are managed based on clinical need and availability. Contacting the surgery shortly before closing time does not guarantee a same-day appointment, and we cannot expect doctors to remain beyond their clinical hours. If you feel your baby is so unwell that you turn up at the surgery without an appointment and insist on being seen immediately, it may be more appropriate to attend A&E or contact NHS 111, where urgent care is available. We appreciate that caring for an unwell baby is stressful, and we’re glad your baby was eventually seen. However, we must manage expectations fairly for all patients. If our service no longer meets your needs, you are of course free to register with another GP practice.
