Very disappointed with the current service at Bethnal Green GP. While the practice itself is clean and the environment is well maintained, the overall standard of service has clearly declined compared to when Dr Harday and the previous administrative team were there. I’ve experienced repeated issues with referrals being delayed or handled incorrectly, causing unnecessary stress and wasted time. Booking appointments has become increasingly difficult, with poor communication and limited follow-up. There have also been instances where appointments were cancelled without proper notice. Patients’ contact numbers are on the system — if an appointment needs to be cancelled, we should be informed in advance, not told only upon arrival. This shows a lack of basic communication and respect for patients’ time. Additionally, the attitude of some administrative staff has been disappointing. Patients deserve professionalism, empathy, and clear communication, especially when dealing with health concerns. I hope management urgently reviews the referral process, appointment system, and administrative standards to restore the level of care this practice once provided.
