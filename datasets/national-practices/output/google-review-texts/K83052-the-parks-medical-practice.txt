I don’t think the other reviews are entirely fair and accurate. Appointments and same day booking phone calls are a general issue for the NHS, and due to a combination of over population, coupled with misuse of the system. The staff are simply running the practice as per policy. And to be honest, they’ve always been lovely considering the demeanour of people they have to deal with every day. I’m grateful that Roade medical practice is on my doorstep. I’ve lived in many different towns and villages, and have had some serious life-threatening issues. I get treated like everyone else - I have to join the queue, but from the very many different experiences I’ve had at other practices (one of which I ended up in hospital for over a week due to not being taken seriously) and the fact I’ve definitely used this practice more than the average person, they have always taken me seriously and with empathy and care. (Yes including Dr Banerjee!) This review is entirely my own thoughts and words. The 2 stars it currently has actually troubled me. It definitely deserves more respect than this! The only downfall and reason for 4 stars and not 5, is not due to the staff, but the location and unfortunately its wide knowledge that the practice is in need of relocation. The parking is a big issue. When you compare Roade practice to that of its sister practices, it is in much need of a bigger building, more facilities and modernisation. I’m sure it will happen with time, but for now the staff are just doing the best they can.
