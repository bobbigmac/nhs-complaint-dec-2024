My wife and I registered with the the practice when we moved to Langholm 18 months ago. Although our needs haven't been great thus far we have nevertheless been struck by friendliness of medical and reception staff and overall helpfulness. Last week I started to suffer from a very painful complication of Covid and, in these troubled times, I was gratified to be able to get through to the practice swiftly, spoken to sympathetically - and arrangements made for a call-back by one of the doctors, who then kindly arranged for a semi-urgent prescription, on the dot of 9am next morning.
