Author: CroissantKid
Date: 2 months ago
Rating: 4 stars
We've been registered with Burvill House for nearly 8 years, and I have never had trouble getting an appointment. Yes, the old system was a pain, but the new system allows you to put your request through quicker and for the doctors to triage you quicker. The doctors have been friendly and respectful and helped me through a myriad of health problems. A big thanks in particular to Dr Elder, Dr Bateman, Dr Tangang who have all been considerate and professional each time I have seen them. My only qualm is the hygiene of the practice - you have to use a touch screen to check in (considering people who visit are sick!). Additionally, to get out of the surgery, you also have to use touch buttons and also not wearing gloves when checking over babies (I also didn't see any visible hand sanitiser on the nurses desk either!) And there is no sanitiser near the check in touch screen every time I have visited! However, locking the surgery toilets feels like a sensible hygiene precaution as the surgery is located in a heavy footfall traffic area, I can imagine a lot of random people wanting to just use it!
