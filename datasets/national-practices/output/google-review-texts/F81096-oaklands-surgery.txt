I had an appointment today at Oaklands Surgery, and I must say—I was genuinely taken aback. The doctor I saw, a gentleman, did something truly rare in today’s world: he listened. He took the time to hear me out, examined me thoroughly, considered my concerns from multiple angles, and treated me with a level of care and attention that has, sadly, become uncommon. Far too often, doctors try to get rid of patients quickly by attributing everything to anxiety. Stomach pain? Anxiety. Headache? Anxiety. Arm pain? Anxiety. Numbness? Anxiety. A sore finger? Anxiety. No matter what you’re experiencing, the answer is often the same: anxiety. Goodbye. Next patient. It’s incredibly disheartening, especially when you know something isn’t right in your body and you just want someone to take it seriously. But this experience was the complete opposite. The doctor believed me. He didn’t judge, didn’t rush, and didn’t dismiss anything I said. He listened, took notes, and engaged fully with my concerns. I’m still in shock, even hours later, simply because such empathy and professionalism have become so rare. Although my health condition remains unresolved for now, what truly matters is that the doctor took real action. He referred me for various tests and investigations, and to different specialists, showing a genuine intention to get to the bottom of what’s going on. That alone means more than words can express—because it shows he cares, and that he’s committed to helping me find answers. I’ve been through numerous surgeries and practices in Canvey, and unfortunately, my experience there has often been disappointing. Coming from London, where many of the surgeries I attended were well-run and supportive, I noticed a stark contrast. In Canvey, I frequently encountered dismissiveness, misdiagnoses, and a sense that I was just another number. It felt more like a conveyor belt than a place of healing: “Next patient, next issue.” But Oaklands Surgery stands out in every possible way. From the very first interaction, the staff have been warm, polite, and genuinely helpful. The receptionists are incredibly kind—something that shouldn’t be rare, but sadly is. The doctors are compassionate, attentive, and thorough. The surgery is well-organised, and even repeat prescriptions are handled with impressive efficiency. In other places, I waited a week for a response. At Oaklands, my prescription is usually sent to the pharmacy within an hour. Truthfully, I’ve never been someone who enjoys going to the doctor. I prefer to be healthy, self-reliant, and active. But when health issues arise, the dread of dealing with the medical system—especially after negative experiences—can feel overwhelming. The thought of going to yet another doctor can make you feel even worse. That’s why I am so incredibly grateful to have found Oaklands. Since switching to this surgery and attending just a few appointments, along with monthly prescription requests, I have been consistently impressed. So much so that a part of me wants to keep this place a secret—because it’s that good. But they deserve full credit. Oaklands Surgery, and everyone who works there, truly deserves five stars. Thank you to today’s doctor for your time, your kindness, your referrals, and your professionalism. And thank you to the entire team—for your dedication, your care, and for making your patients feel truly seen and supported. You are doing an outstanding job. With heartfelt gratitude, Izabela
