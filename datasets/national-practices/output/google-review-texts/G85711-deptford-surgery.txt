Author: Miroslaw Mus
Date: Edited 3 weeks ago
Rating: 1 star
I am extremely dissatisfied with my experience with Dr Antony Bowden. Across multiple appointments, I felt that my symptoms were not properly assessed or investigated. I requested a physical examination and some basic checks, but these were declined without a clear clinical explanation. I also asked for follow-up regarding results that had been referred by another doctor within the same clinic, and this was not addressed. Instead, the conversation shifted away from the issue, leaving me uncertain whether my medical history and ongoing concerns had been properly reviewed. I raised concerns about possible thyroid-related symptoms. Rather than discussing appropriate diagnostic steps or arranging relevant blood tests, the advice focused primarily on general lifestyle suggestions such as reducing tea consumption or replacing white rice with brown rice. While lifestyle advice can be helpful in some situations, it did not feel proportionate to the symptoms I described, and it did not address my request for proper medical investigation. On more than one occasion, I requested a simple blood test to rule out underlying causes, and this was refused without a detailed explanation. I was not given clear clinical reasoning for why further checks were considered unnecessary. As a result, I had to book another appointment and wait an additional 3–4 weeks to seek further support, which caused unnecessary delay and prolonged uncertainty regarding my health. Additionally, appointments frequently started late, which added to the overall frustration and lack of confidence in the care provided. During consultations, I also felt a lack of clarity and confidence in the assessment of my condition. At times, it appeared that information was being searched during the appointment rather than explained with clear medical reasoning. While checking guidance is understandable, the overall interaction did not leave me feeling reassured or supported. Overall, I left feeling dismissed, unheard, and unsupported. I expect a doctor to engage thoroughly with patient concerns, provide clear explanations when declining requests, and ensure continuity of care—especially when another clinician has already initiated investigations. Unfortunately, this was not my experience, and for these reasons, I would not recommend this doctor. I have posted it just now because I found out he still keeps doing this not only for me but other patients I know and had “pleasure” to be his patient..
