Author: M M
Date: Edited 2 weeks ago
Rating: 1 star
I have been a patient at this surgery for over 30 years, dating back to when they were on the other side of Prince of Wales Road. It is incredibly disappointing to see how much the standards have fallen since the move to No. 52. The only reason we stayed so long was the clinical excellence of the long-standing doctors. Dr. Taylor was an absolute gentleman and was exceptionally good to my father; however, he and Dr. Major have now left the practice. It feels as though all the truly great, experienced doctors have either moved on or retired. While Dr. Pal and Dr. Postgate are still fantastic, it is now near impossible to get an appointment with either of them. The only remaining bright spot is Dr. Rose, who is lovely, but she cannot carry the whole practice alone. The biggest issue, however, is the reception staff. The attitude at the front desk is appalling the tone is often bullying and obstructive, creating a "my way or the highway" atmosphere. You are made to feel that if you don't submit to their rude temperament, you won't be allowed to see a doctor. It is stressful and completely unprofessional. Given the current CQC rating and the hostile environment created by the administrative staff, we have finally had enough. After three decades of loyalty, we will be moving to a GP surgery that values its patients and meets the required standards of care.
