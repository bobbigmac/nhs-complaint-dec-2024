Author: Darren Cross

Rating: 5 stars
Regarding the few negative comments, I completely understand that health matters are deeply personal and emotive, and patients are absolutely entitled to voice concerns when things go wrong. That said, with a practice serving well over 6000 patients yet only having around 32 reviews, it's worth remembering that online feedback often skews towards the negative experiences rather than reflecting the full picture. My own time as a patient at Downton Surgery has been thoroughly positive. I've seen three different doctors, the practice nurse, and various admin staff over the past year or so, and without exception they've all been professional, courteous, and polite. There were a couple of minor delays with prescriptions a few months ago, but each time a quick phone call sorted it out promptly and efficiently. Overall, I've felt well looked after and would happily recommend the surgery based on my experiences.
