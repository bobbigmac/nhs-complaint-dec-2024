I attended the surgery yesterday, 25/11 and was waiting outside for the place to open. The lady opened, dead on time, I went inside to reception and saw the same lady who, could not have been more pleasant, professional, helpful and caring to the point of almost doing back flips to help. Due to staff issues an appointment was made for the following day to see a nurse but I was also given a first appointment for that morning with the nurse practioner. At 8.30am, I was duly called in and again received what can only be described as, first class care, treatment and concern. Later in the day, I received a call from the first lady in reception with a revised appointment time, which would be better, due to staffing. This again was another demonstration of first remembering and second acting. I have not seen either of these ladies before but I am in love and can't wait to get back today. Many services today are not very good but, the treatment I received at the surgery was simply wonderful and I cannot say a big enough well done and thank you.

Author: Serenely Perplexed
Date: Edited a year ago
Rating: 1 star
Got noticeably less caring and patient-focused since COVID. Now more difficult to get appointments. Patients have to push and demand to get information, and are abandoned if they don't. Receptionists are routinely unfriendly, arrogant, rude, dismissive and unhelpful. Doctors listen less than ever and seem to rely on patient panels to measure their effectiveness, rather than individual patients. I've seen patients leaving the practice variously in tears and hot rage. During COVID I discovered a very frail old gentleman shivering with cold outside the locked doors, waiting to be admitted for his appointment. He said he'd been told to wait in his car despite not arriving in one. One of my elderly neighbours tells me that she has received zero assistance for many health issues and has given up asking help. Personally I can't think of a single occasion when I have been actively helped by this practice. Nurses, however, are the backbone of the practice. They are courteous, friendly, efficient and caring. Without them it would be a very dark place indeed.
