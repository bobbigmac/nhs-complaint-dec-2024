Author: Amelia Shaw

Rating: 1 star
I am extremely disappointed with the service provided by this practice. The lack of compassion and professionalism is evident in the way appointments are handled. Patients are instructed to call at 8:00 AM or 2:00 PM to book an appointment, only to be told that the slots are either full or limited to emergency cases. If my condition were truly an emergency, I would have gone to the emergency room instead of contacting the practice. Furthermore, I fail to understand the reasoning behind limiting appointments to specific hours, rather than allowing patients to schedule in advance at any time. I called at 1:56 PM and was told I must call back at 2:00 PM. Upon doing so, I was informed that my case was not considered an emergency, and I was unable to book an appointment. This system is entirely inefficient and frustrating. Additionally, the claim that the practice addresses patient concerns within 24 hours is misleading. In reality, patients are forced to wait weeks or even months for an appointment, as it is nearly impossible to book in advance. The practice only offers same-day appointments, which are restricted to either the morning or afternoon—neither of which can be guaranteed to have availability. To make matters worse, the staff's attitude is consistently rude and disrespectful. It feels as though patients are required to beg for an appointment or even their medication. This is especially concerning given that this practice is involved in training future GPs. It is disheartening to see such a lack of empathy and professionalism in a training environment. I hope this feedback is taken seriously, as the current system is failing both patients and the NHS.
