If other medical centres have availability, I’d honestly recommend avoiding this one. I’ve tried using online system many times to see a GP, but almost every time they redirect me to the Urgent Treatment Centre, A&E, or the pharmacy thro msg. There’s barely any chance to actually see or speak to a GP. The person replying isn’t rude, but they only say what they want to say. After they send their message, you can’t reply or clarify anything. If you try to ask again, you have to submit a brand-new request — and with the daily quota only opening at 7am, it’s extremely hard to get through. Overall, the system feels like it’s set up to avoid letting patients access a GP at all.
