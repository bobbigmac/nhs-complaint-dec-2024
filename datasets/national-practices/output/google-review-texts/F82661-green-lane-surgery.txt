Author: Nikki J

I rarely leave reviews, but I feel compelled to share my experience with this GP surgery. I’m naturally a friendly and upbeat person, but from my very first appointment I felt uncomfortable. The doctor seemed to look at me strangely, and when I explained my symptoms, she appeared to make assumptions rather than properly examining or investigating them. I even had to ask whether she was going to check instead of guess, which didn’t inspire confidence. Overall, her manner was cold and unapproachable. More recently, I’ve been struggling with work-related stress and wanted to speak directly with a GP. When I contacted reception to book an appointment, I was told to speak to talking therapies instead. While I understand they can be helpful, I specifically requested to speak with a doctor. The same doctor then called me unexpectedly. I was already feeling vulnerable, and her abrupt introduction immediately put me on edge. When I questioned her tone and expressed that patients should be spoken to with care, she became defensive and didn’t acknowledge my concerns. I ended the call feeling worse than before. Following this, I received a letter from her which, in my view, did not accurately reflect the situation. It also warned that I could be removed from the practice if similar situations occurred again, which I found both unfair and upsetting. I later tried to book an appointment with a different doctor, but the receptionist again questioned why I needed it and redirected me to talking therapies. During the call, which lasted around six minutes, I was told that other patients were waiting and asked bluntly whether I wanted the appointment or not. At that point, I declined, as I no longer felt comfortable engaging with the practice. In my experience, both the doctor’s attitude and the way reception handles patients are concerning. I did not feel listened to, supported, or treated with empathy at any stage. Unfortunately, I would not recommend this surgery based on my experience.
