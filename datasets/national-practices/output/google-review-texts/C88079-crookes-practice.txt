I have been using this practice for nearly two years, and unfortunately, the experience has been very disappointing due to a total lack of continuity in care. In the time I have been a patient here, I have only managed to have one face-to-face appointment. After that doctor went on sick leave, I was passed to another physician with no smooth transition.
