If I could give zero stars I would. My 75 year old mum was previously a patient here but thankfully I have moved her elsewhere. The serious neglect she received from this surgery is unforgivable. She was extremely ill at home and called the doctors asking if she could have a telephone consultation with a doctor or for a home visit because she felt too ill to drive into the surgery because she couldn't stand up due to balance issues and she had a severe headache causing her to not be able to focus. She was told by the receptionist that her symptoms sounded like she had a virus so to stay at home and there was no doctor available to visit and to call if no better the next day. She called the next day and then when explaining her symptoms again she was told she may have vertigo by the receptionist… since when has it been acceptable for a receptionist to make a diagnosis?! This was then finally followed by a doctor calling her telling her that yes she thought she had vertigo too. She prescribed her tablets for vertigo and I went to get these for my mum from the rude pharmacists below 168 medical. When I dropped the tablets off to my mum I could see that she really wasn’t well and her balance issues worried me. The reception staff wouldn’t speak to me on the telephone so mum had to speak to them and again she explained her symptoms and they brushed it off as vertigo… no further message was passed on to any doctor. I stayed with mum all morning and by lunchtime I decided to call 111 as she had woken from a sleep and her headache was worse and her right eye had begun to droop slightly. A consultant called me and then late afternoon a doctor was sent from another surgery to see my mum. From my basic medical experience (I work as airline crew) I said to the on call doctor I feel this is a stroke and the doctor on call said that she was glad to have seen my mum face to face because she too thought that perhaps it may be a stroke and we should get mum to A&E. We had to get my mum down the stairs on her bum into my car (because she couldn’t stand due to balance issues) the ambulance we were told would be at least 6 hours and I was not willing to wait any longer. As soon as my mum finally received her scan it was confirmed she’d had a stroke… she’d in fact had 3 by this point and all because she’d been left unseen by her doctor at 168 Medical. She spent two weeks at Southmead Stroke Unit and I’m lucky she is still here. 168 Medical should be utterly ashamed of themselves for their complete lack of care and attention to the information patients are telling them. As I say I have moved my mum to another practice now and they have been amazing with her.
