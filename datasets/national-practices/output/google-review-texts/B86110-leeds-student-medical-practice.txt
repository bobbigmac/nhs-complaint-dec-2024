Author: Ye Lucia

The system is Chinese-friendly, offering free translation services for both online and offline appointments. Appointments can be made at 8 AM, and you can see a doctor as early as 9 AM.
Practice response date: a week ago
Practice response:
Dear Lucia, Thank you for your review! We are pleased you shared this information. We are committed to providing friendly and accessible healthcare for patients from different language backgrounds. For patients who need assistance, we can arrange a free interpreter for both online and in-person appointments. Appointments can be requested from 8 am, and the earliest doctors’ appointments are usually from 9 am. Thank you for your support.
