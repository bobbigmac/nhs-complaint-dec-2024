I've been with this practice for 63yrs & would have said it was a great practice. But like many other practices it's changed since covid & hasn't returned to how it was previously. The majority of GP's are fantastic as are the reception staff. The appointment system could be improved on...... If you make the appointment yourself on the app, you have to clearly state if you want a F2F or a call on the appointment notes. But when the practice send you a reminder they say "you have a F2F/Tel appointment" it would be better if they clearly stated if the appointment is F2F or a call. I myself thought I had a F2F & turned up at the practice & was told it was a call, but I did see the GP that was supposed to be calling me. I know of a few other patients that's had the same issue. A friend thought he had a call & when there was no call, he contacted the practice & was told it was a F2F. It would be so much easier to advise patients which type of appointment they have booked.
