Six years ago I wrote that this was "a failing Medical Practice which sets very low targets and failed to achieve them MOST of the time. Good Doctors (apparently) if you can get beyond the "trained medical receptionists" aka Rottweilers. Our question? IF it is SO easy to train "Medical receptionists" why not train a few GP's too - it can't take that long either, right? If you have mental health issues or issues of a personal sensitive nature AVOID the receptionists who PRIORITISE if/when you MAY "get a 'phone call" from a GP - they WILL seek to INTERROGATE you - and some are insensitive!! Also, be aware that, if you ring first thing in the morning to TRY to get an appointment, it can take EIGHT HOURS before a GP will even deign to call you - so you won't be getting an appointment THAT day". That, alone, is transformative in our relationship with the surgery. Dr Paula, especially, has handled my wife's ongoing medical concerns sensitively and thoroughly with compassion and understanding. She is part of the new collegiate approach among the GP's at the Practice. Being ill is never any fun, having a surgery that you can trust is vital - and the new team members are helping us to trust HMP again.
