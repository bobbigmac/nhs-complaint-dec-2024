Worst, most disorganised pharmacy I have ever had the displeasure of dealing with. Tried to double charge me for a prescription because their hand written system of marking prescriptions as paid went wrong Txt msgs for prescriptions being ready don't work if you order through the NHS app - and it take them about 20 minutes to find the prescription when you do go to collect. The surgery and pharmacy are not part of the national online prescription service that allows home delivery via 3rd party businesses. They refused to sell asprin for use in pregnancy, despite it being used on the advice of the Consultant and Midwife - they wanted a prescription for it - good idea, lets waste more NHS money by having prescriptions for asprin... which is sold at supermarkets!! really... they need to get a grip. I'm sure many of the local old people are perfectly happy with their slow service and antiquated systems and out of date medical opinions, but they could really do with a kick up the behind and dragging up a few levels. Just a shame there isn't really any alternative locally.
