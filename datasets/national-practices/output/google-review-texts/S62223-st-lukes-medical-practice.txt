I've had mixed experiences with this practice. Due to both my family's needs and my own, I've had to interact with the GPs here quite a lot. The partner GPs seem to genuinely want to help (Dr Jack is genuinely a wonderful and caring GP, and I know she would help if she could) but are unfortunately restricted by the rigid "NHS Lanarkshire way" of doing things. In contrast, the salaried GPs have been consistently unhelpful. The level of care you receive depends entirely on who you see and how much you push for the treatment you need. The reception staff are similarly inconsistent. Alison, in particular, is unhelpful, unfriendly, and doesn’t even make eye contact to even greet you, she has no empathy and makes you feel like a massive inconvenience to her day. On multiple occasions, she has attempted to give medical advice, even recommending an over-the-counter medication to my partner-despite him being severely allergic to it. Overall, this practice requires persistence from patients to receive adequate care, and the professionalism of some staff members leaves much to be desired.
