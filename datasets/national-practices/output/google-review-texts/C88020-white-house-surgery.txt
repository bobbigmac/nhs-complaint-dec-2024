Author: Niamh Pearce

Rating: 3 stars
Staff on reception are polite, helpful, and clear. I've always got through to reception very promptly and been called promptly after submitting a form. However, despite having several issues I've been trying to arrange appointments for, I've only just managed to arrange one. Appointment booking system is confusing and inconvenient - you're asked to fill in a form online, but they will then decide for you if it's an urgent request, and won't take it forward if it is and they don't have appointments on the day. They don't retain the form, so you have to resubmit it for another chance at an appointment, which is deeply inconvenient and time consuming if you've written a lot. Functionally, you need to call at 8am on the day you want an appointment. This is a bit ridiculous for ongoing but 'urgent' conditions, especially for busy people who need to book appointments in advance to clear their calendars. Furthermore, you need to have a phone call with them regardless of having submitted a form, so there's no point unless there's a long phone queue.
