I've only recently started to use this medical centre, although I am very impressed. The first thing I noticed was how well maintained and clean the clinic apeared to be. It didnt take too long to get an appointment and reception staff seemed helpful and polite. The first visit to see a doctor went very smoothly and waiting time in the clinic was decent. I got the impression that this doctor had a lot on his plate workwise and I could see the stress on his face. Unfortuantely, thats just the way things are with NHS at the moment. Medical staff are stretched to the brink and lack of funding for most resources. However, this doctor did a great job, despite the pressure he seemed to be under, made me feel at ease, very polite and helpful and got me everything I needed swiftly, including a set of external appointments. Theres around a 40% shortage of doctors and nurses, so you can understand how stressful the job can be for them at times. I had a second follow up with a different doctor over the phone, again, very polite, helpful and very competant. The nursing staff that dealt with my wound where great also and helped get an important appointment set up very swiftly. I've not had to visit a medical centre for nearly 2 decades, so I was really nervous with my first appointment, but all staff involved made me feel at ease and minimised the stress that ensues when we get ill.
