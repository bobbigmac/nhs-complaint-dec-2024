Misleading pricing & low quality service — ended up paying ~£300 for a basic blood test!! I’m extremely disappointed with how pricing is communicated. On the phone, I was told a basic blood test (full blood count) would cost £60. However, when I arrived, I was charged an additional £180 for a GP consultation, which felt unnecessary just to have blood taken. On top of that, I was then told there would be extra lab fees for sending the sample to an external laboratory — around £120+ more. In total, this came to roughly £300 for a very standard blood test. They on purpose didn’t tell all of these on the phone and I couldn’t expect that the simple blood raw is £180 and plus the fee of the lab is something separate and on top of that! This feels like a bait-and-switch: a low quote upfront, then extra charges once you’ve booked and shown up. To make it worse, the blood draw itself was a nightmare: very old man taking my blood missed the vein three times (he clearly doesn’t know how to take blood), it was painful, left my arm scratched/bruised, and it was a genuinely unpleasant experience. It felt unprofessional and careless. Avoid at all costs!!! Don’t fall for this scam After this awful experience, I found a way better option. If you just need a standard blood test, order Medichecks instead — it’s about 3x cheaper, and the blood draw is only £35 (or you can use a home kit and arrange it yourself).
