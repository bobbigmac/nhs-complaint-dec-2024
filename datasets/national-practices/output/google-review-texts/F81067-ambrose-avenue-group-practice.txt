Very poor experience when I sought help with an eye infection. I was told that the surgery didn't deal with minor eye problems which they had outsourced to a clinic at Specsavers and that I would have to go there. So I went to the Specsavers in Sainsbury's where they diagnosed a style under my lower eyelid. So far, so good. However, when it came to the treatment I was told I would have to go to a chemist and buy specific eye drops, they didn't give me a prescription for this medication. To make matters worse, when I went to Boots they didn't have the drops and gave me a tube of ointment instead and I had to pay nearly £10 for it. Because I was due to go into hospital within days for knee replacement surgery I didn't have time to contact the surgery to query all this. I knew that the operation might not go ahead if I still had an infection. So, I'm not sure what's going on here - it seems like privatisation by the back door and I don't know if anyone else has had the same sort of experience. I still haven't been able to contact the surgery because for the last 3 weeks I've been recovering from the knee replacement operation and haven't been well enough to make a complaint.
