Practice response date: 5 days ago
Practice response:
Thank you for taking the time to share your experience. I am very sorry to hear how frustrated and unsupported you have felt when trying to access care. This is not the standard we aim to provide, and we appreciate the opportunity to respond to your concerns. You raised an important issue regarding telephone access. Unfortunately, due to clinical indemnity and NHS regulations, we cannot provide consultations or medical advice to patients using non‑UK or overseas telephone numbers. This is because we must be certain that patients are physically within the UK when receiving medical advice, and we are legally indemnified only to treat patients located here. We understand how challenging it is when appointments are fully booked, especially when you are unwell. Demand for GP appointments has been extremely high, and at times this leads to longer waits than we would like. If you’d like to get in touch with me directly by phone on 01480 483100 or email at acorn.enquiries@nhs.net we can investigate and support you further. Yours sincerely Victoria Pilkington Practice Manager
