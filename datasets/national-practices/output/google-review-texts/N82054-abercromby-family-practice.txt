Author: Philomène Uwamaliya

Rating: 5 stars
I would like to share my appreciation for this GP practice, which has cared for my mum for many years. My recent frustrations are not related to the practice or its staff. On the contrary, they have consistently helped us understand what to do when appointments are difficult to access. A lot has changed over the years, and government decisions have made things increasingly challenging for both patients and doctors. I would encourage anyone registered with this practice to speak politely with staff, ask questions, and seek to understand the wider issues at play. It may also be helpful to contact your MP to advocate for more resources for GP services and the NHS, rather than allowing ourselves to feel overwhelmed by systemic problems that practice staff cannot change. I have always found all members of staff to be compassionate and caring. Whenever I raise an issue, I approach them respectfully, and they always make an effort to assist. I am truly grateful for the care they provide to my mum. One suggestion I would gently offer when resources allow is for the practice to consider providing some support or information for carers, while fully recognising that patient care must remain the priority. Thank you to all the staff, from the bottom of my heart.
