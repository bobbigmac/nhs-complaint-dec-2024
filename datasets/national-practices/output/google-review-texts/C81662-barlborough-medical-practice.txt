Practice response date: a month ago
Practice response:
Hi, I’m sorry to hear you’ve had issues with your current GP and are also not happy with your initial contact with us. If you contact the surgery on ddicb.bmp@nhs.net our Practice Manager will investigate the specifics of your issue fully for you. As a guide, we provide any prospective patient with details on how they can register as a patient with us (online being the quickest process). Upon basic details being provided (to confirm that they live within our practice boundary), we would then be able register that person as our patient, request access to their medical records and provide them with the required emergency/non-emergency care. At no time would we refuse registration or decline access to our services. However, we do not have the capacity to provide emergency services to anyone who has not yet completed the registration process. We fully understand and appreciate how stressful it can be at times accessing support services, and our guidance as a Primary care provider is to contact 111/999 where a person is unable to access support from their existing GP surgery.
