I rarely leave negative reviews, but after weeks of ongoing issues, with the Glascote clinic, I feel it’s important to share my experience. I contact my GP because I have health concerns, not for convenience or leisure. Over the past three weeks, I have submitted multiple online requests that simply disappeared — despite receiving confirmation emails, there was later “nothing on the system” under my name. I repeatedly chased these requests by email and phone, only to be promised call-backs that never happened. When calling the reception, the attitude has frequently been dismissive, sarcastic, and unprofessional. I have been spoken to with superiority and irony, questioned for calling at all, and made to feel like an inconvenience rather than a patient. On more than one occasion, the phone was abruptly disconnected while I was still speaking, with the assumption that I had finished. This is basic courtesy, and it is seriously lacking. When I finally managed to secure an appointment, the doctor was inattentive and recorded incorrect information in my medical notes. Correcting this error has proven nearly impossible — despite calling daily, being reassured that the issue would be resolved and emailed to me, nothing has been done. These mistakes have had real consequences for my work and wellbeing. I am always polite, calm, and respectful when I call. I try to be patient and understanding. But patience should not be mistaken for tolerance of repeated negligence and poor treatment. This level of disorganisation, lack of follow-through, and disregard for patients is unacceptable — especially within an NHS-funded service. Experiences like this are exactly why people end up overwhelmed and forced to turn to urgent care. Patients wait weeks for appointments, only to be treated with carelessness and indifference once they finally get one. I sincerely hope this practice reflects on how patients are spoken to, how requests are handled, and how responsibility is taken when mistakes are made. Patients deserve better.
