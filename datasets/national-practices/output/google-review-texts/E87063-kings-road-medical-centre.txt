Rating: 1 star
I have had an extremely distressing experience at this practice. I felt treated as a number rather than as a human being. Despite providing evidence that my most recent test in my home country was positive for a very high-risk virus associated with cancer, both the nurse and the GP refused to repeat the test. My medical records clearly show that I am in a higher-risk category: two women in my family have died from this condition, and over the past 15 years I have had both positive and negative test results. Although my last test with this GP was negative, that was almost nine months ago. Given my medical history (I had already two surgeries related to this issue) and risk factors, it is reasonable to consider that circumstances may change and that closer monitoring may be appropriate. I was told that protocol requires waiting five years before repeating the test. However, applying a blanket protocol without properly considering an individual’s documented medical history feels inappropriate and potentially unsafe. Clinical guidelines should allow for professional judgment and exceptions when a patient’s risk profile clearly justifies further investigation. I strongly believe that doctors should be able to exercise discretion in order to protect their patients’ health and, potentially, their lives.
