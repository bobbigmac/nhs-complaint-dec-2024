This isn’t something I usually like to do, as I don’t enjoy spreading negativity—especially towards the NHS, which I respect deeply, along with the hard work of its staff. However, after being with this surgery for nearly two years, I feel compelled to speak up, as it has been the worst experience I’ve had with a healthcare provider. While the doctors I’ve seen have generally been kind and well-meaning, I’ve found that very little has actually been resolved with my health. There has been a consistent pattern of treating symptoms in isolation rather than taking the time to investigate or address the root cause, despite me raising this concern multiple times. The reception staff, unfortunately, have made things significantly worse. I’ve been made to feel stupid and dismissed for asking completely normal, valid questions—questions that anyone would ask when trying to manage their own healthcare. What’s worse is that it’s often been them who have made errors, yet rather than acknowledge their mistakes, they’ve passed the blame or sent me in circles, wasting my time and delaying any progress in getting things sorted. If you’re looking for a GP practice where you feel welcomed, respected, and actually helped, I would strongly advise against signing up here. The lack of empathy and professionalism, particularly at the front desk, has been exhausting and disheartening.
