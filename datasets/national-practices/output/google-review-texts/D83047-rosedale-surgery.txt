Practice response:
Good morning, Thank you for your feedback. Our online appointment forms close only when we reach full capacity for the day. However, patients can still contact the surgery by phone if they require a same-day appointment. These requests are reviewed by a clinician, who will assess whether an urgent same-day appointment is needed or if it can safely wait until the next day. To help manage call volumes and ensure patients receive timely advice, we work with CallEast, a service provided by the East of England Ambulance Service. CallEast staff are trained emergency call handlers who help direct patients to the most appropriate care. If you select the appointment option when calling, your call may be directed to them. While they do not have access to medical records or book appointments directly, they can complete a request form on your behalf, which is then triaged by our clinical team at the surgery. We’re sorry to hear that your recent experience hasn’t met expectations. If you would like to discuss your concerns further, please don’t hesitate to contact the surgery and ask to speak with the Practice Manager.
