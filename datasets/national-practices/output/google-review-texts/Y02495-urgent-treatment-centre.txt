Author: Colin Whitehead
Date: a year ago
Rating: 5 stars
Visited the Breast Screening department in the Treatment Centre in May 2023 with my wife. The department was very caring for my disabled partner and gave her time to get herself organised . Do be aware that appointments can be several hours long and partners / drivers / friends are not allowed into the treatment rooms but if staying have to wait in the department reception area waiting room. They can communicate with patients by mobile phone and can leave for food or 'fresh air' etc. - there are food facilities , toilets etc by the main reception area as you enter the Treatment Centre . Do be aware that parking is very limited directly in front of the Treatment Centre but there is a large 'Drop off' parking area with benches for people to wait ( but it is open to the elements ! ) A 'pay as you leave' scheme operates throughout the hospital and a ticket machine is in the foyer of the Treatment Centre . A small bank loan may be needed to pay for your visit .. mine was £4.50 for about 3 hours!
