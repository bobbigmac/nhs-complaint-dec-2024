One-star due to repeated poor experiences with the reception team and how rude and unprofessional they are over the phone. I phoned the practice because I am unable to access my health records via the NHS app and received error code CID7021, which states that my GP surgery has not granted permission for online access, and therefore, I need to contact them. I previously had full access with no issues and there have been no changes to my personal details. After waiting in the phone queue for over 30 minutes, the receptionist was dismissive and barely let me speak to explain the issue I am having. She told me to submit a Data Subject Access Request (DSAR), without any details about where to find the details for this or who to email the request to, which is not relevant to restoring NHS app access. I did not ask for COPIES of my Medical Records, I asked for ONLINE ACCESS to them, which I have been able to access easily on the NHS app before. When I tried to explain this and ask for the issue to be passed to the appropriate admin staff, she hung up whilst I was still speaking. This is not an isolated incident and reflects a consistent lack of helpfulness and professionalism from the reception team. Patients should not be given incorrect information or brushed off when trying to access services they are entitled to, and I felt she barely listened to what I was asking for.
