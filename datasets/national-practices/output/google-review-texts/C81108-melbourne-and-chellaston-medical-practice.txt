I have been with the practice for over 20 years, alot of changes have taken place during this time. The practice gets a lot of bad pressure/reviews. The practice works hard to accommodate a growing number of patients, and they have some excellent GP's who care for there patients, who take time to understand what that patients wants and needs are. The reception team are brilliant and really helpful. Yesterday I had an appointment and got stuck in traffic due to roadworks, I called ahead so the GP was aware and it enabled me to have my appointment. I saw a nurse for a first time, for an annual check up and she called me to follow up. From the back office staff, reception, prescription team to the GP's and Practice Manager, they really do work hard to deliver for there patients. Not an easy task at times. A very high user of there services.
