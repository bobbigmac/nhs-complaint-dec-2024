Author: Kim Sandford
Date: 6 months ago
Rating: 1 star
I was very disappointed with the level of care and support I received from this practice following my recent operation. After being discharged from hospital on Thursday, I was advised that I required daily dressings for an open wound and was instructed to arrange this through my GP surgery. When I contacted the practice, I was informed in a very abrupt manner that no appointments were available for two weeks, despite the urgency of the situation. I was then told that dressing equipment would be ordered for me so that I could manage the wound care myself, and that it would be available to collect at 6pm the same day. However, when I arrived, I was told that no equipment was available after all. I was advised that I would need to either wait two days or contact local pharmacies myself to try and obtain the necessary supplies, which were those specifically recommended by the hospital (GP was notified by the hospital of this). Throughout these interactions, I found the reception team to be unhelpful and lacking in empathy. Their only suggestions were to ring 111 or to contact the hospital again, despite the hospital having made it clear that my follow-up wound care should be provided by my GP. This experience left me feeling unsupported and placed unnecessary stress on me at a time when I was recovering from surgery and needed appropriate aftercare. I feel the surgery failed in its duty to provide the level of care expected for a post-operative patient.
Practice response date: 5 months ago
Practice response:
Thank you for your feedback. We’re sorry that aspects of your experience left you feeling unsupported. It seems we were able to arrange a series of dressing appointments for you at short notice, but having listened back to your phone call I agree that communication could have been handled better and I’ve addressing this directly with our Reception team. If you would find it helpful to discuss your concerns in more detail, please contact our Practice Manager Danielle Adams (danielle.adams7@nhs.net) and we will happily arrange this. Dr Smith.
