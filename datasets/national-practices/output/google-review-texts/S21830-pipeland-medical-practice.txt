Practice response date: 4 days ago
Practice response:
We’re sorry to read that you felt frustrated when contacting the practice. Like most GP practices, we do not operate a system where every patient can immediately speak directly to a doctor on demand. Our reception team ask questions so that urgent problems can be prioritised and patients can be directed to the most appropriate member of the clinical team. This system exists to ensure patients with the most urgent needs are seen or called back first. Doctors may also decline medication requests if they believe the prescription would not be appropriate or safe. Clinical decisions cannot be discussed in detail on a public forum, but they are always made with patient safety in mind. We would also clarify that prescriptions do not need to be requested solely by telephone. Patients can request repeat prescriptions through local pharmacies, written requests to the practice, or through the NHS App, which many of our patients use successfully. Finally, we would ask that feedback about the practice avoids language that could be considered disrespectful toward staff. Our reception team work extremely hard to support patients and help manage a very large volume of requests every day. If you would like us to review your experience properly, please contact the practice manager directly so we can look into the circumstances and try to help.
