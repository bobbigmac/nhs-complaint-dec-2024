I have had several concerning experiences with this surgery over time, which ultimately led my family to register elsewhere. On one occasion, I attended multiple appointments after feeling unwell. I was reassured that everything was fine, and at one point was advised to stop taking drugs, despite explaining that I do not take drugs or smoke. Two days later, I was admitted to hospital for 17 days with a kidney infection. More recently, my son had blood in his urine. We submitted a sample at the surgery but did not receive any follow-up. After a week, I called to ask for the results and was told everything was fine, which was why no one had contacted us. This lack of communication was very worrying. I also took my daughter to have her toenails checked as they had turned black. During the appointment, cancer was mentioned, which was extremely distressing to us. When I asked about a referral to a specialist, I was told it would take over three months as it was not considered a priority. I chose to seek a private opinion the next day, where I was reassured that she was too young for toenail cancer and that the discoloration was simply dye from black socks, which was easily removed. Based on these experiences, we felt we no longer had confidence in the care and communication at this surgery and decided to move to a different practice.
