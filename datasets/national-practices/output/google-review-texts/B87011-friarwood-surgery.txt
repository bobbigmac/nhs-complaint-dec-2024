Author: Claire Hills
Date: a week ago
Rating: 5 stars
I registered my dad at Friarwood Surgery about 18 months ago when he moved to the area & I can say, hand on heart, it has been one of the best decisions we’ve made for his health & wellbeing. Doctor Hudson is like the old school GP I remember having when I was growing up but have long since thought didn’t exist anymore. A GP who knows her patients & who is interested in getting to know them as a whole. She treats us all with respect & listens to our concerns & is very thorough in her care. If I need to call the surgery on the phone I might have to wait to get through, but when I do speak to someone I always find the staff very willing to help. In person, staff are kind & professional & don’t mind my asking questions. In my experience the nursing team always seem to be running to good time, but yet I never feel like they are rushing us in and out. I spend more time at Friarwood Surgery than I do my own, & if I lived closer, I’d consider transferring there myself!
