Author: Francesca Reynolds
Date: a month ago
Rating: 1 star
I am extremely disappointed with the lack of care and support from Swan Practice during a critical time for my newborn. Following my planned C-section at Stoke Mandeville, my baby sustained a cut to her head. Hospital doctors specifically advised us to book an appointment with our GP to have this checked. This request was also formally sent to the practice in writing while we were still under hospital care. Despite this, the surgery refused to even discuss the issue until my baby was registered. Once registration was completed, I submitted an appointment request via the online triage system. Instead of being seen, I received a text message redirecting me back to the midwives and advising that the issue should be checked before discharge from their care. This directly contradicts the advice we were given by a hospital doctor, who was clear that our GP should assess the injury. The experience felt dismissive and uncaring, as though every effort was made to avoid seeing us at all. At a time when parents should be able to rely on their GP practice for basic care and reassurance, we were left feeling ignored and unsupported. If I could give no stars for a review I would.

Author: T J

Rating: 1 star
DO NOT RING FOR AN URGENT APPOINTMENT. I have a lot of respect for the NHS; we’re very lucky to have it. However, since the Swan Practice introduced this online booking system, it’s been an utterly useless GP service. On their website, it says to book an appointment, use the online form OR call (provides a number). Now, if you call, you’ll wait in a queue for 20-40 mins before someone picks up to tell you they cannot book appointments and you should use the online form! What a waste of time and money. The only thing they can do is fill in the form for you. They’ve completely removed the ability to urgently get an appointment. The online form is too long, possibly confusing for non-tech savvy people and not accessibility friendly. The practise’s website should say ‘Please fill in the online booking form or, if you have trouble completing it, or have other accessibility requirements, please ring the number and a staff member can do it for you. A couple of Docs are great and the staff are generally fine (if you ever physically get there), but bedside manner needs improving.
