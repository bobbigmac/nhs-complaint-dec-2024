Practice response date: a month ago
Practice response:
Good afternoon, Georgina I am sorry to read about your recent experiences and that you feel the service has declined. That’s certainly not the standard of care or communication we aim to provide. We understand how frustrating it can be to face long telephone waiting times and difficulties securing appointments, particularly when it may appear that the practice is quiet. Appointment availability can sometimes differ from what is visible in the waiting area due to telephone consultations, online bookings, and clinicians working on urgent or administrative cases. However, we fully appreciate how this can feel from a patient’s perspective. We’re also very sorry to hear about the issues you’ve encountered with repeat prescriptions. As a Type 1 diabetic, we completely understand that insulin requirements can fluctuate — especially during and after pregnancy, illness, or periods of insulin resistance. Prescription requests are sometimes reviewed against issue dates to ensure patient safety, but we recognise that individual clinical needs vary. We would encourage you to contact the practice and book an appointment with our pharmacy team so we can review your medication requirements in more detail and ensure they are aligned with your current needs. Thank you for your kind comments about our pharmacist and our pre-natal and midwifery teams. We will make sure this positive feedback is passed on to them, as it means a great deal to our staff to know when they’ve made a difference. We value all feedback — both positive and constructive — as it helps us improve. If you would be willing to discuss your concerns further, please contact the practice and ask to speak with myself so I can look into your experience more closely. Thank you again for sharing your thoughts. Karen Cooper-Sollom Patient Liaison Officer
