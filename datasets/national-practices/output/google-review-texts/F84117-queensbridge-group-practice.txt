Author: Elizabeth Hay
Date: Edited 4 days ago
Rating: 1 star
The Surgery appears to be incapable of offering a service or support to anyone with a voice impairment. I have access to both email and text and I am literate in English and I have been repeatedly told that accessing my file "triggers" an instruction NOT TO TELEPHONE ME and still I continue to be caused great anxiety by telephone calls I cannot answer. I have had a voice impairment since 2008 ...fifteen years to discover and implement a way of supporting my communication. In fact rather than improving the situation in that time, my situation has worsened as it is no longer possible to use email to contact the surgery. The poor "Klinik" system which purports to offer a choice of telephone or written contact is often simply ignored. The surgery has a duty to provide reasonable adjustments under equalities' legislation. Why is it OK to exclude voice impairments and make entirely token gestures which are not fit for purpose? This problem seems endemic in the NHS generally but for the last 15 years I have hoped that the Queensbridge service would find a workable solution.

Please rest assured that we take your concerns seriously and are committed to providing reasonable adjustments. We will reach out to arrange a meeting to ensure that we can improve things going forward. Once again, we apologise for any inconvenience caused and appreciate your patience as we work towards a better solution. Kind Regards Practice Manager
