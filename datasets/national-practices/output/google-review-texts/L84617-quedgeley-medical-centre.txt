Practice response date: 2 months ago
Practice response:
Thank you for taking the time to share your feedback, Mr Smith. We are sorry to hear that you are dissatisfied with your recent experience of our Practice. It is standard procedure that we do not contact patients to inform them of blood or BP results unless there is anything of concern or there is any follow up required. We ask patients to either check their results via online services (e.g., the NHS App) or to call us to enquire about results. Unfortunately, we simply do not have the capacity to contact every patient for every results we receive. This should have been explained to you when you attended for your blood test and we are sorry if this was not the case. With regards to the physio call; we think this was a miscommunication from the receptionist you spoke with this morning. What was meant was that there was nothing on your notes to show that the physio had attempted to call you at the time you contacted us; however, you were on the list of patients for the physio to call today. We can see that you were indeed called shortly before 1pm. We apologise that this call was not in the morning as you were advised; unfortunately, the physio clinic was running slightly late today. We trust this response provides some explanation and that your future experiences with the Practice are more positive.
