Author: Celly

Rating: 1 star
It has taken me a few days to digest and reflect on this appointment. And now that my emotions are out of the way I can write a proper review. I am aware how hard things are for the NHS at the moment and I honestly did not have super high expectations but what happened during this appointment left me stunned and very sad actually. I will start to say that I am 8 weeks post partum, and over a month ago I visited my GP about severe sharp pain in my pelvis during night time which would wake me up. I was given a referral letter to attempt to go privately & also was booked for an appointment to see the GPs physiotherapist (booked for 10th march). Due to my severe pain & inability to walk very far as well as the cost I decided to see the NHS physiotherapist as recommended by the GP & I waited. As soon as I entered the appointment, I felt hostility. I explained my problem and was told that this appointment was “pointless” a “waste of time” as she was a generic physiotherapist, not a women’s health therapist. She then compared herself to “online services” vs face to face, explaining that the advice she would give me would be bad quality (like online services) - just made no sense as we weren’t doing this online. But I guess her point was that she had no idea how to help me. She also told me “don’t expect regular physio from us” - not once did I say I wanted this - nor would I ever wish this upon myself, I am a healthy 31 year old - I want to be back to normal, quickly. I have a newborn and toddler at home. She then did a half hearted physical examination which I expressed hurt, and after this she did not explain the pain, what it could be or give me any sort of diagnosis. Just kept telling me I was wasting my time and that this was pointless. She then kept asking me why I wasn’t going private, and asked “is it the excess?” None of this was her business. I was referred to her by my GP. The whole appointment just felt like she did NOT want to help me at all. No empathy, no advice. She went as far as telling me it was my fault for the pain because I hadn’t come to see my GP about it in my first pregnancy 4 years ago (despite telling her this was a post partum issue, yes I had pelvic pain during both pregnancies but nothing like this). She then told me she would send me one sheet of exercises and will see me in 8 weeks. No explanation, demonstration, any information for when , how etc. I’m truly horrified and I’m sure I’ve missed a lot from this. And if she does get a chance to read this, don’t worry, I will go privately & you won’t have to see me again. But next time, please be aware of how you speak and treat your patients, especially those in vulnerable situations, like being post partum. Thankfully I am mentally sound, no PND or PNA, but not all post partum ladies are and an interaction like that could really hurt somebody. And for North Croydon Medical Centre, please do not book appointments for ladies suffering with this seemingly very common issue to a physiotherapist who themselves express that they have absolutely no clue. Especially with how long the waiting times are.
