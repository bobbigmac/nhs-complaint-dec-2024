Practice response date: 2 months ago
Practice response:
Dear Johnny V, Thank you for taking the time to share your feedback. We understand that using online systems can feel frustrating, particularly when you are unwell. Our practice operates a total triage system using Anima, which is widely used across GP practices. Requests are submitted via our website and reviewed by a triage team consisting of a clinician and an administrator, who then determine the most appropriate outcome. This may include advice, investigations, or an appointment where clinically indicated. The surgery is open Monday to Friday, 8:00am–6:30pm. If urgent care is needed outside these hours, patients are advised to contact NHS 111, or attend A&E in an emergency. Where clinically appropriate, weekend appointments can also be arranged. Anima request limits are in place to ensure patient safety and allow timely review by our clinical team. We also offer support for patients who are less confident using online systems, including in-practice assistance, guidance on our website and social media, and regular patient communications. We’re sorry that your experience was not a positive one and appreciate you raising your concerns. Kind regards Caroline Dove Practice manager
