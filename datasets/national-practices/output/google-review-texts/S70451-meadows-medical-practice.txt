Please avoid this GP surgery. It is, unfortunately, a textbook example of NHS neglect. And I make this review as local pharmacist, down the road from this practice. Despite presenting with clear red-flag symptoms, I experienced repeated delays, poor communication, and a complete lack of urgency. Appointments were pushed back, follow-ups were not actioned, and results were not communicated within a reasonable or safe timeframe. At no point did I feel that my concerns were taken seriously. The most worrying aspect is not just inefficiency, but the apparent disregard for patient safety. When patients raise legitimate concerns about their health, they should not have to chase endlessly or feel ignored by the very system meant to protect them. I understand the pressures on the NHS, but this does not excuse failures in basic duty of care, communication, and clinical responsibility. This experience has caused unnecessary stress and anxiety and has significantly undermined my trust in this practice. I strongly advise others to think carefully before registering here
