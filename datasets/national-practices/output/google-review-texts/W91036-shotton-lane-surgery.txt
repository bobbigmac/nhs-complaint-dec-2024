I don’t usually leave 1-star reviews, but this is the worst GP surgery I’ve ever dealt with. Getting an appointment is nearly impossible unless you call the second they open and press 1 repeatedly. When you do they say it's normal and many people leave with those symptoms. For a 15-minute adoption medical, we were charged £80 per person and were even handed another patient’s file (our neighbour’s) — serious data protection breach. But when they need money, they will call you non stop. We’ve been waiting over a month for a simple Aviva form to be filled in. The person in charge is always off sick, on holiday, or never on the same shift. They are explaining that there is a backlog, as per usual. When I had black urine and abdominal pain, I was told nothing was wrong. Some staff are hardworking and kind, and I genuinely feel sorry for them — but this clinic urgently needs proper management and a new working system. As soon as we get documents. Hopefully this month.. maybe not, we will be changing the practice as this is 1 start at it's best.
