I have been with this practice for 16 years, and although I have not had to call on their services very often, the experience has always been very positive. The reception staff are always cheerful, friendly and very helpful. The Doctors have changed since I first registered, the originals were all excellent, and the newer ones I have had the pleasure of meeting have always been efficient, caring and helpful. The other medical staff have also always offered that same level of help and care in a friendly manner. There is obviously a solid culture of good patient care throughout the practice. Sadly I am moving out of the area covered by Oakhill, and doubt I will find another that offers this outstanding level of service.
