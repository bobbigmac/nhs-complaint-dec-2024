Been with Norton Brook for most of my adult life, Doctors, Nurses and Pharmacy staff have always been professional and compassionate when appropriate. Also I have rarely struggled to make a timely appointment. But the reception staff have always been the weak link. There have been a few instances of minor oversights on their part, not putting prescriptions through to Day Lewis when requested was a repeated inconvenience a few years ago but admittedly hasn’t happened lately; or the time that instead of checking me in for my appointment the receptionist cancelled it, I sat waiting for 45mins beyond my appointment time before approaching the desk and being told, without apology, that my appointment was cancelled. That also happened a good few years ago. The reason I’m writing this now is because one, some but certainly not all of the reception staff there are just rude, prone to making snarky judgements and providing commentary that I find unprofessional and off putting. I am definitely someone who has sat on symptoms for longer than I should because of the ‘don’t waste our time with this sort of thing’ attitude I have received during some calls over the last 10 years, including today. I know thats silly, but I can’t be the only one put off my these off putting gate keepers.
