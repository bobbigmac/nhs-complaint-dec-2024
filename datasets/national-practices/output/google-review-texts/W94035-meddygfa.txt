Author: Chris H

Rating: 2 stars
I was on holiday nearby (2023) when my 8 year old son was taken ill with a painful rash. I phoned hoping for an appointment and was told to come in and they’d see what they could do. When I got there, a very grouchy, dour, heartless, middle aged lady receptionist told me they couldn’t book an appointment for my son as we weren’t “registered” (ie. not local) plus they were busy, so she sent us on a wild goose chase to a minor injuries unit an hour away, who promptly refused to see my son as he wasn’t injured (he had a rash not an injury). I phoned NHS 111 who explained that this original surgery shouldn’t have sent us to the minor injuries unit an hour away because, apart from being inappropriate, the surgery had a DUTY OF CARE to us as we were on holiday in the area, so we returned to the surgery after a 2 hour wild goose chase to argue with Mrs Grumpy until she relented and gave us an appointment with a doctor. The doctor was charming and professional and immediately prescribed antibiotics. But he was badly let down by Mrs Grumpy on the reception desk, who did everything, even the preparation of the medicines, with a scowl and a bad attitude. I got the distinct impression that we were treated so poorly by her as we were English , and not local.

I moved from England to Wales a while ago. And was really worried about changing GP’s. I have complex health issues and my GP in England was very helpful and helped me manage everything. I needn’t have worried. The staff at Meddygfa Bala, are warm, welcoming and helpful. The doctors are great. They really listen, are really helpful and sympathetic. And as a bonus they dispense medication from their own in house chemist. Such a great system.
