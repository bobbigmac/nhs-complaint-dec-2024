Author: Fred Parker
Date: a month ago
Rating: 5 stars
Dr. Baxter has been our family doctor for probably the last thirty years. He was extremely kind to my mother when she came to us for the last few months of her life and is always very friendly, easy to talk to and has seen us through the highs and lows of family life. I recently developed rheumatoid arthritis and saw Dr. Devlin who is, I think, quite new to the practice. She was very thorough and concerned and referred me to a specialist. I was impressed with the way she dealt with me but even more impressed when she followed up the appointment with a telephone call. I explained that the appointment was six weeks away and she told me to leave it with her and she would see if anything else could be done. Within a few minutes she rang back to say that she had consulted with another doctor and if I came straight in she could give me a steroid injection. Yesterday morning I could hardly move but this morning I was able to lie on my back in the boot of my old car and refit the boot lock which had gone wrong! Both boot lock and my ancient body are now working perfectly and it is all down to Dr. Devlin, who, really put herself out to help as much as she could. Thank you very much!

Author: Alex Pearse

Rating: 3 stars
This surgery is such a mixed bag. The clinical staff I've seen or spoken to are fantastic, but thats only if you can get an appointment to see them. Day appointments are routinely gone and guarded by confrontational staff even if you ring up within a few minutes of opening. Afternoon appointments go very quickly too and the reception staff do not seem to care if you complain about the service; in fact (and many reviewers make this point) they often become defensive or antagonistic which, is infuriating as a patient. The administrative system seems truly awful but the medical care is first class.
