Author: Richard Porter

Rating: 5 stars
Having registered with West Street around 2004, I rarely called on their services until 5 years ago, but have needed their care frequently since that time. In my experience, the doctors, nurses and administrative staff are all highly professional, experienced, and empathetic, and I have always been satisfied by the service provided. They have been quick to refer me to LDH or further afield when required, and I always feel able to trust their judgement and advice. As an aside, I wonder if some reviewers ever consider what's involved in running GP services - prioritising scarce resources whilst delivering best care is a hugely complicated process, not helped by rude, sometimes offensive, patients abusing reception staff (something I have witnessed first hand many times). It would be nice if more people who have had good experiences would take the time to post reviews here too. I would wholeheartedly recommend this practice.
