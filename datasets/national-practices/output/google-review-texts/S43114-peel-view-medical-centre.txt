Author: Lou Kennedy
Date: 2 months ago
Rating: 5 stars
We moved to this practice after moving house from Kilsyth in October 2024 and we honestly can't believe the difference in care we've received. Getting an appointment isn't a problem, there's no mistakes with prescriptions and the reception staff are lovely on the phones. There's no long queues when calling, no set times when I'm allowed to call for an appointment and I don't have to call every day for weeks on end to get one of the few slots that were released daily like our old practice. The Drs are helpful and listen. The nurse I last seen was wonderful in helping me get an appointment to talk about hormone treatment that had been denied to me for years by my previous GP. She was only taking my BP but was intuitive enough to ask questions and try to help without me even having to ask. It's a breath of fresh air. We joined just hoping it wouldn't be any worse than the last place as are aware of the pressure GPs are under currently but we've been blown away by their efficiency. Thank you Peel View!
