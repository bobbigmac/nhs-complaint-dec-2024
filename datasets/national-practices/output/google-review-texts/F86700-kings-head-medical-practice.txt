Author: Tavit Chamasanyan

Rating: 1 star
I injured myself at home while painting a door frame. I had removed the fire door and placed it in front of a window, but the wind was stronger than expected and blew the door over — it hit me on the head. I was bleeding, had a headache, and decided to go to my GP practice, which is only a one-minute drive from home. The doctor agreed to take a look and said the cut wasn’t deep enough to need stitches. She suggested I go to the pharmacy for glue or wound-closure strips. I went to the pharmacy, where they were happy to sell me the strips, but when I asked if someone could help apply them, they told me they weren’t trained to do that. I went back to the GP practice hoping for assistance, but the receptionist showed no interest in helping and didn’t even bother asking the doctor if she could help. I asked to speak to the manager, but was told the manager only works Monday to Wednesday — and this wasn’t one of those days. I’m extremely disappointed in Kings Head GP Practice. I would gladly register elsewhere if someone could recommend a better practice in the area. On top of that, I’ve been waiting for an orthopaedic appointment since February. Due to mistakes by my GP practice, I’m still waiting — and it’s clear they don’t care. I’ll leave it to the people reading this review to decide what they think of the quality of care at this practice.
