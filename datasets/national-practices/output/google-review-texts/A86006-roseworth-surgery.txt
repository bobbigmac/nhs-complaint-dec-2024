Author: Paul Burton (aura_paulb)
Date: a year ago
Rating: 5 stars
I feel very lucky to be a patient at Roseworth Surgery. I fact, I’ve had to stop telling people how good it is because they generally don’t believe me!! We are all used to the “hasn’t there been a terrible decline in the quality of general practice” conversation. In my involvement with such conversations, I have found that Roseworth Surgery seems literally to be the only practice (anywhere in the country!!) where you can still get all the things that everyone else complains don’t exist anymore. And in my own objective comparison with the general practices that look after members of my own family (eg my 90 year old father) Roseworth is way out ahead. If you phone at 08:30 in the morning you are very rarely in a queue longer than 5 callers and you are usually through in less than 10 mins. If you need to see or speak to a GP that day you virtually always can. The receptionists are all pleasant. They will be assertive when that is necessary but, in my experience, never rude or aggressive. The GPs are excellent. At present there are four I see regularly. All are very knowledgable, clinically strong, sensible, and if they aren’t sure about something they’ll ask a colleague. They are all nice and friendly to interact with. The practice nurses provide a very valuable service which allows the practice to provide excellent health monitoring programmes, prompt vaccination schedules and to remain on top of regular blood test monitoring. In fact I owe my life to the practice’s routine blood test monitoring mechanism for cardiovascular disease that picked up my Leukaemia in 2022 just as the malignant cells were about to break out of the bone marrow. The IT systems are up to date and work well and, in particular, repeat prescriptions and access to your own personal medical information run very smoothly. In my case, I interact very effectively with the practice via their interface with the NHS app. In summary, I can wholeheartedly recommend Roseworth Surgery at every level.
