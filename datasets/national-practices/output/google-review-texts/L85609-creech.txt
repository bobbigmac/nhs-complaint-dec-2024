Author: Natalie Virag
Date: 5 months ago
Rating: 1 star
I had a very disappointing and unprofessional experience with a physiotherapist at this surgery. Before my appointment, she assured me over the phone that I would receive steroid injections at my second visit to help manage my long-term wrist injury and nerve pain. I live with constant pain and take medication daily, so I was genuinely hopeful for proper pain relief and care. When I arrived, the appointment felt rushed and lacking in empathy. The physiotherapist repeatedly tried to make me bend my injured hand, even after I explained that it causes severe pain and that I suffer from PTSD because of the injury. She showed little understanding or compassion and came across as quite hostile and dismissive. She then changed her mind about providing the steroid injections, despite previously promising them, and told me to wait another two weeks. For a patient in daily pain, being told to keep waiting after being reassured otherwise is incredibly frustrating and unprofessional. There was also a nurse present who was rude and unsupportive, which made the whole experience even more uncomfortable. I left feeling unheard, disrespected, and still in pain. Patients deserve compassion, consistency, and care — not indifference and hostility. Unfortunately, this practice showed none of those things. I’ll be moving to another surgery where patients in pain are treated with the professionalism and respect they deserve.
