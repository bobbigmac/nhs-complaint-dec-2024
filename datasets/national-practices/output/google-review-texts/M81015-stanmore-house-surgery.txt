Author: SUSAN COLLEY
Date: 3 days ago
Rating: 5 stars
My husband and I, in our eighties and late seventies respectively, have been patients at Stanmore Surgery for 10+ years. We have seen the practice evolve during this time. It has always been friendly, welcoming and efficient but to begin with the idea of seeing any doctor rather than your own was paramount. I disliked this, as I believe trust in the doctor and empathy is very important. Now Stanmore House actively encourages use of the same doctor. We have found this very beneficial as both my husband and I suffer from long term conditions, and we both feel that our 'own' doctor knows us extremely well and we have great trust in her. Our doctor does not work in isolation, and we have found all personnel at Stanmore very efficient and welcoming. Referrals, where necessary, are made quickly and developments in treatment are carefully recorded and discussed fully with the patient. Prescriptions are efficiently dealt with. Both of us feel that Stanmore House, operating within the constraints of the NHS is everything a practice should be.
