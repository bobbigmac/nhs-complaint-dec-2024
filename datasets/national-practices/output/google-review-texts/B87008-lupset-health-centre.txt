Author: DAIINAS
Date: 2 months ago
Rating: 1 star
GP review (Fit Note / Patch system experience) Last year I started registering with a GP for one simple reason: I needed a fit note to properly cover a period because my previous Medical Certificate physically expired and my workplace dispute with my employer was dragging on. Registration was “successful” online, but in reality it was a mess. The online registration went through, but I had to wait around 14 days for any response. When I went back to the practice in person a second time, I was told nothing was showing on the system and that my registration was not recorded anywhere. In the end, I was given a paper form to complete again. Overall, the process took about a month, and I only received my first fit note shortly before New Year. When I realised the work situation could continue and I would need an extension, I acted early: I submitted the request one week before the expiry date to allow time for processing. The response I got was that I would have to wait a full month before a doctor would “review and approve” it. At the same time: I have not seen a doctor even once, there has been no consultation, there has been no real assessment of my condition (depression/not depression, health issues/no health issues), communication is essentially only with reception/admin, not a clinician. I was told that if the request is for a fit note extension, an appointment is not necessary. Fine, but then the obvious question is: what exactly is being “reviewed” for a month if no appointment is needed and no assessment takes place? The digital Patch system (since 25 September 2025) has been another nightmare. The practice signage says the online system is meant to be quicker and to avoid waiting in telephone queues. In my experience it’s the opposite: you ask a question, someone replies digitally, and that’s where it ends. It feels like talking to a wall. In practice, it’s mainly patient ↔ reception, and only rarely patient ↔ clinician. There is also something I can’t ignore: every time I have visited the practice in person (Health Centre Lupset), the only queue is at reception. The waiting area for doctors is empty, or has one or two people. I have never seen more than three people waiting there. If patient flow to clinicians is that low, why does a fit note approval or extension take a month? It simply doesn’t add up. Practical verdict: registration was chaotic, the fit note process is slow and opaque, and the Patch system does not speed anything up in my experience. For someone with a genuinely serious mental health situation (for example, depressive tendencies), being told to “wait a month with no consultation” can be dangerous.
