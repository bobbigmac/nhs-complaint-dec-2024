Author: Jamie Krell
Date: a month ago
Rating: 2 stars
I’ve been at Holmes Chapel Health Centre for about a year since moving to the area, and honestly, it’s been really frustrating. I know reception staff have a tough job, I’ve worked in health centres myself, so I try to be understanding, but it often feels like everything is a struggle. From the start, even signing up felt like a burden. The front desk staff were rude and seemed uninterested in answering basic questions. It often makes you feel more like a nuisance than a patient. Dealing with prescriptions has been another source of stress. My first new prescription wasn’t signed correctly, which is fine, mistakes happen, but I was only given one month instead of the two months I used to get at my previous surgery. When I asked about it, I got a curt email citing surgery policy. I understand rules exist, but the tone was unnecessarily rude, especially for a common antidepressant. On top of that, it’s frustrating having to spend an extra £60 a year because of this. I also had a test for a condition a few months ago that came back clear. When my symptoms didn’t go away and I called, I was told nothing could be done over the phone and that I had to submit an e-consult. I understand the process, but the unwillingness to help over the phone felt rigid and unhelpful and it seems like a pattern. Moving to a new area and dealing with a new surgery has been daunting, and overall it’s been a tough experience. That said, I do want to say I’ve had one experience with a medical professional that was actually brilliant. Dr Marriott was absolutely fantastic, kind, understanding, empathetic, and kept my expectations grounded on what could be done to help. He wasn’t able to wave a magic wand, but he was brilliant.

Hi Ms Griffiths, I’m sorry to hear that you had difficulty booking your annual review. In order to book an annual review, all you need to do is visit our website and click on ‘contact us online’. Select ‘I have an admin or routine care request’ and then ‘routine care’, there you will be asked to fill in your details before being asked which type of request you need help with, select ‘long term conditions review’ and submit the form.
