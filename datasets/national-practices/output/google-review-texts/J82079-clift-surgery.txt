Author: Paulina Brissenden
Date: 2 months ago
Rating: 3 stars
There are many good things about Clift Surgery. On most occasions I managed to get same day appointments for urgent issues as needed, the on-site dispensary is very convenient and the staff there is always helpful and friendly. I need annual blood tests and the nursers are always very empathetic, accommodating and great at putting nervous patients at ease. In terms of facilities, the latest addition of the Pharmabox has been great, too. Unfortunately, in the past I have been met with lack of empathy from some of the reception staff and I am very sorry to say that the quality of healthcare is inconsistent and very much depends on which doctor you see. The wait time for some routine appointments like cervical screening is very long, 3+ weeks last time I tried to book. Regrettably, it seems that like many other NHS services, the Clift Surgery is overstretched and struggling to meet the demand from the ever expanding village.
