Author: Larisa L
Date: a year ago
Rating: 5 stars
Have been patients for 3 years now, so have visited a lot for myself, 3 kids, hubby, and my mom. All the people working in this GP have been very nice to us and attentive to our needs. In most cases the appointments are available on the same day (urgent) or within 2 weeks. It's great that they have morning and afternoon call times for urgent appointments as sometimes something happens in the afternoon and you have to wait till morning in other GPs. The online appointments booking could be better, only was able to book there a few times. The Pharmacy is in Morrisons just next door, not as a great as having it in the same building, but still works well. Parking spaces always available. Office is not the newest, but not too bad either. Waiting for the appointment can take up to 30 minutes, but most of the time it is not that long. I would give it 4 stars, but I feel like so many people are giving low scores for even minor inconveniences, that someone needs to balance it.
