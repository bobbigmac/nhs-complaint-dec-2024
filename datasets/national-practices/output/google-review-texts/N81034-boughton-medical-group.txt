Let’s be clear - there will always be occasions with any service, anywhere in life, where things do not go as well as we want or deserve, and of course that is regrettable. But I have now been a patient at this practice for ten years and would make the following observations. Neither my wife nor I have ever had an unsatisfactory experience there. The staff, whether receptionists, nurses or doctors, have always been welcoming, courteous and professional. Before I retired I was a GP in a cutting edge practice in the Midlands, and yet I can tell you we had problems with appointments throughout my 35 year career, even though demand was far less than now! General Practices will always have that problem in an unlimited access, free service. Demand is ever increasing, but in my opinion Boughton Health Centre does a pretty good job of trying to keep up with the demand in these difficult times for the NHS. And their behind the scenes administrative and IT systems are first class. Yes, there will always be bumps in the road on individual occasions, but in my opinion the practice should be congratulated on the overall service it provides. We consider ourselves lucky to be patients there. Thank you.
