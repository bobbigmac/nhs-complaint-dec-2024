Unfortunately, I’ve only had bad experiences here. First, my friend who is registered as a temporary resident was almost turned away at her appointment because her temporary resident time allocation had run out (she made the appointment 4 week earlier when she was within that timeframe and that was the earliest time they could see her). Thankfully after her explaining the situation, they let her see a doctor. I too asked for an appointment to do a health check up because there were a few health concerns I had. It was booked 3/4 weeks in advance because that’s the earliest they could see me. I took the day off work, paid someone to drive me to the centre, only to wait in the waiting room for 15 minutes until the doctor came out and said something came up and they won’t be able to see me today. My partner also went in recently. He needed a full check up. He booked in advance and had to ask work for a holiday day to be used to go to the doctor. He also arranged for someone to drive him there. He got there and the same thing happened, he was told his doctor had an emergency appointment so he couldn’t see him anymore. I understand there is a hierarchy when it comes to emergency and non-emergency patients in a hospital. But this is a doctor’s practice. I should be able to trust that when I book an appointment, I will see a doctor.
