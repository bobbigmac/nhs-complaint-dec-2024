I'm extremely disappointed with the level of care and service I've received at this surgery. I called to request a very urgent appointment as I am unwell and in need of medical attention. Rather than being offered the support I needed, I was told to complete an online triage form — which I did, despite feeling unwell and struggling to do so properly. The response I received was dismissive: a message instructing me to go to the pharmacy. This is not acceptable. I clearly stated I need to have blood tests and a proper medical discussion with a doctor. It seems now the system is set up in a way where patients are pushed away rather than helped. The doctor did not call back, just sent another message telling me to go to the pharmacy. This kind of interaction feels cold, unhelpful, and quite frankly, lazy. When I contacted the reception, I was told — as if scripted — that they are "unable to help" and I must keep filling out the online forms. There appears to be no management on site to escalate matters to, which is concerning. It feels like the staff are hiding behind protocols and systems rather than offering any real human support or care. The lack of accountability is astonishing. I have experienced excellent care at surgeries in London where the reception staff are polite, compassionate, and empowered to help. The doctors there make the effort to call and speak directly to patients — especially when a matter is marked as urgent. The contrast is stark and highlights just how broken this particular practice has become. It’s important to remember that under the NHS Constitution, patients have the legal right to be treated with dignity and respect, to be listened to, and to be involved in decisions about their care.This includes having access to appropriate clinical support when needed — not being bounced around with impersonal online forms and robotic replies. Patients deserve better. We deserve to be treated like human beings, not case numbers in a queue. The is actually my first ever appointment at this surgery.
