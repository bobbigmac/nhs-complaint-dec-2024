Author: Dave Bramwell
Date: 11 months ago
Rating: 5 stars
The reception team are always helpful, polite, and supportive. Being able to pre-book is a big advantage, especially as someone who works. It can sometimes be a few days before a timed appointment or an appointment with a specific GP is available but there's always the option of calling first thing for an urgent appointment, and late night Friday or Saturday morning appointments are also very useful, regardless of them being with GPs from other surgeries. The GPs are brilliant, they really take the time to listen to what I say, then ask any questions for clarification and take the time to discuss the problem and find the best possible outcome. It makes me feel that I matter, rather than previous surgeries I've been at, where they just want patients in & out as quickly as possible. The nurses are also excellent, especially as someone who doesn't do well with needles. I haven't had any issues in the 3 or so years I've been registered with Castle Street Medical Centre. All in all, a brilliant surgery and I'm really happy that I'm registered with them. My thanks to everyone there for how brilliant they are.

Decided to make an effort and write a proper review of this brilliant surgery to say THANK YOU and share my positive experience with this practice. I hear (especially after Covid), some awful things from friends about not being able to get appointments & bad service with other doctors. I'm grateful that this surgery are so caring about their patients. I've been a patient for 8/9 years. Not in often (luckily) but there have been a fair amount of routine things plus some health issues that have made me seek advice and needed referral to the hospital, sometimes pretty urgently and some very odd (covid nails & lung inflammation after covid!). They get you in as quickly as possible with a nurse or doc or will arrange out of hours telephone appointments if necessary. It is rare you have to wait a long time, sometimes they offer a same day slot. The reception staff are lovely, the nurses and doctors professional, cheerful & I have found actually LISTEN, share useful information, take action, with complete patient choice over the direction to take going forward. I have fed a couple of complaints/suggestions through during my time as a patient one for the surgery and one regarding wider NHS correspondence and these were taken on board, - the practice manager phoned to chat (a number of years back). Today I attended, having not been for a very long time, rather embarrassed but desperate with an A4 page of things impacting greatly on my daily life - I didn't know whether they were connected or not. The GP was so kind and thorough. We have an action plan going forward with potentially 3 problems going on simultaneously. Am so grateful. Nearly didn't go because the symptoms keep changing & I felt like I wouldn't be taken seriously. Should have known better and not waited so long - as this surgery really do care.
