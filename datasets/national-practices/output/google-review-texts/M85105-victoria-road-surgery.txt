Thank you for taking the time to share your feedback. We take all feedback seriously and appreciate patients letting us know about their experiences. At Victoria Road Surgery we operate a total triage system. This means all appointment requests are submitted through triage and reviewed by our clinical team. We respond to triage requests on the same day and aim to deal with most problems on the same day where possible. If you have any concerns about your registration or accessing care, the best first step is to speak with our reception team who can guide you through the process.
