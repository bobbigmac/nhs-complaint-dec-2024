Author: Ramya Sivathas
Date: a week ago
Rating: 5 stars
I’ve been with this surgery for many years and had great experiences before the pandemic. Like many health services, things became more challenging during and after the pandemic, particularly with long waiting times for appointments. As someone with ADHD, I often struggle with things like remembering symptoms or even remembering to book an appointment when I need one. The online form system that used to close around 12:30pm sometimes added to that stress, as many days I would only remember after that time. Recently, the surgery has extended the hours for submitting the form, and that change has really improved my experience and perception of Brunswick Surgery’s service. Over the years I’ve seen several different GPs of this Surgery. Some have been absolutely wonderful, while others haven’t been quite the right fit for me. Last year, after several appointments where my symptoms weren’t improving, I became discouraged and decided to focus on making my own lifestyle changes and doing some research, which did help with my wheezing issues. Towards the end of last year, however, I began experiencing some new symptoms unrelated to the wheezing. I was hesitant to see a doctor again, but when things started getting worse, I finally made an appointment. Initially, I believe I was misdiagnosed with threadworms, and the medication unfortunately made my symptoms worse and left me barely able to sleep at night. When I saw Dr Ravindran again about the worsening symptoms, I finally felt listened to and properly cared for. She took the time to ask the right follow-up questions and was able to correctly diagnose low iron, chronic constipation and haemorrhoids. Thanks to the medication she prescribed and the advice she gave, I’ve been able to sleep properly again. This review is really to recognise and appreciate doctors like her who truly listen and take the time to understand their patients. It can make a huge difference to someone’s quality of life. Thank you. 👏
