Booking system is diabolical and not suitable for chronic illness sufferers with multiple symptoms. I have been investigated for complex symptoms since January 2025. Understand that resources are tight, but the new process of emailing and then triage is not suitable for those with many symptoms. A 10 minute slot, 3 weeks in the future, doesn't allow the GP to diagnose properly or allow me to request referrals clearly. I'm also not clear who triages? More staff training is needed for some call takers, who are abrupt and defensive. A little empathy will go a long way. I'm currently going through a series of appointments, spaced out over months. Most of the GPs are knowledgeable and understanding, but it would be hopeless if I was seen by a random GP every time, it would take 10 mins for me to explain my issues. I try to see the same GPs but it's not an easy task. The sooner I can get my referrals the less I need to stress about contacting the GP which is a real shame. Finance constraints and business models have taken over patient care and safety. I have a rare chronic condition that requires urgent attention if I have a fever. Although I have not been acutely ill recently, the option to go to Ashford Acute if there are no more appointments at the GP practice is a welcome additional assurance. Otherwise, it'll be A&E for me maybe?
