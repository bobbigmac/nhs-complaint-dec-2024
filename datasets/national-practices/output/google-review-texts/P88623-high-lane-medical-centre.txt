Author: Sophie Dyson

Rating: 5 stars
Couldn’t ask for a better Doctors Surgery. I’ve been registered at High Lane Medical Centre for 26 years with Dr Mathewson. I’ve struggled with migraines for as long as I can remember but was officially diagnosed at 15. Dr Mathewson and Dr Evans were very supportive and persistent with neurology referrals when I was struggling with chronic migraines. More recently I was finally started on CGRP injections (prescribed by my neurologist at Salford Royal) that have completely transformed my quality of life. It is a lengthy process and can be difficult whilst waiting but the doctors followed up referrals and supported me in the meantime and I am incredibly grateful for their support. They have also done a lot for my family including my dad, nana and grandad who have had more serious health conditions and surgeries. I have nothing but positive things to share about High Lane Medical Centre and I hear about my friends and colleagues experiences with their GPs and only feel more gratitude for having such an incredible GP and doctors surgery.
