Practice response date: 4 days ago
Practice response:
Thank you for sharing your feedback. I am sorry to hear about your experience and that you felt the team were unhelpful this is not the standard of service we aim to provide, and I apologise for any upset caused. Regarding your NMC Health Verification form, our GPs are unable to complete this type of document because it falls under private, non NHS contractual work. According to British Medical Association (BMA) guidance, GP practices are not required to undertake private reports or occupational health assessments that are not part of NHS funded services. Health declarations linked to employment or professional registration (including NMC forms) are classed as private work, meaning practices may choose whether or not to provide them. As a practice, we have taken the decision not to complete this category of forms, and I am sorry this has impacted your NMC process. However, you do not need to travel back to your home country. The NMC allows health declarations to be completed by a range of registered healthcare professionals, not only GPs. Many nurses, occupational health providers, and private medical services in the UK can complete the verification for you. I am also sorry to hear that you found the staff rude. I will be reviewing this with the team to ensure communication is always courteous and clear.
