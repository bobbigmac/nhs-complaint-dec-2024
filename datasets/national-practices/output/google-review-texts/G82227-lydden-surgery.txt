On 21 January went on line to see if I could book an appointment as I have been suffering with an increasingly painful back. I received a message saying booking an appointment on line was no longer available. I was referred to the triage and submitted the requested information I received a fairly quick response stating a clinician will call me on 12 February. As the pain on my lower back is becoming debilitating I attempted to call the surgery to see if this my condition could be treated a bit quicker. After being No. 1 in the queue for 19 minutes. I gave up. One has to wonder if the triage system is working. Hopefully someone at the surgery will take note. Previously my experience of Lydden surgery has been excellent. UPDATE 22nd January Since writing the above review I was contacted by the surgery and an appointment was arranged too see a doctor. I greatly appreciated this. So I have changed my star rating.
