I want to start by saying that I know most of this is due to funding issues and the general inconvenience of trying to access any service in a rural area. But I also want to share my experience in case it can be helpful for others. I have never once felt like I have been helped by seeing a doctor/ nurse at this practice. The treatment is adequate, I suppose I have never died yet and simple services like vaccines are fine. That said, the experience is so arduous that I now avoid seeing a doctor wherever I can. Upon phoning the reception for an appointment, I may be catching them on a bad day, but I almost never feel heard. I have been told to go to a pharmacy or a&e over booking an appoinment - at times over the same concern - when I feel capable of assessing my need for the appropirate service and felt an appointment was neccesary. I have waited weeks for appointments only to be told the morning of that they are cancelled. I have waited for 45m to 1h in the waiting room due to overburdened staff. Staff are always different (potentially 'locum' although I can't say for sure) and never have time to listen to my case. Ultimatelty this is less efficient because I have to repeatedly make appointments for the same issue when my concern wasn't understood well enough to be given the correct treatment. I recieve no communication about what is happening with tests, which I understand is relatively normal accross different surgeries, but I also don't find coherent results linked to my online profile for multiple weeks after tests are complete. (Again, I would like to give the surgery grace here as I'm not sure if this is a system issue). AI / automated phone system has glitched and hung up on me part way through the exessively long booking process, more than once. Phone wait times are incredibly long and while I do get results when looking up the page to book appointments online, there is clearly some miscommunication here because I'm then greeted with a page telling me that this surgery has no online booking service. I would sincerely like to see this service improve for the people who rely on it. I understand that nationwide initiatives often struggle to apply to rural settings and this surgery definitely struggles from this. That said, I wonder if there is anything that could be done to improve the situation as I know that health is incredibly important and I worry for the more vulnerable members of our community trying to work with this system. Thank you for taking the time to read if you made it to the end.
