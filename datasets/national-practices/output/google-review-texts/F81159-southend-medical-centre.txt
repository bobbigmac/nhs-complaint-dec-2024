Practice response:
Dear Patient, sorry to hear you have had a poor experience at the practice and thank you for bringing this to our attention. I would like to address each point raised. In relation to the Doctors manner, I will raise this concern with them to reflect and improve going forward, we don’t always get it right, we can always learn and do better. I hope this is the case on your next visit. I can see a thorough consultation and completed actions following the appointment as agreed. If you would like to raise this as a complaint, please email southendmedical.centre@nhs.net and we will investigate and respond in line with our complaints policy. Our telephones remain busy, however I can report a vast improvement! Before we implemented online access we would experience over 1000 calls per week and this is now averaging between 400-600 calls per week, so patients are accessing services online. This doesn’t mean the surgery is less busy, it means the practice must deal with administrative and medical requests in a different way. You can go to our website and complete an admin or medical request, this will be triaged by the team and you will be notified of a resolution or action, along with an appointment which can be telephone or face to face if appropriate. Triage does mean if your need is less urgent that someone else’s you may be required to wait a little longer for your appointment. We are currently running at government guidelines, we can offer an appointment within 2 weeks. We have lots of healthcare professionals available which we signpost patients to and often get an appointment within 5-7 days. If you specifically need a doctor’s appointment sadly due to high demand and low availability of doctors in general practice nationally there is a shortfall. Please be reassured we offer over 900+ appointments per week which all get used. I agree surgeries should be able to ‘close their books’ like dentists are able to when they reach a high number of patients, we have increased to over 7300 patients. Sadly, we are not allowed to refuse registering patients within our catchment area. Sadly local developments in our area are agreed without the infrastructure of schools and surgeries. Any concerns with 111 should be raised directly with the team at 111.
