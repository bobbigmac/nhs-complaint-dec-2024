Author: Sam Ingram

Rating: 3 stars
My personal experience here has only been positive, as each time I have attended after booking an appointment through 111 and I have been seen and given a prescription relatively quickly. However, HUC clearly needs to do something about the communication and expectation management to people as they check in, because there are a lot of negative reviews on here related to long waits and a perception that this is a walk-in centre. To my knowledge, this is an Urgent Treatment Centre which is different - and it is strongly recommended that people call 111 first to get a booked appointment, rather than just turn up - as they may face a very long wait (similar to A&E). HUC leadership - I think it would be worth you reviewing some of the negative reviews on here and considering what could be done differently to manage people's expectations about clinical priority and waiting times.
