Subject: Feedback and Complaint Regarding Appointment Delays and Communication Dear Practice Manager, I hope you are well. I am writing to share some feedback regarding my recent experience at the practice, as I believe it highlights an area where patient care and communication could be improved. I attended the practice for an appointment scheduled at 8:50am. At 9:05am, noticing that I had not yet been called, I approached the receptionist to check whether there was a delay. I was informed that there was one patient ahead of me and that I simply needed to wait. After a further ten minutes had passed, I asked again, as my appointment was now 25 minutes overdue. I also requested that the doctor be informed that patients were waiting. A few minutes later, the receptionist advised me that the delay was due to the previous patient requiring more time. By this point, my appointment was already 30 minutes late, and I felt I was no longer able to wait. I therefore asked to reschedule. While the receptionist was checking for alternative dates, I tried to explain—politely—that it would be helpful if patients were notified when delays exceed 10 minutes. This would allow people to manage their time appropriately, especially those who have work, childcare, or other commitments. Unfortunately, the receptionist did not appear willing to hear this feedback and instead focused on explaining why delays occur, without acknowledging the inconvenience caused or the simple improvement I was suggesting. I fully understand and appreciate that delays can happen in healthcare and that some consultations require additional time. However, it would make a meaningful difference if the practice could implement a system to proactively inform patients when delays become significant. This would demonstrate respect for patients’ time and help avoid unnecessary frustration. I am sharing this feedback with the hope that it will help improve the experience for all patients. I value the care provided by the practice and trust that this concern will be considered constructively. Thank you for taking the time to review my message. I would appreciate confirmation that it has been received and information on whether the practice intends to adjust its approach to communicating delays. Warm regards, Victoria Andreyeva

Dear Ashby Barrett, Thank you for your review and providing your responses. It's disappointing that we are not able to achieve a higher score from you. Your feedback has been noted. I do understand the frustrations in obtaining an appointment. We actually offer more appointments compared to the standards the GP contract states each day. We are constantly reviewing our appointment system in line with governmental advisory and we are changing the way the patient obtains a same day appointment from 18th August 2025. For a same day appointment, you can complete the online consultation form found on our website under "contact us online", which will be reviewed by a GP on the day. This beats the queue on the phones. The admin queries section on the online contact us form is open throughout the core hours of the surgery, and we aim to respond on the day of receipt, but can take up to 3 working days. Of course, if a patient cannot complete this form themselves, the ability to call the surgery or walk-in will still be available where the receptionist will complete the form on behalf of the patient. Regarding your repeat prescription, it is the patient's responsibility to request this in advance.
