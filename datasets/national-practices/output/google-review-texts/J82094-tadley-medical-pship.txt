My experience with Tadley GP over the past six years has been disappointing. It took three years to finally receive a diagnosis of hypothyroidism, despite multiple abnormal blood test results during that time. Each time, I was reassured that everything was “normal.” I even received a text message from a GP stating I would never develop hypothyroidism, a statement that is now untrue, as I now take daily medication for it. When abnormal blood tests are identified, I am told follow-up testing will be arranged, yet on several occasions I have undergone further blood tests only to discover that the specific marker requiring review has been omitted. This pattern has repeated itself numerous times. Requests for further investigation into ongoing symptoms have often been refused, leaving me with no option but to pursue private testing. Unsurprisingly, these private results have also shown abnormalities.
