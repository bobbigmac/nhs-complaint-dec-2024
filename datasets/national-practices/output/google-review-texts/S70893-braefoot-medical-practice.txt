I am usually quite forgiving and always back my teams, as I am more than well versed in the trials and tribulations faced by many a service member in our NHS pride. Despite this, even my patience wore thin with this bunch. My own admin team and practice staff in my workplace do not have the audacity or propensity to conduct themselves and behave in such a manner as I'd witnessed in Braefoot today. Ended up not going through with registering in this practice, as the ratings on Google were in fact proven to be spot on in accuracy. Exemplifies the rot in our National Health Service, permeating through this great country. If you are serious about continuing to function as a service, I would seriously consider re-training your admin staff or having a named partner discussion about other options, because this is rather unsustainable. Excusable if it were just myself who'd had a one-off bad experience. However, judging from previous reviews, the current state of dealing with patients is utterly deplorable and warrants action. We are at a stage in our healthcare system wherein we can expect and rationalize not receiving immediate treatment, however not getting a thorough and clear explanation of how a system functions is thoroughly unacceptable. Do better.
