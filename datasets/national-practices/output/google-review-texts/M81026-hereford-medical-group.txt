Dear Rebecca Thank you for sharing your feedback about the waiting time for a routine appointment. I understand that waiting for six weeks can be frustrating, and I want to provide some clarity on why this might be happening under our total triage system. Total triage is designed to ensure that every patient receives the most appropriate care based on their clinical needs. In responding to this comment, I have not looked at your individual record as I cannot comment on this forum. However, I would like to assure you that the information that you submitted about the reasons that you need an appointment will have been reviewed by an experienced GP. They will have decided on the priority of your appointment based on these comments. Here's how it works: 1. Prioritisation of Urgent Cases: Our triage system allows us to assess the urgency of each patient's needs. This means that patients with urgent or more severe conditions are prioritised and seen more quickly. As a result, routine or non-urgent appointments may have longer wait times. 2. Efficient Use of Resources: By directing patients to the right healthcare professional or service, we aim to use our resources efficiently. This ensures that GPs and other clinicians can focus on patients who need their expertise the most, which can sometimes lead to longer waits for less urgent cases. 3. High Demand and Limited Capacity: Like many practices, we face high demand for appointments and have a limited number of clinicians available. This can contribute to longer waiting times, especially during peak periods. Unfortunately, the funding that general practice is given is not unlimited so it is not always possible to increase the number of clinicians available. 4. Safety Netting and Alternative Services: While waiting for an appointment, we provide safety netting advice and may suggest alternative services, such as NHS 111 or local pharmacies, for issues that can be managed elsewhere. Like you, we would like to see a shorter wait for routine appointments. We are continuously working to improve our systems and reduce waiting times while ensuring that all patients receive safe and effective care. If your condition changes or you feel your needs are more urgent, please do not hesitate to contact us again. Regards Ceri Chaplin, Managing Partner, HMG
