Practice response:
Dear Books Nomad, Thank you for taking the time to leave feedback. We’re very sorry to hear about your experience, and we apologise for the inconvenience caused by the need to leave the room during your appointment to collect equipment. This clearly disrupted the flow of the consultation and did not meet the standard we aim to provide. We will review our room setup procedures to help ensure that necessary items are available in advance, especially for scheduled baby checks. We understand your concern about the examination surface. While adult examination couches are routinely used in NHS settings for infant checks, they are clinically appropriate when used correctly and with proper supervision. Your baby’s safety is always our priority, and we regret any moment during the consultation that felt unsafe or uncoordinated. We also want to reassure you that all NHS GPs are trained to carry out 6-week baby checks as part of their core training. If a GP ever feels uncertain about a particular issue, they are encouraged to consult with colleagues or seek advice from hospital paediatricians. This is a normal part of providing safe, collaborative care. We understand your interest in seeing a GP who specialises in baby health. While there is no separate paediatric specialist role within general practice for routine checks such as the 6-week baby check, we can reassure you that all NHS GPs are fully trained to carry these out. If you prefer to see a different GP, our reception team can do their best to accommodate this depending on appointment availability. Thank you again for your feedback. We’re glad to hear you felt the doctor was kind and caring, and we will certainly use your comments to help improve the experience for families attending baby checks in the future. If you wish to discuss this further then please do contact reception and ask to speak to our practice manager. Warm regards, Magdalena Nagadowska, Practice Manager
