Dr Canning has looked after my mum, dad,sister,brother in law and myself for many years - we were patients from the original Dr Barrett days. I think he is brilliant, thoughtful, caring and knowledgeable. He is also very down to earth and approachable - you can, and I have, spoken to him about everything and anything. My late mum and dad adored him, and he was brilliant with them. I have read all the reviews and I can honestly say that Dr Canning is the best GP I have ever had, I am 60. The repeat prescription system may need a redesign, reception can sometimes be a bit frazzled and the mad rush for appointments is a shambles - but this isnt the fault of the practice, that seems to be NHS standard practice.The surgery will also book non emergency appointments for a week or so ahead and this is a huge improvement. Any time I have needed to speak with Dr Canning, I have explained to reception politely, respectfully and in detail - I have always managed to get access to Dr or a nurse. Talking of nurses, the Asthma nurse, Siobhan (sp) is brilliant. I have had asthma for 56 years and she is the best asthma specialist I have ever worked with. When I lived abroad I was treated by an asthma professor, he was brilliant and Siobhan carried on from his regime, made it even better, and changed the quality of my life immeasurably. She is also very friendly, approachable and a joy to work with. Summary - some of the patient facing experience and systems could be improved, the standard of medical care, empathy and attention has been brilliant.
