As is the case with Royston Vasey, if you spend your holidays at RM Condor, you'll never leave. If you want luxury and a relaxing holiday atmosphere this isnt quite the place you may have had in mind. However, there are plenty of outdoor activities to take part in and regular excursions to interesting places near & far. Local hostelries are very accommodating & you will find many a local tell tales of the Battle of Brothock Bridge, after which you can grab some local cuisine at the 1.2.3. Recommended.
