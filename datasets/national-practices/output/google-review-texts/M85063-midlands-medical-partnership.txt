I recognise people’s issues with the surgery so I’d like to offer some advice. You need to learn how to use the systems and play the game. Firstly you request an appointment via a link which is triaged around 11 - so you don’t need to ring at 08:00 in the morning and queue. You can raise an appointment request before 11:00 and it will be reviewed. The link you get sent can be reused after 08:00 on any day - not before - so you don’t need to ring up for a link every time just reuse the old one - put details in the application. When calling the survey always be polite because being rude to the staff doesn’t help. The staff have always been very kind and caring to me and dealt with my queries. If you have a prescription query then don’t call in the morning, call after 11:00. The doctors have always been very pleasant to me and delivered. They have been very good with my children. Sometimes you don’t always see the same doctor by that’s the climate today, that’s just how things work. The paramedic is always lovely with the children I have. I have no issues, you just have to work appropriately with the surgery to get the result you want, if an appointment can be telephone then take the telephone. I am very content.
