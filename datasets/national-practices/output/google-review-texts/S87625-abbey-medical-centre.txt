Save Your Life – Stay Away from This GP Practice This place should be shut down. It is full of incompetence, and I strongly advise others to stay away. My wife passed away from cancer, and in the early stages her GP practice bears responsibility. Her symptoms were repeatedly dismissed as anxiety and other minor issues, and false assurances were given, including promises that her hospital scan appointment would be brought forward, which never happened. It was only later, when she attended A&E, that doctors discovered a lump in her abdomen. At the time, I chose not to pursue blame. What happened after her death, however, was completely unacceptable. My wife passed away at the start of the weekend, which resulted in her remaining in the mortuary for an extended period. On the morning following her death, at around 8:10 am, I attended the GP practice with the paperwork provided by the paramedics. I clearly explained that urgent confirmation needed to be sent to the Renfrewshire Registrar so that burial could take place the same day, in line with our religious requirements. Despite the urgency of the situation, there was no doctor available at the practice. I was instead given a printed document that should have been sent directly to the registrar. I contacted the registrar twice and was informed that they had not received any information from the GP practice. When I phoned the GP surgery again, I was wrongly told that I was asking for a printed death certificate, which was not the case. I was then instructed to return to the surgery so they could photocopy the same document and send it to the registrar. This caused further unnecessary delay and distress. At a time of profound grief, the lack of organisation, poor communication, and absence of basic responsibility from this practice was deeply upsetting. I will not stop raising concerns about this GP practice. We will also be moving to another GP surgery.
