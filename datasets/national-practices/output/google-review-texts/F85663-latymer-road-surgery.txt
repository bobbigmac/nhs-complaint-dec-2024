Author: Viktoria S
Date: Edited 3 months ago
Rating: 1 star
Latymer Road Surgery – A Most Frustrating Experience It is with considerable disappointment that I feel compelled to write this review. My experience with Latymer Road Surgery, located at 2A Latymer Road, has been, quite frankly, exasperating from beginning to end. Despite calling promptly at 8am, and being informed repeatedly that I was first in line, I would remain on hold for an inordinate 30 minutes, only to be told, rather curtly, that all appointments had been filled and I should “try again tomorrow.” This scenario repeated itself more times than I care to count, and it is nothing short of maddening. Attempting to resolve the matter in person is equally futile. One cannot simply walk into the building, as the door remains locked at all times. Instead, you are left to stand outside and ring a bell like some sort of unwelcome intruder, only to be met with scepticism and a blunt interrogation regarding your reasons for visiting. There is no option to book an appointment at the desk, and the overall atmosphere is anything but warm or welcoming. The staff, regrettably, do little to alleviate the situation. Their manner is neither friendly nor helpful, and the rigidity of the system seems deliberately designed to discourage access rather than facilitate care. In the end, it took my school’s direct intervention to secure an appointment, as I was missing an increasing amount of school due to ongoing health concerns. That, in itself, should speak volumes. I have since transferred to a different surgery and the contrast could not be more striking. The staff there are personable and accommodating, and booking an appointment is no longer an Olympic-level challenge. One feels like a valued patient, not a persistent inconvenience. I would not recommend Latymer Road Surgery to anyone in genuine need of timely, accessible medical care.
