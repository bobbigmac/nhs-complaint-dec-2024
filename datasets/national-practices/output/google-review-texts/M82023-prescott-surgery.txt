Author: J Chandler

Rating: 4 stars
I first went to Prescott Surgery to register my father who was on palliative care 6 years ago. I couldn’t fault the service they gave him and they were so much better than the practice my husband and I were registered with so we transferred there too. Despite the challenges of the pandemic, they have remained available when necessary ever since. Family in other parts of the country have huge difficulties getting through to their doctors and one (who has cancer) is no longer able to phone the surgery for an appointment - new procedures mean they have to email their request instead! I wish the parking situation was better but otherwise I’m very happy with the service we’ve received, it’s very much appreciated.
