Extremely poor care and long-term neglect I have been dealing with serious neurological symptoms for over three years, and my experience with this GP surgery has been deeply distressing and unacceptable. For over two years my symptoms were dismissed and ignored, despite repeated appointments and clear deterioration. I had to constantly push and advocate for myself just to be taken seriously. An ENT referral was delayed unnecessarily, and when I was finally seen it became clear that urgent investigations (such as MRI) and treatment should have been arranged much earlier. Due to the lack of action and dismissive attitude from this surgery, I was forced to attend A&E and out-of-hours services, as my own GP would not help. Hospital letters, A&E notes and out-of-hours reports were not read or acted upon, and communication from the practice is extremely poor. Consultations often involved being talked over, dismissed, or having my physical symptoms blamed on my mental health, despite clear evidence this was not the case. One GP, Dr Holt, even said to me “What do you expect me to do about it?” — a comment that was shocking, unprofessional and left me feeling completely unsupported. Only minimal tests were offered, with vague and dismissive language such as “the jury’s out”, while I was left in severe pain and distress with no support plan. This surgery is not equipped to manage long-term or complex illness, and patients with serious conditions should think very carefully before attending here. There is no continuity of care—every appointment is with a different GP, meaning I have to repeatedly explain years of medical history, which is exhausting and retraumatising. As a result of prolonged neglect, both my physical and mental health have significantly declined. I am now unable to care for myself and require a carer, despite previously being extremely independent. This has been devastating and entirely avoidable had appropriate care been provided earlier. I would not recommend this surgery to anyone with a serious, ongoing or complex health condition. Had to make a full complaint and awaiting the surgery’s response.
