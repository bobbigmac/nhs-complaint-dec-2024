Practice response date: 3 weeks ago
Practice response:
Thank you for your comments regarding accessing an appointment at the Surgery. All medical requests are reviewed by a GP or Advanced Care Practitioner, who will decide the urgency of the health condition detailed by a patient and the appropriate appointment arrangements to manage their health condition. For those patients deemed to have a new urgent health concern that need immediate review, a phone or face to face appointment with a member of our same day clinic service will be arranged. For health concerns that do not require an immediate response, or relate to an ongoing health matter, a book ahead routine appointment will be confirmed, which may also be offered as either a telephone call or face to face appointment, as determined by the reviewing clinician. Our routine waits are currently up to four weeks; we apologise for the frustration this can cause our patients and ideally we would wish to see everybody sooner, but it is essential that we prioritise the delivery of safe care to those with the most pressing health care needs.
