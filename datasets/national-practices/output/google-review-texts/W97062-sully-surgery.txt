Author: Michalina Bujak
Date: a month ago
Rating: 1 star
I am sharing my personal experience following recent contact with this GP practice and specifically my communication with Dr Calum Forrester-Paton. I am currently 8 weeks post laparoscopic gynaecological surgery. When I was assessed earlier this month, I was still able to walk and manage basic daily activities. Over the last 5 days, my symptoms have significantly worsened to the point where I am now struggling to function normally and my pain has become severe and constant. I contacted Singleton Hospital, Swansea, and spoke directly with the gynaecology department. After I described my symptoms and explained that my condition had clearly deteriorated, I was advised that my GP must urgently refer me to the gynaecologist on call for further assessment. I relayed this information to my GP practice. However, following communication with Dr Calum Forrester-Paton, I was informed that my case was not considered an emergency and that I should wait for a gynaecology clinic appointment. I was also advised that if I felt worse, I should attend A&E. When I attended A&E, I was informed that they only assess for immediately life-threatening conditions and do not manage post-operative gynaecological complications unless they are life-threatening. I was advised that ongoing post-operative issues should be managed by gynaecology or via GP referral. This resulted in a situation where the hospital advised urgent GP referral to gynaecology, A&E advised that they do not manage post-operative gynaecological complications, and my GP did not arrange the referral despite clear worsening of symptoms. I explained that my condition had significantly deteriorated compared to my earlier assessment and that I am now unable to carry out normal daily activities. I did not receive clear guidance on how to manage my worsening symptoms while waiting, despite the serious impact this has had on my physical and mental wellbeing. I am sharing this review to accurately reflect my experience and the difficulties I faced in accessing appropriate post-operative care.
