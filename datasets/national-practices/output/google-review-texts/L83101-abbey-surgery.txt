Author: Shannon Bennett
Date: a month ago
Rating: 2 stars
I recently had a phone appointment with a male doctor at Abby Surgery and the experience was extremely disappointing. He spoke to me very poorly, did not listen to my concerns, and was dismissive throughout the call. I explained that I was in a lot of pain and that a pharmacist I had spoken to over the weekend had recommended further antibiotics, yet he refused to prescribe them and did not seem to take my situation seriously. After the appointment, I checked my report on the NHS App and noticed that the condition I had explained I believed I had was not even recorded. This felt careless and unprofessional. He also booked me in for blood tests without clearly explaining what the tests were for, and then said he wanted to see me again however, he was unable to arrange this follow-up appointment himself. The call felt extremely rushed! Instead, I was told to ring reception separately. When I did, I was informed that there were no appointments available for three weeks, which is not good enough. If a GP specifically asks to see a patient again, you would expect them to be able to arrange that appointment rather than leaving the patient to repeatedly phone reception with no success. Each time I call, unless it is exactly at 8:30am, I am told there are no appointments left. It feels almost impossible to be seen. I also attempted to make a complaint about my experience, but there is no email address available to do so only a phone line. Given my recent poor experiences, this puts me off ringing and makes it feel even harder to raise concerns or be heard. Especially if you want a paper trail of the complaint. Overall, this experience left me feeling unheard, unsupported, and frustrated and I feel the appointment system, communication, and complaint process need serious improvement.
