I was born in this village and Potterals was always great. Compared to friends that lived in other places and when I lived elsewhere and overseas, I always thought the doctors here were professional and it was easier to get an appointment than other NHS GPs, even post-Covid when I moved back. Recently they have changed their appointment system so now you have to share a lot of personal and private information in a form, rather than getting an appointment. They make you play 20 questions before they permit you to see a GP. You can't choose which doctor you go to. Why is it not just a calendar so you can book yourself in at a convenient time with a doctor you trust who knows your medical history? The answer? They have created the system to discourage people from booking an appointment. It is a form of friction that discourages people from seeking medical help, simply put. That isn't the only reason I am giving this 1* review. Dr. Ridout botched a vaccination for flu on my mum, causing her severe pain and disability in both arms that required prolonged steroids use to bring under control. It is clear based on symptoms that the injection was put into the wrong part of the arm, leading to near paralysis at the site (upper shoulder) later on the same day. He also insulted my dad after we finally got him to go to the doctor and he was so humiliated by the experience that he never went to the doctor again, and his condition worsened. He died in August 2023 from heart failure. It's such a shame to see this once reliable institution succumb to the same poor care we see everywhere else in the NHS. Really sad. But what can we do? Nothing, it's the NHS. Just hope you don't get sick.
