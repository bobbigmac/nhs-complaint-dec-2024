Author: A Paterson
Date: a year ago
Rating: 4 stars
The doctors at this medical centre are first class and give their patients time to explain their symptoms and address these. The pharmacy, also, provides and excellent service, with friendly and helpful staff and it is the same with all the reception staff. The only downside is telephoning for an urgent appointment. Here, the recorded message goes on for nearly a minute. Then there is a long queue with information on where you are in the queue. Then there is screening by non-medically trained staff who decide whether you should be telephoned by a doctor or not. The doctors are good at calling back and will decide if they need to see you or not. So the process of seeing a doctor face to face is long- winded and laborious. With the current broken state of the NHS, together with the large number of patients, perhaps the system for appointments isn't surprising.
