Very poor communication and handling of appointments. I was asked by the surgery to book a GP appointment, and I made clear I was overseas on business for one month. I was told this would not be a problem. After waiting more than two weeks, I was then informed whilst overseas (and may I add waiting up for the phone call whilst fighting jet lag) the doctor would not speak to me because I was abroad and was simply told to call back again after I return home. No explanation was provided as to why the doctor wanted the appointment, leaving me unnecessarily anxious about my health. Do they want a blood test, is it routine, serious?? It’s cruel to leave patients in this state of limbo. This situation could have been avoided entirely with basic organisation and clearer communication. I have thoroughly reviewed the NHS policies and there is no strict or enforced reason why they cannot attend an appointment whilst the patient is overseas. I simply feel the doctor is being lazy and trying to reduce her workload. I’m shocked at the inhumanity of her care. I will say however that the receptionist was wonderful and kind. It is the doctor that should be ashamed and question their duty of care. This is not the first time Deal Tree has let me down. Refusing medication for my reactive hypoglycaemia (likely due to budget) which resulted in me having 3 hypoglycaemic episodes whereby I lost consciousness even though it was proven to help my condition. The NHS and my particular doctor are frankly quite shameful.
