Rating: 1 star
It’s a shame there isn’t an option to give zero stars. In the past, Canbury Medical Centre lost my urine test sample and the issue was effectively brushed aside without any proper accountability. On another occasion, I asked reception to book two appointments because I needed two completely different issues examined. I was told that one appointment would be enough. However, when I saw the doctor on the day, I was told that two separate appointments were actually required, so I was only able to discuss one problem. To make matters worse, I am still dealing with muscle pain that I first raised with a doctor seven months ago, and it remains unresolved. My GP, Dr Manson, advised me to stop running, effectively restricting my daily activities. When the problem did not improve, I was told to “use this app to exercise that area”. However, the link I was given does not even include exercises for the affected area. When I asked Dr Manson whether there was a physiotherapist available, she finally started to take action, but by that point all that had happened was a significant waste of my time. Once I was referred to the physiotherapist, Ms Kemila, she quickly arranged the necessary tests. After the results came back, I tried to book a follow-up appointment, but the earliest available telephone consultation was more than two weeks away — despite the fact that the conversation would likely take less than a minute. Reception then told me to resubmit the online triage form and mark it as urgent. However, the result was exactly the same appointment date. This clinic is clearly not functioning properly. Mistakes of this kind can lead to delays in treatment and, in some cases, could potentially put patients’ health at risk.
