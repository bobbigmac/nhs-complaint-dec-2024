Practice response date: 2 years ago
Practice response:
I’m sorry to hear of your wait this morning. I can see that the GP you saw had done 13 consultations before yours unfortunately several of these were complex and one lasted over 30mins. We had 8 GPs consulting this morning together with 2 paramedics and 2 health care assistants doing blood pressures and blood tests. It is difficult to close our list to new registrations due to government regulations. The list size remains static at around 7000. What has risen is demand for consultations with the average patient now needing nearly 7 appointments a year. Unfortunately it is difficult to reduce waits at the surgery while ensuring patients consults are of a high quality and cover all important issues. Our solution would be a larger surgery and staffing but this is very unlikely with the current funding arrangements which involves approx £160 per patient per year for an unlimited number of consults. If you would like to discuss further please contact our practice manager. We do take criticism seriously and are always looking for ways to improve our service.
