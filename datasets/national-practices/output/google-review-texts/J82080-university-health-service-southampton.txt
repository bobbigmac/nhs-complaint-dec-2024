We joined this practice 15 months ago and it is excellent. Staff are friendly, kind and helpful. Repeat medication requests are processed very speedily and available for collection at a nominated pharmacy in a matter of days. Routine appointments can be booked online and same day appointments are available by phoning the surgery. I phoned at 8am, caller number 1, answered within 20 seconds and saw a GP 50 minutes later! If only all gp surgeries were like this there would be less pressure on emergency departments. 5 stars from us, thank you.
