Practice response date: Edited 2 months ago
Practice response:
Thank you for taking the time to leave your review. We are pleased to hear your positive comments regarding our clinical staff and would like to address the concerns you have raised regarding practice management. As an external commentator, you would not be expected to be familiar with the internal contractual, regulatory, and commissioning requirements under which NHS GP practices operate. For that reason, we are unable to agree with the conclusions you have drawn regarding NHS funding or practice decision-making. Julie Wright is a long-standing, dedicated, and caring member of our management team. Decisions regarding patient registration and removals are not made unilaterally and, in this case, were fully supported by all practice partners. To clarify, removing patients who live outside our catchment area does not increase practice income. If financial gain were our motivation, it would be illogical to ask any registered patients to leave, regardless of where they live. GP practices are contractually required to prioritise care for patients residing within their defined catchment area, and length of prior registration does not override this requirement. We are reviewing and removing all patients who no longer side within our boundary. Additionally, our practice list size has grown significantly and now exceeds what our premises can safely and effectively accommodate. To ensure safe access and continuity of care, we must retain capacity for patients who live within our catchment area. Regarding your comments about your son’s diabetes care, we strongly refute any suggestion that prescribing decisions were made on the basis of cost rather than clinical appropriateness. If you have ongoing concerns about his treatment, these should be discussed directly with a GP via a booked appointment so they can be addressed properly and confidentially. Online forums are not an appropriate or effective way to review individual clinical care. We understand that being removed from a practice can be upsetting, but these decisions are made in line with NHS guidance and contractual obligations, with patient safety and service sustainability as our priority. Dr Jacques Ransford Senior GP Partner
