I am giving 1 start as zero is not an option. Normally happy with the GPs and nurses available but am appalled by what happened yesterday. I booked a follow up appointment for my 1.5 year old daughter as advised by one of the GPs 2 weeks earlier in case of new earache and flu symptoms. I saw Dr Ilyas who said my child was looking fit an healthy. Ok, my child wasn’t terribly ill but was clearly coming down with the flu - anyone could tell. Because she had covid in August and a bad viral infection in October, I asked Dr Ilyas if she could listen to my child’s lungs. She categorically refused on the grounds that “listening to your child’s lungs would be OVERDOING it. Your child is fine I can see it”… Who on earth says that? I didn’t ask for immediate blood tests or anything out of reason… I simply asked if she could listen to my child’s lungs, something that other doctors would normally carry out as routine. I was still within my 10-min appointment timeframe so Dr Ilyas could’ve simply listened to my child's lungs, even just to reassure me. She showed absolutely no empathy and kept refusing. When I said “do you realise it will just take you one minute?” She said “doctors sometimes do too much for their patients and your daughter doesn’t need her chest checking”. I find this shockingly unprofessional and tactless. My child has now got a bad cough and a fever (39). Great job Dr Ilyas! You definitely saw it. Your professionalism, and I would say medical skills, certainly need a touch of improvement.
