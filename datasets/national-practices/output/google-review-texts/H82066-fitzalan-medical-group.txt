My experience with Fitzalan has been mostly very good. The serve a large area with a varied demographic, with many elderly patients Including my mother who is 83 with Parkinson's. We both visit the surgery quite often, and have had no bad experiences. It must be a tough job for the team dealing with such a busy surgery, but the staff maintain good humour and are helpful and polite, as are most of the patients. I would recommend Fitzalan to friends and family, and suggest a little patience and resilience to those who find themselves frustrated by our woefully neglected NHS after 14 years of Conservative Party's underfunding.
