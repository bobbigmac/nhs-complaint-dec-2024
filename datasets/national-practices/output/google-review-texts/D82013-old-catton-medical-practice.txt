What I do know is that she made it very clear I was wasting her precious time with something as apparently “trivial” as an ear infection. It also seems Friday is not the day to see her — she looked annoyed, tired, and ready to go home, and she made sure I felt it. Overall: dismissive attitude, no clarity, no accountability, and no sense of patient care. The reviews speak for themselves — and unfortunately, I now understand why.
