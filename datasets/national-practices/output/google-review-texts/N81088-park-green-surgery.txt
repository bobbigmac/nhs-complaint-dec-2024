Author: Chiara
Date: 5 months ago
Rating: 2 stars
I've stuck with the surgery because there is one doctor there who does listen, although almost impossible to actually see her due to the way the booking process is now run. I find it a totally demoralising, time-consuming and utterly frustrating place to deal with. Unless you are up at 7.55am and waiting on the computer you will be unlikely to get an appointment. Even then, its pages of stuff to fill in and your'll prob get given an appointment when it suits them, rather than being able to choose a time and a doctor that it suits you. Medication is frequently dispatched incorrectly, very slowly or not at all. There seems to be no awareness of issues for neurodivergent people in terms of managing the booking process, the waiting room etc., let alone if you have experienced any trauma and find it hard to go to the doctors. The receptionists seem to be trained to obstruct access to doctors. The phone system is either inadequate or being misused, calls regularly get cut off before being answered. When calls are answered, there is pressure to discuss your medical issues and if you refuse to do so, then you will basically get ignored. The new blood test website doesn't work either. Qute simply a very poorly run and staffed practice which could and should be doing a better job to care for its patients.
