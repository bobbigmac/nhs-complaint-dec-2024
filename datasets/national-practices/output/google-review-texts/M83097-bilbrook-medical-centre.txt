On the morning of 19/12/25 (Friday) I called to request an appointment as I wasn’t feeling very well which included weakness, chesty cough, bringing up green phlegm and a temperature. I was told no appointments can be made and to take some paracetamol and rest, when I begged for even a phone call appointment just for someone to assess me to see if I need my vitals taking, I was given one for the next day (Saturday.) By the time I had my phone call the next day I couldn’t breathe so went to New Cross hospital. My oxygen levels were dangerously low at 83% and I was put on 4 litres of oxygen. I reminded in hospital all weekend, reliant on oxygen along with antivirals and steroids - I went home Sunday night. By Monday (22/12/25) morning I had the displeasure of having to call the doctors back again. This time I needed to request anti sickness medication due to the side effects of my hospital prescriptions. Because they were making me sick they were stopping me from breathing which was putting me at risk of going back to hospital and back on oxygen. I explained my symptoms, medication and my hospital stay on the phone and why I need the anti sickness medication and was told “no appointments available today - call a pharmacy”. For a patient who has compromised breathing to be neglected twice within days is absolutely abhorrent. I have never felt so neglected, unseen and untrusting of a doctors surgery in my life. If you can’t be trusted to take breathing issues seriously, what do you take seriously?
