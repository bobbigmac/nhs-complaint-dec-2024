Reception staff are always helpful and nice mannered. However, had extremely bad bacterial tonsillitis and a pretty terrible overall experience. Had explained all my symptoms to the doc over the phone (which was difficult to do as I was continually interrupted) and was told that I should ‘just continue what I’m doing’. This included salt baths, x2 500mg paracetamol and x2 200mg ibuprofen -which is pretty standard for viral tonsillitis. However, I already had explained that 4x daily of the above meds was not touching the sides of my pain. In fact the pain was so intense I hadn’t slept all night. I had also explained the pain was predominantly down one side of my throat and that my uvula was stuck to the side of tonsil. I also could not swallow, eat solid foods and felt generally very unwell. I managed to get the doctor to eventually prescribe me so pain relief only to fail to send the prescription to my chosen pharmacy! Following a horrendously painful day and my second sleepless night I ended up having to phone NHS 24hr and was seen by an out of hours GP at approx 2:00am as they felt my symptoms to be quite serious by this point. They were querying a potential peritonsillar abscess (still waiting to find out if that was the real cause as I’m writing this the night of). If your not aware if left untreated a tonsil abscess can block your airways and also become septic … pretty serious stuff. Unfortunately I felt rushed through a telephone appointment with little information provided and had to pull teeth to get painkillers that were never even sent. Being a qualified physiotherapist myself I have found the treatment to be less than desirable and will be seeking a new GP as soon as possible following this chain of events.
