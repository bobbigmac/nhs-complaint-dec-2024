Practice response date: 10 months ago
Practice response:
Thank you for your review. I am sorry you found your experience of care unsatisfactory. Primary care nationally has had to evolve over recent decades as there are nowhere near enough GPs to see all patients about all problems. We have a number of paramedics employed by the practice and PCN who are highly trained professionals. They work under the supervision of a GP. The receptionists work to a protocol as to which problems are suitable for the paramedics. This helps to free up appointments with the GPs to deal with more complex problems and management of chronic conditions. From your description you were appropriately assigned an appointment with a paramedic. We work to a policy of only prescribing antibiotics where clearly needed. This helps to prevent the development of antibiotic resistance. It is not uncommon for patients to be seen at a stage of illness where assessment does not indicate antibiotics are needed. In this situation patients are advised to seek medical attention should they get worse- which sounds like what happened in your case. If you are unhappy about the care you received I would encourage you to contact the practice directly with a view to making a complaint. This will allow us to thoroughly investigate what happened and make any changes necessary to improve patient’s experience of care in the future. Regards. Dr Alex van den Bos. GP partner
