Author: Alex Wood (Alex)
Date: Edited 5 years ago
Rating: 5 stars
One of the few genuinely independent pharmacies left, with two very experienced pharmacists who you can develop a relationship with in the way you can with your doctor, and in the way it always was before big business chains took over pharmacies in the UK. I'd recommend here if; (a) you'd rather support local business, (b) you're looking for advice on medicines or conditions - that the pharmacists are happy to give you in a quiet side room for free, (c) if you have an ongoing problem which would benefit from another professional knowing you as well as your doctor, and (d) if you need vaccinations or medication for travel to other countries - these have a full travel clinic and are highly ethical about telling you the financial costs of each option and the reality of how much at risk you are so that you can make the decision of what to go for, rather than receive the normal sales pitch that you get in most other travel clinics.
