Author: Leonard Allaway

Rating: 5 stars
Dr Haroon at the Chatham Street Surgery in Reading has been brilliant at helping me with my health concerns. He is thorough and efficient, thus enabling me to cover a lot in each appointment. He got all the tests, diagnoses, and medicine that I needed quickly and efficiently, so I wasn't having to suffer any difficult Waits or anything like that. I also really like the complimentary approach he takes as well alongside traditional medicine. The surgery itself is also very efficient, and waiting times for my appointments and tests have always been within just a few days or maybe a week. There is an online Appointment Booking system on their web site, but I think it might only work during their opening hours. Good doctors often run a bit late, so you might find yourself having to wait in the waiting room for a while if your appointment is later in the day. At first this was a bit of a put off, but then I quickly figured that if I let a few of my To-Do outstanding tasks build up over the days leading up to my appointment, I can quite happily sit in the waiting room, carrying out these tasks to pass the time. There is usually plenty of seating available in the waiting area, but I would advise wearing a mask just in case. Back in last August 2024, Annalise at the reception was a great help in getting me started and registered at the surgery. She made me feel totally at ease, was very professional, and didn't make me feel under pressure in any way whatsoever. I was very happy with the conveniences that she explained, that makes contacting and booking easy and user-friendly. I was offered an introductory health check with the nurse, and was advised that I could have "a full MOT" if I discuss this with the doctor. It was also pleasant and reassuring when Annalise identified with me about the importance of proactive healthcare, to reduce future problems and less future burdens on the NHS.
