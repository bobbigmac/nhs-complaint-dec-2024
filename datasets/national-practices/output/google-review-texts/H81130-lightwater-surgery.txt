Author: Natalia W

Rating: 2 stars
After being with this surgery for more than 2 years I just want to share my experience especially for females and send a message to not give up. I was seen for severe fatigue by a lady doctor as I had anemia since child and low iron my whole life. Eventually after blood test they believed me and got me strong iron tablets. When I asked the lady doctor if I will have to continue my whole life like this I got told it’s because of my periods, of course, and there’s nothing I can do. I didn’t give up and went to see another doctor within this surgery, only to find out I’m coeliac which is why I was fatigued and why just iron tablets didn’t work (because I never absorbed them). Never give up if you know something is wrong with you. A year and a half later I started having severe stomach pains that would immobilise me for days. A lady doctor told me to maybe stop eating crisps, go home and drink some water. Turns out I have h.pyroli bacteria and ulcers in my stomach, found by another surgery after I changed them. I just wanted to share my experience here for any other females that get dismissed. Don’t ignore your symptoms and just ask to see another doctor until you get heard. It probably shouldn’t be this way, we should be heard the first time round.
