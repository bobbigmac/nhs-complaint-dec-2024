My family and I were visiting the area when my daughter developed an ear infection. We came to this surgery after looking it up and making an emergency appointment on the phone. I was pleasantly surprised as there are not a lot of POC in the area and we are a mixed race family and I'm always a little on guard in case of biases in such areas. Making the appointment was easy, the receptionist was so welcoming, the doctor (Barney, I believe) was awesome, and the in house pharmacist was great too. It was genuinely a better experience than I sometimes have with my own doctors at home! We do visit the area from time to time and it's great to know that we can come to this surgery. Would absolutely recommend.
