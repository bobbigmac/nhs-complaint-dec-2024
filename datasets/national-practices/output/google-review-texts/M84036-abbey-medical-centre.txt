Instead of allowing patients to call and book an appointment, especially in urgent situations, the practice forces everyone through an online form that is unnecessarily complicated, time‑consuming, and difficult even to find over internet. This creates a completely avoidable barrier to care. It’s unacceptable that at a time when my child is seriously unwell, I’m left struggling with a digital form instead of being able to speak to someone who can help.
