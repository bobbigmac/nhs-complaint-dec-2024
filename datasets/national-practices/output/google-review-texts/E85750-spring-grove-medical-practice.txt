It’s been nearly two years since my daughter and I joined this GP, and I honestly can’t recommend it enough! Like any busy practice, there can occasionally be minor delays or administrative issues, which is understandable given the number of patients they care for. Overall, the reception team is caring and helpful. What truly sets this GP apart is the quality of its doctors. Dr. Fabio Conti is, without a doubt, the most dedicated doctor I have ever met in a UK GP. He is caring, thorough, and incredibly attentive to every detail. He never stops until he finds the root cause of any concern. If needed, he promptly arranges blood tests or specialist referrals and ensures you receive the best possible treatment. Words aren’t enough to describe how amazing he is at his job! I also want to highlight Dr. Luthra, the GP’s director, who is extremely professional and ensures the highest standards of care. Additionally, during emergency same-day appointments, we’ve met several doctors, and Dr. Simon Rodrigues really stood out as another fantastic doctor who provided excellent care. If you’re looking for a GP that truly prioritizes patients and their well-being, I highly recommend Spring Grove Surgery for you and your family!
