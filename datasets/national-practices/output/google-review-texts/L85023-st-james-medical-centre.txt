Practice response date: 3 months ago
Practice response:
Thank you for taking the time to leave your feedback. We do understand how frustrating it can be to try to contact the practice outside of normal working hours, particularly for patients who work during the day. Under the national General Practice contract, online services such as appointment requests are required to be available during the practice’s core opening hours, which are Monday to Friday 8 am to 6.30 pm (excluding bank holidays). For this reason, our online consultation system is currently only accessible during those times. All requests submitted online must be reviewed and appropriately triaged by our clinical team, which can only safely take place when the practice is open. Kind Regards Tracey Pike, Practice Manager
