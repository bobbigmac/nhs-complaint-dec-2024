Author: Kazimieras Mikelis
Date: a week ago
Rating: 4 stars
Genuinely, my experience with this clinic is a mix of good and bad. But it's trending in a positive direction. The main negative and area to improve is that they sometimes come off as very dismissive, particularly the reception. We all get frustrated, but it'd be healthier to say "I'm having a bad day" than being curt or passive-aggressive with the patients. This is rare, however. I also had some difficulty registering with them. I had to call them many times and I felt like I may fall through the gaps. It was very stressful, knowing I need my repeat prescriptions. Now onto the good part. The biggest positive is that I've been able to see a doctor several times now that I needed a consultation. None of these were urgent, but it's still great in today's context. Most of the reception staff have been excellent, and the few times I dealt with admin -- also great. They were were accommodating, we joked, the atmosphere was great. I have also been able to get various admin work done by mail. I send them a letter explaining what I need, and it's done in a couple of days. I'd like to think that this saves time for both sides. Finally, I attend a private GP practice, which means that I have to broadly keep both clinics up to date with my medical history. I've asked Hamilton Medical to provide me with all my clinical data so I could give it to my private GP, and it looks like it was a lot of work. I feel bad about asking them to do so much, but they've really showed up for me. So with all that said... it's a mix of good and bad. But someone's doing something to improve the clinic, and it shows. I hope they can build their clinic into something that's known for excellent patient communication, and then it'd be 5 stars from me.
