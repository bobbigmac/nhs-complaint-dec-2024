Author: Nikhil Patel
Date: 2 weeks ago
Rating: 1 star
I am extremely disappointed with the service I experienced during my recent visit to the GP clinic. I had to wait for approximately 45 minutes despite arriving on time for my appointment. This was particularly distressing as I was accompanied by my 6-week-old baby, who became increasingly irritated and uncomfortable during the prolonged wait. When I approached the reception desk to politely inquire about the delay, I was met with a rude and dismissive response from the receptionist. Instead of showing understanding or empathy toward a mother with a newborn, her attitude was unprofessional and discourteous. What I find especially unfair is the double standard in time management. If a patient is even five minutes late, appointments are often rescheduled or patients are made to wait excessively. However, when the clinic runs significantly behind schedule, patients are simply expected to accept long delays without explanation or apology. Respect for patients’ time and circumstances—especially when a newborn is involved—should be a priority in any healthcare setting. I sincerely hope the management addresses these concerns and improves both punctuality and staff professionalism in the future.
