I am writing to formally raise a complaint regarding my experience at Highbury Grange Medical Practice, specifically concerning the conduct of reception staff. I initially registered with the practice due to its convenient location; however, the behaviour I encountered was unprofessional, dismissive, and distressing. On the day in question, I contacted the practice earlier in the morning to book an appointment and was informed that availability existed at 3:00 pm, but that I would need to attend the practice in person. I left work early and arrived at 2:50 pm to explain my symptoms and the advice I had received earlier. Before I was able to speak, the receptionist abruptly told me to leave and return at 3:00 pm. I complied politely and thanked her before leaving. When I returned at 3:00 pm and queued at reception, a couple with a baby entered behind me. The receptionist repeatedly shouted over me to them, stating loudly, “Hey! Hello! Hello! You need to listen when I’m calling you, thanks,” in a manner that was rude and unnecessary. I waited patiently while she attended to them. When it was finally my turn, I explained my symptoms and was told dismissively that my appointment was actually at 3:30 pm and that I could either wait elsewhere or leave and come back. I chose to step out briefly and returned at approximately 3:22 pm, at which point I went to sit in the waiting area. In front of other patients, the receptionist scoffed audibly with a colleague, called my name, and spoke to me aggressively, stating: “You can’t just walk in here whenever you want, you need to check in with me, you understand.” I was extremely embarrassed by this public reprimand and apologised despite having received no prior instruction to do so. I would like to note that the doctor I saw was kind, professional, and informative. I was advised that a follow-up appointment would be arranged and that I would receive a message confirming this; however, no message was ever received. As I was leaving, I politely said “thank you, goodbye” to the receptionist and was met with a visibly disgusted look in response. Two days later, following advice from A&E, I contacted the practice to book an appointment for a GP referral for a scan. The receptionist I spoke to stated bluntly, “I’m not booking an appointment for a scan, we don’t do that here.” When I explained that I required a referral as instructed by A&E, she responded abruptly, “Right… no we can’t,” and ended the call. Due to these repeated experiences of rudeness, lack of professionalism, and refusal to provide appropriate assistance, I have since registered with a different GP practice. I find it deeply concerning that patients seeking medical care are treated in this manner, particularly when they may already be unwell or distressed. I hope this complaint is taken seriously and that appropriate steps are taken to address the conduct of reception staff, as it reflects poorly on the practice and directly impacts patient care.
