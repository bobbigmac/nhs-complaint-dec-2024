Practice response:
I'm sorry you've not got on with our new appointment system. Since 1.10.25 GP surgeries have been contractually bound by the government to have an online consultation method in some form or other. When researching our options, the benefits of Rapid Health were that there was a consistent approach made across triage decisions, and an emphasis on prioritising 'health need' rather than 'health want' i.e. a same or next day appointment is offered to those who medically need it rather than because you want it sooner. More routine requests will be offered appointments further in the future, but the waits for these e.g. 10 days in your case is still much sooner than what we had to offer for routine appointments prior to this system (often 28days plus). Try us again when you next have need to and you will find the appointment on offer will depend on the urgency and medical need of the problem.
