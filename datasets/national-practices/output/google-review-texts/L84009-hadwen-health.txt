Author: Martin Johnston
Date: 6 months ago
Rating: 2 stars
You have to really fight to get an appointment here. Between the depressing hold music (yes, really) and over 50 minutes on hold, it feels like they don't want you to bother them with your problems. Then, once you finally get through, you are just told to phone back later, no medical advice or anything else offered. What a waste of time. Instead of the same pre-recorded message on loop while you are on hold, why not tell people non-emergency appointments are full so they don't have to waste their time on hold. The online E-consult system worked well years ago, but clearly it worked too well because it was scrapped. I assume because the qty of enquiries were too much for staff to keep up with. I understand some of their reasons for how they do things- extremely high demand, limited resources etc. I don't have issue with this, it's the having to wait on hold that is the main frustration. It's 2025, there's so many better ways to handle this, that are more convenient for the public, many of us work during practice hours. (Automatic callback service when it's your turn etc.) If you can manage to persuade a patient advisor that your problem is urgent and very serious, they will prioritise you and see you often same day. This is often an uphill battle and you really have to fight your case. Part of their "comprehensive training" I imagine... It feels like lying or exaggerating an issue is the only way to get seen, while you are essentially punished for being honest. One time I actually made it all the way for an in-person appointment, just for the practitioner to give me a link to the NHS website to self refer. No other advice offered. ???? My partner and I have had so many frustrating experiences with this place over the years. It's a common theme looking at the other reviews too. Told to drop off a sample on a specific day, and upon arrival the practice was closed. Even though their online opening hours said they should be open. Fantastic. I've never been rude with the staff here, which makes conversations a bit awkward. No appointments? Okay, thankyou!!! I understand it's a thankless job, with lots of strain on staff. But it feels like things get worse here each year that goes on, with no effort to improve things. They also have recently added me on some SMS mass marketing list for crucial updates such as: "our new friendship walking group" and "wear sunscreen". There is no opt out option on the messages, so I guess the only way to get removed is to phone up!! Brilliant. I will say, once you have been seen for an urgent issue they are for the most part okay. I'm usually in and out within 2-3 minutes when I have had the fortune of getting an in person appointment. The phone system and handling of non urgent appointments are by far my biggest gripe with this place. Other things, like a practitioner swearing (not aggressively) during my appointment, I can look the other way, it doesn't impact my service. Please, please try harder to collect patient feedback, and act on this feedback. You must have all the stats for hold times etc. Lots of us hate the ordeal of trying to contact a GP and avoid it wherever possible. Often easier just to ignore a medical problem or hope it goes away, than psych yourself up to sit on hold for such long amounts of time. However, sometimes things are serious and we don't have a choice. Fun fact, I wrote this entire review while on hold.
