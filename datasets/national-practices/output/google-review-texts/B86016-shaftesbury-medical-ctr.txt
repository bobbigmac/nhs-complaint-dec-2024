Practice response date: 6 months ago
Practice response:
Dear Nicola Kubinski I am sorry you were upset by our response to your request for a letter to support your PIP appeal. As a practice we do not provide PIP letters of support. PIP is a functional assessment that evaluates an individuals ability to perform daily tasks, and is not just about a diagnosis. A GP letter would not have the specific functional details required for a PIP assessment and would therefore not significantly influence the DWP's outcome of an appeal. We do provide summary printouts which contains key medical problems and treatments which may help with your claim. Thank you for taking your time to leave your feedback. Julie Sutcliffe Practice Manager

Practice response date: 7 months ago
Practice response:
Dear Laura Stevenson, Thank you for taking the time to leave your feedback and for your kind comments about our reception team. I completely understand your request to have our online system available outside of regular hours, as this would be more convenient for patients who work during the day. When developing our new online appointment system, we carefully considered this option. However, we felt it could be unsafe for patients to submit requests outside of staffed hours because some patients may not fully appreciate the urgency of their condition until contacted by a doctor. Our online system is available from 7:30 am to 4:00 pm, Monday to Friday. This schedule was deliberately chosen to accommodate patients who wish to contact us before starting work, as well as those who might prefer to submit an online form during their lunch break. For non-urgent requests , our team will send an appointment link, which can be used to book a consultation with a doctor at any time during the week—available 24 hours a day. We also offer some online appointments with our nursing team, appointment links are usually sent with the invitation to attend. Additionally, the NHS App can be used to order prescriptions anytime, 24 hours a day throughout the week. We hope that by offering this flexibility and using appointment links where appropriate, we can balance patient safety with convenience. Thank you again for your valuable feedback, which helps us continue to streamline our services and ensures we are moving in the right direction. Julie Sutcliffe Practice Manager
