Restricted GP access, not what the government requires! Under NHS policy from October 2025, GP practices in England are supposed to keep online consultation tools open throughout core working hours so patients can request appointments and advice at any time during the day (not just first thing in the morning, to avoid the 8am or Monday morning scramble). However, this surgery routinely closes online appointment and eConsult submissions once its own internal quota is reached, at an arbitrary point during the day. This effectively shuts patients out before the end of core hours and goes against the government’s access requirements. Patients are not informed in advance when online access will close. Restricting digital access like this is neither transparent nor convenient, and it is not aligned with current NHS access policy. The result is a system designed to fail anyone who is working, has caring responsibilities, or is unable to submit an eConsult first thing in the morning, leaving many patients without a fair opportunity to get the care they need. Also, they still don’t allow to book appointments via the NHS app either. It’s really not the COVID era any longer! It’s a shame because the Doctors and nurses themselves are great, and this ruthless uncaring system lets this Surgery practice down in a massive way (and my rating reflects only the admin system rather than the medical staff)
