Author: Helen Carpenter
Date: 4 months ago
Rating: 4 stars
It's diametrically opposite from the medical practice whom I was previously with. The telephone system operates a callback service; meaning even if you're low down in a queue, you can get on with doing other things whilst you wait. The length of time you wait for a routine appointment is probably on a par with most other surgeries, however, I specifically requested a combined blood-test/G.P. appointment, and my wait was only around 3 weeks. Definitely very reasonable. The practice itself is very impressive. They complete small procedures on-site, and there are also specialists in various disciplines. Once at the practice, things run like clockwork. Today, my blood test appointment was a whole 2 minutes late! However, the HCA, (Kay) made very short work of finding my vein, and I was out within 5 minutes. This is incredibly impressive, as I'm not the easiest person to take blood from, even by very experienced phlebotomists. It wasn't rushed at all, just efficient. This theme carried through to my appointment with my G.P., Dr Alexandra Baker. The previous patient left her consulting room about 10 minutes before my appointment, which allowed Dr Baker to tie up loose ends from her appointment, and prepare for mine. Already, my notes were on one of her screens and a recent letter from my Consultant was on another screen; pre-empting queries I may have had. Dr Baker was friendly, genuinely interested, professional, and I left not feeling as if I'd been rushed out, of somehow short-changed in any way. Her demeanor isn't in any way patronising, which can be the case where there's an imbalance of knowledge/"power". I left with the outcomes I was expecting, and hoping for, when I went in. The entire medical practice has a feeling of serenity about it. I'm not in any way an anxious patient, although if I were, I think the whole atmosphere is very calming. The whole place has been well thought through and planned, it hasn't been cobbled together and is completely fit for purpose. I'm only relatively new at the practice, as I had nearly a year's hiatus due to a serious medical condition taking me away from the area. However, so far, I can't fault my treatment. My Dad's treatment, hasn't been as positive, which I do feel a little guilty about as I suggested he joined this practice. He had a routine blood test which picked up that his HbA1c was borderline pre-diabetic. I'm diabetic myself, plus the blood test results come back highlighting any causes for concern. Unfortunately, this wasn't flagged up by his G.P. No phone call. No letter. Nothing. Weeks passed, then he was invited to join a series of classes for newly diagnosed diabetics/pre-diabetics. In the interim period, I'd had chats with him about what changes he could make, as it seemed nothing was forthcoming from his G.P., as I'd have hoped it would be. Yes, appropriate action was taken in terms of enrolling my Dad on the "healthier diabetes" course, sadly the opportunity was missed to promptly discuss his abnormal blood result. He's not a hypochondriac, so it was really water off a duck's back, (he's 85 and has seen a lot of life!) If it was a patient that had anxiety about their health, this would have possibly heightened their concerns. In my opinion, and his, this was remiss of his, (new) G.P. His experience of St. Stephen's Gate Medical Practice, not mine, is what's dropped the 🌟.
