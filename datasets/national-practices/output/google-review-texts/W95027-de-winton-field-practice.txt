Author: Kev Green
Date: a year ago
Rating: 5 stars
I’m relatively new at the practice and joined with trepidation because of continuity of care. I need’nt have worried. From my now numerous interactions I have nothing but high praise for the practice which is immaculately presented and without question the cleanest health care facility I’ve experienced. The receptionist are always well presented, polite, and more importantly, eager to help. Ignoring the obvious early morning phone call rush, (not the fault of the practice) the receptionists direct you to the right person to see, and the best times that suit you if at all possible. The GPs are of a high standard. Dr Jenkins is empathetic along with being exceptionally diligent and thoughtful. You can’t say that about many these days. A mention has to be given to Dr Orr for her kind and caring demeanor, again, a quality not all GPs possess. There has to be a shoutout to “old school” nurse Sam. What a delight. She puts you at ease quickly. Not an easy task for demanding patients. I speak as I find, and I recognise the grumbles about parking, which ironically is one thing out of the practices control, and I acknowledge that not all patients will feel as I do. But that won’t sway me. I tend to know a thing or two about healthcare and I know for certainty that Tonypandy Health Centre is a shining example of how a practice can and should be run. Well done to all involved. Regards, Kev
