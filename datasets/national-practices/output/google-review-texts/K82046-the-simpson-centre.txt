Been registered here a very long time. Since moving to their new premises the surgery has improved significantly, though were always better than most. The surgery are very responsive to all queries online or via phone, and call you back straight away with clear information and direction. They call you with results promptly also. I have never struggled to get appointments for myself or child. Reception team are polite and helpful overall. The in-house nurses are great and efficient meaning that you can have appointments at surgery rather than going to larger centres or the hospital for many things. Premises are clean, spacious and function very efficiently with a chemist attached. Dr Robinson, Dr Owen and Dr Younas have all been very good and I've had excellent care during pregnancy and with my baby. Having seen the care at another local surgery I can wholeheartedly say the Simpson Centre is a very good practice.
