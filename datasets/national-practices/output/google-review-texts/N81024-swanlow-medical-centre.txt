Author: Gemma Louise
Date: a year ago
Rating: 5 stars
I've had a number of medical issues over the last few months requiring blood tests, hospital referrals and being seen by several different GPs at swanlow medical centre. I cannot fault the service I've received. Yes there are some wait times but that's not a reflection on the swanlow medical staff but a reflection of how underresourced the NHS is. Reception team - always been polite and really helpful. GP's / Nurses - have been amazing, despite having a number of medical issues over the last few months, they've treated me with respect, care and consideration. Online form via NHS - I absolutely love having this as an option as it allows you to submit details without the need to be on the phone waiting. I can see how it can be difficult for those not tech savvy or perhaps the older generation but for me personally, this works really well. I've recently been able to have a back and forth conversation with a GP via text message - form submitted at 8.08am and advice provided by 8.48am - service was excellent and the GP was really kind and helpful.
