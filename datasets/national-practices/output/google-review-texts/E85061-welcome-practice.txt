Author: Daniella Kay
Date: 3 months ago
Rating: 2 stars
As a new patient at The Welcome Practice, there are several things I appreciate and a few important areas that I believe need improvement. I understand that the practice sees many patients daily and that the staff work very hard, but it’s important to share honest feedback so that patient care can continue to improve. Firstly, the punctuality of the GP practitioners needs urgent improvement. Patients should not be waiting long after their appointment time, especially vulnerable patients. Waiting more than 10 minutes past the scheduled time already feels too long — and during my first new patient appointment, I waited 45 minutes, which is unacceptable. While I understand that some appointments may occasionally run over, such long delays should not be a regular experience. Improving punctuality would greatly improve patient comfort, reassurance, and trust in the service. Secondly, the admin process needs to be more prompt and consistent. Whether it’s medications, sick notes, prescriptions, or other health issues, practitioners should make sure that patients are offered clear alternatives when something is unavailable or when we are unsure of our options. Communication is key, and patients need to feel fully informed. Ensuring that practitioners take time to understand and prioritise patients’ needs is extremely important, especially for those who are newly registered. I also feel the practice would benefit from maintaining a calm, supportive, and organised atmosphere. Every patient has different needs and copes differently, and the environment plays a huge part in making people feel safe, respected, and comfortable. On a very positive note, I want to highlight Amina at reception, who was exceptional. She was caring, helpful, and supportive throughout my registration process. She assisted me with setting up access to my medical records online, answered all my questions at reception, kept me updated about my appointments, and helped with my prescriptions. She is genuinely lovely, and the practice needs more receptionists like her — her kindness and professionalism truly stand out. Overall, I hope the practice takes this feedback seriously and works to improve punctuality, communication, and overall patient support. As patients, we have the right to share our honest experiences, and I believe these improvements would help transform the service into a true five-star experience, especially for new patients joining the practice. Because of the quality of my new patient consultation and the exceptional support from Amina at reception — I am giving this practice 2 out of 5 stars. These were the only reasons I did not give a lower rating. Without those two experiences, my review would have reflected much less. After looking at the clinic’s online reviews, I noticed that many other patients have also shared negative experiences, with a large number of one- and two-star ratings. This shows I am not the only person concerned about the standards and reputation of the practice. As a new patient leaving this review, I truly believe management needs to take these issues seriously and work on them urgently. Improving punctuality, communication, admin efficiency, and overall patient care is essential if the practice wants to rebuild trust and provide a service that meets a five-star standard. Thanks, Daniella Sandja
