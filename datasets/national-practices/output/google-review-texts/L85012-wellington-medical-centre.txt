Practice response date: a year ago
Practice response:
Dear Chris Blatchly, We're sorry to hear you are unhappy with our phone lines and felt it necessary to leave a negative review. Our phone lines are on emergency calls only between 12pm - 2pm each day because our staff need a lunch break. We receive around 300 phone calls per day so to keep the lines open at all times would mean our staff don't get a chance to go out to have a break during their working day. We do however always have staff covering this period so the phones are not completely shut off, you are still able to get through to us for urgent or emergency calls and the phone message does state this when you call during these times, so if you urgently need to get hold of the Practice while our emergency line is on you are able. This is the same situation when the phone message says we are at capacity. Unfortunately we are an incredibly busy practice and receive up to 300 medical requests from patients each day and when all our appointments have been used we put this message on the phones to advise patients that we will not be able to offer an on the day appointment before their call so they are aware. You can still get through on the phone for other queries or for emergencies if needed, or you can use our online form which stays on throughout the day from 7:30am until 5pm. When we are closed for staff training you can again still get through to us on our emergency line if your call is urgent, as stated in the phone message. Our Practice is closed for one afternoon per month for essential staff training and this has been approved by the ICB and is the case for all practices in Somerset. Closure dates for the year are always advertised on our website or on our Practice newsletter. We would highly recommend having a look at our monthly newsletter which is available in reception or on our facebook page, as this includes important information about when we're going to be closed, what our emergency line is and further advice with this, among other things. We apologise you don't think this is treating patients fairly, however we do everything possible to advise our opening times, closure days, explain our emergency line etc for patients so they understand why we do this and why it is necessary. There is always a way to get in touch with us if you need to urgently and our duty GP each day will always provide advice or signpost to the most appropriate service for you if required. As mentioned above we are an extremely busy practice with almost 18,000 patients but we do have to consider our staff welfare as well. We hope this helps explain our procedures to you in a bit more detail. Many thanks Management Team
