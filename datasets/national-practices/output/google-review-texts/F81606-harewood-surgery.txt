Author: Juergen Neuhoff
Date: Edited 6 months ago
Rating: 1 star
This surgery has been seriously let down by the NHS lately, despite the doctors best intentions to treat the patients. The doctors have been unable for months to offer me a treatment for a chronic leg pain and vertigo condition, hence, upon my request, they tried to do a referral to a NHS pain management / neurologist, but the latter service has virtually collapsed. They also tried in vain to do a referral to an MRI scan which got rejected by the NHS. The only alternative is to spend a lot of money for a private consultant. Or to go to an Urgent Treatment Centre in Clacton or Colchester. And as regards finding the right medicines: A better option nowadays is to consult a pharmacist, not the doctor, to find the right combination of medications. The doctors at the Harewood Surgery simply haven't been able to find the right prescriptions for my chronic condition for almost a year now. Also, why has booking a timely appointment with a doctor at the Harewood Surgery become more difficult now, as is the case with most surgeries nowadays? Why on earth could you only book an appointment in the morning? They used to have an appointment calendar. And why only telephone appointments in the case of Dr Coggan? Update April 2, 2025: The doctors, nurses, and receptionist are friendly and professional, well done here! But again, this surgery is being let down by the NHS. Had another referral to an NHS specialist rejected a month ago, was never told about this, only found out recently. And the surgery's appointment system is getting worse, it now uses a poor quality 3rd party online service based on accurx.nhs.uk. You are not able to book urgent appointments anymore, unless you have online access and/or a smartphone, this can be dangerous for patients when you are offline. Was told yesterday that it would take 3 to 4 weeks for an appointment with Dr Coggan. Update Sep 16, 2025: The Doctors and nurses are doing a fantastic job, despite the poor circumstances and underfunding of the NHS. However, the practice management needs improvement. The practice manager sometimes mixes up online requests. E.g. online responses mentions medications which we didn't ask about, while ignoring other requests. Also made wrong claims about not knowing specialists for referrals. Finally, the NHS and accurx software used by this surgery is very unprofessional and user-unfriendly and is no substitute
Practice response date: a year ago
Practice response:
I’m sorry you are unhappy with the service. We have to have a limit on the appointments we can have in a day for both patient and clinician safety. We therefore have a set amount of appointments available from 8am, once they are full there is no more availability. The members of staff who are not doctors work extremely hard and keep the surgery going, which is difficult to see as a patient I understand. Please discuss your case with the practice manager as I believe this to be unfair but would like to discuss privately.

Author: Lynda Roberts
Date: Edited a year ago
Rating: 3 stars
This is by far the best surgery I have ever been to. The staff are friendly and welcoming, and take time to listen to patients. UPDATE jan 25. I am very sad to see this previously amazing surgery go downhill fast since covid. Dr Coggan is the best GP I have ever seen but these days it’s impossible to get an appointment with him unless you can wait for 2 weeks, and then they’re all taken! Emergencies can phone on the day for a call back and Dr Coggan is very good at responding if he’s there. When my husband attended with chest pains and saw a nurse practitioner there was no ECG or bloods done and consequently we had to call an ambulance a couple of hours later and he was taken in with a heart attack. I am grateful to the ambulance crew but the delay of several hours could have had an unthinkable outcome. Now that Dr Coggan is only there on alternate weeks it will be even more impossible to see him. The receptionists at this surgery are lovely, as is Nurse Nancy, but generally the current state makes us afraid to be ill!
