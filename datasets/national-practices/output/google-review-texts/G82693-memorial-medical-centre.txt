Author: Emily Cooper
Date: 7 months ago
Rating: 5 stars
My family and I have been registered with the Memorial Medical Centre for nearly twenty years and I cannot song the praises of all the staff highly enough. Always kind, professional and incredibly hard working to meet the needs of the many, many patients they deal with daily. It is fair to say that this is a busy surgery but that is the same of most surgeries up and down the country, the NHS is stretched beyond its limits and desperately needs more funding. This is most certainly not the fault of the staff and I have witnessed some terrible behaviour from patients over the years which is unforgivable. The doctors at the Memorial Medical centre are friendly, knowledgeable and super efficient. Sadly we have had to make many calls on them over the years and we have always been listened to and advised in full. One of the doctors suspected Leukaemia in our daughter and within 24 hours, she was tested and admitted to the Marsden for treatment. That doctor saved her life. It’s also almost forgotten now, how hard the staff worked during the COVID19 pandemic. They took dangerous risks every day to keep their community safe. They still are the heroes we all clapped for every week and in my opinion, they all deserve a golden handshake and an honour from the king.
