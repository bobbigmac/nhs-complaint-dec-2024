Honestly, as a Chinese international student, this practice showed me a cold, overly sensitive, and extreme side of British people. Most of the time, I understand that the efficiency of the UK healthcare system is low, and I’m willing to adapt to that. However, when I was changing my address, the receptionist was very rigid and insisted that the proof must show both the address and my name on the same page of the contract. I couldn’t find such a document on the website, and I was anxious and didn’t want to waste time, so I asked whether they could make an exception if I provided the official website information and the payment page instead. He refused. I became impatient and left angrily. In the end, I followed their requirements, resolved the issue, and even received a confirmation email. What I never expected was that a week later, I received a letter from the practice manager removing me from the patient list. On the surface they appear polite and friendly, but behind the scenes they report and complain without any communication or notice. This lack of tolerance and complete absence of prior explanation was shocking. They do not seem to see themselves as doctors or public service administrators, but instead use the privilege of operating as an independent GP practice to act like businesspeople serving customers. Truly impressive.
