Practice response date: 2 weeks ago
Practice response:
Thank you for your feedback. Sports medical clearance forms are specialised medical documents that require a clinician with appropriate experience, training and indemnity coverage in sports medicine to review and sign them. Many GP practices will not sign such documents at all, precisely because they fall outside routine NHS GP work and carry specific medico-legal implications. Within our practice, these forms are reviewed and signed only by the clinician who has the appropriate training and indemnity to undertake this work. Unfortunately, that clinician is currently away, and for governance and insurance reasons other members of the team are not authorised to complete these documents. This is not a matter of unwillingness to help but one of professional responsibility and regulatory compliance. It is also important to clarify that this request relates to non-NHS, private administrative work, which GP practices are not contractually obliged to provide. While we do try to assist patients where possible, we are fully within our rights to decline or defer such requests when appropriate cover is not available. We understand that your son has travel plans in the coming days; however, last-minute requests unfortunately cannot be fulfilled particularly for non-NHS documentation that requires a suitably qualified clinician to review and sign. For future requests of this nature, we would recommend allowing sufficient notice so that the appropriate clinician is available to assess the form properly and medical arranged. Kind regards, Practice Management Team
