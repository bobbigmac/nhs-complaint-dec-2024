Author: Ian O’Dowd
Date: 4 months ago
Rating: 5 stars
I transferred to Wragby Surgery approximately 12 months ago now and my only regret is not doing it sooner. I’ve found the receptionists to be welcoming, friendly and extremely helpful. The little things they do, like acknowledging you when you are waiting, and a ‘I’ll be with you in a moment’ - Excellent. Every member of staff you are there to see comes in person to collect you from the waiting area. (Unlike other practices that rely on an LED screen to send you in search of the correct door down a long corridor.) Once in the treatment rooms I’ve found every member of staff to be extremely knowledgeable, highly skilled and totally professional. I would gladly recommend Wragby Surgery.
