I have been registered with this GP practice for over 17 years, and unfortunately my experience in recent years has declined significantly. Getting an appointment is extremely difficult, and communication with reception staff is often stressful and unclear, with little reassurance about callbacks or next steps. While the doctors themselves have always been professional and helpful once seen, the organisation and administration are very poor. Prescription handling is a major issue, with poor coordination with the pharmacy regularly causing delays of up to 48 hours, even for antibiotics. There are also QR codes displayed in the practice for submitting suggestions or complaints, but these do not work. The fact that broken feedback systems remain displayed without staff awareness is concerning. Overall, I now dread having to contact this GP, which is disappointing after being a patient for nearly two decades.
