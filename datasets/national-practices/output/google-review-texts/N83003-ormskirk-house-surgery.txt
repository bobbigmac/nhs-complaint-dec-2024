⸻ I have been suffering with ongoing back pain for over two years, and the care I have received from this surgery has been nothing short of dismissive and unacceptable. Despite repeatedly attending appointments and explaining how severe the pain is, I have been continually fobbed off with the same response – physiotherapy. No scans, no proper investigation into the cause of the pain, and no real effort to actually diagnose what is going on. Just the same cycle of being sent away again and again. My most recent appointment with Dr Sword was particularly concerning. My back was visibly swollen and red hot to the touch, yet he did not even examine it. No physical assessment whatsoever. Instead, he simply told me to see a physiotherapist again. I left feeling completely dismissed and shocked that a doctor could ignore such obvious symptoms without even checking them. After this appointment I contacted the surgery to complain because I felt my concerns were not taken seriously. I was then booked for physiotherapy, but the next available appointment was three weeks later, leaving me to continue suffering in pain with no treatment or support while waiting. After waiting those three weeks, I arrived 10 minutes early at 8:20am, checked in on the screen at the surgery, and sat waiting. After 45 minutes, I went to reception only to be told that my physiotherapy appointment was actually at a completely different location. This was never communicated to me when the appointment was booked. The app stated the appointment was at the surgery, and the check-in screen at the surgery also showed my appointment there. So after waiting weeks for help, my time was completely wasted due to yet another communication failure. Sadly, my experience is not the only concerning one within my family. My nan attended this surgery complaining of chest pain, dizziness, headaches, nausea and problems with her eyesight. These are extremely serious symptoms, yet her blood pressure was not even taken during the appointment. Just one week later she suffered a major stroke and has now lost movement and speech. When she was later assessed in hospital, doctors also discovered she has Aortic stenosis, a serious heart condition that had also been completely missed. Situations like this make it feel as though patients at this surgery are simply being dismissed rather than properly assessed. When serious symptoms are not investigated and basic checks are not carried out, the consequences can be devastating for families. This surgery used to have a good reputation, but the standard of care now feels like it has drastically declined. Patients are being dismissed, not examined properly, and left waiting weeks for help while serious conditions are overlooked. At this point I have completely lost confidence in the care being provided here. If these issues are not taken seriously, I will have no choice but to escalate this further through a formal complaint to NHS England and the Care Quality Commission, because patients deserve far better than what is currently happening here
