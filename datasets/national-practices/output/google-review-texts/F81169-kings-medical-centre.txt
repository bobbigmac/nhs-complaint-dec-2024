Rating: 5 stars
This is a wonderful GP practice. As a family with two young children, we have accessed their services several times over the last few years, for issues with varying degrees of acuity. The online request forms are great, and are always responded to very efficiently and are triaged appropriately. (It would be even better if the forms were accessible to submit during hours when the practice is closed for convenience, but I understand why this may not be available for patient safety reasons.) Today, I had submitted a request for an appointment for an issue that had been picked up at my child’s health visitor review, and was offered an appointment the same day. I have seen a variety of different doctors and the practice nurse over our time here, all of whom have listened attentively and acted promptly on any issues. Appointments are almost always on time or close to it; as a doctor working in secondary care, I know how incredibly hard this is to achieve! This team are excelling in patient-facing care; I rarely write reviews but felt compelled to after being consistently impressed with their service.
