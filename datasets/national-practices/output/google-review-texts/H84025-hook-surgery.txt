I’m very new to this clinic and this was my first interaction, but it left me feeling uncomfortable. I visited because I was locked out of the repeat prescription app. Once it started working again, I asked about ordering my prescription and was told my records hadn’t transferred yet, which I understand. However, when I mentioned HRT, the receptionist commented that I was “too young” for it. This felt inappropriate, medical opinions should come from clinicians, not reception staff. I went through a long and difficult process to be diagnosed with perimenopause and to access HRT, so hearing a dismissive comment like that from a receptionist was genuinely upsetting and offensive. Ignorance is bliss!!! I can’t reply to the reply the surgery has provided. I would really like to know the reason why you are not addressing the real issues. It’s about your comment regarding my age and nothying to do with processes. Ignoring the facts will not make your actions right!
Practice response date: 3 weeks ago
Practice response:
Dear Miss Minty We are sorry that you feel this way. Our reception team are non-clinical staff and follow practice protocols when advising patients about appointment and/or medication requirements. They are not making medical decisions but relaying the process that needs to be followed. In this instance the member of staff was trying to be helpful and explain why a GP appointment would be needed. You were being advised of the most appropriate way to request the medication which was through e-consult, the doctor would then decide if you need to be seen or the mediation can be issued.
