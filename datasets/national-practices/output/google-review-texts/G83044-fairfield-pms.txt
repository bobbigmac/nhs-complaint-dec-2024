I am in a wheelchair that I cannot propell myself. I have had bloods done at the practice before, because it’s MUCH MUCH easier to get there than the hospital, and my husband (who is our only source of income) doesn’t have to take a day off to take me there, he can do it on his break. So in the last two months I’ve needed bloods done again, and I was told by the health care professional who requested them, who works at the practice , that I can have them done there. I called to make an appointment the first month, and the receptionist told me they only do bloods for the elderly. Ok. Husband and I struggled to get to the hospital- there usually isn’t space on the bus for my wheelchair as there are baby buggies. Needed bloods again. Told healthcare professional what happened, got assured that they can be done at the practice. Called to make appointment, got to speak to another receptionist. Well, this receptionist decided to treat me like an absolute stupid idiot, and said that it doesn’t matter what I was told by the healthcare personnel, they don’t do bloods there at all. She just decided then and there. So, the medical staff is great, but apparently the receptionists decide what level of care you deserve and if they don’t like your voice they will make your life unnecessarily difficult. Really puts me off going to the GP.
