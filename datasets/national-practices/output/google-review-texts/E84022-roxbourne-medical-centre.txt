Author: Maya Constantin

Rating: 5 stars
I visited the surgery to request medication for my mum, who has dementia and unfortunately had a fall on Sunday. I spoke with the gentleman at reception with the ponytail, and he was incredibly polite, calm, and efficient. He suggested submitting the request through the online system. I was a bit hesitant at first, but when I explained the situation and my mum’s condition, he kindly helped me complete the request on my phone — it literally took about two minutes. To my surprise, a doctor replied shortly after with a link to book an appointment, and I managed to secure one for the very next day. I was genuinely impressed with how quickly everything worked. I would definitely recommend trying the new online system — it’s actually very easy. And if you’re unsure, the reception gentleman with the ponytail is extremely helpful and will guide you through it.
