This is a late review but one worth givinv but last year I was very poorly and was having a high fever, ear and throat pain but with no other flu symptoms. I rang through to the gp and was told that it could be covid. They recommended that I follow the usual protocols. I also shared that I was having pains through the night when I went to pee so they asked me to take a sample in. By the time I got to the gp, I was freezing and could no longer pee even though i had the urge. I asked if i could see the gp even though I didn't have an appointment, my ears and throat were also hurting. Even though Dr Rasheed had an appointment coming up, he kindly agreed to see me briefly. After examining me, he decided to send me to the hospital but agreed that I wasnt fit to drive. My body ached all over and I was tired, almost falling asleep waiting for an ambulance. When he nor Debbie could get through to an ambulance he asked if family could take me because he didn’t want to wait much longer. Luckily, my brother was available and took me to the hospital. By that time I couldn't walk and was assisted to A&E from the car by a kind police officer while my brother parked. I was seen promptly and within a short time I was on iv antibiotics. The diagnosed early sepsis and I spent 7 nights in hospital receiving treatment. I spent another 3 weeks at home recovering with additional oral antibiotics. I am forever grateful to the team at Parkhouse Surgery but even more grateful to Debbie who asked the doctor to see me and especially to Dr Rasheed who took the time to examine me and made the right call. I can proudly say without fear of opposition that Dr Rasheed saved my life, not just by making the right decision to send me for further treatment but also for saying I wasn't fit to drive because I was incoherent by the time I got to the hospital and have no memory of the journey to the hospital. I dont remember much of what happened as I deteriorated quickly, but my brother said I was confused and almost incoherent other than crying out for pain and saying I felt like I was going to die. Thank you, Dr Rasheed from myself and my family. We are forever thankful! By taking the time to listen and agreeing to examine me even though you didn't have an appointment to do so, you showed care beyond your job role. You and Debbie even walked me to my brother's car and helped me in. May you forever be blessed! This is a great example of going above and beyond what would reasonably be expected. Thanks Debbie for always listening and being empathetic.
