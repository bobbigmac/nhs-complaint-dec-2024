Author: Euan Halliwell
Date: 7 months ago
Rating: 5 stars
I’ve been registered with Banchory GP for nearly my whole life. From my very first jabs as a baby, to helping with my physio issues, the removal of a concerning skin tag, and aiding in the diagnosis of my ulcerative colitis. Every time I’ve needed them, I’ve felt cared for and respected, from the receptionists to the nurses and the doctors. The NHS is one of the UK’s greatest achievements. It helps us all, regardless of financial capabilities. However, its funding is limited, and patients need to be prioritised by the triage advisers at reception. I know some of the negative reviews probably come from a moment of anger from people who are frustrated with how things work, but GPs have to prioritise people who are most in need. That is not an easy or pleasant responsibility, and the staff deserve far more credit for managing this pressure and, disappointingly, facing abuse at times from patients unhappy about not being aided immediately and as they see fit. It can be easy to criticise when you’re stressed or unwell, but I’ve only ever seen a team trying their best to help people. They have always been wonderful, and I am exceptionally grateful for the practice and all those who work there!
