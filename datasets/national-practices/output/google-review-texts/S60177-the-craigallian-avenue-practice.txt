Author: Mahesh Dillimuni -Journalist

Rating: 1 star
I have had a very distressing experience trying to access GP care at this practice. For several days, I repeatedly called in the mornings to book a GP appointment, as instructed, but the phone line only accepts a very limited number of calls and then cuts off. This made it practically impossible to speak to anyone. I also sent an email explaining my medical conditions and requesting help to arrange an appointment, but I did not receive any response. Due to this ongoing difficulty, I attended the surgery in person. Despite clearly explaining that I had been unable to get through by phone and that I have multiple ongoing medical conditions, I was refused an advance appointment and repeatedly told to “call in the morning,” even though I explained that this system was not accessible to me. I also brought printed medical summaries and politely asked reception staff to pass them to a GP for review. This request was refused. I found this very concerning, as reception staff should not act as a barrier to clinical assessment. I understand GP practices are under pressure, but patients with long-term and complex health issues still need reasonable access to care. This experience felt dismissive and lacking in compassion. I hope the practice reviews its appointment system and reception procedures to ensure patients are treated with dignity and appropriate support.
