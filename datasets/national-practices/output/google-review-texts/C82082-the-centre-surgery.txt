Thank you for your review. Patient’s are expected to make the necessary arrangements to arrive for their appointment on time, for the courtesy of our staff and other patients. Our clinicians see patients in back-to-back clinics with 10-minute appointments, therefore if you were 10 minutes late, you would have missed your appointment. Our policy regarding latecomers was put in place last year, following daily occurrences of clinics running behind because patients were turning up late. This has a knock-on effect for patients who do turn up on time, and are then delayed being seen due to clinicians trying to accommodate latecomers. Most surgeries have a similar policy, and we would encourage you to be on time for future appointments to avoid being told to rebook. We do ask patients to respect each other’s space, and stand back if there is already a patient at the reception desk to allow them privacy. However, if patients do not feel comfortable providing a brief summary of their issue at the reception desk, there are signs which state they can ask to discuss their issues in a side room with a receptionist.
