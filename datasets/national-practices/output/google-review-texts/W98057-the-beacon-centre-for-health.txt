Avoid This Practice at All Costs – Utterly Incompetent and Frustrating This has to be one of the worst doctors' surgeries I've ever dealt with. There’s only ever ONE doctor on, and they’re consistently running late—we're talking delays of 30 minutes to over an hour as standard. It's as if they think your time means nothing. Appointments? Good luck. You’re told to ring back weeks later at exactly 8am, where you’ll inevitably face a jammed phone line, a pointless queue system, and a receptionist who tells you—surprise!—there are no appointments left. It’s a vicious cycle of inefficiency and poor management that leaves patients stranded without care. When you do finally get in to see someone (after jumping through hoops), you're met with junior-level doctors who seem completely naive and out of their depth. There’s a clear lack of experience and confidence—it doesn’t inspire trust when you feel like you're being diagnosed by someone who still needs supervision. This place is a complete shambles. Understaffed, overbooked, and run with zero urgency or regard for patient wellbeing. Avoid if you can. They are failing their community.
