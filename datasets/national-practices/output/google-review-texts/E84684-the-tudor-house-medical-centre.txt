Author: Moggy Maz
Date: a week ago
Rating: 5 stars
Over the past few years, both my parents have been patients at this surgery, and I honestly cannot speak highly enough of the care they have received. My father was becoming increasingly unwell over time, and my mother continues to have a number of health challenges. Throughout all of this, the team at the practice have been incredibly attentive, responsive, and open-minded. Whenever concerns arose, they were prompt to get in touch, willing to listen, and receptive to suggestions about how best to support my parents’ needs. What stood out to me most was how collaborative the approach always felt. The doctors worked closely with me to ensure my parents were comfortable and that their medications were reviewed and adjusted where necessary. It never felt like a rushed or transactional process — it felt like genuine care. While every GP we encountered was excellent, I spoke most frequently with Dr Shikha Gosain. I know how incredibly busy GPs are, yet whenever I spoke with her she gave me her full and undivided attention. I never once felt hurried off the phone. She is extremely knowledgeable and consistently offered thoughtful, practical suggestions when we needed to adjust my parents’ medications. Over the last few years, I quietly carried the awareness that my father’s health was declining. Dr Gosain understood this too, and she handled those conversations with a level of sensitivity and compassion that meant a great deal to me. My father passed away in January. What I will never forget is that on the Monday morning following his passing, Dr Gosain called me personally to offer her condolences. We spoke for around fifteen minutes. It was such a small gesture in one sense, but in another it meant everything. In moments of grief, kindness like that stays with you. I remain deeply grateful — not only to Dr Shikha Gosain, but to the entire team at the surgery — for the care, patience and humanity they have shown my family. Mahnaz Khan
