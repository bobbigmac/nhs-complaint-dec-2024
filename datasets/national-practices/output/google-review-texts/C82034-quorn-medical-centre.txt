Author: Ayla Dyett
Date: a month ago
Rating: 4 stars
Since moving I have had a lovely experience with this surgery. All of the receptionists are kind and lovely. When I was really ill on one occasion, one of the receptionists went out of her way to go and get water to give to me in the waiting room. The doctors, nurses, and midwives are all so friendly as well. It’s such a nice feeling to feel supported, especially during pregnancy. My only feedback is to the surgery’s overall booking system. I had moved from a surgery in which you were able to book appointments via the NHS app. Both telephone and in-person. It really simplified the process and the system seemed to work really well despite the surgery being so busy. Here, if I request an appointment via the NHS app, it isn’t done like that and you are told to call in. This can be hard when you may be busy, with a newborn like myself, or if the surgery is closed on the only days you are free. I assume the centre joined the NHS app due to the national contractual obligations to have an online booking system but I only wish it was made more use of.
