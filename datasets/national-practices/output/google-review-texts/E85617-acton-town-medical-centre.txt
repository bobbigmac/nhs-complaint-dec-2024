I haven't used the Acton Medical Centre in years as I had a few really bad experiences with them over the years. Recently I answered a call, was invited for health check and blood work. The receptionist was very polite and accomodating, and found the right date and time for me, so I went ahead with it. Upon arrival I was surprised to see an empty waiting room, and I was called in by the nurse right on time. Both things were unheard of during my previous visits. The nurse, Rebecca, was very polite, knowledgeable, professional and on top of that also very friendly. She did everything she had to do quickly and professionally. While waiting to be seen, I couldn't help but hear a few conversations with the receptionists, who were very polite and offered same day appointments to the callers. I have to say that the transformation the Medical Centre has experienced is astonishing. Previously it didn't deserve more than 2 stars (thanks to Sachiko, the absolutely brilliant receptionist, otherwise it'd have been 0 stars if possible). My experience today was brilliant and I will definitely reach out to the centre again if the need be, instead of sorting things out privately
