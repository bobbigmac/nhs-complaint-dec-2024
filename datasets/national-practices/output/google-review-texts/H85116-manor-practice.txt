I have been with this surgery for a long time but I am very disappointed with the service at present. Since the management changed and the previous GPs left the level of service in this practice is very low. Before the new management, the surgery was sending us for a blood test every year. The new management does not bother to do this - I did not have blood test for 14 months and my husband for more than two years until we requested the blood test online. Needless to say that a large part of my request was simply ignored. Not only that, but the bone profile which is important part of the yearly blood test for every patient, was not included in my blood test form for unknown to me reasons, even though I specifically requested calcium levels to be tested. I also requested parathormone (PTH) levels test (after parathyroid adenoma operation) which was also ignored. Who am I to ask for this?! But I was shocked that when the receptionist went to ask the clinician to record this test on my blood test form, the GP recorded “Se thyroid peroxidase Ab conc” and asked the receptionist to tell me that this was the Parathormone test! These are two completely different things! I cannot believe that a qualified doctor can confuse parathormone with thyroid peroxidase?! I did not need the thyroid peroxidase as I do not have autoimmune thyroid disease like Hashimoto’s, etc. So I had to go again and tell them that Parathormone is PTH and this was what I needed! The Indian/Asian receptionist insisted that I did not know and the clinician told her that the thyroid peroxidase was the parathormone! And I went again. I was given an appointment with the nurse practitioner who finally listened to me, asked me if PTH was the parathormone and added it to the blood test form. The blood test results were sent to the surgery the same day I had the test but only two of them were recorded on the NHS app. After a few calls the receptionist told me on 18.03.26 that all the tests arrived on 11.03.26 but they were still staying with a GP! I suppose they forgot to record them. And I made another online request for my test results to be recorded on the NHS app! Most of them were recorded late in the afternoon on 18.03, but not the parathormone result! I really hope this is not the same GP who told the receptionist that the “thyroid peroxidase” is the “parathormone”! If a qualified doctor does not know the difference between them, I am really worried about my health now! I also requested ultrasound of the thyroid gland and explained in the online form the reasons for that. This request was completely ignored! I was not even informed if my request was rejected! I see a GP once or twice a year but I want to see a fully qualified doctor. Since January 2025 I asked for a GP appointment on 05.03.26, which is more than a year since my last appointment. However, I was given appointment with a nurse practitioner, even though I specifically requested to see a doctor. On what basis does the management in Manor Practice decide whose life they could put at risk?! Why am I not entitled to see a doctor? I graduated from three universities; worked as a diplomat in different British embassies abroad; represented a big government organisation at High Court and Appeals Court hearings in London; worked really hard and paid all my national insurance contributions and taxes during my entire working life. Only after I submitted a written complaint to the surgery I was allowed to see a qualified doctor instead of the nurse practitioner again. The doctor was very helpful, very knowledgeable and sorted all my requests which were previously ignored.
