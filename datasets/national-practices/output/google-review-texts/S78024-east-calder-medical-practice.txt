Author: Sarah

Rating: 5 stars
I moved to East Calder in 2019 and I've had nothing but good experiences with ECMP. This is a surgery that is over-stretched with all the new developments in the area and while they're under a lot of pressure, they still manage things very well. I had a baby in 2022 and the level of care for both myself and my daughter was amazing and continues to be. As long as you're organised and leave plenty of time to book in for routine appointments you're all good and for genuine emergencies, the reception staff are always very kind and quick to find a way to help and will go above and beyond, often calling back to offer additional support. I honestly think the staff here do an incredible job in challenging conditions and I rate them very highly. As with all surgeries, some of the GPs are better at listening than others, but the staff at ECMP are under appreciated for the service they provide in such a busy location.
