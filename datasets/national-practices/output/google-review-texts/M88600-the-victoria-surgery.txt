I have to say the reception staff at this surgery are extremely rude and unnecessarily difficult to deal with. Once you finally get to speak with a doctor, everything is fine — professional, respectful, no issues at all. But with reception, it feels like they want to act as if they are the doctors themselves, questioning and interfering far beyond their role. My message to them is simple: stick to your job — answering calls and booking appointments. Do not overstep your limits. Another serious issue is the inconsistent way patients are treated. When members of the Asian community come in and speak with the reception staff in their own language, suddenly the service becomes excellent, polite, and helpful. Why is this not the same for everyone? Reception staff must treat all patients equally and professionally, regardless of who they are. The reception team needs proper training in respect, professionalism, and equality. And if they cannot provide that, they should be replaced with staff who can.
