Author: J S
Date: 10 months ago
Rating: 1 star
Where to start. I have been a patent here for a number of years and there are a couple of really great doctors here, Dr. Butcher and Dr. Arkley are a real credit to their profession. The dispensary team are also really lovely, the lady who used to work at boots is so proficient and friendly, and the other lady is also very helpful and caring. They are a joy to deal with. However, this is all over shadowed by the abject horror of the reception team. To get access to any of the above, you have to navigate a gauntlet of toxic, ill informed, entitled and bullying reception staff. They frequently give out completely incorrect information and then blame the patients, there is zero accountability for mistakes made and the practice manager is evasive and unaccountable. There is one particularly bad receptionist (mentioned in other reviews) who is so hostile and bullying that I can’t for the life of me work out how she continues to be employed in a care giving role. I recently was refused an appointment for an issue that she said wasn’t their responsibility. After 2 months of this saga and 3 refused appointment requests, I had to screenshot the nhs website to prove it was in fact their responsibility. I was then reluctantly given an appointment, with no apology or explanation. I was sent a letter about a change in the service recently and when I phoned up, the receptionist asked who had told me about this change as they wouldn’t have said that. She then did an abrupt 180 when I told her it was a letter from them. I actually feel sick with anxiety at having to make an appointment and I tent to suffer with ailments until they are intolerable, just to avoid having to go through the utter hell of talking to the reception staff or battling with Anima. I have been in tears on numerous occasions just trying to access basic medical care. This bullying, rude and medically ill informed team needs addressing as a matter of urgency. I have been reluctant to write a review for fear of the repercussions next time I have to phone, but then I realised there is literally no way they could be any more hostile and unpleasant that they currently are. The anima appointment system is also completely redundant and if you have job you basically can’t ever use it as it’s only open at for 15 mins in the morning when you are driving to work. There is no alternative to this as the reception team refuse to help if you are unable to use anima. It is such a shame that a good clinical team is being overshadowed so catastrophically by a handful of people who are so unpleasant.
