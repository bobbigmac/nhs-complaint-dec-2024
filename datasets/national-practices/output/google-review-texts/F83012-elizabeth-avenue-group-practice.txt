Positives: - Dr Ramesh is absolutely amazing. - They reply to E-Consults quickly. - The waiting times for appointments aren’t usually long. - If you walk in, the reception team can be helpful. (Caveat below) Negatives: - My experiences with Dr Anita Baker have been unpleasant & lacking in empathy. - You can’t reply when the doctors email you, so you have to do a brand new econsult each time you want to respond which is unnecessarily time consuming. - The reception staff can be a bit useless if you call up. They say they’ll follow up on something for you & you never hear back. Overall: If you’re happy to visit reception in-person when you want to speak to someone and you request a good doctor for your appointments, you’ll have a positive experience here. If you get unlucky with the Doctor you’re assigned, you might have a bad experience here but you can always request a specific person if that happens. If you’re not someone who is technical, you’ll struggle, as they direct you online for everything.
