I recently moved to Macclesfield and registered with Broken Cross Surgery. Based on my experience so far, I do not understand why this practice has such a high rating. In my opinion, the rating does not reflect the actual quality of care or organisation. Today I attended an appointment and checked in at the door exactly as instructed. I waited for almost an hour. When I eventually approached reception to ask why I had not been called, I was told that I had missed my appointment because I had supposedly not checked in. After I challenged this and some time passed, I was informed that the doctor had not noticed my check-in and therefore could no longer see me that day. The reason I waited so long before approaching reception is simple: during my previous appointment at this practice, the doctor was running almost an hour late. At that time, reception confirmed that everything was fine. I therefore assumed the same situation was happening and did not want to interrupt staff unnecessarily. That assumption turned out to be a mistake. I also want to point out that during the entire time I was waiting, no other patients were called in to see this doctor. For this reason, it is unclear why I could not be seen at all, even for a shorter appointment. What makes this situation worse is that no one apologised. The situation was treated as normal. I am writing this review as a warning to others: arriving on time and checking in as instructed does not guarantee that you will actually be seen. I strongly recommend going to reception to make sure the system has registered you correctly.
