Dear Ms Russell, I am sorry to read how this interaction made you feel. Reaching out to your GP practice can be difficult particularly when you’re already feeling vulnerable and the last thing anyone should experience is feeling dismissed or unsupported. Our reception team manage a very high volume of calls each day and follow a structured triage and booking process to ensure appointments are allocated safely and fairly. However, that should never come at the expense of compassion or clear communication. Your feedback highlights how important tone and explanation are, especially when appointments have reached capacity. When same-day appointments are fully booked, our team should explain the alternative options available which may include urgent triage lists, signposting to appropriate services such as the chemist, online consultation forms, NHS 111, or arranging follow-up contact where clinically appropriate. I also note your comments regarding the online form. The intention behind encouraging telephone contact in some circumstances is to ensure urgent concerns are identified quickly and managed safely. I will review the wording on our website to ensure it accurately reflects how patients can best access support and that it does not create confusion or unrealistic expectations.
