Author: Fiona Gaines

Rating: 1 star
I left a 5 star review of this surgery about three and a half years ago, but I have removed it as I would not like to mislead people into thinking this standard has been maintained, because my experience in recent months evidences it has not. Staffing levels have dropped, especially for nurses (although I am told this will improve next month), and this has impacted my husband physically and mentally. His medical needs are such that he requires an appointment at least once a week, but to maintain this we have been informed we have to take him into Salisbury (20 miles away), which is not possible for me, and no alternative has been offered other than to go to the MIU in Shaftesbury. This is not the standard of service expected from a practice that was rated "Good" by the CQC (an organization I will be contacting). Surely some contingency plans to cover basic medical needs should be in place - what has happened to patient care? I have lodged a formal complaint with the surgery. To her credit, the nurse who last saw my husband tried to arrange a visit from the District Nurse, but this has not been deemed viable.
