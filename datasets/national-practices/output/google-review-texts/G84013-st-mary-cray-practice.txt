I wanted to take the time to leave a review because I feel it’s important to reflect my genuine experience, particularly in light of some of the low ratings currently visible. When I registered with St Mary’s Cray Practice after being moved into the area, I felt extremely anxious. I have been relocated multiple times by the council, and as someone with ADHD and complex health needs, that instability has taken a real toll on my mental health. Continuity of care has often been lacking, so I was bracing myself for another difficult experience. Instead, I was met with diligence, kindness, and genuine professionalism. From the outset, the staff were forthcoming, thorough, and compassionate. I felt properly listened to and taken seriously, rather than rushed or dismissed. What stood out most was how carefully my health was reviewed as a whole. My ADHD, mental health, and physical health — including the fact that I have one kidney — were all considered thoughtfully and with care. That level of attentiveness made a huge difference to me. After repeatedly having to explain my situation elsewhere, it was incredibly reassuring to feel that my history had been read, understood, and respected. I am very aware of the pressures facing the NHS, which is why this experience mattered so much. In a system that can sometimes feel fragmented and impersonal, this practice offered care that felt humane, patient, and genuinely supportive. I wanted to share this because my experience has been a positive one, and it deserves to be reflected here. Thank you to the staff at St Mary’s Cray Practice for your professionalism, kindness, and thorough care — it has made a real difference to my wellbeing.
