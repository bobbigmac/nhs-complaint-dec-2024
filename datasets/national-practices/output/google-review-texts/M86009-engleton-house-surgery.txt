I wanted to leave a really positive review after my recent appointment. I was previously registered at another surgery where it was very difficult to get appointments, even when something felt urgent. I was rarely offered appointments in a short space of time and never managed to be seen on the same day. This practice has been completely different from the start. They actually contacted me themselves to arrange my new patient check-up, which already made me feel very welcomed. During my visit, I was seen by Dr Javeria Ikhlaq who was incredibly kind, professional and reassuring. She explained everything clearly and handled what was quite an uncomfortable situation with real care and respect. The nurse who saw me in my first visit (I’m sorry I didn’t catch her name) was also wonderful – very gentle, supportive and made me feel at ease throughout the appointment. I’m genuinely so happy that I’m now registered here. It really feels like I’m in the right place and in very good hands. Thank you to the whole team for the care and professionalism you showed.
