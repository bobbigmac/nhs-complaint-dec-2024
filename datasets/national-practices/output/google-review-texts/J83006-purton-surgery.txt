Author: Michael Post
Date: 7 months ago
Rating: 2 stars
The general service at Purton Surgery is good but their practice of restricting the supply of appointments to manage incoming demand is poor. I’m sure a lot of surgeries do similar things but it’s a fallacy to tackle incoming demand rather than improve supply management. Having to phone at 8:30 on a specific day each week and wait on hold to maybe eventually get through and hope there are appointments left is really bad service. Appointments are also only released for 1 week at a time, so if you can’t get a suitable time this week, you have to wait until the next week to try again. The appointments offered are also often telephone calls, which would be fine if the time could be scheduled rather than “sometime in the morning/afternoon” which is very difficult to manage around work. Overall, I’d give the actual medical service 4/5, 5/5 on some days, but the experience of using the surgery only 2/5.
