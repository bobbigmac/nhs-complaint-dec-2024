Given that hypertension is a serious condition requiring monitoring and timely intervention, this level of neglect is completely unacceptable. Coming from a developing country, I expected the UK’s healthcare system to be more efficient and patient-focused. However, the lack of care and communication at this practice is shocking. I strongly urge Shimmin & Partners to improve its responsiveness, duty of care, and communication with patients. If this level of service continues, it could have serious consequences for people’s health. Given my experience, I do not recommend Shimmin & Partners to anyone who values timely and attentive healthcare.
