Author: carole wright
Date: a month ago
Rating: 5 stars
Great Ayton health centre is a fantastic surgery. I originally moved from a small country practice when I moved to stokesley I wanted that feeling again. It’s a feeling of all the doctors and nurses knowing every bodies names and not to be lost in the system as are you can be in bigger practices. The staff are excellent, the doctor I go see is absolutely brilliant. The nurses all know I have a fear of needles and are so gentle and patient with me. If you ring the surgery at 8.30am they can usually fit you in the same day, not always as the appointments fill up pretty quickly. To me that’s a sign of a good practice. The rating from CQC is excellent. Patient care comes above all else. Great practice and I can’t thank them enough.
