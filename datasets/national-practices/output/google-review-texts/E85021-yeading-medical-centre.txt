I have been using this surgery for over 10 years and prior to this review, I would have given it a 1 star as I had often found their GPs very dismissive, always rushing you, not fully listening to you. I mean I even had 1 of the female doctors (Dr Sonali Bose) telling me literally as soon as I came in through the door, 'what can I do for you' even before I had a chance to get anywhere near the chair to sit down. She was so rude and lacked empathy during my appointment with her, that I burst into tears. She was only concerned I was crying because she didn't want me leaving her office in tears and people wondering if she upset me. Anyway, all of these were until I met the wonderful Dr Sana Ahsan who has been nothing short of wonderful, a God sent really. She patiently listens to my concerns with empathy, is thorough and actively looks for solutions, sometimes even going over and beyond to get a response when she sends a referral. I absolutely refuse to see anyone else apart from her. Thank you so much, Dr Ahsan. The nurse, Mrs Balvinder Johal (I think she is fairly new) is also very lovely and is great at her job. There is also a lovely guy at the reception, I think his name may be Ibrahim. He's always polite, with a smile and helpful both on the phone and in person. The female receptionist however comes across rude, reluctant to help and dismissive! The only other negative I'd point out is when you see the GP, they allow you to speak only about one issue (a notice about this is even there on the wall). And if you want to talk about any other thing, you need to book another appointment. I think this is such a hassle especially if it's something that requires urgent attention. As long as you are within your 10mins, then one should be able to discuss more than 1 issue!
