Very unhelpful systems in place for everything. When you need a medication review there is no communication about that. You find out you can't get your medication when you need it and aren't allowed to request it. At that point you have to use the phone system which tries to talk you out of phoning them at every stage. At that point they will let you have your medication late, and tell you that someone will contact you for a review. For the medication review, they might send you a text with a questionnaire to complete, at which point you assume it's done. It is not. 6-8 weeks later someone will call you first thing in the morning then immediately text to say you missed your medication review appointment, which nobody notified you was happening. The text tells you to call the surgery to arrange a medication review. When you call the surgery, they wonder why you have called for this, since it could be another 6-8 weeks before they can book you an appointment for a medication review. So the advice is for you to call the triage system at 8:30 on one of the few days that the pharmacist works. Of course you will have to call 20+ times before the line stops saying engaged and lets you join the long queue, again being reminded at every stage that they don't want you to call them. They like to record how many missed appointments from selfish patients are wasting how many hours of their time. But I can't help but wonder what percentage of the missed appointments and people blocking up the phone lines are people attempting to rebook an appointment that nobody told them they had.
