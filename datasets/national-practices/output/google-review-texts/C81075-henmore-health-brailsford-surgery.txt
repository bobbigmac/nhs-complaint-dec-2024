Author: Norm Peckett
Date: a year ago
Rating: 5 stars
In late September 2023 I was advised to get a small-ish wound on my face looked at by my doctor. This advice was given by a relative-in-law who had suffered skin cancer many years ago. Within an hour of ringing the practice and sending a photograph one of the team saw me and within a week I had an appointment with a specialist at Derby General who diagnosed a BCC (base cell carcinoma). Within a month I'd had another appointment with a surgeon and was booked in for a day operation to remove the man BCC and another small one on my forehead. I have just been discharged by the surgeon so I thought it time I thanked everyone in the 'chain' who passed me on for treatment. In a difficult period for the NHS it seemed to me to be an example of how it can deliver a really good service. Sincerest thanks again to everyone involved. Norm Peckett
