I had a very upsetting experience with both Murray’s Pharmacy and Glebefields Medical Centre. My doctor approved my pain medication on 27th August through Pharmacy2U. On 29th August, I cancelled that order so I could collect my medication locally. When I went to Murray’s Pharmacy, I was told they didn’t have capsules and to ask reception upstairs to change my prescription to tablets. Reception did change it, but instead of sending it to the local pharmacy, they sent it back to Pharmacy2U without telling me. Later that day I returned to Murray’s Pharmacy, but they still hadn’t received anything and again sent me back upstairs. When I spoke to reception a second time, the two ladies there were extremely rude and dismissive. They questioned me over and over about why I keep coming to the pharmacy, criticised me for not nominating a local one, told me I should have enough medication, and spoke to me in a way that made me feel judged and disrespected. I explained that I was travelling because my grandmother is dying, but they showed no compassion at all. This whole situation was stressful and distressing.
