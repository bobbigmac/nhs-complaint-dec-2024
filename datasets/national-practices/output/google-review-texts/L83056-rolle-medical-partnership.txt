Author: Alicia Kingsland
Date: Edited a day ago
Rating: 4 stars
Update 19 March 2026. I thought I should change my review from 1 star to 4 star. Since the implementation of their accurex system, this practice has done an incredible turn around in relation to its care and organisation. Same day response, helpful receptionists, no stray referrals, caring gp and nurses, who seem very thorough in their consultations. Face to face appointments where needed and telephone consults where not. They are definitely a practice I would recommend now. The only reason for keeping it at 4 star rather than 5, is that the majority of appointments (especially same day) are held at the hospital (Rolle) which is really really challenging to park at (or anywhere near). Also they use the pharmacy in the hospital for the pharmacy appointments which wasnt a good experience for my child. Disorganised. Chaotic. 3 weeks for a telephone consultation, longer for face to face. lengthy hold times on phone Dont do actions they say they will multiple times over, think they are so time pressed they don’t actually have time to read any clinical history or study other professionals clinic letters. Prescriptions written wrong multiple times (or sometimes not written at all!), many many phone calls to resolve - each time you have to hold 15 mins + for them to pick up the phone. No one takes ownership of problems or mistakes , you’re just added back to the pile and the process starts over again . Who knows where your personal information will be sent. Randomly referred one of my children to Torbay hospital although we live in Exmouth. Other child needed referral sent 3 times to rd and e for them to receive information. Receptionists are gatekeepers. They say they are not medically trained… Incredibly stressful, multiple times for multiple family members. Literally the worst possible service imaginable. Pick another gp practice. Anyone! We thought we had signed up to a local practice. Actually pretty much all appointments are by hospital where there is no parking whatsoever.
