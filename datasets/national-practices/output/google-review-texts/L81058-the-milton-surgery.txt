Been at this surgery for years (alongside my family) and it’s gone massively downhill sadly. Some of the admin/reception staff can be quite dismissive and it actually gives me anxiety calling them because of this. Appreciate they have a tough job but it doesn’t excuse it. Prescriptions never seem to be handled properly, items tend to be missing off repeat requests for myself and my kids when I go the pharmacy to collect. There’s no rhyme or reason to it and I’ve then had to make further appointments just to get them reinstated. I’ve been told prescriptions have been sent to the pharmacy when they clearly haven’t also. The GPs are lovely once you can get to them. No issue with them whatsoever. It’s very difficult to actually get an appointment. The econsult form gives an indication that if it’s not urgent then you’ll get a call at some point. It’s very hard to stay by your phone to wait for a call when you don’t even know what day it’ll happen. Would be better if you could just pre book an appointment!
