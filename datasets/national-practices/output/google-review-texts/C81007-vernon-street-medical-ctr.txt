Author: Nonnie Fox
Date: 2 weeks ago
Rating: 2 stars
The staff at this surgery are so kind. Unfortunately their decision to move to an AI appointment booking model, with no alternative via phone or in person, has impacted my ability to access my GP severely. I live with chronic pain and other disabilities, this booking system keeps telling me to call myself an ambulance and that no appointments are available for me to book. If these symptoms occurred in a generally healthy person they should absolutely go to hospital, but A+E would not be able to help me as it's not an acute condition. I tried calling to explain this situation and was met with "we can no longer book appointments over the phone", the lonely receptionist told me to try their Ask A Question option instead. I did so, however you only get 300 characters (including spaces) to ask said question. Trying to fit the relevant information, that would explain the need to see my GP and not A+E or yet another Physio referral, is impossible without it reading as hysteria. I can't go to another GP, the other Vernon St surgery also uses this system and I simply cannot consistently walk/bus) to further surgeries (I don't drive and taxis are not in the budget). I can't imagine that I am the only one being negatively impacted by this god awful system so if you need a new GP and are disabled? Look elsewhere if you can.
Practice response date: 2 weeks ago
Practice response:
Thank you for taking the time to share your experience Nonnie. We are sorry to hear that you have felt unable to access care in the way you need, and we appreciate you raising these concerns with us. We would like to reassure you that the new online triage and automated booking system has been introduced across all our access routes to ensure fair and consistent access for every patient. This does not mean that patients who are unable to use the online system are left unsupported. Our reception and care navigation team have been fully trained to complete the online triage with patients over the phone or in person at the front desk, using our practice iPads where needed. No patient should feel excluded from accessing care because of digital barriers. We have reviewed your case and can see that an appointment has been arranged for you with a clinician. We recognise, however, that adjusting to a new system can be challenging, especially for patients living with long‑term conditions or disabilities. The system has now been in place for two months, and while most patients are benefiting from shorter waits and no longer need to queue on the phone, we recognise that a small number of patients are still experiencing difficulties. Your feedback is extremely valuable in helping us understand these impacts, and we are actively reviewing and refining our processes as part of our full change management programme. If you would like us to discuss this further to explore reasonable adjustments, please contact the surgery. We are committed to ensuring all patients can access safe and appropriate care. Thankyou again for your feeback.
