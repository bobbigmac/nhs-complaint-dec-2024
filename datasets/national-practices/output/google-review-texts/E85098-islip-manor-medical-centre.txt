People without Clinical Empathy should not be working at a Local GP I contacted this GP practice after several days of severe and worsening neck pain that was affecting my ability to sleep and function normally. Despite clearly explaining the duration, severity, and escalation of my symptoms, I was met with a rigid administrative response and offered an online appointment scheduled many days later. There was no sense of urgency, no meaningful clinical triage, and no evidence that patient safety or suffering were being properly considered. Administrative staff in a GP setting are the first point of contact for unwell patients and therefore carry real responsibility. When that role is carried out without basic clinical empathy or appropriate escalation, it becomes a barrier to care rather than a gateway to it. Systems and policies are not an excuse for ignoring worsening symptoms. People who cannot recognise urgency, listen properly, or act with compassion should not be gatekeeping access to primary healthcare.
