Seems like a very shady and misleading method to fill 5 Star Reviews. That aside - I am not sure what has happened in the past few months, but this practice has gone down hill fast. You call first thing in the morning, receptionist now makes you triage online rather than taking a moment to speak to you, you then wait all day to hear back from them only to be told minimal information and that you now have to re-triage if you actually want an appointment. No matter what you are calling about, even if you were on the phone with your GP and the call got disconnected, the only thing the receptionist seems to say is “fill out the triage form online”. No follow up, no care for patients at all, receptionist literally says fill out the form and hangs up on you. Honestly appalling how little they seem to care these days when just months ago they were fine. I will not accept being busy as an excuse for providing no care and no empathy for your patients. I will say that the female physicians I have seen while visiting this practice have all been so empathetic and wonderful. My concerns are mainly with reception and overall organization and communication.
