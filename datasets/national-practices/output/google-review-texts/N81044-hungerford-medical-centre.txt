The GPs at this surgery are absolutely fantastic. They are genuinely caring, knowledgeable and thorough. They repeatedly demonstrate care that thinks out side of the box, and is actually individualised. I couldn’t fault the medical care here at all. However they are incredibly let down by the reception team. The customer service is non-existent. We have repeatedly been met with rudeness, unhelpful responses and a patronising tone that makes accessing care far more stressful than it needs to be. There’s a real sense of dismissiveness when you speak to them - as though callers are an inconvenience rather than the reason the service exists. It often feels like the phone version of ‘keyboard warriors’ which is such a shame when kindness, patience and basic professionalism are all that’s needed (and also what they’re paid to do) The contrast between the excellent GPs and the reception experience is stark. It’s disappointing that such a brilliant clinical team is overshadowed by a front of house approach that doesn’t reflect the standard patients should reasonably expect. I hope the surgery takes this feedback seriously because the potential for a truly outstanding practice is there but they are repeatedly let down by the women answering their phones.
