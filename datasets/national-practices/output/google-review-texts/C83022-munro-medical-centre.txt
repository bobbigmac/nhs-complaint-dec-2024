I requested written confirmation of my child’s registered address and the date it was changed. Instead of informing me that I could make a free Subject Access Request under UK GDPR for a system printout of the address history, I was given an OTC form and charged £50. Part of the documentation provided was simply a printout from the medical system containing address history data. Patients should be clearly informed of their right to request their personal data free of charge under data protection law, rather than being directed towards a paid administrative service without explanation. Very disappointing experience.
