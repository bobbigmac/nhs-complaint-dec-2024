## Medicines and tests

**147 reviews** in this category. 82% are 1-star.

### Summary

**Repeat prescriptions** (50%) are the main issue: requests rejected despite being on the system, having to phone to chase, and staff saying they will order but still rejecting. Patients describe prescriptions as "rejected," "delayed," or "not issued." **Test results** (6%) and **investigation follow-up** appear less often but are serious when raised—no results, delayed communication, or no clear next step after tests.

Some reviews praise named staff (e.g. phlebotomy, blood tests) for being quick and efficient. The negative majority focus on prescription handling and administrative failures.
