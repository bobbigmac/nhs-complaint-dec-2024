## Positive feedback

**113 reviews** in this category. 81% are 5-star.

### Summary

Patients praise **helpful staff**, **good access** (easier online booking, improved appointment availability), and **good clinical care** (thorough GPs, nurses who listen). Named staff (doctors, nurses, receptionists) are frequently thanked. Themes include: "always able to get an appointment," "much easier doing it online than by phone," "blood tests quickly and efficiently," "warm and welcoming."

Some note the practice has "improved a lot" or that PATCHS works well. A minority mention they would recommend the practice to others. This category captures explicitly positive reviews that balance the negative feedback elsewhere.
