## Clinical care

**474 reviews** in this category. 74% are 1-star.

### Summary

Many reviews mix clinical and access issues: **telephone access** (24%) and **reception conduct** (16%) appear because patients describe being unable to speak to a GP or being passed between reception and admin. **Clinical quality** (19%) covers perceived poor assessment, wrong advice, or feeling not taken seriously. **Continuity of care** (2%) and **listening and respect** (1%) are less frequent but significant—patients wanting to see their usual GP or feeling dismissed.

Positive feedback (15% 5-star) often singles out named doctors or nurses for being thorough, listening, and caring. The negative majority describe being fobbed off, not listened to, or receiving inconsistent advice.
