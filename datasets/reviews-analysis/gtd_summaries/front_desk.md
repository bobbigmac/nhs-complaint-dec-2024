## Reception and front desk

**386 reviews** in this category. 82% are 1-star.

### Summary

**Reception conduct** (37%) is the main concern: patients describe staff as rude, dismissive, unprofessional, or condescending. Complaints about not being listened to, being fobbed off, or feeling judged are common. **Telephone access** (30%) overlaps—reception handling of calls, putting people on hold, or giving inconsistent advice. Confidentiality breaches (names, DOB, address overheard in the waiting area) appear in several reviews.

Some patients acknowledge reception is under pressure and that staff "try to help," but the majority of feedback is negative. A minority of 5-star reviews praise specific receptionists or note recent improvement.
