## On-site experience

**30 reviews** in this category. 90% are 1-star.

### Summary

**Waiting room and delays** (67%) are the main concern: long waits after arrival, appointments cancelled while waiting, and distress for vulnerable patients (e.g. with young children or ailing health). Self check-in machine faults and poor in-practice flow are mentioned. Some describe sitting for 30–35 minutes before being told the appointment was cancelled, with no apology.

A small number of 4-star reviews acknowledge reception tries to help and that delays are sometimes unavoidable, but the majority are highly negative about the on-site experience.
