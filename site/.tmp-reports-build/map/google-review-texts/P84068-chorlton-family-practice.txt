Author: Stephen Pumfrey
Date: 2 weeks ago
Rating: 4 stars
I have been registered with this practice for over 20 years, and it has evolved and expanded. I am currently very satisfied with the care it provides. It has improved in recent years in two ways. First, online contact, via its website and the PATCHS system, has transformed my ability to get timely appointments, responses to queries and the general level of communication. Secondly, it seems that staff changes to the running of the reception desk and administration have improved efficiency and approachability. The latter was necessary. I know from my own experience and that of others that in the past it had led to mistakes, frustration and even to people transferring from the practice. As a result I am happy to recommend it strongly. One does not always get face-to-face appointments, consultations with GPs as opposed to other medical professionals, and not routinely with the same GP. This does not unduly bother me, although a return to seeing the same GP who knows my history would be welcome. The online contact is good and developing. However, I have found it difficult, both via PATCHS and the website, to navigate to the relevant page if I am looking to make a non-urgent enquiry rather than an urgent appointment. I expect there will be further improvements.
