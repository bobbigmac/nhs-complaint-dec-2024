To begin with, I think it's important to acknowledge how the clinic has improved in recent years. The introduction of an automated call queue process, the arrival of some kind & proficient administrative team members, and some integration with the app, Patient Access, have been changes for the better. But that being said, improvements from 'abysmal' to just 'very poor' should not be tolerated from our public services. Booking an appointment in advance is impossible, forcing clients to sit in a phone queue to see if there are any same day appointments as the only means of getting an appointment is a disgrace, seeing the same Doctor even in follow up to tests and referrals made by them is not possible (you have to go through the same day lottery), and, whilst some of the administrative team are kind, professional, and helpful, I'm sorry to say that some of them can only be described as vindictive; treating patients with nothing short of contempt. I know it's not always possible, but if you have any other option, choose a different clinic: The Elms Medical Centre is nothing short of disgraceful.
