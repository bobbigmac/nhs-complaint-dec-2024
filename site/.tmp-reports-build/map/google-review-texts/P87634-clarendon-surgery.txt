Practice response:
Dear Mr Dean Thank you for taking the time to share your feedback. We are genuinely sorry to hear how frustrating and upsetting your experience has felt. We recognise that navigating healthcare systems can be particularly challenging for patients who are neurodivergent, and feeling unheard or unsupported is never what we want for our patients. We are sorry if our website has been difficult to navigate. Our website is regularly reviewed by Greater Manchester Integrated Care Board (GM ICB) to ensure it meets accessibility standards; however, we understand that “accessible” does not always mean easy to use for everyone. We are currently reviewing alternative website providers to see how we can further improve clarity, navigation, and ease of contact. All our Doctors appointment requests are triaged by a GP to ensure patients are directed to the most appropriate clinician based on clinical need. We encourage patients to use our online consultation forms where possible, as these go directly to the duty doctor and support timely responses. However, online access is not the only option. Patients who are unable to, or prefer not to, submit requests online can telephone the practice, where a receptionist will complete the form on their behalf, or attend the surgery in person to receive support face to face. Please note that our phone lines are extremely busy, and waiting times inevitably increase when reception staff are assisting patients with completing forms, but this support remains available. All triage requests are reviewed and responded to within 24 hours via the contact method chosen by the patient. With regard to ADHD referrals, the specialist service is unable to accept referrals without a completed screening questionnaire being returned, as this is a requirement of the service. There is usually an option to return the completed form via an available link included in the accompanying text message, (valid for five days). We do however, appreciate the difficulty you have described and have taken this feedback on board. Going forward, we will also ensure that a return email address is clearly included within the message to provide an alternative way of responding. If you need further help or would like to discuss your concerns in more detail, our Reception Manager would be happy to assist and can be contacted via the surgery. We also have a dedicated Care Coordinator who can also support patients who need additional help accessing our systems. We also have an active Patient Participation Group (PPG), which meet regularly to share feedback, discuss patient experiences, and explore ways we can improve our services. We would welcome your engagement, and the Reception Manager can provide further information or help you get involved if this is something you would like to pursue. Thank you again for taking the time to share your experience. Your feedback is important to us and helps us continue to improve the care we provide.

Based on clinical need, patients may be offered a face-to-face appointment, telephone appointment, or be directed to another expert service such as pharmacy, physiotherapy, mental health team, social prescribing etc….
