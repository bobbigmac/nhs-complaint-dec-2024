This was my first visit to this GP clinic with my daughter, who was unwell. Unfortunately, the clinic has no parking, and nearby street parking is limited to 30 minutes. We had to park further away and ended up arriving 10 minutes late. Despite explaining the situation, they told us there was a strict 10-minute late policy and refused to see us. I had never heard of this rule before, and it was not communicated clearly beforehand. We were only referred to this clinic because our original GP had no availability, and the experience was extremely disappointing. I felt there was a lack of flexibility and compassion, especially towards a sick child. I would not recommend this clinic to parents or anyone who might require understanding and timely care.
