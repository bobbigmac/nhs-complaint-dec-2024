We are especially concerned to hear that you tried for 6 weeks to get an appointment. Appointments can be requested in three ways: Online using PATCHS By calling our reception team (our average call waiting time is around 3 minutes) By coming into the surgery, where a Patient Adviser can help you When sending a request, the more information you provide for the GP to review, the more likely the appointment offered will meet your needs. Clear details about your symptoms help the GP decide how quickly you need to be seen and what type of appointment is best for you.
