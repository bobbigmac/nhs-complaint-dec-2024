My mother was waiting for an eye operation and every time I called the surgery they claimed that they were waiting for the hospital to contact them. I don't blame the staff but I do blame the management for not hiring enough staff, not giving enough training and not having good procedures in place. So overall, not great, but the staff are trying their best. Expect things to go wrong but don't get angry with them and they will do their best to put things right as soon as you tell them what's gone wrong.
