This lack of consistency and the conflicting information from staff is extremely frustrating and unprofessional. Had I been advised at the time of my first call this morning that it was unlikely I would receive a call back, I would have rescheduled immediately while appointments were still available. Instead, I have been left waiting all day, only to be told I now need to start the process again next week. This entire experience follows an already distressing and, frankly, concerning blood test appointment. During the test, the nurse attempted to draw blood from my left arm but was unsuccessful. She then inserted the same needle into my right arm, again unsuccessfully, and a third time into another site using the same needle. I was left with severe bruising due to how aggressive the procedure was. Afterwards, I looked into this and was shocked to learn that reusing the same needle for multiple injection sites is not in line with health and safety regulations. I find it deeply troubling that this occurred, and it has left me feeling not only physically hurt but also incredibly unsafe in your care. All of this is part of a larger issue I have faced at this practice: I have repeatedly had to fight to have my symptoms taken seriously, and even getting basic follow-up care feels like a battle. The reception staff regularly provide conflicting, inaccurate, or vague advice, which only adds to the confusion and stress. At this point, I feel neglected and disrespected as a patient. I will be looking into registering with another GP practice, as I feel the standard of care at this one has significantly declined during my time here.
