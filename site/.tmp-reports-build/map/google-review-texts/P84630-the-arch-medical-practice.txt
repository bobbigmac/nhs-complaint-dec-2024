Dear Sydney Thank you for taking the time to provide feedback regarding your recent experience at the practice. I am sorry to hear about the distress and frustration this caused you and appreciate the opportunity to respond. My name is Lesley Hyde and I am the Practice Business Manager and part of my role is to monitor and investigate any concerns or complaints made to the practice. Please se below my response to the issues you have raised: Sample Collection Arrangements We apologise for the inconvenience caused when you attended the practice to drop off a sample at 6pm and were advised that it could not be accepted at that time. We understand how frustrating this must have been for you. For clarity, samples must be received by the practice by 4:00pm to ensure they can be included in the scheduled daily laboratory collection at 4:30pm. Samples received after this time cannot be accepted, as they may not be stored within the required conditions and timeframes set by the laboratory. Accepting samples outside of these parameters risks inaccurate or invalid results, which could affect patient care and safety. We do have a poster displayed in our reception area outlining the sample cut off time; however, we acknowledge your feedback that this information may not have been sufficiently visible prior to your visit. As a result of your comments, we have now placed additional signage on our front entrance door and updated our practice website to make sample submission times clearer and more accessible. You can view this update here: https://www.thearchmedicalpractice.co.uk/practice-information/sample-collection/ Our clinicians usually advise patients of the relevant sample drop off times when issuing sample pots. If this did not occur in your case, we sincerely apologise. We have reminded all clinical and reception staff of the importance of clearly communicating sample cut-off times to patients to help prevent similar situations in the future. Access to the Practice Building We are sorry that you felt unable to re-enter the practice and that this added to your distress. For staff safety reasons, when a receptionist is working alone at the front desk and their colleague is temporarily required elsewhere, the front door may be placed on a buzzer system. In these circumstances, patients are still able to gain access by pressing the buzzer and would be let in by a member of staff. We regret if this was not clear at the time and if it appeared that the practice was closed earlier than advertised. I can assure you however, the practice was still open. Pain Management and Ongoing Care We are sorry to hear about the level of pain you are experiencing. Prescribing decisions, including pain relief, are made by clinicians based on individual clinical assessment and current medical guidance to ensure patient safety. A GP would only prescribe “pain killers” if they felt there was a clinical indication to do so. If your symptoms are persisting, then I would encourage you to arrange a further appointment with a clinician so that your pain and ongoing symptoms can be reviewed again. Next Steps We take all feedback seriously and are grateful that you have brought these matters to our attention, which has already led to improvements in how we share information with patients. If you would like your concerns to be investigated further under the NHS complaints procedure, or if you wish to discuss my response further, please contact me at the practice (0161 226 0606). We are always looking for new members to join our PPG, if you would like further information of if you are interested in joining is, please click the below link: https://www.thearchmedicalpractice.co.uk/practice-information/patient-participation-group/ Yours faithfully Mrs Lesley Hyde Practice Business Manager
