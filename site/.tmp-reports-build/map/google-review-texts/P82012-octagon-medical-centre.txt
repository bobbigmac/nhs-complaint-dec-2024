Author: Kal Chudasama
Date: Edited 10 months ago
Rating: 3 stars
Patient Feedback I have been with the practice for around 36 years and have seen many changes over time, especially with staff turnover — particularly among receptionists, where there seems to be a high turnover and a lack of consistency. The doctors have also changed in recent years, but the current team is very good. I have spoken to and been seen by various GPs, and I appreciate that treatment options are always explained thoroughly, including both the pros and cons. It’s reassuring that I can always speak to a doctor over the phone when needed, and face-to-face or out-of-hours appointments are available upon request. However, I feel that out-of-hours appointments are not well-promoted, and more awareness should be raised around this service. Despite the many changes, the care and quality from the MSK team, doctors, and nurses remain consistently high. Prescription requests made via the app are handled promptly and sent quickly to my nominated pharmacy, which is very efficient. I have given 3 stars due to the attitude of some of the receptionists, which has impacted my overall experience. Suggestions and Areas for Improvement: 1. I would like the option to speak directly with a practice manager or someone in a managerial position in person to address any issues, without the need to email or raise a formal complaint. 2. Some reception staff could improve their communication and approach with patients. On one occasion, I went to collect my medical records and had a phone appointment booked to discuss results. The GP called during my work hours, and when I called the surgery back to explain, the receptionist was dismissive and defensive. I was told the receptionist who made the booking was in training, but the conversation lacked empathy and professionalism. This needs to be addressed to ensure better patient interactions. 3. I would like the ability to book appointments via the app again, as I was previously able to do. It would be great if the surgery could look into reinstating this feature. 4. Where possible, it would be helpful to have continuity by seeing or speaking to the same GP consistently. Lastly, I do appreciate the improved phone queue system — it’s a noticeable improvement compared to before.
