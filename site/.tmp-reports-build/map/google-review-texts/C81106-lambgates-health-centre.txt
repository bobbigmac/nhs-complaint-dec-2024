Practice response:
Dear Paige, Thank you for your feedback. We're glad to hear that you found the Accurx online form convenient for booking your appointment. However, we're sorry if the way your concerns were addressed during your consultation made you feel uncomfortable. Our aim is always to provide supportive and patient-centered care, and we appreciate your insight on how we can improve communication. A GP may ask, "What were you expecting from this appointment?" to better understand your concerns, expectations, and what you hope to gain from the consultation. This question helps ensure that the doctor addresses all your worries, provides the right level of support, and offers the most appropriate care. It’s not meant to suggest that you shouldn’t have attended or that you were expecting a specific outcome—rather, it helps the GP align their approach with your needs, whether that’s reassurance, further tests, treatment, or advice. If this ever feels unclear, you’re always welcome to ask for more explanation during your appointment. Kind Regards Lambgates Health Centre
