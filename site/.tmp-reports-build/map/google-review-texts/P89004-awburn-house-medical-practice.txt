I have been registered at Awburn House for over 20yrs and generally would consider myself to be an infrequent flyer of requiring GP services other than the normal required check ups and jabs! Over more recent years, with age, I’ve been seen for consultations for hernia repair to ectopic heart beats and menopause support through a shared care arrangement with my private menopause doctor. I have had no issues accessing a timely appointment based on a telephone triage of my difficulties. I have found the reception staff to be helpful, the doctors I have seen to be exceptional particularly Dr Hanna and Dr Burke, both listen and gave appropriate time to any concerns or issues. The online service is fantastic and a recent email request to trial an increase via my menopause doctor was actioned immediately. They are ever changing to make services more efficient and patient centred and access local support to receive alternative patient appointments when they are full, such as the clinic in Hyde. I work within the NHS clinically and have listened to many patient’s experiences of their own GP services, which are over stretched and under resourced, and feel thankful for the service Awburn House provides. Keep it up you are doing a good job!
