However, this still requires taking a significant amount of time out of the working day, which is simply not feasible for me. I have previously requested ring back which hasn't happened. Beyond my own circumstances, I am concerned that this system places real barriers in the way of elderly, disabled, or otherwise disadvantaged patients who may not be able to access or navigate online systems at all. While the online form may be efficient for some, it should not be the only realistic route to care. Under the NHS Constitution and the Equality Act 2010, GP surgeries are required to provide fair and equal access to healthcare. A system that depends almost exclusively on timed online forms does not meet that obligation. I therefore request that the practice urgently reviews its appointment booking system to ensure there are meaningful alternatives available — for example, allowing a proportion of appointments to be booked by phone or at reception, without disadvantaging patients who cannot use the online system. The irony was, that after coming away from the surgery, I went home to access the online form, only to receive a message that the online booking system was full and try again!
