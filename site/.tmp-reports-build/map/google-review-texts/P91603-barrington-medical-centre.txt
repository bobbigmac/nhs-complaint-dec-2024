We are keen to start trying for a family and so as a first point of call, I thought it would be sensible (and fair) to have a chat with my GP. While I appreciate the NHS is on its knees and really struggling, I couldn’t believe her attitude and approach. While I wasn’t expecting any form of treatment or even guarantees, I really was just hoping for an empathetic and consultative conversation about my options, next steps, tips and advice. I was so taken aback by her approach, I’m embarrassed to admit I became quite teary. At that point she smirked, waved her hand in front of me and said ‘this anxiety won’t be doing anything for you’. I couldn’t believe in this day and age one woman would speak to another in this bemittling manner.
