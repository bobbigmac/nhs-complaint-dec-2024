These agreements have mandated clauses set by our governing body, GM ICB and we follow these in order to remain safe and compliant with strict regulations on prescribing. Our practice policy is therefore one that allows our clinicians to work within their scope of practice, and we defer specialist prescribing to the clinicians who have expertise in their fields unless an SCA is in place.
