Author: Paul Dutton
Date: 3 years ago
Rating: 1 star
It would be comforting to feel that these reviews change anything, but here goes. Greenmount Medical Centre, on Brandlesholme Road was an excellent Practice until they amalgamated with others in the area. We were never told why that was, but experiences since suggest to me that per capita, here in Greenmount we were lucky to have a good GP:Patient ratio (with up to seven or eight front-line GPs), (nearly all of whom were truly excellent) some first-class front-line Nurses and a lovely team of Admin staff that always made you feel welcome. Waiting times for the telephone were rarely more than five minutes and you were always able to get a face to face appointment with a GP within two weeks,-sooner if you agreed to one other than your first choice. I remember there being facilities sometimes for early morning or evening/ Saturday appointments. The growth of the Practice to being part of TFH was introduced very quickly and stealthily; all of a sudden we had several times more (probably up to twenty times more) patients and far fewer front-line GPs to take care of them. Funding gave us money that was spent on privatising the administration of the practices, redefining of roles, introduction of a heinous telephone system and several iterations of computerised management, more than one legal constitution of the new “business” and total chaos for all the patients. Not surprisingly, many complained. Then many more, so that some more GPS and more qualified Nurses have left. I wrote to the Practice Manager three times over a number of months, also the Care Quality Commission and only received a reply seven months later from the Practice Manager that patronisingly complimented me on my IT skills ( they are not very good) and then claimed that the new systems in place are working and that the patients are no longer waiting so long to receive urgent treatment in the NHS. She also said that many of the problems had been down to COVID, although the decay was set in far before that. I now know that the Practice Manager has left, “for a far bigger job” elsewhere and that the real problem, as a few of us expected has been a migration of GPs away from their jobs here. Nevertheless, I believe successive governments have been complacent and blind in letting a lot of these things happen. Why do we need a Triage system to filter out less demanding cases to newly-defined roles for cheaper employees? Why do we need outside administrators to run our surgeries? Why do we allow barely-qualified staff to take on so many of the appointments GPs used to handle? The answer, I fear is “money” and yet so much of it has been spent and wasted elsewhere with expensive IT software that didn’t work (Lorenzo), hordes of HR and IT admin staff and all sorts of roles created in pursuit of Diversity and Equal Opportunities problems that seem to be far worse elsewhere than in the NHS. Our telephone staff at Greenmount number only two some days and they have to handle hundreds of incoming telephone calls and still answer the bell at the door! I for one would be glad to help for nothing if that would help, but please everyone, write to your MP to get some support for the restoration of 1:1 appointments.
