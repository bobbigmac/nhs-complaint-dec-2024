I have been with this practice for over 12 years, whilst it can be difficult sometimes to get an appointment, you can always turn up on the day and wait. As this is a first come first served system you will have to wait your turn. They operate a card system before they actually open so if you get there at say 07:30 and you're the first person there, pick the card marked 1 and when the practice opens at 09:00, book yourself in you will be seen first, simple! Get there after 09:00 go to the window they will book you in and you wait until your name appears on the wall message, again simple! If the waiting room looks full don't despair, many of those might be waiting for other services like nursing services, flue jabs, or child inoculations etc. If you are a registered patient and you need to speak to your GP but not necessarily see your GP you can ring, ask to speak to them and after a few questions from the staff they will decide if it might be better you attend in person if necessary or if not they will include you on a ring back from your own GP. When your GP either finds time between patients or at the end of the patients in the waiting room, you will receive a call back by your GP, again simple... I don't understand some of the complaints I have seen posted, Yes sometimes the staff could friendlier, but they too are human, with their own problems, and the person before you might have been one of those self centered winging buggers who turn up on the day hoping to jump in front of all those other patients who have appointments .
