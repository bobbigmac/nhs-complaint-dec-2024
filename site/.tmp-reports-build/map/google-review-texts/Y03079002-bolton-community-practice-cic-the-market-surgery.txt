Author: Jodie Leigh
Date: a year ago
Rating: 1 star
I’d be apprehensive to say this practice even deserves one star. I had been registered here for the past approximately 15 years and throughout attended this practice for multiple ongoing problems which I feel have been ignored and unseen to despite countless visits. The main problem is not the qualified healthcare staff but the overall system and management, I work in healthcare myself and understand that sometimes no matter how well patients are treated by staff they are still failed by the declining system of the NHS. Although I have felt ignored by some practitioners whose names I cannot remember, nurse Karen and mental health practitioner Ian have been great and have always listened to my health issues thoroughly. The reception staff answer calls with rude manner (I can’t say all as I have had kind receptionists previously but the majority of time not) and they appear uninterested in answering your queries and concerns. The appointment availability is unacceptable, attempting to arrange an on the day appointment at 8am often leaves you without one and trying to pre book an appointment leaves you with a 3-7 week wait and there is no acknowledgement of urgent symptoms. Sometimes they’ll tell you a GP will contact you over the phone on the day to assess your symptoms and then no one rings you all day from the practice. The final straw for me was when I rang last week describing tachycardia, feeling faint and CHEST PAIN and they told me they had no appointments for 4 weeks, neither did they advise me on what to do or where else to go for urgent medical attention. I have since changed practice and they have been a huge improvement.
