At over 30 weeks pregnant, I developed a persistent and irritating cough that worsened when lying down. With no other symptoms, I made an appointment at Quayside Medical Practice for medical advice. The practitioner (not a doctor) diagnosed it as reflux and prescribed Gaviscon. Unfortunately, there were no available appointments with a doctor either that day or for several weeks. However, after several days of taking the medication, my cough worsened to the point where I couldn’t sleep or eat. My throat became increasingly painful, and the cough became uncontrollable. I returned to the clinic and was seen by the same practitioner, as there were still no appointments with a doctor. During this appointment, I was in tears, explaining how difficult it was to manage daily life due to the constant coughing and pain. The practitioner advised me to stay strong and continue taking the Gaviscon and painkillers. Despite this, my condition continued to deteriorate. Eventually, the pain in my throat and lungs, along with the uncontrollable cough, became unbearable. I went to A&E, where an X-ray revealed a chest infection, and I was prescribed antibiotics. Despite completing the first course of antibiotics, my symptoms worsened, with severe pain around my lung and ongoing coughing. I had to return to A&E for further treatment, where I was given a higher dose of antibiotics and morphine for the intense pain (as other painkillers were ineffective), which was caused by a rib fracture from the persistent coughing. After completing the second course of antibiotics, my symptoms finally began to improve. Looking back, I feel that my concerns were not properly addressed by the practitioner at Quayside Medical Practice. Despite being heavily pregnant, I was not seen by a GP, and my symptoms were dismissed, leading to significant delays in receiving the proper treatment. This experience has taught me the importance of seeking care at A&E for serious symptoms, rather than relying solely on the advice of a clinic. The entire ordeal was traumatic, disrupted my family routine, and led to a prolonged recovery.
